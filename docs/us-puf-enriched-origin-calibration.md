# PUF enrichment followed by calibration at original household grain

`enriched_origin_calibration.run_enriched_calibration` connects the reviewed
survey/lineage, PUF diagnostic and original-origin cap implementations in a
26-node graph. Its initial execution evidence uses invented inputs and targets.
It does not authorize a genuine fit, release, tax calculation or CD scoring.

The source composition preserves the reviewed cap branch `0e5c73e8` and PUF
consumer `4c700ed6`. Their common survey ancestor is `74f71138`. The new runner
and count adapter have their own implementation identity. Upstream source,
PUF, count computation, solver, thresholds and store implementations are
unchanged. Acceptance of this composition also requires the PUF consumer's
pending final review; a source transport never upgrades its fit-admission flag.

## Execution and the prefit boundary

The runner executes prefixes of 17, 20, 23, 24 and 26 nodes. The 20-node prefix
contains the actual donor, host and recipient matrix and excludes fit/apply/
attach nodes. Before allowing fitting, the runner reconstructs the qualified
donor and transport, authenticates their materialized values and producer,
checks the full host against the original clone, and reconstructs the actual
matrix and placement. Donor S006 supplies donor DESIGN weights only.

The six PUF nodes retain the development recipe: one E00900 target, the wage
proxy and four MARS indicators, two trees, seed 578 and zero tolerance 0. Both
survey arms use their detail clone role. This output remains a development
diagnostic outside tax inputs and scoring admission.

The count adapter declares the attached float64 PUF output as an input. This
creates a real attachment-to-count dependency: graph declaration order has no
execution meaning. After validating its complete declaration and context, the
adapter invokes the unchanged national age-count implementation on exactly
its person/household projection. It declares platform-bitwise scope because
its new dependency has that scope. The calibration's structural dependency
then follows the completed population, including counts and PUF attachment.

A cold staged pass visits 110 nodes: 26 unique kernel calls and 84 prefix hits.
A complete REQUIRED pass restores 110 node visits; the final manifest reports
26 hits. Fresh replay makes all graph kernels and graph source codecs fatal.
Fixture-definition and target setup remain separate work and are reported as
such. A final-manifest count is not the total work across all five prefixes.

## Original anchors and enriched successors

The development calibration retains the original 18 S0101 training cells,
500 Adam steps, learning rate 0.05, seed 0, incoming multiplier 4, original
design multiplier 8 and free mass. Invented tests use the existing invented
target bundle. No heldout family or target standard-error objective is added.

Before fitting or calibration, the native/clone lineage freezes each original
survey household's prepared DESIGN anchor D and preclone IMPORTANCE anchor I.
Each descendant retains its original unhalved DESIGN anchor; the group cap is
`sum(descendant weights) <= min(8D, 4I)`. For invented D=10 and I=6, the cloned
incoming weights are 3+3 and the group upper bound is 24. The pair 20+4 is within
that bound; 24+24 is not. The repeated stored DESIGN references do not create
two independent budgets. The accepted final weights are the solver's checked
float64 vector, without an additional exp/log conversion.

The issued weight successor includes the actual calibration node key and the
validated diagnostic artifact's type, producer key, artifact key and payload
digest. Its outer storage key also binds that complete successor payload.

The original clone receipt continues to describe the original clone content.
It is not relabelled after adding PUF values or count columns. A separate issued
enriched-parent binding authenticates the exact original budget, source and
PUF artifact chain, materialized enriched content, typed metadata, Frame mass
log and full graph ledger. A distinct successor binding permits only the
declared calibration weight transition and its one actual mass record.

The materialized verifier reconstructs the selected mask and reconciles every
ordered detail output against actual matrix, application-state and raw-target
codecs on both cold execution and replay. It also recounts the 18 household
columns. A new full-content hash cannot substitute for those checks. Budget
and diagnostics artifacts must agree with their producer receipts and exact
accepted weight digest.

## Cache identity and physical preservation

Within each live FILTER, matrix, attach, count and reweight transition, carried
cells retain their physical storage, column order, axes, typed metadata, masks,
weights, original anchors and ownership. Frame.mass_log and
Population.mass_ledger are checked separately. Structural transitions preserve
the entire ledger prefix plus the recomputed actual record.

At explicit cache replay boundaries only, the accepted store's nullable
integer/boolean encodings may clear hidden backing values under missing slots.
Dtype, mask and observed bits remain exact. All other table dtypes retain the
physical comparison. The three source-owned demographic state columns retain
their explicit zero-null-backing invariant even at replay boundaries.

Object-valued strata use the actual store's tagged scalar encoder across cache
loads, because object-array bytes contain process-local addresses. Their typed
scalar tags, values, float bits, masks and ordered axes remain exact. This is
not a general object-column or pandas-equality exception. The initial scoped
invented Frame inventory contains no object-backed table columns. Neither
correction changes the accepted store format.

The v2 store and its live Population observer affect shared graph identities.
The old genuine v1 store cannot be relabelled or treated as v2 hits. Any later
genuine execution needs a fresh bounded namespace, pinned source/runtime/input
admission, the same full verifiers, and its own reviewed execution proposal.

## Limits and later pruning

This connection tests engineering contracts, not national or CD accuracy,
concentration quality or a scientifically sufficient PUF model. B19001 and
B25003 remain reserved, with no scoring exposure or new target activation.
Generated clone IDs do not create independent survey support: support counts
and ESS diagnostics need the original source/cohort/native origin clusters.
Any later household-preserving pruning must retain those origins and original
budgets, recompute the retained group sums and diagnostics, and bind the new
population explicitly. This change does not implement or approve pruning.
