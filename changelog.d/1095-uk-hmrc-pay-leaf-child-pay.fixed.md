The HMRC pay leaf (`hmrc_spi_pay`) now reads a dependent child's pay. It takes `CHILD.CHEARNS` from the raw child tab, just as it takes an adult's `ADULT.INEARNS`, and `frs_hmrc_spine_leaves` declares and checks `child.tab`. Before this, a child whose pay was above the personal allowance counted as a taxpayer with no HMRC employment income. They dropped out of the employment-income rows or sat in too low an income band. The fix comes from Vahid's final review of PolicyEngine/microcosm#1663 and María's reply of 19 November 2026.
