The lock's UK side moves from policyengine-uk 2.123.0 to 2.130.1, and the UK extras of microcosm-build, microcosm-data and microcosm-frame require >=2.130.1 (microcosm#1095). The branch first moved to 2.127.0 and then, after review, to 2.130.1, the first release published after it (2.128.0 to 2.130.0 never reached PyPI). policyengine-core moves to 3.33.3 on the UK side, which is what policyengine-uk's own floor resolves, and stays at the US side's own 3.32.5 pin (microcosm#1086); a test pins the engine and core that every UK install path resolves. Nothing the UK build writes is renamed, removed or re-typed. What the build's engine reads return does change. Up to 2.127.0: `uc_reported_capital` and `pension_credit_reported_capital` become formulas of the new input `benunit_reported_capital`, a stored value still wins, and a stored `uc_reported_capital` is projected by GDP per head as Pension Credit's already was (pe-uk#2201), which completes microcosm#1200's property rule for Universal Credit; a claimant whose partner cannot claim is assessed as a single person (pe-uk#2084); DWP's announced 2025-26 rates replace CPI projections (pe-uk#1925); the Housing Benefit Guarantee Credit passport needs receipt and each family's Council Tax Reduction uses its own scheme; a unit's own claimant or partner no longer counts as its child; Carer's Allowance stops above the earnings limit for reported recipients too; savings and dividends are banded above property income; the employer Class 1 secondary threshold is £5,000 a year; and the road-fuel litre proxies are re-based on DESNZ pump prices. From 2.128.0 to 2.130.1: families move onto Universal Credit when DWP closes a legacy benefit they receive, tax credits from 2025-26 (pe-uk#2119); statutory sick, maternity and paternity pay count as Universal Credit earned income, and paternity pay in household benefits and HBAI income (pe-uk#2365); Gift Aid extends the basic rate band instead of reducing allowances (pe-uk#2271); a child on the working parent childcare entitlement keeps the universal hours (pe-uk#2177); the benefit cap's ESA and disability exceptions follow the claimant and partner (2.129.0) and its earnings exception counts statutory pay (pe-uk#2399, 2.130.1); a reported income-related ESA or income-based JSA award ends when the claimant or partner is in remunerative work (2.129.1); and the Pension Age Winter Heating Payment counts in household income. Six parts of the build follow. The CGT taxable-income measure is net income less the allowances, as the engine now stacks gains on it, and no longer takes Gift Aid off the allowances. The salary-sacrifice relief by rate class extends a Gift Aid donor's bands by the grossed-up gift, as the engine's `earned_income_tax` now does (ITA 2007 s.414(2)(b)), so the classes still add up to the engine's tax; without it the national calibration refused, and the three relief rows declare the extension in the contract. The universal-only childcare row counts children on the universal hours who are not on the extended entitlement, as the published difference does. The UC take-up lockstep test names a third case the population does not mirror: a pension-age family moved onto Universal Credit at the legacy closure (SI 2014/1230 reg 60A). The concept mapping declares `would_claim_uc_at_legacy_closure` left at the engine's default, so every family moved off a legacy benefit claims Universal Credit; uk-data#534 draws it at DWP's Move to UC claim rates, which are not yet a Chronicle fact, so the calibration's 2025 Universal Credit rows rest on that default. And the liquid-asset reason names the three company-capital inputs of Universal Credit's regulation 77 (2.130.0), which default to no company and which the build sets none of. Re-derived with no change to data: the uprating pins (the version stamp; every pinned value, the ONS population growth included, is unchanged), the concept coverage (288 inputs to 299: eleven owned-company inputs and `would_claim_uc_at_legacy_closure` join, `uc_has_limited_capability_for_work` leaves; the build writes none of them), the stage texts that cite the locked engine, the H2 fixture's dependency, the coverage manifest's source digests and `APPROVED_UV_LOCK_SHA256`. One engine test changed its expectation at 2.127.0: the single-claim rule gives a 24-year-old with a 17-year-old partner the single young allowance.
