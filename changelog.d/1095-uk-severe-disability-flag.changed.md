The UK build no longer stores `is_severely_disabled_for_benefits`, the tax credit severe disability condition, and leaves it to policyengine-uk's formula (A4 of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095; María's signature of 2026-11-11). The formula reads the DLA care, PIP daily living and Attendance Allowance categories the build stores, and Armed Forces Independence Payment, which the build never writes, so on the stored categories it is the condition `frs_disability` used to store. A stored copy overrode the formula in every projected year, so a change to PIP's points thresholds never reached the tax credit severe disability elements or the working-age council tax reduction's child amounts. `frs_disability`, the SPI income chain and `spi_benefit_coherence` stop writing it, the release and calibration boundaries drop a stored copy should one appear, and the eFRS parity register, the release input coverage manifest and the export gate list it as formula-owned.
