A check-only loss-share floor per target family for the UK local dense solve (microcosm#1207). `UK_LOCAL_FAMILY_LOSS_SHARE_FLOORS` in `uk_runtime/local_doctrine.py` sets the minimum share of the loss weight that DESNZ's local energy family (2.5%) and the BUS01 region journeys (1.25%) must carry. `assert_uk_family_loss_share_floors` refuses a weight vector that leaves either family below its floor. It runs on the row-metadata rules' weights in `prepare_uk_full_solve`, and the problem's bindings record it beside the loss-weight receipt. The weights themselves do not change.
