The UK lock moves from policyengine-uk 2.151.0 to 2.162.3, the newest on PyPI; policyengine-core stays at 3.33.3 (microcosm#1095). From 2.155.0 the engine takes population growth from the OBR's March 2026 determinants, so the per-capita mixed-income and GDP indices of the uprating pins move, and so do the household-weight projection the release divides by, the bonus sacrifice anchor and the growth path the CGT projection gate pins. From 2.162.2 the engine's `savings_interest_income` is the interest outside tax-free accounts: a file with no ISA income contract is read as gross, and the engine takes `tax_free_savings_income` out of it when it loads the file. The frame still writes gross interest. So the HMRC SPI interest amounts by income band now measure the interest outside ISAs, as SPI Table 3.7 counts it, and the ONS interest diagnostic adds the ISA part back. `individual_savings_account_interest_income` now has a formula, so the concept mapping no longer binds it. The concept coverage adds 22 inputs. `would_claim_wales_childcare_offer` (2.155.0) stays at its default, recorded with its error, until Chronicle carries the Offer's take-up (María's ruling of 2026-10-25). These new inputs also stay at their defaults, recorded with their errors: the three Housing Benefit inputs (main-phase ESA, the protected family premium and the severe disability premium's joint-occupier exclusion) and the five exceptions to the two-child limit (2.161.0); Northern Ireland's Energy Price Guarantee inputs (2.161.1); and the ISA dividend and non-cash ISA interest inputs, the Lifetime ISA's flows and the two purchase flags (2.162.2).
