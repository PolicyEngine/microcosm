The UK lock moves from policyengine-uk 2.151.0 to 2.162.3, the newest on PyPI; policyengine-core stays at 3.33.3 (microcosm#1095). From 2.155.0 the engine takes population growth from the OBR's March 2026 determinants, so the per-capita mixed-income and GDP indices of the uprating pins move, and so do the household-weight projection the release divides by, the bonus sacrifice anchor and the growth path the CGT projection gate pins. From 2.162.2 the engine's `savings_interest_income` is the interest outside tax-free accounts: a file with no ISA income contract is read as gross, and the engine takes `tax_free_savings_income` out of it when it loads the file. The frame still writes gross interest, and now declares that legacy-gross contract itself, in every H5 it writes and in the datasets it hands the engine, rather than rest on the engine's default. So the HMRC SPI interest amounts by income band now measure the interest outside ISAs, as SPI Table 3.7 counts it, and the ONS interest diagnostic adds the ISA part back. The SPI interest counts by band now count the recipients of interest outside ISAs too: their filter reads the frame's gross interest less its ISA part, so a person whose only interest is ISA interest is no longer counted. `isa_cash_interest_flat_rate_charge` (2.162.2), a tax on the cash interest in non-cash ISAs from 2027-28, joins the HBAI deductions that the survey-concept gross income adds back, in the LCFS, NTS and ETB VAT stages. From 2.162.2 the engine keeps each student loan holder's recorded plan and repayment when it projects the dataset (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2636), where it used to relabel them by a course started at 18, so the SLC rows read the plans the stage dated, at 2025. `individual_savings_account_interest_income` now has a formula, so the concept mapping no longer binds it. The concept coverage adds 22 inputs. `would_claim_wales_childcare_offer` (2.155.0) stays at its default, recorded with its error, until Chronicle carries the Offer's take-up (María's ruling of 2026-10-25). These new inputs also stay at their defaults, recorded with their errors: the three Housing Benefit inputs (main-phase ESA, the protected family premium and the severe disability premium's joint-occupier exclusion) and the five exceptions to the two-child limit (2.161.0); Northern Ireland's Energy Price Guarantee inputs (2.161.1); and the ISA dividend and non-cash ISA interest inputs, the Lifetime ISA's flows and the two purchase flags (2.162.2).
