The 13 HMRC SPI savings-interest band targets now reach the 2025 calibration year in two legs (uk-data#541, microcosm#1095, María's ruling of 9 October). They move to the dataset's year, 2024, by ONS household interest income, the index of the variable's history. From there they move by GDP per head, the index policyengine-uk's dataset projection grows `savings_interest_income` with. The SPI donors take the same path: the stage rebases them to 2024 by the history index, and the engine projects the frame to 2025.

The band targets' 2023-24 to 2025 factor moves from 1.182 to 1.192. The donor rebase is unchanged. An engine lockstep test holds every indexed SPI component's rebasing index, and every HMRC SPI amount family's projection index, to the engine's projection table, and names savings interest's history index apart.
