SPI savings interest is now rebased, and its 13 HMRC band targets projected, by GDP per head (uk-data#541, microcosm#1095, María's ruling of 9 October). GDP per head is the index policyengine-uk's dataset projection grows `savings_interest_income` with. Both had followed the ONS household-interest index that the variable's `uprating` attribute names, which grew 2.27 times from 2022 to 2024 against GDP per head's 1.09. The engine projects a loaded dataset by its uprating table, though, and the attribute only carries a situation input forward.

The SPI donors' savings interest falls by about 52%, and the band targets by 8.4% (their 2023-24 to 2025 factor falls from 1.182 to 1.083). An engine lockstep test holds every indexed SPI component's rebasing index, and every HMRC SPI amount family's target index, to the engine's projection table.
