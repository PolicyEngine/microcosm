Three local-authority target rows from DESNZ's sub-national energy statistics bind in the UK local dense solve (microcosm#1207). They are `desnz.subnational.electricity_kwh`, `desnz.subnational.gas_kwh` and `desnz.subnational.gas_connected_households`, census family `domestic_energy`, for England, Wales and Scotland. The kWh rows keep DESNZ's split between authorities at the `lcfs_consumption` stage's Energy Trends level, and DESNZ's region rows stay unbound (`no_higher_control`). The gas-connected rows are each authority's census households times its gas meters over its electricity meters. They join the census household partition (`census_gas_connected_share`) and take the census-household uprating. The local metrics `energy/electricity_kwh`, `energy/gas_kwh` and `energy/gas_connected_households` read each household's stored spend at the dataset period and turn it back into kWh at the stage's prices. Orkney and Shetland, with no mains gas, are signed deferrals. A new vendored resource, `desnz_local_energy_facts.json`, carries the authority meter counts and the LFS household rows the appliers read.
