The UK local line binds region controls for the high-value homes family (microcosm#1301, the issue's 6a). Three new two-level targets, `microcosm.high_value_homes.over_1m_by_region`, `over_1_5m_by_region` and `over_2m_by_region`, bind the derived Land Registry stock at the nine English regions and Wales. They carry the same measurement block and record-set selector as the constituency and local-authority cells, so each row is the cross-grain control for its area cells, and the three `no_higher_control` local routes in `uk/cross_grain_declarations.json` are gone.

**Local line only.** A new contract key, `calibration_lines`, names the lines a target binds on; these three declare `["local"]`. The national spine's `main_residence_value` is the WAS donor's, not a Land Registry placement. The national compiles now merge the derived facts beside the Chronicle feed too, as the local ones already did: both loaders in `full_targets.py`, the release-cut certifier, the compile-parity tool's national receipt, the national reference generator (`--derived-facts` names another resource), the UC matrix diagnosis and the incumbent-surface evaluation. `load_uk_national_target_inputs`, the certifier's calibration reference and the UC matrix diagnosis then drop the local-only rows right after the compile, before reconciliation, the measure exclusions and the frozen-register check. The national registry version is therefore unchanged. `register_completeness.calibration_line` receipts what each line dropped.

**Fan-out.** `tools/generate_uk_target_references.py` now honours a two-level target's `area_scope` by level. The region rows fan out over the English regions and Wales, which is pinned at country level `W92000004`. Scotland and Northern Ireland are not cells.

**Measures.** The bound values `owner_occupied_home_over_gbp_1m`, `_1_5m` and `_2m` are per-household indicators computed in `measure_simulation.py`. Each applies `high_value_home_mask` at the threshold's frame-basis value to the frame's stored `main_residence_value` and `tenure_type`, never an engine-uprated value, exactly as the local cells and the release gates do. A persisted column of any of the three is refused.
