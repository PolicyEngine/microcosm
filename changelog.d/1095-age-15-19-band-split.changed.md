The two national UK age targets for 15 to 19 by sex are split into ages 15, 16-17 and 18-19 (uk-data#563, microcosm#1095; María's ruling of 2026-10-13). A fit to a five-year band leaves the ages inside it free to move: uk-data#563 found weights that fit the band while holding 35% too many 16-year-olds and about half the 18- and 19-year-olds, and nothing in this contract bound the mix inside the band either. The ages 16 and 18 are where the child and qualifying-young-person rules change. The six rows read the same ONS mid-year estimate by single year of age as the two they replace, so each sex's three rows sum to its old band. The contract has 400 targets (396 before) and 1,283 active references (1,279). In the registry accounting the incumbent's `ons/female_15_19` and `ons/male_15_19` move from mapped to excluded, since no single row has their definition, and the six rows are contract-only declarations; the compile-parity receipt signs the two incumbent rows as fixture-only and the six as ledger-only. Within the `ons_population` family every other row's loss weight falls to 149/153 of what it was.
