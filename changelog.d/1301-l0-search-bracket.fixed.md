The `target_records` L0 budget search no longer clamps a warm start into its bracket, re-probes a penalty it has measured, or bisects a bracket that has collapsed onto a bound (microcosm#1301). `calibrate` takes a `budget_search_bracket` whose default stays [1e-7, 10], so every caller that does not pass one, the US contract included, searches as before. A warm start outside the bracket is refused rather than silently moved to the bound while the receipt recorded the value requested. The search never spends a probe on a penalty within 1e-6 decades of one it has measured; a probe on the floor or ceiling that asks to go past it stops the search as `search_floor_reached` or `search_ceiling_reached`, and a bracket narrowed onto a measured penalty stops it as `bracket_exhausted`, beside the existing `acceptable_within_tolerance` and `budget_exhausted`. The warm edge probe now follows the first probe's own steer: a warm start on the floor that asked for a smaller penalty was sent half a decade upward. When the edge still asks to go past it, the search probes the bound itself next, so a budget out of reach stops there by name within three probes instead of bisecting toward the bound until every probe is spent; one within reach is interpolated between the bound and the edge. The search receipt and its `budget_search_done` event record the bracket and the stop reason.
