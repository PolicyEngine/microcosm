The SPI band donors place each tape record by the total income it realises on the frame as SPI's total income holds it: its income leaves at their factors net of employment expenses, without the gift leaves, which are reliefs, plus the rest of the published total, no longer floored at zero (#1428; Vahid's review). Before, a record's gifts and employment expenses added to its income, so a donor with large gifts of shares, or with expenses, sat in too high a band. With every factor at one the realised total is the published one. The savings rebase bands the tape the same way.
