`Frame.with_weights`, `Frame.place`, `Frame.select` and `load_frame_checkpoint` no longer deep-copy every table. Under pandas copy-on-write, frames from the three `Frame` operations share the memory of unchanged columns with their source, and the loader keeps the columns it decoded without a second copy. `calibrate` gains `return_frame=False`, which runs the same weight checks and returns the weights and diagnostics with `frame=None`.
