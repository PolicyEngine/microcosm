The UK lock moves from policyengine-uk 2.164.2 to 2.165.1 (microcosm#1095). 2.164.3 (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2736) counts other investment income in market income and HBAI household net income, and 2.164.4 (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2746) in `capital_income`. So the stages that band households on HBAI income with its deductions added back now count it, as the LCFS, NEED and NTS gross incomes they match do, and the WAS stage's `capital_income` predictor now matches the donor's gross income from all financial investments (`DVGIINVR8`). 2.165.0 (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2750) adds the company car and car fuel benefits in kind and the Class 1A NICs on them; the build writes none of their nine person inputs, so both benefits stay zero, as before, and the inputs are signed at their default. 2.165.1 (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2754) lets a family keep Tax-Free Childcare in place of a smaller Universal Credit award, and takes no debt deductions from an award only a reform creates. No economic assumption moves, so the vendored engine pins, the bonus-sacrifice anchor and the CGT projection path keep their values; only their version stamps move.
