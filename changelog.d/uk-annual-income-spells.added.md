The UK spine now records each adult's year of income beside their current income. HMRC's income statistics count the income each taxpayer received over a tax year (they cover individuals liable to UK income tax), while the spine's income columns are current, annualised incomes (usual weekly pay times 365.25 / 7), so a person who started, left or changed a job during the year sits in another HMRC band than their current pay gives, or in none. The year the descriptors describe is a proxy for the tax year: the 12 months before each interview (April 2024 to March 2025). A new stage, `frs_annual_income_spells`, runs after `frs_hmrc_spine_leaves` and before the SPI support channel. It reads the FRS 2024-25 adult, job and household tabs (pinned by size and sha256, read selectively) and writes six person descriptors: the share of the year paid at today's employee pay and at today's self-employment profit; annual pay from other spells, chiefly a recent leaver's last job at its usual gross pay, scaled to the DV pay concept by the stage's measured ratio and uprated by average earnings, else a donor's rate; ceased self-employment; and the shares of the year a private pension (after retiring in it) and contributory JSA, ESA and Incapacity Benefit (after leaving work) were in payment. No current column changes, so poverty, benefits and the engine's tax are unchanged; the descriptors are inputs for the calibration's annual HMRC measures, which follow separately.

On the SPI channel, `hmrc_spi_income_spine` holds a drawn row's descriptors to its drawn incomes. The tape's income is the tax year's, so each share is 1, and the twin's other-spell amounts stay only where the tape drew none of that income; an undrawn copy keeps its twin's descriptors. A new release-blocking stage gate, `uk_stage_frs_annual_income_spells`, checks the descriptors' invariants, the measured usual-to-DV pay ratio (0.95 to 1.10), annual over current employee earners (1.0 to 1.15) and, where ten or more households carry it, that at least 0.7 of the pay outside the current job is observed rather than drawn from a donor. The receipt follows UKDS disclosure control: a support band per spell source, and weighted figures only over ten or more households. The descriptors join the export surface's reviewed extra columns, the SPI income family requires the stage before it, and the roster moves from 47 to 48 stages.
