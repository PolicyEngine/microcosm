The ONS Annual Population Survey's people in employment by SOC 2020 major group bind as nine calibration targets (microcosm#1490, María's ruling of 4 November 2026), one per major group, each over the UK and the twelve region-tier areas (108 references). They read Nomis APS218 for January to December 2025, all persons and all employment types, through PolicyEngine/chronicle#411. Each counts the people whose `soc2020_major_group` is that group and whose `employment_status` is full-time or part-time employed or self-employed. Out-of-work adults keep their last job's group and are not counted, and the APS also counts unpaid family workers. Before these rows, nothing in the fit fixed how employment splits across occupations, which PolicyEngine/uk-ai-study#16 reads. Sub-major groups are not targets: the sub-major draw takes them from the same APS records.
