The dense UK line's `uk_local_target_fit` gate reads its own reviewed-exclusion register, `uk/local_target_fit_reviewed_exclusions.json`, committed empty, so a failing local cell can be signed after a build by a data-only change (microcosm#1301). Keys are the gate's `family/area_code/metric` cell names. An in-force entry lifts the fence for its cell only and the cell stays bound in the solve; stale, expired and not-yet-in-force entries fail, and an entry scoped by `release_lines` to another line stands aside. The gate now evaluates entries on the build's review date, the clock the other register-reading gates share, rather than the wall clock, and records `evidence_absent` without it. The `uk_local_area_support` exclusions become gate-only: an entry keyed `local_authority/<code>` or `constituency/<code>` excuses its area at the gate and defers none of its cells, and an entry for another line stands aside. The Isles of Scilly and City of London cell deferrals and the families bound despite the floor are now constants of `tools/generate_uk_local_target_references.py` (`SUPPORT_FLOOR_DEFERRED_AREA_IDS`, `SUPPORT_FLOOR_BOUND_FAMILIES`), held in lockstep with the register by tests, so the generator reads nothing from it and the committed local references and membership regenerate byte-identical. The size evaluation's red-row ledger keeps signed cells, which still breach the bound.
