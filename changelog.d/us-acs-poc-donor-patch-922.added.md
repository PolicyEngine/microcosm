`tools/patch_us_acs_donor_columns.py` writes a patched copy of a published US national donor H5 for the County Files proof of concept's ACS staging (microcosm#922, plan microcosm#1647). It adds reviewed all-zero columns on the entity the US engine gives them (`strike_benefits`: no donor or ASEC pppub23–26 row has `OI_OFF` 12; `passive_partnership_s_corp_income`: the donor holds partnership and S-corp income as one combined `partnership_income`), refuses a column that is already present with any other value, keeps every other table, column, dtype and root attribute, and writes a receipt with both SHA-256 digests, the row counts, each added column with its reason, and the tool's commit. A restore step for the three ASEC work columns is reserved for a later commit. The published donor is read only, and no default changes.
