Three held-out checks join the UK register's rows for young people (María's go of 18 November 2026, PolicyEngine/microcosm#1663).

- **RTI pay at 18 to 24.** PAYE RTI's payrolled employees aged 18 to 24 times their mean gross monthly pay times 12, averaged over 2025 (`monthly_window_count_x_mean`), against the model's employment income of its employees at the FRS interview at that age. Their count binds, so the row reads pay per young employee, which the static cost of the employer NICs relief for young employees rests on.
- **The young PIP award mix.** DWP's PIP cases in England aged 16 to 24 with the daily living component at the enhanced rate, and with the mobility component at the enhanced rate, against the model's people with that age and award. England's PIP binds at 16 to 19 and 20 to 24, so these rows check the share at the enhanced rate within those bands. Each cell and its complement rest on three or more survey households, unlike the award-by-age crosses that keep the regional and Welsh PIP rows to single breakdowns.
