The UK QRF tail-concentration gate no longer checks the report columns `spi_benefit_coherence` (microcosm#1100) replaces on every SPI-channel row. The stage zeroes the income-tested legacy reports (contributory and income-based JSA, Income Support, Working and Child Tax Credit, income-related ESA, Severe Disablement Allowance) and restores IIDB, AFCS and bereavement support from the FRS twin. After it these columns hold only FRS respondents' own reports, so the donor-tail broadcast the gate looks for (#462) cannot occur in them; their concentration is that of a small population of genuine reporters and their clone copies. The surface reads the superseded columns from the stage's declared operations and lists them in its details as `superseded_by_spi_benefit_coherence`.

`hmrc_spi_other_income` and `hmrc_spi_taxable_termination_pay` stay on the surface: they are real stage-1 draws. They failed national release-cut certification as genuinely concentrated instruments, rare on the SPI tape, whose top 100 records carry nearly their whole mass whether counted by row or by independent record; the CGT clones and SPI copies also take them past the carrier floor. They are deferred behind national-line reviewed exclusions, time-boxed to 2027-01-14, until the gate is re-armed on independent draws (microcosm#1277).
