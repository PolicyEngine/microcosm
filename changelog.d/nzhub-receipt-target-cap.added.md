Version-two receipt contracts can now declare `"target_exceeds_eligible_mass": "assign_all_eligible"`. When a stratum's take-up target exceeds the weighted mass left in its eligible pool, assignment then flags everyone in that pool instead of refusing, and the receipt audit records a `shortfall`: the target minus that mass. With the key, the audit records as null a target-to-mass ratio or a target total too large for a float. In an exclusion group, a capped stratum takes its whole remaining pool, so later programs in the group lose those people and may be capped in turn. Contracts without the key assign, audit and refuse exactly as before, validation refuses any other value, and no country contract declares the rule yet.
