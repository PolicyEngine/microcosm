The UK local surface binds DESNZ domestic electricity and gas consumption and gas-connected households by July 2024 constituency, beside the local-authority cells (microcosm#1095, "Constituency grain"). DESNZ publishes no constituency cut, so the new committed resource `uk/desnz_constituency_energy_facts.json`, built by `tools/build_uk_desnz_constituency_energy_facts.py` from the pinned feed and the committed atomic supports, splits each authority's published 2024 total over its constituencies by DESNZ's own LSOA and data zone figures for the same year. Every authority keeps its total, so the seat and authority cells agree within each region; the 2.4% of households in the 990 small areas that straddle two seats are split by census households. A census household split was measured and not taken: it makes use per household uniform within each authority and moved seats' gas by a median 4% and up to 38% against the small-area split. The seat cells take the authority cells' level factor per fuel and read their gas-connected share off the split's meters; Orkney and Shetland, which has no mains gas, is signed absent for gas, as its two councils are. Bus boardings stay at region grain: the feed carries BUS01 by region and scope only, so seat rows could only spread a region's total by population. The 1,894 new cells are ledger-only in the local compile-parity receipt. Every compile of the UK references now reads the committed derived resources through `uk_runtime.derived_facts`.
