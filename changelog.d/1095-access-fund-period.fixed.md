The UK spine reads an implausible calendar-month access-fund award as the annual award it almost certainly is (microcosm#1095, from the review of #1100). The FRS weeklyises `ACCSSAMT` from the reported amount and its period code `ACCSSPD`, and on the 2024-25 tab the calendar-month amounts run up to the size of a whole annual award, so an award read as monthly could annualise far above every award the survey records as annual; #1089 had to sign an input-mass exclusion for it when calibration took its household to the weight bound, and #1100 retired that exclusion once the household moved off the bound, leaving the record itself unchanged. A calendar-month award (code 5) that annualises to more than twice the largest annual-coded award (code 52) on the same tab is now read as its per-period amount, `ACCSSAMT` × 52/12, which repairs fewer than 10 awards on the 2024-25 tab and leaves large but plausible monthly payments as reported; every other award stays as the FRS weeklyised it, including the few with no period code. The threshold moves with the tab's largest annual-coded award, so a tab with calendar-month awards but no annual-coded award now refuses rather than repairing nothing, and the stage's checkpoint evidence records the paid awards by period code, the threshold and how many awards were repaired (from the review of microcosm#1121).
