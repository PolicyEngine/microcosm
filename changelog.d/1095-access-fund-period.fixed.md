The UK spine reads an implausible calendar-month access-fund award as the annual award it almost certainly is (microcosm#1095, from the review of #1100). The FRS weeklyises `ACCSSAMT` from the reported amount and its period code `ACCSSPD`, and on the 2024-25 tab the calendar-month amounts run up to the size of a whole annual award, so one award read as monthly annualised far above every award the survey records as annual; #1089 had to sign an input-mass exclusion for it when calibration took its household to the weight bound, and #1100 retired that exclusion once the household moved off the bound, leaving the record itself unchanged. A calendar-month award (code 5) that annualises to more than twice the largest annual-coded award (code 52) on the same tab is now read as its per-period amount, `ACCSSAMT` × 52/12, which repairs one award on the 2024-25 tab and leaves a large but plausible monthly payment as reported; every other award stays as the FRS weeklyised it, including the few with no period code.
