A new UK spine stage, `uc_housing_costs_claim`, stores policyengine-uk 2.180.0's `would_claim_uc_housing_costs_element` from DWP's award statement (B1 of microcosm#1710). A linked FRS claim whose statement carries no housing element (BENEFITS.UCHOUSEL 0) where the locked engine pays one is false, so the flag carries only the zeros the engine can't explain. Every other benefit unit keeps the default: unlinked units have no statement of their own, and an SPI-channel unit's copied statement no longer belongs to it, since the SPI income chain redraws the copies' incomes and benefit reports. The stage runs just before `uc_take_up`, so the take-up draw sees the awards it leaves. `frs_spine` carries the statement as the person carrier `frs_uc_housing_element_status`, which the release drops; the flag joins the export surface and, like the other take-up flags the build writes, the concept mapping's take-up bindings, so the gate battery's vintage pins, the release cut's digests and the concept coverage follow.
