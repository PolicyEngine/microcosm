The FRS spine's root stage now stores each person's `months_since_last_birthday` (`uk_runtime/birth_month.py`), so every engine read of a build, the stages', the calibration's and the release's, places the same date of birth (microcosm#1095). policyengine-uk places a date of birth from the whole age and this column, and the date decides the two-child limit, the State Pension scheme and the Pension Credit qualifying age. Left unset, the engine spreads each single year of age and sex evenly over the year by weight, in an order set by a hash of `person_id`, so a file's birth months depended on the weights and the identifiers it carried: the release, at the calibrated weights, read a different spread from the one the solve fitted. The stage spreads the months the engine's way once, at the FRS weights, in an order set by an identity-keyed draw on the person's stable identifier; a copy of a person keeps their months. The column is allow-listed on the export surface. An engine test holds the stage's spread equal to the engine's own `stratified_uniform` and shows that stored months do not move with the weights while unset ones do.
