The LCFS consumption stage now reads the National Travel Survey's cars (UKDS SN 5340, End User Licence: the vehicle and household tabs, survey years 2022-2024, at the interview weight W3) for two changes from microcosm#1095 B3 (PR 2b), both as the 16 October plan designed them.

- **Zero-emission cars by income (J2).** Each car is still zero-emission at VEH1103's licensed-car share nationally, but now by the household's income fifth: the national share times the fifth's relative risk among the cars NTS households keep, normalised over the frame's own cars so the national share is unchanged. The NTS ranks England's households by total gross income, McClements-equivalised, so the frame ranks `household_gross_income` over the engine's before-housing-costs equivalence scale. A car is zero-emission in the DVLA-linked 0 g/km CO2 band, and a car without a linked band is left out. The households' uniforms are unchanged, so only each household's threshold moves; a fifth on fewer than 10 zero-emission cars would pool with its neighbour (a statistical floor). The rule is `per_car_zero_emission_share_by_income_quintile`.
- **Petrol and diesel levelled separately (K).** `level_road_fuel` keeps ONS 07.2.2's household level but no longer the diary's petrol/diesel mix. Each fuel is levelled to the total times its share of household-paid spend: HMRC's litres times DESNZ's cars share of the fuel's road use (the litres audit's benchmark), times the share of car miles a household pays for (a kept car's private and commuting miles; business miles and company cars are not), at the 2024 pump prices. The `road_fuel_level` gate rechecks each fuel's total against the mix it recomputes from the vendored rows. The mix rule is `household_paid_litres`.

A new private input, `--nts-vehicle-tab`, joins the spine tool, and the NTS household tab now also feeds `lcfs_consumption` under the same pin. The H2 fixture gains a synthetic vehicle table and W3 on its households. `obr.fuel_duties_cars` stays held, its reason restated with the household-paid shares (María's ruling of 29 October 2026).
