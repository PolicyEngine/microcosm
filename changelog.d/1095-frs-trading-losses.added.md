The UK root stage splits the FRS self-employment income (SEINCAM2, which carries a loss as a negative amount) by its sign, as uk-data#528 does: profits go to `self_employment_income` and losses to `trading_loss` as a positive amount (microcosm#1095). policyengine-uk reads `trading_loss` from 2.131.0 (pe-uk#2085): income tax relieves the loss against general income, Class 4 against trading profits, tax credits against the claimants' income, and HBAI household income counts it as negative income. Before this the spine dropped every loss. The engine uprates `trading_loss` into each projected year as it does `self_employment_income`, so a loss persists like a profit. The concept mapping gains a `signed_part` transform, which splits the sum of the farm and non-farm self-employment concepts into the two inputs so that they never both hold an amount. The pinned incumbent (uk-data 1.56.16) does not carry `trading_loss`, so it joins the reviewed extra columns of the export surface. A hand-computed case shows that the salary-sacrifice rate classes follow the loss-relieved income and still add up to the engine's `earned_income_tax`.
