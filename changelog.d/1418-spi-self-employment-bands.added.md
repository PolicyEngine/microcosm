The national calibration binds SPI 2023-24 Table 3.10's self-employment bands up to £10,000 (#1418, row 15; María's ruling of 2026-10-29). The new targets are `hmrc.spi.self_employment_income.count_by_self_employment_income_band` and `amount_by_self_employment_income_band`: the individuals with self-employment income, and that income, in the eight bands from £1 to £10,000. That is the small-profit end the Budget's trading allowance measures reach. Table 3.10 counts every individual with a self-employment source, taxpayer or not, so the targets carry no taxpayer filter. Each person is banded on their own self-employment income after loss relief, and the top band is closed at £10,000. The bands above stay with Table 3.6's rows by total income, so the two tables don't pull against each other. Counts grow to 2025 with HMRC's projected growth of all taxpayers (Income Tax liabilities statistics Table 2.5). Amounts grow by that count index times the OBR's mixed income per head, so each band's mean moves as the engine moves incomes; on mixed income per head alone, each band's implied mean would have fallen 3.5%. Both families sit in the zero-support floor's HMRC scopes, so a band no household carries is dropped and receipted, not refused.
