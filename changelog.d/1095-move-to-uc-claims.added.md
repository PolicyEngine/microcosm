The UK spine now draws `would_claim_uc_at_legacy_closure` at DWP's Move to Universal Credit claim rates (uk-data#492, ported from uk-data#534; microcosm#1095). policyengine-uk 2.128.0 (pe-uk#2119) moves a family off a legacy benefit DWP has closed and takes its other legacy awards away; the family then claims Universal Credit if `would_claim_uc` or this flag is true, and the engine's default for the flag, true, put every such family on Universal Credit.

A new stage, `uc_legacy_closure_take_up`, runs after `uc_capital_coherence` and before `pension_credit_take_up`. A benefit unit with a member reporting Universal Credit, a unit whose `would_claim_uc` is true, and a unit reporting none of the six legacy means-tested benefits keep the engine's default. Every other unit is drawn at (r - p) / (1 - p), clipped to [0, 1]: r is DWP's claimed / (claimed + did not claim) for the unit's combination of legacy benefits (the all-households rate where DWP reports none), and p is the combination's share of Great Britain's such units whose `would_claim_uc` is true, at the spine's design weights (uk-data#534's share is unweighted; María's rulings of 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17); the probability applies in Northern Ireland too. So, in expectation, the weighted share of Great Britain's drawn units claiming at the closure in each combination is DWP's rate wherever p is at most r; where p exceeds it the receipt flags the combination (`rate_unreachable`), never p itself. Two limits carry over from uk-data#534: the drawn cohort includes units on the Pension Credit route, whose legacy benefits policyengine-uk does not close, and DWP's rates count every household sent a notice, entitled to Universal Credit or not. The rates are DWP's Stat-Xplore households sent a migration notice in Great Britain, notices July 2022 to June 2026, vendored from the pinned Chronicle feed (`uk/dwp_move_to_uc_facts.json`); households still in progress are left out. Each unit's uniform reads an identity-keyed stream.

The flag joins the export surface's reviewed extra columns, and the policyengine-uk concept mapping binds it as a take-up input, so no take-up input is left at the engine's default.
