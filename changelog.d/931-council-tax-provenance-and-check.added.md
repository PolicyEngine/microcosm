The council-tax bill's provenance at stage 5 (`frs_council_tax`) and a stage-health check that holds council-tax reductions within the bill (#931), unwired: no stage writes the provenance and no gate declares the check, so no value moves. Later in #931 group (d), stage 5 writes the provenance, council-tax localisation reads it, and a transferred gate declares the check.

- **The bill's provenance** (`microcosm.build.uk_runtime.frs_council_tax`). `derive_council_tax_provenance(household, household_raw)` names the branch `derive_council_tax` takes for each household's bill outside Northern Ireland, and `not_levied` there, as a `StringDtype("python")` series on the bill's index, for the column `FRS_COUNCIL_TAX_BILL_PROVENANCE_COLUMN` (`council_tax_bill_provenance`) that stage 5 will write. The first value that holds names the row:
  - `not_levied` where raw GVTREGNO is 13 (`NORTHERN_IRELAND_GVTREGNO`, beside `SCOTLAND_GVTREGNO`): Northern Ireland pays domestic rates, not council tax;
  - `missing` where CTANNUAL is negative or blank, or zero where CTPAID is 1 and no reduction is reported, so the bill takes the cell mean;
  - `observed_gross` where CTREB is 1 and a positive CTREBAMT is added back to the netted CTANNUAL;
  - `unknown_reduction` where CTREB is 1 without a positive CTREBAMT, whose bill is the larger of the netted CTANNUAL and the cell mean;
  - otherwise `observed_gross`, the netted CTANNUAL, which stays zero where CTANNUAL is zero and CTPAID is not 1.

  The four values are `FRS_COUNCIL_TAX_BILL_PROVENANCE_VALUES`. One branch function, `_council_tax_branches`, chooses both the bill and its provenance from the same masks, and `derive_council_tax` returns its bill, bit for bit as before. `frs_council_tax_provenance_operation_parameters()` is the `derive` declaration (`output` and `formula`, the precedence above in raw codes) that the stage will declare. `council_tax_provenance_evidence(frame, provenance)` is the stage's receipt form: `{"stage": "frs_council_tax", "bill_provenance_sources": {value: rounded_count}}` for all four values, zeros included, counting distinct source households before the copies through `record_source_households`. It refuses an input that is not one-dimensional, has the wrong length or holds a value outside the four, by texts that name the column and the four values, never a value or count read.
- **The check** (`stage_health`, check `council_tax_reduction_within_bill`). It reads the phase's frame and no receipt key beyond the stage match every stage-health check makes. The households whose `region` is one of `excluded_regions` leave first, with their persons. Then each remaining household's `household_reduction_columns`, and each of its `person_reduction_columns` summed over its persons through `person_household_id`, must be at most its `bill_column` plus `absolute_tolerance`, through the shared `microcosm.build.bounded_amounts.amounts_within_bound`. Every value read of the bill and of those columns must also be finite and non-negative. The household ids must name each household once over the whole table before any leave.
  - Its details are booleans: `households_checked`, one per reduction column under `columns`, and `finite_nonnegative`. A frame with no household left to check passes, with `households_checked` false.
  - Its failure texts name the stage, the check and columns or parameters, never an id, a value or a count. A missing frame, a malformed declaration, a missing or repeated column, a non-numeric column, a repeated household id and a person whose household is in no table each fail by name.
- **Bindings** (`battery_bindings`). The stage-health binding hands the phase's frame to the new check, as to the band donor and copy area checks, and routes its four keys: `bill_column`, `household_reduction_columns`, `person_reduction_columns` and `excluded_regions`. Its tolerance is the routed `absolute_tolerance`. `UK_GATE_REGISTRY_MODULES` gains `microcosm.build.bounded_amounts`, which the whole registry now reaches.
- **Counting stage.** `frs_council_tax` joins the counting stages in `graph_kernels`, since its module now counts through `receipt_counts`. The counting test's control moves to `frs_employment`.
- **Keys.** UK only. `uk.stage.frs_council_tax@1`, every spine kernel through `graph_kernels`, the spine gates (`stage_health`, `battery_bindings`), the full battery's preflight and calibrated gates and the national gates (whose registry modules now include `bounded_amounts`) change hash, and the nodes after them re-key. No stage contract, phase code, country or solve fingerprint, declaration, generated file, `gates.json` or H2 file changes. No NZ, BE, AM or US key moves: `microcosm.build.gates`, `country_spec` and `source_manifest` are untouched, and only the UK stage-health check calls `bounded_amounts`.
