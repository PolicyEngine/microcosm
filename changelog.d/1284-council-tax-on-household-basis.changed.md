The UK national register puts the council-tax dwelling stock on the household basis of its area (microcosm#1284). The dwelling stock (MHCLG and StatsWales taxbase lines, Scotland's CTAXBASE chargeable dwellings) and each nation's official household estimate bound the same weighted household count of an area at two published levels, so the solve traded one family against the other. A new national-only declaration in `uk/cross_grain_declarations.json`, `household_basis_partitions`, now rescales each English region's, Wales's and Scotland's dwelling total onto its household control, and its bands by the same factor, the area's published households per chargeable dwelling: 0.980 to 1.023 across the eleven areas on the 735820c feed. The bands keep their published mix and the level follows the household estimate. María signed the assumption on 2026-10-16: households per chargeable dwelling are the same in every band of an area. They are not exactly, since multi-household dwellings concentrate in lower bands. A tripwire refuses a factor further than 0.035 from one, the measured 0.023 with a margin, and the national closure check requires every rescaled total to equal its control. Northern Ireland has no council-tax rows. The local band cells follow the national rows by signature.
