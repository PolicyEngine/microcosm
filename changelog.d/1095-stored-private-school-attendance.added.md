A new last spine stage, `private_school_attendance`, stores policyengine-uk's `attends_private_school` at the spine's weights (uk-data#568, microcosm#1095). The engine imputes attendance with a formula that ranks households by net income at their weights, so every simulation re-imputed it: a reform to income tax, National Insurance or benefits switched pupils in or out and moved the fee VAT, a population under one million people of weight imputed none (pe-uk#1905), and the calibration and the release read different flags once the weights changed. The stage materializes the engine's attendance once, at the calibration year and the spine's design weights, from the stored `attends_private_school_random_draw`, and stores it, as the root stage stores `months_since_last_birthday`; no stage after it copies or re-keys a person. Stored attendance no longer responds to income changes or to reforms of the attendance parameters. The column joins the export surface's reviewed extra columns. The pupil series the attendance is held against stays ISC's member-school census: DfE's count of all independent-school pupils, the series María chose on 2026-10-16, is not yet a Chronicle fact.
