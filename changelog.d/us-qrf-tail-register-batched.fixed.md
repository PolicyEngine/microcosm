A per-run QRF tail-concentration exclusion register that does not match the checked surface is now a batched terminal failure instead of an early raise. The release is still refused (whatever `--allow-qrf-tail-concentration` says, and `--evidence-release` refuses it outright), but `qrf_tail_concentration.json` with every column's measured share and carrier count, and the #568 calibrated-weight sidecar, are now written, so the measured surface a corrected register needs survives the failure.
