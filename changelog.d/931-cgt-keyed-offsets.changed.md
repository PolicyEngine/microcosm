The CGT gains redraw (`hmrc_cgt_gains_spine`) and the BADR claims in the asset-type stage (`hmrc_cgt_asset_type_spine`) key each systematic walk's offset, and each plan's quantile jitter, on its own label instead of taking the next draw of one Generator (#931; María's ruling 6 of 7 Nov). The redraw built one Generator from its seed (552) and the build period and handed it to every Table 3 cell walk, every income band's pooled walk and every (gain band, income band) plan in run order, so the n-th uniform belonged to whichever walk or plan ran n-th: a cell that gained or lost its gainers, or skipped on its carried target, moved the uniform of every walk and plan after it. Each is now an identity-keyed uniform under the declared seed, read once:

- a cell walk is keyed `cell:<income band>:<age group>:<region group>` and an income band's pooled walk `pool:<income band>`, both salted `hmrc_cgt_gains_spine:offset:<period>`;
- a plan is keyed `plan:<gain band>:<income band>`, salted `hmrc_cgt_gains_spine:jitter:<period>`;
- a BADR band is keyed on its bounds, `<lower>|<exclusive upper>` (`open` for the top band), salted `assign_badr_qualifying_gains:offset:<period>`, under the BADR seed (555). The band offsets never depended on rows, but each was its band's position in one draw of every band's offset, so a facts revision that added a band moved the offset of every band after it.

Bands and age groups are named by their lower bounds, region groups by their labels. The period moved from the Generator's seed into each salt, so the declarations still read "seed combined with the build period".

- **Shared scalar.** `microcosm.build.stochastic_assignment.stable_identity_uniform(key, *, seed, salt)` returns the draw `stable_identity_uniforms([key], ...)` gives for one label, and refuses a sequence. The duck-typed `random()` adapter the walks read (`_KeyedOffset`) moved from `isa_subscriptions` into `cgt_imputation`, beside `_walk_bands`; the ISA subscriptions take the adapter from `cgt_imputation` and the scalar from `stochastic_assignment`, and their walk offsets and band jitters are the uniforms they were, bit for bit. `property_components`' one receipts-walk offset stays as it is (ruling 6).
- **Declarations.** The redraw's `seed_mixing` and the BADR `realization` texts say the above, and the sub-AEA remainder's `mapping` text no longer names a walk and plan draw order, since there is none. Each is identical in the code dictionary, `sources.yaml` and `source_stages.json`. `hmrc_cgt_gains_spine` and `hmrc_cgt_asset_type_spine` now compute through `stochastic_assignment`, so both bind it, as the derived binding rule requires.
- **No nesting promised.** The offsets and jitters are the same at every geographic clone count, and whichever other cells walk or skip. The walks themselves stay pooled: the rounding carry runs through an income band's cells, the pooled walk takes the band's net shortfall over its unassigned gainers, each plan stratifies members pooled from every cell, and each BADR band walks its whole pool. A change in one cell can still move the assignments and amounts of the other cells in its income band, and a plan's amounts whenever its members change. Through the rake it can move cells in other income bands too: the rake seeds every cell from the frame's own (age group, region group | income band) gainer shares and fits the seed to the joint and to the Table 6 age and Table 5 region margins, which span every income band, so a change in one cell's gainers moves the raked targets of cells in other income bands, and with them possibly those cells' assignments and amounts. On a synthetic frame, adding one cell in the lowest income band moved all 20 positive raked targets of a two-cell higher band, by up to 0.8 per cent.
- **What moves.** Every Table 3 walk's offset and every plan's jitter changes, so band membership near each walk boundary and the within-band quantiles move. On three synthetic frames, 9 of 1,002, 9 of 3,000 and 126 of 1,512 gainers changed gain band, and 338, 2,920 and 1,300 changed amount; no non-gainer moved. On the third, 70 of the 1,512 gainers crossed the annual exempt amount, between a Table 3 plan and the sub-AEA remainder (none on the other two), so the liable set moves, and with it the households stage 46 splits into arms and the row count from there. Every BADR band's offset changes, so each band's walk selects its claimants at new positions. On three synthetic frames of equal weights no claimant row of the old walk claims in the new one, while each frame's claimant count moved by one or two; the main asset types moved with the claims. ISA amounts do not move: on three synthetic frames every ISA output column is unchanged. The stage contracts of both stages, the H2 fixture's declarations and the 22 values of its synthetic asset-type facts (which the fixture tool sizes to the frame after the redraw), the coverage manifest's 25 source digests and `source_stages.json` are regenerated. Every spine node re-keys, since `graph_kernels` and `stochastic_assignment` changed and the spine's root binds `stochastic_assignment`, and on a bound spine the national line re-keys 5 of its 6 nodes, all but the bound checkpoint, since the national target, problem and gate kernels hash the country spec, which this change edits; values move from stage 44 on, and `isa_subscriptions` re-keys through its `cgt_imputation` binding and its own module with no change in its output.
