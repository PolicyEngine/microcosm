The gambling participation gate (`uk_stage_lcfs_consumption_gambling_participation`, microcosm#1359) lets the stake scale reach 2.5 instead of 2.0 (María's ruling of 2026-10-26, microcosm#1095). The scale is what the stage multiplies the added regular players' drawn stakes by to bring their mean to the seen players'. Full spine builds, on main after #1381 and on this branch, put the National Lottery's at about 2.03, against 1.82 to 1.86 on the stage-only runs that set the bound, so every full UK spine stopped at this gate. A draw more than 2.5 times off the seen players' mean still fails.
