Assign immigration status and years since entry on the ACS rows of the ACS local-area release (microcosm#1020). The immigration stage ran only on ASEC donor rows, so every ACS person reached both engine passes with `immigration_status_str` and `ssn_card_type` missing and the reviewed-null fill made them citizens with a valid SSN, and `years_since_us_entry` was never stored, so every person got the engine default of 5. The ACS loader now keeps the native `CIT`, `YOEP`, `POBP`, `HINS3`-`HINS7`, `COW`, `SCHG`, `ESR` and `MIL` fields (`MIL` renamed `ACS_MIL`, since CPS `MIL` is military health coverage). A new staging stage, `microcosm.build.us_runtime.acs_local_immigration`, runs the immigration stage's residual method on the ACS rows through a CPS-named view of those fields: measured `CIT` citizenship, the exact `YOEP` entry year in place of the `PEINUSYR` bins, and the Pew worker and student controls less the counts the donor rows already deliver on their pooled weights (floored at zero), so donor plus ACS pools to each control; the receipt records the control, donor, residual, ACS and pooled count per status, and the release tool refuses a receipt whose targets are not that residual. It fills `years_since_us_entry` on both spines as an arrival-based status-duration proxy (period minus measured arrival year for the foreign-born, age for the US-born), which can clear the five-year bar early for people who obtained qualified status after arriving; the receipt reports the bar-status weight that would flip under a one- to five-year adjustment lag. The ASEC path is unchanged. Staging runs the stage and its per-spine `acs_local_immigration_signal` gate before the input-null audit and records a receipt; the release tool refuses a staging summary without it, the reviewed-null fill refuses to default-fill any of the three columns, finalize hard-fails on the gate (missing, constant or default-valued labels or clocks, labels against measured `CIT`, or a non-citizen share or undocumented total outside the stage's bands), and packaging requires that gate passing and bound to the packaged H5. Staging must be rebuilt.
