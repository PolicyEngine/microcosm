Every UK target the calibration-year compile binds from an older fact now carries either a declared uprating index or a declared hold in uk/uprating_holds.json (matched_period, in_year_snapshot, control_rescaled, reviewed_no_index); the compile refuses an undeclared, unsigned, expired or stale hold (a register entry is in force only with an approver, an approval date and the status approved), and a local area target that declares an index is uprated at authoring and at compile as national targets are (microcosm#1123).
