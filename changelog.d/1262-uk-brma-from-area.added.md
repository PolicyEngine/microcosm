A placed household's Broad Rental Market Area now follows its assigned area (microcosm#1262, B1). `tools/build_uk_brma_area_layer.py` places every 2021 LSOA of England and Wales and every 2022 output area of Scotland in one BRMA from Chronicle's raw registrations (PolicyEngine/chronicle#314): England and Wales by population-weighted centroid in the VOA May 2020 and Rent Officers Wales May 2012 polygons with policyengine-uk-data#516's six overrides, Scotland by centroid in the Scottish Government's polygons corrected by Rent Service Scotland's postcode lookup with microcosm#1092's two overrides. The layer, `uk/brma_area_layer.json` (192 BRMAs, OGL v3.0), reproduces the region-by-BRMA support of policyengine-uk-data#516's census table and microcosm#1092's 63 Scottish BRMA-by-council pairs exactly; its provenance records both checks. The post-geography node `uk.full.geography.brma` (`--brma-localisation area`, the default on the atomic assignment; `none` for measurement builds, refused by a release candidate) reads each placed Great Britain clone's BRMA off its LSOA or output area, so its LHA rate describes the same place as its local authority and constituency. Northern Ireland and the national line keep the spine's `frs_brma` draw. A terminal release-blocking gate, `uk_local_brma_enum_domain`, rechecks the rewritten column against the engine's enum; the local gate battery has seven entries and the dense and full-battery contract digests move with it, while the certification part digests do not.
