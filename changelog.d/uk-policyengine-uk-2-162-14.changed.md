The UK lock moves from policyengine-uk 2.162.8 to 2.162.14, the first release on PyPI that carries PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2695 (2.162.13 never published). Since 2.157.0 (policyengine-uk#2066) the property allowance election (`uses_property_allowance`) worked out every benefit for the whole population on each of its comparison branches, which made Microcosm UK microsimulations several times slower (policyengine-uk#2694); #2695 settles the elections the winter fuel payment charge cannot change without it and keeps every election's result. Before the release, the lock installed #2695's head commit (b71ce793, labelled 2.162.12), whose package code 2.162.14 releases unchanged; this branch's local-area cut ran on it and records policyengine-uk 2.162.12, and its national is built on 2.162.14. 2.162.9 to 2.162.12 add the 2010 to 2012 minimum wage history and the Employment and Support Allowance permitted-work limit set from the national minimum wage, and impute non-workers' wages once per participation step (outputs unchanged). The uprating and bonus-sacrifice engine pins keep every value, and the concept coverage finds no input added or removed.
