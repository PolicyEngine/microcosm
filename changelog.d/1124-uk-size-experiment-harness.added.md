A selection-only experiment harness for UK dataset-size builds (microcosm#1124): `tools/run_uk_size_experiment.py` (`build-cache`, `control`, `run`, `score`, `publish`) over `uk_runtime.size_experiment` and `uk_runtime.size_experiment_scorecard`. It reads a finished `--dataset-households` graph build's flat evidence (digest-checked against `evidence-index.json`) and its pool frame (read-only), caches a skeleton pool and a household profile, and first re-runs the stored refit, which must reproduce the stored weights before any variant is read. Experiments refit the stored support under another target-weight rule, L2 penalty or baseline floor, search again from the stored dense solve, or score a refit-level rotated holdout; each writes a receipt (settings, solver options, losses under the training weights and fixed `grain_equal` and `grain_family_equal` yardsticks, wall time, peak RSS, code pin) and its weights outside the repository and the run directory. The scorecard scores the dense reference, the stored run and each candidate on household total, nation shares, the household-composition rows, per-area support at both grains (with the absolute ESS floor and the relative-collapse rule), fit by grain and family, the stretch-cap share, chi-square distances and per-area representativeness against a sampling-noise null, and evaluates the six pre-registered acceptance criteria. Published aggregates suppress small unit counts.
