A selection-only experiment harness for UK dataset-size builds (microcosm#1124): `tools/run_uk_size_experiment.py` (`build-cache`, `control`, `census`, `run`, `score`, `publish`) over `uk_runtime.size_experiment` and `uk_runtime.size_experiment_scorecard`. It reads a finished `--dataset-households` graph build's flat evidence (digest-checked against `evidence-index.json`) and its pool frame (read-only), caches a skeleton pool and a household profile, and first re-runs the stored refit, which must reproduce the stored weights before any variant is read. The census records what the stored support and its stretch caps allow before any variant runs: mass capacity against the dense reference by nation and household type at refit baseline floors 0, 0.1, 0.5 and 1, per-area ceilings under the relative-collapse rule, the search's capacity bound k_min, the L2 penalty scales that place the λ grid, each rule's loss shares, and the early dataset-size triggers. Experiments refit the stored support under another target-weight rule, L2 penalty or baseline floor, search again from the stored dense solve, or score a refit-level rotated holdout; a new search and its draw are saved in the graph's own encodings, so later refits and holdouts name it with `selection_from` and run on that selection without searching again; each writes a receipt (settings, solver options, losses under the training weights and fixed `grain_equal` and `grain_family_equal` yardsticks, wall time, peak RSS, code pin) and its weights outside the repository and the run directory. No release gate runs in the harness: a configuration the solver chain refuses is recorded as a failed receipt with its reason and the run moves on. The scorecard scores the dense reference, the stored run and each candidate on household total, nation shares and nation capacity under the stretch cap, the household-composition rows, per-area support at both grains (with the absolute ESS floor and the relative-collapse rule), fit by grain and family, the stretch-cap share, chi-square distances and per-area representativeness (household size, tenure, head age, household type and income decile) against a sampling-noise null, and evaluates the six pre-registered acceptance criteria. Published aggregates suppress small unit counts and small effective sample sizes.
