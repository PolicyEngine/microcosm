The UK spine now sets policyengine-uk's `is_looked_after_by_local_authority` for children placed in foster care (microcosm#1095). The FRS 2024-25 question instructions put foster children under 16 in the carer's benefit unit. Nothing set the flag before, so the engine counted them as the carer's own children in its child tests: Universal Credit's child element and youngest-child rule, Child Tax Credit, Pension Credit, Tax-Free Childcare, extended childcare, the Working Tax Credit childcare element and the legacy benefits.

The `frs_relationships` stage sets the flag for a child-table member under 16 whose household-grid code toward an adult in their own benefit unit is foster child (5), or toward whom that adult records foster parent (9). It follows the relationship code alone: few such children are recorded, and the carer's local-authority fostering allowance (`ALLOW3`) covers fewer still.
