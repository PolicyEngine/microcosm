SPI support-channel copies and income band donors now draw their area by the FRS rows' rule (#931; María's ruling of 25 Nov). `spi_copy_areas` runs after `spi_housing_shell` and reads the council-tax band the shell drew for each copy: a copy draws within its region by the households of that band (L2), or by all households where the region holds none of it (L3), and its parent's decile is still not read. Before, a copy drew at L3 by all households and the shell drew its band from region, composition and incomes alone, so band and local authority were nearly independent: in the K = 15 comparison, upper bands sat in authorities with few such homes, and one authority's Band G stock row started past the solver's cap. The shell now prices each Great Britain copy's bill at the expected Band D of that same cell, one exact reference per region and band taken before the band draw, and `council_tax_localise` localises it from there as before. The copy stage's declared area draw names the levels L2 and L3 (the sources schema's `area_draw.levels`), as do its evidence and the shell's council-tax evidence.
