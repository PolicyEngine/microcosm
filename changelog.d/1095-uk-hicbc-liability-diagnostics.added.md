Two national rows from HMRC's Child Benefit statistics (August 2025 release, Table 16) join the UK target contract as measured diagnostics (microcosm#1095, #1288 item 10; María's ruling of 3 November 2026): the Self Assessment taxpayers liable to the High Income Child Benefit Charge in 2024-25, counted in the model as the persons whose `CB_HITC` is above zero, and the charge's revenue, the sum of `CB_HITC`. Both are the publisher's provisional figures. They are measured at the calibration year against 2024-25's figures under a signed reviewed hold (the charge's £60,000 to £80,000 thresholds are the same in both years), and held out of the solve: the model's charge sits well above HMRC's, a gap in who receives Child Benefit at high incomes rather than in the weights, and HMRC's count covers Self Assessment returns only. The evaluation measures them on every candidate. The national references and their membership are regenerated on the pinned feed.
