`obr.council_tax` binds the main-residence share of OBR's council tax receipts: the calendar-2025 window is scaled by England's chargeable dwellings less its second homes and empty dwellings, over its chargeable dwellings (MHCLG Council Taxbase, October 2025). The frame carries only main-residence bills, so binding the whole line pushed them up (María's ruling of 2026-10-29, microcosm#1095 D14f).
