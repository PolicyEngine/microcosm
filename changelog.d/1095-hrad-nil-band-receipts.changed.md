The UK property purchases stage allocates households' additional-dwelling (HRAD) purchases at or below GBP 250,000 from HMRC's Table 8a receipts instead of individuals' share of all transactions (microcosm#1095). The residential nil-rate band was GBP 250,000 for all of 2024-25, so a purchase in that band owes no standard-rate SDLT and other buyers' receipts there are surcharge: at Table 6b's gross HRAD receipts per transaction and per pound, their GBP 265m is about 48,100 HRAD transactions and GBP 7.05bn, against the 13,400 transactions the all-band share left them. Households take the rest of the band; above it they keep uk-data#573's shares, which probably still overstate them, since the standard rates make up most receipts there. Households' 2024-25 target falls from 186,589 transactions and GBP 68.81bn to 151,928 and GBP 63.71bn, both above uk-data#573's lower bounds, and the lowest band's share of their purchases from 54% to 43%. The stage's derivation records the nil-band receipts, the implied HRAD and its shares.
