The measurement-period guard now checks provider bindings as well as plain-column ones. A `parameter_gated_threshold` or `baseline_flag_crosstab` row reads its columns as the frame holds them, so one that declared a period its value reads do not hold (an engine value computed at the calibration year under a declared 2023, say) was labelled with one year and measured at another. Each value the provider reads must now be held at the declared period: an entity count read through the adapter's column holds no period (it is ones), while a member reduction reads a count as the frame stores it and is checked like any column; a stored or name-dated measure holds its own year, a stored column on a frame without period metadata is read as it is, and the engine computes anything else at the calibration period. A provider whose reads are not known (a counterfactual delta, a caller's own kind) may declare only the calibration period. The guard runs once per row, before the band, and a count declared at the frame's own period now binds as ones. No packaged row moves: the 15 CGT provider rows read measures dated to the 2024 they declare, and counts.
