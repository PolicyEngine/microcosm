The local line computes each metric from its declaration. `compute_household_metrics` reads every constituency, local-authority and BRMA metric's `policyengine` binding in `uk_population_targets.json` through the national rows' binding interpreter (`prepared_binding_values`), on columns `UKMeasureResolver` computes, and sums person and benefit-unit bindings onto their household with the engine's `map_result`. A rebind therefore moves the national and local lines together, and a local declaration that carries a key the local line does not honour (a measurement period, a band, a mapping other than to the household, or a filter that names an entity, a concept or a reduction) refuses. Ten local declarations change so they say what the code computed: the four UC child bands count UC benefit units by `uc_calibration_child_count`, the FRS-role composition the national UC-by-children rows read; the private rents read `benunit_rent` summed to the household; the high-value homes and the gas-connected households read resolver measures that apply the stored period and price basis (`owner_occupied_home_over_gbp_*`, and the new `domestic_gas_connected` beside `domestic_electricity_kwh` and `domestic_gas_kwh`). The interpreter gains `in` lists, lower-inclusive ranges and subtraction in value expressions, and refuses a predicate that declares `input_period` or `price_basis`, which it cannot honour. On a national frame, the 59 local declarations' 43 metrics (61 columns over the three grains) are identical to the old code's, bit for bit, except `ons/equiv_housing_costs`, which no active target reads: it differs by float32 rounding because the difference is now taken in float64. The local references change only the entity they record: `benunit` for the UC child bands, `household` for the private rents. The incumbent extractor reads the incumbent file through one resolver, so its three `high_value_homes/*` columns move on a Microcosm-release incumbent: the old extractor read the stored main residence value and tenure at 2025, where the engine uprates the value, against thresholds deflated to the dataset period, while the resolver reads them at the frame's period, as the build's local line already did. The local rule-1 comparison therefore moves on those three metrics; the build's local line does not. An incumbent without FRS benefit-unit roles, such as the enhanced FRS, cannot measure the UC child bands: the local extractor does not prune listed measures, so it refuses and names `benunit.uc_calibration_child_count`, which the national scorer's signed incumbent-unresolvable register already records.
