Two report-only additions for microcosm#1207. The UK measure resolver's receipt (carried in every build's measures receipt) gains `bus_engine_parity`: policyengine-uk's `gov.dft.bus.boardings_per_trip` and `yield_per_boarding` beside the boardings per trip and fiscal-year yield the `lcfs_consumption` stage priced each area at, with each relative gap marked against a 0.1% tolerance. The engine prices bus fares itself from the dataset's trips, so this is the check that its pricing still matches the stage's; an engine without those parameters records why it has none. The calibration diagnostics' `consumption_drift` block gains the full-rate VAT base (consumption times the ETB VAT stage's full-rate expenditure rate) at design and final weights, since no bound row holds it.
