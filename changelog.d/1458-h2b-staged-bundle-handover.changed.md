UK builds hand their staged dataset bundle, and a failed build's graph evidence, to the build's emitter with `stage_dataset_bundle` instead of committing it with `HfApi`: every file goes by path with its role (an output's `outputs` key as a role name, so `dataset` and `calibration_diagnostics`; the manifest, `staged_manifest.json` and `sha256sums.txt` as `build_manifest`, `staged_manifest` and `checksums`), its SHA-256 and its size, and the emitter's receipt becomes the `staged_dataset` record. The build waits for the emitter's first upload attempt for at most 30 minutes (`STAGED_DATASET_WAIT_SECONDS`) and records a bundle still queued then as `failed` with the emitter's last error code, or `UPLOAD_PENDING`; the emitter keeps it queued, and the next emitter on the machine uploads it. The record's error codes gain `CREDENTIAL_MISSING`, `PRESERVED_FILES_MISSING`, `PRESERVED_BYTES_CHANGED`, `UPLOAD_PENDING`, `HANDOVER_REFUSED` and `EMITTER_UNAVAILABLE`. After staging, the build rewrites the manifest, `sha256sums.txt` and the national build record only when the receipt lists them in `copied_files`. The remote-staging check before a build is the emitter's `check_write_access`, so a remote stage is refused up front without a running emitter. A build whose environment sets `HF_HUB_OFFLINE`, as the Modal runner does for every build, hands no bundle over and records it as `local_only`. `tools/stage_uk_rowwise_candidate.py` starts a short-lived emitter with only its Hugging Face module. `HuggingFaceDatasetStorage` keeps only `download_file`, for fetching a staged bundle.
