The national UK contract binds four more DfE funded-childcare counts by single year of age, England, from the January 2025 census (María's ruling of 2026-10-21: bind every DfE childcare count the model can carry, even on few records, and sign out only those with a real reason; microcosm#1278, #1095): working parent registrations at ages 1, 2 and 3 (`dfe.funded_childcare.working_parent_children_age_<n>`) and universal registrations in nursery at age 3 (`universal_nursery_children_age_3`). Each counts persons in English households with whole-year age `n`; from age 2 the rows leave out children in primary education, as DfE's registrations leave out reception. The working parent 2 to 4 row (the take-up fitter's anchor) and the universal-only row stay bound beside them, unaffected. The contract's accounting notes sign out the rest of the table, each for its reason: the three age-4 cells, the working parent and universal nursery registrations at age 4 and the 4-year-olds in reception (DfE's January census reports ages as at 31 December, when about two-thirds of funded 4-year-olds are already in reception, while the model's whole-year age is the age at interview spread over the survey year, with fewer 4-year-olds in primary education, so binding the single age-4 cells would move weight between 4-year-olds in school and in nursery because of the reference date, not the data); the 9 to 11 months cell (policyengine-uk gives whole-year age 0 no working parent hours); reception at age 3 (no FRS 3-year-old is in primary education); the totals and multi-age cells that sum or copy bound cells or include an uncarried or signed-out cell; the universal all-provision cells (nursery plus reception); DfE's eligible-children and all-children estimates (built from population and survey-based inputs); and the registered percentages.
