The national UK contract binds seven more DfE funded-childcare counts by single year of age, England, from the January 2025 census (María's ruling of 2026-10-21: bind every DfE childcare count the model can carry, even on few records, and sign out only those with a real reason; microcosm#1278, #1095): working parent registrations at ages 1, 2, 3 and 4 (`dfe.funded_childcare.working_parent_children_age_<n>`), universal registrations in nursery at ages 3 and 4 (`universal_nursery_children_age_<n>`), and 4-year-olds in reception (`universal_reception_children_age_4`, every 4-year-old in primary education, which pins the share of 4-year-olds in school). Each counts persons in English households with whole-year age `n`; from age 2 the working parent and nursery rows leave out children in primary education, as DfE's registrations leave out reception. The working parent 2 to 4 row (the take-up fitter's anchor) and the universal-only row stay bound: the first is the sum of ages 2 to 4, on the facts and on the measures; the second is the nursery ages 3 and 4 less the working parent ages 3 and 4 on the facts, and on the measures wherever a working parent child keeps the universal hours, as it does from policyengine-uk 2.128.1. The contract's accounting notes sign out the rest of the table, each for its reason: the 9 to 11 months cell (policyengine-uk gives whole-year age 0 no working parent hours), reception at age 3 (no FRS 3-year-old is in primary education), the totals and multi-age cells that sum or copy bound cells, the universal all-provision cells (nursery plus reception), DfE's eligible-children and all-children estimates (built from population and survey-based inputs) and the registered percentages. The contract has 439 targets (432 before) and the registry scope 380 (373): seven more contract-only declarations (194).
