The US fiscal materializer now accepts `ledger_filter_age_lower_bound` / `ledger_filter_age_upper_bound` — the Ledger stamp of a fact's `age >=` / `age <` rows — beside the compiled `age_lower_bound` / `age_upper_bound`, but only where the two edges are equal and the spec's materializer (`population_age`, or the age-banded `policyengine_variable` branch the SSA SSI by-age counts use) actually slices on that band. On the pinned Chronicle feed this clears the 939 Census population-by-age and SSA SSI age-band targets the guard refused over the whole compiled registry. A restated edge that disagrees, has no compiled counterpart, sits on a spec whose materializer applies no age band, or comes from a fact with `age` as a dimension is refused and names both values; so is any restated edge the compile does not attest was stamped from a `>=` / `<` constraint row (the new `age_bound_stamp_source` metadata on every compiled age-band spec reads `constraint_rows` unless the fact has a dimension named `age` or `age` plus a bound suffix (`age_upper_bound`, …), whose operator-less value the Ledger stamp can write under the same key); an exact-age restatement is refused; and `<=` / `>` rows (stamped `_upper_bound_inclusive` / `_lower_bound_exclusive`) stay refused by bare key, since the compiled band drops the operator.
