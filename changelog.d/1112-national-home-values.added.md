The national release now carries Land Registry home values (microcosm#1112). A new spine stage, `regional_home_values`, runs after the high-value home support copies and gives every England-and-Wales owner household with a value the one its region's uprated Land Registry distribution holds at the household's weighted mid-rank among the owner households of its FRS region, at the spine's weights. It ranks as the dense line's value node does (ties broken by a keyed draw; the support copies spread over the top stratum by their own draws), reads the region level of the same knots resource (`uk/high_value_home_value_knots.json`; Wales is `W92000004`), and moves `property_wealth` by the value's change. Scotland, Northern Ireland, renters and owners without a value keep theirs, and the council-tax band is untouched.

Before it, the national line solved the bound spine with the WAS-imputed values after the regional uprating, not the regions' Land Registry values. The dense line is unchanged: the stage keeps the value it replaced in `main_residence_value_rank_basis`, the dense value node receives that column through its slice and ranks on it (refusing if it is missing), and the release boundary drops it on both lines.

The value reaches three national rows: `obr.sdlt`, through the main-home purchase value, and `ons.land.land_value` and `ons.land.household_land_value`, through `property_wealth` and policyengine-uk's land intensity; corporate land value does not move. The region knots run up to each region's largest sale and down to its smallest, as the dense line's constituency knots do, so the national line places more homes in the top surcharge bands than the derived stock facts; the tails are to be fixed on both lines together (#1112, María's ruling of 2026-10-17).
