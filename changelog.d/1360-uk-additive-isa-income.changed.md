The UK producer now stores interest outside ISAs in `savings_interest_income` and ISA interest apart in `individual_savings_account_interest_income`, and declares policyengine-uk's additive ISA income contract (`{"version": 1, "representation": "additive", "projection": "additive"}`) on every frame and H5 it writes; `tax_free_savings_income` is left to the engine, which adds the ISA parts. Frames carry the contract they were loaded with, so the enhanced-FRS reference and older Microcosm files keep their legacy gross reading, and a build refuses them as inputs. Base-year cash and tax are unchanged: predictors that read gross interest read the two parts together and the SPI stage-2 draws keep their salts. From the base year the engine grows each part by its own index instead of growing gross interest and taking off an unchanged ISA part, which removes a small overstatement of taxable interest in projected years (microcosm#1360).
