UK employee pension contributions are capped at the pay they come out of, on FRS rows and on SPI-channel rows. On an SPI-channel row the forest still decides, from the row's own drawn pay, whether it has an employer contribution; a member whose FRS twin has an ASHE rate takes that rate times its own pay, so the amount follows the redrawn pay, and any other member keeps the forest's amount, bounded by ASHE's top rate.
