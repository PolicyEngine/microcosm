UK employee pension contributions are capped at the pay they come out of, on FRS rows and on SPI-channel rows. An SPI-channel row takes its employer contribution as #1069 sets an FRS row's: its FRS twin's scheme record times its own pay, so a twin's ASHE rate carries onto the drawn pay and a twin outside any employer scheme gives none. Only a row whose twin has no pay keeps the forest's draw, bounded by ASHE's top rate.
