The UK LCFS stage bands recipient households for the NEED energy rake on gross cash income, the concept the donor (LCFS p344p) and NEED band on (microcosm#1164). No spine stage wrote a gross income, so the recipient fell back to its HBAI net income and sat in too low a NEED income band. The engine's `household_gross_income` is not the concept either: it adds capital gains and the imputed value of NHS, education and transport spending, about £15,000 a household on average, which put the recipient in too high a band (a 7.8 % NEED residual on the K25 spine). The recipient's gross income is now its HBAI net income with the deductions policyengine-uk takes to reach it added back (council tax and domestic rates, income tax, National Insurance, student loan repayments, employee and personal pension contributions, maintenance and child payments, LVT), from the new `uk_runtime.household_income` helper; the stage materializes those for the band alone, and an engine-lane test fails if the engine's HBAI deduction list changes. On the K25 spine its distribution sits close to the donor's (weighted medians about £46k and £49k). The rake then converges at 2.40 % electricity and 2.82 % gas in the income margin, a cross-margin gap with NEED's other margins, so the `energy_rake` gate's residual rule moves from 2.5 % to 3.5 % on María's ruling of 2026-10-12. Electricity and gas by income move on every row; the DESNZ level holds.
