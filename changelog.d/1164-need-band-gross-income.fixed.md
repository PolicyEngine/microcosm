The UK LCFS stage bands recipient households for the NEED energy rake on the engine's `household_gross_income`, the concept the donor and NEED band on (microcosm#1164). The donor bands on LCFS gross income (p344p), but no spine stage writes `household_gross_income`, so the recipient fell back to its HBAI net income and sat in too low a NEED income band: the rake then gave richer households the lower bands' mean electricity and gas. The stage now materializes `household_gross_income` with its income predictors, for the band alone (it is not a QRF predictor). Electricity and gas by income move on every row; the DESNZ level and the NEED margins still hold.
