Per-clone engine blocks now stand for the pool when the pool's clones differ (#931). In every simulation a dense measure block builds (`--engine-blocks K`), policyengine-uk's `shareholding`, `corporate_land_value` and `consumption_shareholding` are set, at each period the block computes, to the block's own value times `c_b = f_b·S_b/S`: its pool-mass factor times its allocation-key total over the pool's. The block first checks that the engine's own key total is `f_b·S_b` and that the engine carries each key and the weights by one factor per year. A guard then records every formula each simulation runs and judges policyengine-uk's whole-population formulas against a declaration: the share formulas corrected, `months_since_last_birthday` and `attends_private_school` stored, the engine's deciles and relative-poverty flags refused, and the rest zero under the parameters checked. Nothing a block or batch computes may fall in the Universal Credit rebalancing years (2026 on), where policyengine-uk draws new-claimant status by benefit-unit position.

- **What exact means.** `engine_population_representation.exact` now holds only when every block applied its correction, passed its checks and got a clean verdict, and then means equal to one engine over the pool to float32 rounding, not bitwise. `exact_basis` (`pool_share_injection_with_population_guard`) names this basis, and `exact_scope`, which the release's `engine_measurement` limitation copies, is rewritten. A receipt without `exact_basis` predates the change, when exact meant identical copies. The measure step records a violation, which leaves the run not releasable. The release verdict, the local pre-flight, the dense assembler and the data contract read the same fields as before; none requires `exact_basis`.
- **Spine batches.** A spine engine call whose frame carries `household_clone_index` with more than one clone runs one simulation per clone, each reading the pool's shares (`c_b = S_b/S`) and judged under a pass that raises. A frame without the column, or with one clone, takes one simulation as before. Stage 52 (`private_school_attendance`) restates policyengine-uk's attendance over the whole frame from household-local engine values, with microdf's `MicroSeries.percentile_rank` and the engine's own parameters. Over one simulation it stores the engine's flags after checking the restatement bit for bit; clone by clone it stores the restatement. Its evidence gains `rank_route` and `engine_parity_checked`.
- **The shared pieces.** The country-agnostic machinery (pool shares, the checks, injection, the formula-run recorder, judging, `EngineBlock` and batches) is `microcosm.frame.adapters.engine_population`, and the UK declaration is `microcosm.frame.adapters.policyengine_uk_population`. Each block and batch first checks the declaration against the installed policyengine-uk; a single block and an unbatched spine call never read it. A re-pin that changes the whole-population list or a pinned formula source therefore leaves every dense block uncorrected and the run not releasable, and refuses every batched spine call, until the declaration is reviewed again.
- **What moves.** No spine frame carries `household_clone_index` yet, so every spine call takes one simulation and no spine data moves (frame, H5, stored flags), but stage 52's evidence gains its two fields on both lines, so its stage-evidence digest moves. Identical copies take `c_b` within float64 rounding of 1, so the correction leaves their measures bitwise as they were, and a single block, the national line's, gains none of the new receipt fields. Today's dense receipts gain `exact_basis`, `share_correction`, `population_reach` and `annual_income_projection_factors`; `block_sensitivity` now names engine variables, so `present_in_this_run` can list them, drops the SLC entry and gains `corrected_formulas`; and each block's resolver receipt gains `engine_pool_shares` and `engine_population_reach`. Every spine stage and the full and national target kernels re-key, and the engine-calling stages also bind the two new modules. The annual-income factor, the one data change, has its own fragment.
