`microcosm-build-uk --release-role dense --inspection-export` exports, packages and stages a candidate's H5 for inspection even when a structural full-build gate blocks the artifact. It is candidate-only (refused with `--release-candidate` and by the national role), records `inspection_export` in the request, the export descriptor and the manifest parameters, leaves the bundle unreleasable and keeps the blocked exit status. The kernel lives in `uk_runtime.inspection_export`, so default node keys are unchanged.
