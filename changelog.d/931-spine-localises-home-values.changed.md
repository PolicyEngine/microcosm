The WAS home-value localiser #931 group (c) added unwired is now declared: `was_wealth` reads each England and Wales owner's main residence off its assigned constituency's knots after the chain's second segment, its later segments condition on that value, and `regional_property_uprating`, dividing by the draw the localiser keeps, returns each of those homes to its knot value. `regional_home_values`, which wrote the regions' Land Registry values after the support copies, retires, so the packaged spine has 60 stages.
