Add the `compatibility_recertification` release type: a published US calibration release republished byte for byte with a measured `compatible_model_packages` claim for a newer policyengine-us. It carries the parent's published manifests as its identity, replays the native loaders under the four tested wheels, records the engine's reviewed column changes in both directions, and embeds the drift of the critical rows and headline totals between the built-with and tested engines. Publication refuses a child whose parent tag does not serve the carried manifest bytes. `tools/build_us_compatibility_recertification.py` fetches the parent, builds the candidate and scores it; see `docs/us-compatibility-recertification.md`.
