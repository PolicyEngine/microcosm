The national rule-1 evaluation (`candidate_score.prune_incumbent_unresolvable`, microcosm#578) bounds its pruning probes by the reviewed incumbent-unresolvable register instead of a fixed ten: three probes per signed measure, and the clean probe. A signed measure's targets can fail over more than one probe; with nine signed measures, a candidate built after microcosm#1439 needed eleven, so the fixed bound refused a candidate whose every failure was signed. Each failing probe still prunes at least one target or refuses, and the bound still stops a measure that keeps failing.
