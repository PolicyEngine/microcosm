UK builds mark the outputs that rest on the SPI 2022-23 Public Use Tape (SN 9422), whose Special Conditions add two obligations to the End User Licence: no attempt to attribute a record to a named individual, and analyses of the tape go to HMRC at least ten working days before they are distributed or published (microcosm#1095). `microcosm.build.uk_runtime.spi_dependency` reads which of a run's stages declare the tape among their artifacts. The spine's build record carries the marker (`spi_public_use_tape`), the spine provenance in the calibrated candidate and its signed diagnostics carry it on, and both release assemblers restate it in `release_manifest.json`: on the microdata's own artifact entry (`spi_public_use_tape_dependent`) and, with the conditions, under `licensed_data`. The assemblers rederive it from the stages the signed provenance names and refuse a recorded marker that disagrees. How SPI-dependent outputs are released stays María's ruling; the marker records the dependency only.
