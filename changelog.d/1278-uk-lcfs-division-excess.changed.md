The LCFS stage now levels the COICOP divisions' relative over-draw before it holds each recomposed total to the donor's maximum (microcosm#1278; María's rulings of 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24). A division's ratio is the spend the chain drew for it per frame household over the donor's mean of the same spend per donor household: for housing and transport, the remainder against the donor's total less the same parts, since the levelled electricity, gas, heating fuels, road fuel and priced bus fares keep their published levels and are no over-draw (Vahid's review of microcosm#1383, item 9); for any other division, its whole draw. A division above the divisions' shared ratio, measured once before any levelling, comes down to it, and no other division moves: measured again after each cut, the shared ratio would fall until every division sat at the lowest one's, close to levelling to the donor totals (María's ruling of 2026-10-24). The recompose operation declares the rule as `level_division_excess`, the recomposed-totals receipt records each division's ratio and factor, the shared ratio and each total's `levelling_factor`, and the stage-time gate refuses a missing or undeclared levelling, a factor outside [0, 1], a division that moves though it is not above the shared ratio, and a total whose remainder moves by other than its division's factor.
