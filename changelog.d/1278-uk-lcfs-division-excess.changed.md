The LCFS stage now levels the COICOP divisions' relative over-draw before it holds each recomposed total to the donor's maximum (microcosm#1278; María's ruling of 2026-10-23). A division's ratio is its frame total per household over the donor's mean per household; the shared ratio is the twelve divisions' together. A division above the shared ratio comes down to it, and no other division moves. Only the chain's own draws move: a division's whole draw, or a recomposed total's remainder, so the levelled electricity, gas, heating fuels, road fuel and priced bus fares keep their published levels. The recompose operation declares the rule as `level_division_excess`, the recomposed-totals receipt records each division's ratio and factor and each total's `levelling_factor`, and the stage-time gate refuses a missing or undeclared levelling, a factor outside [0, 1], a division that moves though it is not above the shared ratio, and a total whose remainder moves by other than its division's factor.
