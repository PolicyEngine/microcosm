The UK full build assigns geography on the shared atomic-geography operators: households are keyed by their explicit spine lineage (`uk.full.identity`), one atomic area is drawn per household by census household count within FRS region under a keyed stream, larger geographies derive from the support mappings, and the shared gate must pass before the pool checkpoint. `--geography-assignment legacy` keeps the sequential ladder draw for measurement builds; release candidates refuse it and pin the three supports (#931).
