UK firms can carry a paybill (microcosm#1705). `uk_runtime/firm_paybill.py` sets policyengine-uk's `firm_paybill` to the firm's headcount times the mean annual gross pay of its SIC 2007 section from ONS ASHE Table 4.7a (*Annual pay - Gross*), 2025 revised edition, sheet `All` (all employee jobs, full-time and part-time). ASHE annual pay covers the tax year ending 5 April of the reference year, so the 2025 edition is tax year 2024-25. The section means are committed under `tests/fixtures/uk/firm_paybill_2024/` with the zip's and workbook's sha256s, and `tools/extract_uk_firm_paybill_table.py --check` re-extracts them from the downloaded zip. ONS suppresses section U, which, like the `UNKNOWN` section, takes the table's all-employees mean; `uk_firm_paybill_fallback_counts` counts the firms that do (none in the 2024-25 test build).

The export adds `firm_paybill` (`UK_FIRM_PAYBILL_TABLE_COLUMNS`) only when attributed rows carry it, and `firm_paybill` is now `assumed` in `UK_FIRM_POLICYENGINE_UK_INPUT_STATUS`: every firm pays its section's mean, and ONS employment, which counts working proprietors, stands in for employees. ASHE's annual figures leave out jobs held for under a year and youth rates, so the means lean high. `build_uk_firm_dataset(paybill_dir=...)` and `tools/build_uk_firms.py --paybill-dir` turn it on and record the CSV's hash, the weighted paybill and the fallback counts in the receipt; without them the table and receipt are unchanged. The receipt also records `uk_firm_paybill` (status `assumed`, the method and tax year 2024-25) and the weighted paybill and employees split by `in_frame`; a paybill source with any `data_vintage` other than 2024-25 raises `ValueError`.

Measured on the 20-iteration thinned 2024-25 test build (seed 42), the weighted paybill is £1.071 trillion on 26.7 million weighted employees, 6% above ASHE's own 24.9 million jobs times £40,445. That excess is the out-of-frame stratum: zero-turnover traders outside the frame (`in_frame` false) carry about 2.43 million weighted employees and about £103.8 billion of paybill (about 9.7%), and in-frame paybill is about 4% below ASHE's jobs times mean. Their paybill is kept, not zeroed. No firm has a zero headcount, so working proprietors get a paybill too: sole traders carry about £123 billion and partnerships about £45 billion (about 16% together). On the full 2024-25 build (2,946,015 rows, 1,000 epochs) the weighted paybill is £1.0255 trillion and no firm falls back to the all-employees mean.
