DWP's record of the Universal Credit carer element now decides carer status on linked claims (B3 of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095; María's ruling of 2026-11-11). On the FRS's linked UC claims, the build held a carer on about seven in ten claims whose award DWP records with a carer element; of those it missed, about four in five recorded no care at all and most others care below 35 hours. DWP pays the element only for caring at least 35 hours a week for a severely disabled person, so where no claimant or partner of such a claim reports 35 or more care hours or Carer's Allowance, `frs_spine` gives the UC row's person 35 care hours, the lower edge of the first band at that line, and `frs_disability` holds the claim's carers at the line caring for a severely disabled person whatever the care tab says. Claims without a linked record, and every other person, keep the survey's answers. Both stages' receipts count the people moved in rounded counts and support bands.
