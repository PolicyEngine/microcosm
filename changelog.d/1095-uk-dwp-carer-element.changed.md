DWP's record of the Universal Credit carer element now decides carer status on linked claims (B3 of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095; María's ruling of 2026-11-11). On the FRS's linked UC claims, the build held a carer on about seven in ten claims whose award DWP records with a carer element; of those it missed, about four in five recorded no care at all and most others care below 35 hours. DWP pays the element only for caring at least 35 hours a week for a severely disabled person, so where no claimant or partner of such a claim reports 35 or more care hours or Carer's Allowance, `frs_spine` gives 35 care hours, the lower edge of the first band at that line, to the claimant or partner who reports more care hours, or to the UC row's person on a tie. On a claim DWP also records with the LCWRA element, a carer with LCWRA gets only that element (UC Regs 2013 reg. 29(4)), so the carer isn't the claimant who holds it: one whom the LCWRA proxy flags (`is_disabled_for_benefits`, unless their own ESA award lacks the support component, as policyengine-uk's formula reads it, and as Y1 keeps it on such a claim) is passed over while another isn't (Vahid's review of microcosm#1583, Should 2; María's decision of 2026-11-12). Then `frs_disability` holds the claim's carers at the line caring for a severely disabled person whatever the care tab says. Claims without a linked record, and every other person, keep the survey's answers. Both stages' receipts count the people moved in rounded counts and support bands, with the raises that go to the partner of the UC row's person and the LCWRA claims where the proxy moves the raise or flags every claimant.
