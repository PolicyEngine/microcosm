The UK LCFS stage starts a mobile-home NEED cell at its NEED mean when every household in it drew no kWh, so the spine builds again after #1270 (microcosm#1240). Mobile homes have no NEED property-type row, and the energy rake takes them to the all-dwellings mean, the incumbent's translation. A household whose drawn spend is at or below the standing charge has no kWh, and the rake, which multiplies, cannot move a cell in which no household has any: the `energy_rake` gate stopped main's spine build at such a cell once #1270's copies changed the frame and with it the draws. The `price_domestic_energy` step now declares `zero_kwh_cells_at_need_mean` (the accommodation margin's `all_dwellings` cells). Before the first sweep, the rake starts each declared cell in which no household drew kWh at the cell's NEED mean (electricity over every household, gas over the gas-connected ones) and leaves a cell with any positive household to the rake. The households of a started cell take its NEED mean in place of the spend they drew, at or below the standing charge, so their energy spend rises above the draw, as the incumbent's translation for mobile homes has it; the rake is not biased, since the accommodation margin holds the cell's mean wherever it starts. The `energy_rake` receipt lists the cells it started. The gate fails on a started cell the stage does not declare and on any other cell with a zero mean, and its details count the started cells without naming them.
