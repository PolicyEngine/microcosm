The UK spine now draws `would_claim_carers_allowance` for the carers the Carer's Allowance earnings limit keeps out (microcosm#1319). The flag followed reported receipt (#882), and a carer above the limit cannot report the allowance, so no reform that widens it reached a new claimant; the Independent Review of Carer's Allowance Overpayments (July 2025) estimates that the April 2025 rise of the limit from £151 to £196 a week brought about 61,000 more people into eligibility. A new stage, `carers_allowance_take_up`, runs after `child_benefit_take_up` and keeps reported receipt as its anchor. One engine materialization at the survey year reads `is_gainfully_employed_for_carers_allowance` at the 2024-25 limit, and a person outside Scotland whose `care_hours` reach the engine's hours condition (35 a week), who reports no allowance and whom that test holds gainfully employed claims at the take-up contract's new `carers_allowance_newly_entitled` rate, 0.6 (María's ruling of 2026-10-20, sensitivity 0.45 to 0.75), on an identity-keyed uniform. Reporters stay true, and carers under the limit who report no allowance stay false. Scotland stays closed, since Carer Support Payment reads the same flag and the engine applies it no earnings test. The receipt bands support on distinct source households, the stage has no health gate, and the roster moves from 45 to 46 stages.
