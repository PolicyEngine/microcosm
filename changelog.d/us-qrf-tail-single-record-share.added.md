The US release's QRF tail evidence now reports each QRF output's largest single record. The tail gate tests the top 100 records' share of a column's weighted absolute mass, so one record can carry a fifth of a column and pass: in the #1251 rebuild one tax unit of weight 644.7 carried 21.7% of line 32's weighted absolute mass at initial weights, while the top 100 tax units carried 44.9%. `microcosm.build.gates.single_record_concentration` measures a column's top-1 share, the weight of that record, the top-k share and the carrier count, on the gate's own population. `qrf_tail_concentration.json` records it for every numeric QRF output, dense and thin ones included, under `surface.single_record_concentration`. It is reported only: it adds no threshold, and the gate's verdict and details are unchanged. A column with no mass, or a mass beyond the float range, reports no share. The release dry run carries the share and the weight on each row at base weights, and the post-export probe compares the block with a release that recorded it.
