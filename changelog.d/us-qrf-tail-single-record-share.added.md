The US release's QRF tail evidence now reports each QRF output's largest single record. The tail gate tests the top 100 records' share of a column's weighted absolute mass, so one record can carry a fifth of a column and pass: in #1259's seed-0 rerun of the #1251 chain, one tax unit of weight 644.7 carried 21.7% of line 32's weighted absolute mass at initial weights, while the top 100 of its 16,001 carriers carried 45.1%. `microcosm.build.gates.single_record_concentration` measures a column's top-1 share, the weight of that record, the top-k share (the gate's own share, capped at 1) and the carrier count, on the gate's own population. `qrf_tail_concentration.json` records it for every numeric QRF output, dense and thin ones included, under `surface.single_record_concentration`. The measurement adds no threshold and changes neither the gate's verdict nor its details. It runs after the gate, and an output it cannot measure goes under `unmeasured` with the reason rather than failing the run. A column with no mass, or a mass beyond the float range, reports no share. The release dry run carries the share and the weight on each row at base weights, and the post-export probe compares the block, unmeasured reasons included, with a release that recorded it.
