The UK spine's LCFS consumption stage names its household adult and child counts for what they are, and its stage notes load in full (microcosm#1095, the rest of uk-data#486). The LCFS G018 and G019 counts were renamed to the engine's person flags `is_adult` and `is_child`, and the recipient summed those engine flags per household; policyengine-uk#1896 deprecates them. The predictors are now `household_adult_count` and `household_child_count`, and the recipient counts its members from `age` with the survey's age-18 split, so the values and the imputation are unchanged. The stage's `notes` were a plain YAML scalar containing " #890", which YAML reads as the start of a comment, so the 642 characters after it were dropped at load; they are now quoted.
