Constituency and local-authority rows now follow the ten-household floor for their published figures (María's ruling of 2026-10-23 on the local line): a local row resting on fewer than ten source households keeps its place without its model figures, and a published local row that would give it back by difference is withheld beside it, as national, regional and BRMA rows are. The area-support rule still decides which local rows bind. `uk_withheld_row_sets` takes its span per block: the national, regional and BRMA rows together, and the local rows one grain and area at a time, since the local surface is too large for one dense span and binds many families in each area; a difference across areas or grains is outside the check. The report guard matches a withheld local row by its own `family/area/metric` key, and a bare area code only for a withheld BRMA row (`uk_withheld_gate_areas`), so a published row of another family in the same area may still be named.
