Constituency and local-authority rows now follow the ten-household floor for their published figures (María's ruling of 2026-10-23 on the local line): a local row resting on fewer than ten source households keeps its place without its model figures, and a published local row that would give it back by difference is withheld beside it, as national, regional and BRMA rows are. The area-support rule still decides which local rows bind. `uk_withheld_row_sets` takes its span per block, since the local surface is too large for one dense span: the national, regional and BRMA rows together; each region's rows of every grain, so a region row less its published local cells, or a region's constituencies less its local authorities, is caught; and each local family with the higher-grain targets the cross-grain declarations or a nested measurement tie it to, so a national row less a family's published cells is caught too (Vahid's review of microcosm#1383, item 1). The blocks overlap, so a row one block withholds beside a thin one is protected in every other block that holds it, and the blocks run again until none withholds another row. Local rows need the frame's household region. The report guard matches a withheld local row by its own `family/area/metric` key, and a bare area code only for a withheld BRMA row (`uk_withheld_gate_areas`), so a published row of another family in the same area may still be named. BRMA rows publish from ten households under the national block's check, and beside Scotland's local rows in its region's block where a run carries them (María's rulings of 2026-10-17 and 2026-10-24).
