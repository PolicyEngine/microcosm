The `spi_benefit_coherence` receipt now shows how much of the SPI copies' contributory JSA and ESA rests on pension payments, which the stage does not reduce the awards for (Vahid's review of microcosm#1426). For each award it gives the support and weighted award of the copies whose pension payments are above the award's threshold. Pension payments here are `private_pension_income`, the SPI tape's PENSION, over 52 weeks. The thresholds are £50 a week for JSA, above which JSA Regs 1996 reg 81 takes every pound off the award (policyengine-uk's `gov.dwp.JSA.contrib.pension_disregard`), and £85 a week for ESA, above which ESA Regs 2008 reg 74 takes half of every pound. The figure is the most such a reduction could take off. It is withheld where one or two source households carry it or the rest of the award. The stage's docstring now also says why the awards are not reduced, and that its weekly work tests average the tape's annual pay over 52 weeks, which understates part-year claimants.
