The UK spine's per-clone engine batches go through the read-year window of microcosm#1615 (#931). Each clone's simulation is built by the same construction as a call's one simulation: `PolicyEngineUKEngine._read_year_simulation` projects the clone's dataset to the call's year on the adapter's own parameters, stops the Universal Credit rebalancing loop at that year, and constructs the simulation through `_simulation`, which filters policyengine-uk's `EntityIdWarning`. An engine block's annual-income and counterfactual simulations are built by the block's wrapped factory inside the measure resolver's cut. policyengine-uk builds each year only from earlier ones, so no value at the call's year moves. Tests (engine-free, made-up data): both adapter routes project, open the cut and construct in that order, with `_simulation` constructing while the cut is open, and an engine block's later simulation is built inside the resolver's cut.
