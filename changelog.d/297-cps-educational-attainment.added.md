The US fiscal-refresh build can carry educational attainment as an auxiliary person column, `cps_educational_attainment`, beside the raw Census `A_HGA` it is classified from (microcosm#297; the student debt pilot, Project 2). `--cps-educational-attainment` joins each person to the pinned official CPS ASEC person member on (income year, `PERIDNUM`), checks the Census household, person and age identity, and never predicts or defaults a value; a support clone takes its source person's code. `--asec-attainment-source INCOME_YEAR=PATH` supplies local archives, and unmapped years are fetched and verified against the same pins.

The classes are the seven ACS S1501 population-25-and-over categories (less than 9th grade; 9th to 12th grade, no diploma; high school graduate or equivalency; some college, no degree; associate's, occupational and academic together; bachelor's; graduate or professional), plus `not_in_universe` under 15. The ACS PUMS `SCHL` map is defined and tested; the ACS-local lane does not read `SCHL` yet, and the stage refuses ACS-spine rows. The degree-ROI classes are `associates_degree` (two-year) and `bachelors_degree` (four-year) against the high-school baseline.

Provenance. `microcosm.data.stored_inputs.US_AUXILIARY_STORED_COLUMNS` flags the column as auxiliary (carried for conditioning and calibration, not consumed by rules) and joins the stored-input register.

Targets. The US target compiler binds ACS 1-year S1501 state cells for the population 25 and over (one per state and class) as person counts of the column (family `census_educational_attainment`, materializer `population_attainment`), and leaves the universe totals, the 18-to-24 universe and Puerto Rico unbound. The fiscal-refresh materializer counts them, and a frame without the column refuses rather than binding zeros. The pinned Chronicle feed carries no S1501 facts, so no release binds them until PolicyEngine/chronicle#347 merges and the US feed is re-pinned.

The receipt is `experiments/297-cps-educational-attainment-receipt.md`, run on Modal with `experiments/modal_297.py`.
