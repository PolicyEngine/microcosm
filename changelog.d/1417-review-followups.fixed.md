Follow-ups from the reviews of microcosm#1417, #1109 and #1416. `tools/check_uk_itl_projections.py` refuses `--current-income-for-annual-measures` unless `--diagnostics` or `--references` names the in-sample basis: this tree's target references say what a build of this tree binds, not what a file built before the annual income descriptors bound, so on that basis every band and area cell of such a file read `in` at the default `--year`. `uk_runtime.itl_differential` names the engine variables its cells read (`TAX`, `TOTAL_INCOME`, `IS_SP_AGE`, `AGE`), and new engine-free tests hold them, and each bound cell's mask, to the contract's `policyengine` bindings of the three bound families through the build's own binding interpreter, so an edit to a binding's filter, band variable or band reading fails until the check follows it. The note on `NATIONAL_TOLERANCE` cites the vendored band counts (at the calendar-2025 window five of the eleven bands hold under 400,000 taxpayers, and the top three hold fewer than the smallest area cell) in place of a claim that national cells sum over millions; the tolerances stay 5% and 10%. The check imports `UK_HMRC_ITL_UK_GEOGRAPHY_ID` instead of repeating it. The US fiscal refresh's reviewed `estate_income` exclusion also asks for re-adjudication when the flag is absent and the raw base becomes the reference, and the `materialize_target_bindings` docstring limits the plain-row period refusal to a row that declares a period other than the calibration period.
