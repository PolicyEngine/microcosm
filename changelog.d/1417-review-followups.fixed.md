Follow-ups from the reviews of microcosm#1417, #1109 and #1416. `tools/check_uk_itl_projections.py` refuses `--current-income-for-annual-measures` unless `--diagnostics` or `--references` names the in-sample basis: this tree's target references say what a build of this tree binds, not what a file built before the annual income descriptors bound, so on that basis every band and area cell of such a file read `in` at the default `--year`. It also refuses an empty reason, which turned the current-income route off, and the receipt records the reason. `uk_runtime.itl_differential` names the engine variables its cells read (`TAX`, `TOTAL_INCOME`, `IS_SP_AGE`, `AGE`). New engine-free tests hold the contract's three bound `policyengine` bindings to what the check reads, every key but `notes` and `metric_name`, and run the build's own `materialize_target_bindings` on a `UKFrameTargetAdapter` against the check's masks for all 35 bound cells, band edges from the compiled spec and area geography included. An edit to a binding's filter, band variable or band reading (whole pence, an upper bound, a period factor) therefore fails until the check follows it. The note on `NATIONAL_TOLERANCE` cites the vendored band counts (at the calendar-2025 window five of the eleven bands hold under 400,000 taxpayers, and the top three hold fewer than the smallest area cell) in place of a claim that national cells sum over millions, and a test reads the note against the rows; the tolerances stay 5% and 10%. The check imports `UK_HMRC_ITL_UK_GEOGRAPHY_ID` instead of repeating it. The US fiscal refresh's reviewed `estate_income` exclusion also asks for re-adjudication when the flag is absent and the raw base becomes the reference, and the `materialize_target_bindings` docstring limits the plain-row period refusal to a row that declares a period other than the calibration period.
