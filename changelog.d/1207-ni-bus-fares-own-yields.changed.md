Northern Ireland's bus fares are re-priced from its fiscal-year yield to calendar 2024 with its own published yields (microcosm#1207). The `price_bus_journeys` area declares `price_index: {basis: own_fiscal_yields}`: the yield `R / (B - C)` of the fiscal years opening in 2023 and 2024, from the area's own DfI receipts, journeys and full-fare-concession journeys, weighted three months and nine as `calendar_year_window` weights them, over the 2024-25 yield. It replaces England's local bus fares index, a declared proxy that carried the January 2025 rise in the English fare cap Northern Ireland did not have. The factor moves from 0.982 to 0.989, so Northern Ireland's fare per trip rises by about 0.7%. `sources.schema.json` admits the new basis beside a published index.
