Bind UK pension contributions by range of total income from SPI Table 3.8 and stop clipping reported personal pension contributions (#1069 c8; PolicyEngine/chronicle#302 via #305). Four targets in the `hmrc_spi` family, each fanned over the table's total-income bands for tax year 2023-24: net-pay-arrangement contributions (the engine's `employee_pension_contributions`) and relief-at-source contributions (`personal_pension_contributions`), amount and count. Amounts move to calendar 2025 by the engine's average-earnings index, counts by HMRC's projected taxpayer growth for the band, as the other SPI counts do. Active national references go from 1,224 to 1,276.

The FRS spine no longer clips personal and stakeholder pension contributions at the 95th percentile of every PENPROV amount. The clip removed 28% of the reported amount (1.8% of rows) against the relief-at-source total the new rows bind; the engine caps the relief itself at the annual allowance.
