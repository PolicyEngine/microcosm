Spousal maintenance now has its own column, `spousal_maintenance_income`, the input policyengine-uk 2.149.0 adds (pe-uk#2572; microcosm#1320, María's rulings of 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23). It is ALIAMT, alimony or an allowance from a previous partner on the adult's own behalf (its usual amount ALUAMT where ALIUS says the last was not usual), plus APAMT, the allowances from a husband or wife who lives away, which the FRS asks only of married adults and which leave `private_transfer_income`. Universal Credit (UC Regulations 2013 reg. 66(1)(d)), Pension Credit and Housing Benefit count it, Housing Benefit less £15 a week where the family includes a child; the child maintenance in `maintenance_income` counts in none of them. APDAMT, the household bills such a partner pays directly to third parties, stays a private transfer. An adult tab without the three alimony columns refuses, and the column joins the export allow-list. ALIAMT is an approximation: the FRS asks for alimony or an allowance "from a previous partner", so it also holds payments from a former cohabitant, which policyengine-uk's `spousal_maintenance_income` leaves out and Universal Credit and Pension Credit do not count; the survey does not say whether the previous partner was a spouse or civil partner (Vahid's review of microcosm#1383, item 13).
