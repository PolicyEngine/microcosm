`obr.sdlt` now binds households' SDLT, not the whole OBR line (uk-data#564, microcosm#1095). The OBR's Stamp Duty Land Tax line covers every buyer, and HMRC's other buyers (companies, partnerships and collective investment schemes) pay about a third of the receipts; policyengine-uk charges households' `stamp_duty_land_tax` and adds `corporate_sdlt` beside it, so binding the whole line pushed households' SDLT up by the companies' share. The target is the OBR line's calendar-2025 window (three twelfths of 2024-25, nine of 2025-26) times individuals' share of HMRC's 2024-25 SDLT receipts, residential (Table 8a) and non-residential (Table 8b) together, both from the pinned Chronicle feed. uk-data#569 (a draft) subtracts policyengine-uk's corporate SDLT statistic instead, which needs the unreleased pe-uk#2322.

A new target value operation, `calendar_year_window_scaled_by_ratio`, carries the rule: the `calendar_year_window` of the reference's own series, translated by the quotient of two operands that each pin their own period, may come from another publisher, and may each sum a declared number of cells (`expected_member_count`). The quotient must be a share in (0, 1], as for `scaled_by_ratio`.
