The UK ISA subscriptions stage (`isa_subscriptions`, stage 53) gives every holder tied on a balance one weighted mid-rank (#931; María's ruling 7 of 7 Nov). Half of each subscriber's ISA amount key, and of each Lifetime ISA payer's payment key, is the person's balance rank, blended with a keyed uniform. The rank sorted balances stably and gave each row its own mid-rank, so tied balances ranked by row position, although its docstring said ties share a rank. Ties are common: the copies and clone halves made after the WAS wealth draw carry their root's ISA totals, so a single-adult one ties its root, and QRF draws repeat a few atoms. A root sits before its copies in the table, so it ranked below them, as clone 0 would below its clones once the spine clones households at its start. The stage was also not invariant to row order where balances tie: on a synthetic walk of 400 holders with balances on five atoms, permuting the rows moved 372 amounts, and none with distinct balances.

- **The rank.** `_weighted_rank(values, weights, ids)` orders the rows by (value, id). Every member of a tie group takes the group's mid-rank, `(F(x-) + F(x)) / 2` over the total weight. A value held by one row keeps its own, `(c - w / 2) / total`, computed as before. Both callers pass the person ids, so the keyed uniform alone orders tied holders, exchangeably, and each rank is the same bit for bit whatever the row order.
- **No seed, no declaration.** The rank draws nothing, and no declared text says anything about ties, so no seed, salt, manifest key or generated file changes. The band walk and the Lifetime ISA strata stay pooled.
- **What moves.** Nothing where no balance ties: on three synthetic frames of distinct balances every output is unchanged, bit for bit. Elsewhere:
  - Tied holders' ranks move. A singleton's rank is unchanged bit for bit wherever the rows arrive in id order, as the spine's tables do, but its amount can still move, since the band walk and the within-band order are pooled over the holders a tie moves past.
  - On three synthetic frames with balances on five atoms, every tied holder's rank moved; 248 to 260 of the 363 to 376 tied subscribers and 329 to 344 of the 420 to 430 other subscribers moved amount; and 46 to 56 of the 52 to 68 Lifetime ISA payers, all of them tied, moved payment. No balance moved.
  - With three geographic clones per holder on five atoms, clone 0 now gets the least of its three in about a third of the holders, against about 43 per cent (ISA amounts) and 40 per cent (Lifetime ISA payments) before.
  - Of the 99 UK kernel hashes only stage 53's moves. Node keys move from stage 53 on: 3 of the gated spine's 81 nodes and 35 of the in-graph build's 113. The national line on a bound spine keeps its keys.
