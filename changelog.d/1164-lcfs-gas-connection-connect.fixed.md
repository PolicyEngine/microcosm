The UK LCFS stage connects households to gas where its draw leaves a region below the published DESNZ share (microcosm#1164). The connection step only disconnected: it walked gas-positive households down to each region's share of gas meters over electricity meters. The old energy split gave gas to 98 % of LCFS donors, so every region could be cut to its share; the labelled split gives gas to about 80 %, against the about 85 % of homes DESNZ counts gas meters for, so nine of the eleven Great Britain regions fell short and the frame's gas-connected share fell to 79 %. The `price_domestic_energy` step now declares `connect_rule: gas_payer_probability_order`: two weighted fits on the chain's predictors give each zero-gas household the probability that a donor like it pays for gas (a gradient-boosted classifier) and the gas such a donor pays (a regime-gated QRF of the payers' gas spend), and in a region below its share the zero-gas households are connected in an identity-keyed order drawn in proportion to that probability, by the disconnection walk's weight-fitting rule, until the share is met. The NEED rake, the household ceilings and the DESNZ level then run as before. The `energy_rake` gate requires the receipt to name the declared rule and salt and waives a region's shortfall only where it ran out of households to connect.
