Builds now send their calibration diagnostics to the collector at staging and at release (microcosm#1599). Builds and the Modal image require microcosm-emitter 0.15.0 instead of 0.14.0, and microcosm-build's `microcosm-emitter-interface` pin rises to `>=0.15.0,<1` (the lock and `APPROVED_UV_LOCK_SHA256` follow, as does the installed-emitter job's pin). Every emitter Microcosm starts with its Hugging Face module has its collector submissions on. `microcosm-publish-release-build` names each build for the collector (UK builds as licensed, US builds as not), so the emitter submits its calibration diagnostics after the release commit. Once a staged bundle or publication has landed, the build or command waits up to ten minutes for the collector to accept them, and warns without failing when the collector receives nothing (`skipped`), refuses them at intake or has not accepted them yet; the publication command prints the collector's status as `collector_submission`.
