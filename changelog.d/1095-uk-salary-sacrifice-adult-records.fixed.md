The salary-sacrifice forest now predicts only for adult records. The FRS asks the question on adults' job records, so the forest is fitted on adults. Since `frs_spine` carries a dependent child's `CHILD.CHEARNS` as `employment_income` (PolicyEngine/microcosm#1663), every child with pay was given a forest prediction. A dependent child who was not asked (`is_hbai_dependent_child`) now sacrifices none of their pay, and the stage's receipt counts them beside the people not asked who have no pay. The stage's SPI bonus draw now also takes adults alone, as its rule says: SPI-channel adults draw from base-FRS adults. Paid children had joined its donors with no bonus and moved its pay cells' edges. The fix comes from Vahid's final review of PolicyEngine/microcosm#1663 and María's reply of 19 November 2026.
