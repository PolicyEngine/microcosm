The salary-sacrifice forest now predicts only for adult records. The FRS asks the question on adults' job records, so the forest is fitted on adults. Since `frs_spine` carries a dependent child's `CHILD.CHEARNS` as `employment_income` (PolicyEngine/microcosm#1663), every child with pay was given a forest prediction. A dependent child who was not asked (`is_hbai_dependent_child`) now sacrifices none of their pay, and the stage's receipt counts them beside the people not asked who have no pay. The fix comes from Vahid's final review of PolicyEngine/microcosm#1663 and María's reply of 19 November 2026.
