The UK stage receipts and stage checks now read a spine of more than one geographic clone (#931; María's rulings of 10 Nov). Every new count is of distinct true source households, rounded through `receipt_counts`. The spine still builds one clone by default, so no value and no gate verdict moves. At one clone only the `spi_support_channel` and `spi_income_band_donors` receipts change, with the band donor check's details and failure texts and the clone count's refusal texts.

- **Fold receipts.** Every FRS-trained fit folds its training rows through `household_lineage.fold_geographic_clones`: the SPI income stage's reference and stage-2 forests, the SPI employment and housing shells, the UC reporter redraw, UC capital and salary sacrifice. On more than one clone, each of these stages' evidence gains `geographic_fold` (`household_lineage.geographic_fold_receipt`). It gives the clone count; the source households the fit selected, and those whose clones differ on what the fit reads, both rounded as every receipt count is; and the share that differ, given only where ten or more of the selected households differ and none or ten or more do not, and otherwise the count of those that differ again. A one-clone spine's evidence is unchanged.
- **Twin check.** `cross_fit_transport` takes `twin_identities`, read only to find each recipient's twin among the training rows. The SPI income stage passes each copy's twin through its forests' fold, so the check reaches the copies of every clone. Each copy's uniforms stay keyed on its own identity; on one clone the two keys are the same.
- **`spi_support_channel`.** Its receipt gives the frame's clone count. Its two pension-age counts are rounded distinct source households carrying the mass beside them, on rows of positive weight: the pension-age base households', and those of clone 0's pension-age SPI copies. Both are the same at every clone count, and the masses are unchanged.
- **`spi_income_band_donors`.**
  - At every seating, each funding row keeps its stratum and its rounded donor households and reads `<3` for its masses and factor. The receipt gains two booleans: `funding_within_share`, every factor at least one less the declared reallocation share, to the stage check's relative tolerance; and `funding_matches_bands`, the bands and the funding strata each carrying the reallocated mass.
  - On a capped seating, each band's donors and carriers, and the donor count, are rounded distinct source households. Each band's weighted taxpayers and carriers' mean age, and the reallocated mass, read `<3`, and each band's carrier regions are left out. A full seating keeps the plan's exact band counts.
  - The result keeps the exact figures, and its frame, out of its repr.
  - A band short of candidates or seats is refused naming the band and, on a cloned spine, that a clone fell short, without a count.
- **The band donor check.** It reads the funding from the two booleans and requires one less the receipt's reallocation share to reach its minimum funding factor; its details drop the smallest funding factor. On a capped seating it recomputes the seats from the transferred phase's frame. A seat is a band donor row whose id holds no ordinal in a copy-id field minted after the band donors, since every later layer keeps the row it copies at its own id. Every declared band must seat a donor, every seat must hold one carrier, and each band's seats must split evenly across the clones; the bands' mass there is the stage's `funding_matches_bands`, since later layers reweight the seats. The details then carry the clone count, the seating, the mass scale, the booleans and the count of bands. No failure text gives a count. The stage-health binding hands the frame to this check alone, `copy_id_table` joins the gate registry's modules, and the transferred phase's own code hashes it.
- **Clone count.** `geographic_clones.geographic_clone_count` takes the calling stage and refuses a malformed clone index in fixed sentences naming it, with no count. The six support-clip stages, property components, the CGT support split and the fold receipts read the count through it, and the WAS wealth and Lifetime ISA stages bind the leaf for it. The copy-id field the clones' ids are minted in, `GEOGRAPHIC_COPY_ID_FIELD`, is named in the leaf, and `geographic_expand` re-exports it.
- **Clone-aware thresholds.**
  - The six support-clip stages (WAS wealth, WAS Lifetime ISA, NTS bus travel, LCFS consumption, ETB VAT and ETB services) and property components give `clone_count` from two clones up. The support-clip check multiplies each declared row allowance by it, and the property components check its two row maxima.
  - `regional_home_values` counts the source households at the value cap in clone 0's rows, reading each row's clone from its id through the copy-id table, so its check's one maximum holds at any clone count. It refuses a frame whose clone index disagrees with its ids.
  - The take-up signal receipt counts each flag's population support and distinct values in clone 0's rows, and the apprentice draw its supports. A figure measured over every clone is withheld where clone 0's count or the count over every clone is one or two.
- **Constants table.** `test_uk_k_sensitive_constants.py` classifies each count-like constant of the UK spine, and each count-like maximum in the packaged gates, by what it counts and how it meets the clone count, and pins it to the code.
