The two PIP daily-living caseload rows, `dwp.pip.daily_living_standard_claimants` and `dwp.pip.daily_living_enhanced_claimants`, are now England-and-Wales diagnostics beside `obr.pip` (uk-data#490, microcosm#1095). DWP has reported PIP for England and Wales only since executive competence moved to the Scottish Government on 1 April 2020, and Chronicle stamps its January 2025 caseload K04000001. Under the Great Britain default pin the rows matched no fact, so they were dormant.

Scoped to England and Wales, the rows resolve to 1.283m standard-rate and 1.608m enhanced-rate claimants, and a calibration fits both within 0.1%. But fitting them pushes the heaviest support family's weight from 1,061 to 1,241 times the positive median, over the `uk_weight_ratio` limit of 1,151. So they leave the fit. Each is a diagnostic reference on `obr.pip`, pinned to its exact January 2025 fact: checked, and never fitted. Diagnostic references now accept a fact dated by month.

The contract holds 388 targets and 1,229 active references.
