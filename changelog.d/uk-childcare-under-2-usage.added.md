Children under 2 take their own working-parent take-up. `frs_take_up` draws, for each child of whole-year age 0 or 1 in a benefit unit that claims the working-parent hours, whether it uses them, at the new contract rate `extended_childcare_under_2_usage` (keyed on `person_id`, seed 0, salt the output); a child that does not gets `max_free_entitlement_hours_used` 0, so policyengine-uk funds it no hours, and every other person keeps the engine's default of 30. DfE registers far fewer of the youngest eligible children than of those aged 2 to 4 (mostly parental leave), so one family rate fitted at 2 to 4 funded too many of them. `childcare_copy_take_up` re-draws the copies' children against their own unit's re-drawn flag. The take-up signal gate measures the share over the children under 2 in claiming units; the column is allowed on the export surface. The rate is provisional (0.75) until the tool-version-6 childcare refit fits it against DfE's working parent rows at 9 to 11 months and age 1.
