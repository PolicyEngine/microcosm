Children under 2 take their own working-parent take-up, one rate per DfE age (the age on 31 December of the model year, as DfE counts registrations; computed without the engine from the stored age and months since the last birthday, as policyengine-uk places the date of birth). `frs_take_up` draws, for each child of DfE age 0 or 1 in a benefit unit that claims the working-parent hours, whether it uses them, at its age's contract rate (`extended_childcare_age_0_usage`, `extended_childcare_age_1_usage`; keyed on `person_id`, seed 0, salt the output); a child that does not gets `max_free_entitlement_hours_used` 0, so policyengine-uk funds it no hours, and every other person keeps the engine's default of 30. DfE registers far fewer of the youngest eligible children than of those aged 2 to 4, and fewer at 9 to 11 months than at age 1 (mostly parental leave), so one family rate fitted at 2 to 4, or one rate for both ages, funded the wrong number of them. `childcare_copy_take_up` re-draws the copies' children against their own unit's re-drawn flag. The take-up signal gate measures the share over the children of DfE age under 2 in claiming units against their ages' weighted rate; the column is allowed on the export surface. The tool-version-7 childcare refit fits both rates against DfE's working parent rows at 9 to 11 months and age 1, each child counted at its DfE age.
