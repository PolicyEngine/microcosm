Add `us_runtime/one_population_selection.py`, the selection library for d2283's national one-population file. It keeps every CPS source household of the named income years and fills a fixed household budget with ACS households in the support-mix bake-off's nested order (the bake-off's salt, every ACS key ranked, ineligible households dropped, replicate `r` taking the `r`-th disjoint block). It holds the unit keys, the zero-adult and group-quarters eligibility flags, the plan, starting weights (`arm_initial_weights` without clones, scaled so weighted persons equal the Census age-band total) and a manifest with the rule version, counts, threshold draw and digests of the selected keys. It refuses duplicate or unclean keys, a CPS side above the budget and a short eligible set rather than clamping. No build calls it yet.
