A report-only check of single years of age (microcosm#1095; uk-data#600's age check): `tools/report_uk_single_year_ages.py` gives a built file's weighted persons by sex and single year of age (0 to 89, and 90 and over) beside ONS's mid-2025 UK estimates (MYE2, vendored in `ons_mid2025_single_year_age_by_sex.json`), and the same for 80 to 84, 85 to 89, 90 and over and 80 and over. No target binds the estimates. A cell that fewer than 10 source households carry reads "<10", and so does every band that holds such an age.
