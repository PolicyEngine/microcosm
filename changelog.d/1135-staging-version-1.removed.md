The version 1 staging writer (`microcosm.build.staging`, `StagingRunBundleWriter`) is removed with its exports from `microcosm.build` (`DEFAULT_STAGING_PREFIX`, `LATEST_STAGING_POINTER`, `RUNS_INDEX`, `STAGING_SCHEMA_VERSION`), its producer tests, the hand-over's `latest_pointer` and `run_index_entry` arguments and the in-memory emitter's `latest_staging.json` and `runs.json` handling. No build writes `latest_staging.json` or merges into `runs.json` any more. The US build's `--staging-prefix` option and `POPULACE_STAGING_PREFIX` are removed, since version 2 runs are always under `runs/`, as is the build record's `staging_settings.prefix`. Publication still reads a version 1 `staging` block, so a US build certified before this change can be published.
