A new last spine stage, `uc_new_claimant_draw`, stores policyengine-uk's `uc_new_claimant_health_element_draw` (2.179.0, PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2877), the uniform per benefit unit that the engine compares in each rebalancing year with `gov.dwp.universal_credit.rebalancing.new_claimant_share` to pay the lower new-claimant LCWRA rate (microcosm#1095). The claims most likely to be new get the lowest draws. Within the units the engine pays Universal Credit with the LCWRA element at the calibration year, the stage orders claims by recency, the completed months from DWP's claim start (FRS BENEFITS.UCSTART) to the interview, then by the age of the youngest claimant or partner, youngest first, and gives each unit the midpoint of its slice of the population's weight, so the draw is uniform within the population at the stage's weights. Every other unit takes an identity-keyed uniform, so the draw is uniform overall and the engine keeps the parameter's share each year. A unit without a linked claim start (an unlinked reporter, a take-up entrant, a legacy-closure mover or a unit of an SPI-channel household) takes months drawn from the linked claims whose youngest claimant or partner is in its age band (16 to 24, 25 to 34, 35 to 44, 45 to 54, and 55 or over, pooled until they rest on 30 source households). `frs_spine` carries the claim recency as `frs_uc_claim_months`, which the release drops. The draw leaves `UK_ENGINE_DEFAULT_INPUTS` and joins the export surface's allowed columns (`terminal_gates` and `gates.json`), so the data contract's gate digests follow, and the signed entry for `uc_receives_new_claimant_health_element` is restated for María's signature.
