The FRS spine books the interest of the FRS accounts it dropped (microcosm#1418). `frs_spine` routes NS&I Direct Saver, National Savings and pensioners' bonds, guaranteed equity bonds and gilt interest to `other_investment_income`, which policyengine-uk taxes as savings income beside `savings_interest_income`, and takes gilts out of `dividend_income`; fixed-rate and guaranteed bonds join deposit interest, and the tax-free products (savings certificates, SAYE, Premium Bonds, the Yearly Plan, friendly society investments) stay out. On FRS 2024-25 only the NS&I Direct Saver and gilt rows record interest in the new group. FRS rows now carry the column, so the SPI income stage rewrites it on SPI rows instead of adding it, and the ONS D.41 diagnostic counts it.
