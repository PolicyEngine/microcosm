Concept mappings now refuse a `product` binding unless it reads exactly one usual rate, held as a float, and one annual flow counted in weeks, in either order. Before, `Product(liquid_financial_assets, weeks_worked)` validated, although the first is a stock; so did a product of usual weekly hours and a year's income. Concepts now also refuse an `annual_flow` temporal basis with any period but `year`. Every committed binding and concept already meets both rules, so no binding, coverage report, golden or pinned hash changes; policyengine-uk's `hours_worked`, usual weekly hours times weeks worked, still validates.
