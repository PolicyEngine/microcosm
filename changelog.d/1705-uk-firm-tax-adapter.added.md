`uk_runtime/firm_policyengine_uk_rules.evaluate_uk_firm_taxes` reads every policyengine-uk firm tax (VAT, employer NIC after the Employment Allowance, apprenticeship levy, corporation tax, bank surcharge and business rates, plus the Employment Allowance and automatic enrolment employer contribution as informational outputs) for an exported UK firm table, handing policyengine-uk only its firm inputs and keying each value by `firm_id`; `weighted_uk_firm_tax_totals` sums each output by `firm_weight` (microcosm#1705). It drops, with a warning, any other `firm_*` column that is not a known policyengine-uk input. Taxes whose inputs the export does not set yet come out as zero: corporation tax, the bank surcharge and business rates always (no profit, rateable value or bank flag), and employer NIC, the levy and auto-enrolment unless the build is given `--paybill-dir`.
