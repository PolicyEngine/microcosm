A stock table of homes in England and Wales, and the land under them (microcosm#1524, workstream 1 of microcosm#1521). `tools/build_uk_stock_cells.py` pools every home VOA counts into cells of an MSOA × a council tax band. Each cell holds VOA's count at 31 March 2026, the private and social counts, homes seen sold, the owner-occupied share, the mean value and 13 value knots at calendar-2024 prices, and its building cost after depreciation. Land is value less building, never below zero, at one national cost level κ. Authority and constituency figures are sums of cells. MHCLG's land value estimates stay as the comparison. The builder reads public administrative data only (HM Land Registry Price Paid and the UK HPI, the ONS NSPL, VOA's CTSOP 1.1, 3.1 and 4.1, UCL's Price Paid–EPC linked file, Tax Policy Associates' model constants and facts of the Chronicle feed), each checked against its Chronicle registration; addresses never leave its caches.

Five resources under `uk/`: `stock_cells_value.json`, `stock_cells_structure.json`, `la_land_shares.json`, `constituency_land_totals.json` and `stock_cells.provenance.json`, read through `uk_runtime.stock_cells`. The provenance records every input and licence, the level and its status, the variants, the decision rule and the measurements. Price Paid, the UK HPI and the NSPL, whose Chronicle manifests state no licence, carry the licence the home-stock builder states, marked `license_source: declared`. Until ONS's dwellings rows and capital stocks reach the pinned feed (PolicyEngine/chronicle#439), the committed build takes E46Y and the net and gross capital stock of dwellings as stated parameters, read from the workbooks that PR pins; the builder refuses a feed that carries one of them in a shape it does not select. At κ = 0.795 the decision rule passes; the stock-value measurement does not. The method and the results are in `docs/uk-stock-cells-land-1524.md`.

The land side (`stock_cells_structure.json`, the two rollups and the provenance) leaves the solve fingerprint as `solve_scope.UK_POST_SOLVE_RESOURCES`, so a land rebuild re-keys no solve kernel; the value block stays in, since the frame's home values will be read from it.

New country-neutral modules under `microcosm.build`: `pinned_inputs`, `weighted_quantiles`, `cell_pooling`, `residual_land` and `derived_resource`. The UK high-value home stock library delegates its knots, digests and pins to them, with no committed byte changed; its dwelling cache is now named for the NSPL it indexes, and the cache of 13 October 2026 is reused.
