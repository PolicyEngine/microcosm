The US ACS-local release builder gains an optional county calibration rung (`--geographies state,cd,county --soi-mode state_county`): county population and IRS SOI return and AGI rows keyed on 2023 county-equivalents, deterministic county holdouts, a county solver profile (loss cap 10, per-record weight cap 5, both configurable), and provisional county reliability grades from source-household effective sample size, written to `county_reliability.json`. Runs without county flags reproduce the historical release surface (microcosm#1378, for the LWI County Files).
