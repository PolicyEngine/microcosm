Amend New Zealand's benefit-unit rule (G8 method addendum B8, 2026-11-15) so
CREATE builds a household whose reference person is under 18 with no
co-resident parent, or whose reference person is a dependent child while
another child has no co-resident parent, instead of refusing the whole frame.
Such a reference person is no longer a dependent child: they head a unit, and
any other child in the household with no co-resident parent joins it. This
holds at any age under 18, including a parent under 16, and in a household of
minors only, which the rule refused before. Every frame that built under the
old rule builds the same units. By the NZ hub's count on the pinned donor, 9 of
57,240 households tripped the old refusal. The CREATE receipt's
unit-construction counts gain `parentless_reference_person_heads` and
`younger_independent_teen_parents`; with the second, the three teen-parent
counts partition parents under 18. The rule file's hash and NZ's composition
fingerprint change.
