Amend New Zealand's benefit-unit rule (G8 method addendum B8, 2026-11-15) so
CREATE builds two kinds of household it refused before, instead of refusing
the whole frame. A reference person under 18 with no co-resident parent is no
longer a dependent child: they head a unit, and the dependent children who
name them as parent 1, and every other dependent child in the household with
no co-resident parent, join it. This holds at any age under 18, including a
parent under 16, and in a household of minors only. A reference person who is
a dependent child with a co-resident parent stays dependent, and another
dependent child with no co-resident parent now follows the reference person's
caregiver chain into the unit that holds them. A household where a dependent
child's caregivers never reach an adult (anyone who is not a dependent child)
is still refused. Every frame that built under the old rule builds the same
units. By the NZ hub's count on the pinned donor, 9 of 57,240 households
tripped the old refusal. The CREATE receipt's unit-construction counts gain
`parentless_reference_person_heads` and `younger_independent_teen_parents`;
with the second, the three teen-parent counts partition parents under 18. The
rule file's hash and NZ's composition fingerprint change.
