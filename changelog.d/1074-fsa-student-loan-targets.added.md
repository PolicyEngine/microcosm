The US build can calibrate the SCF-imputed federal student loan balances to Federal Student Aid's portfolio tables (microcosm#1074, the student debt pilot). A new runtime module, `fsa_student_loan_targets`, binds the FSA facts Chronicle stores (PolicyEngine/chronicle#310) to the person columns `federal_student_loan_balance` and `federal_parent_plus_loan_balance`: the balance and borrower count of each state and DC, of five borrower age bands and of nine debt-size bands, and the Parent PLUS balance beside the balance of every other loan type. A borrower is a person whose own plus Parent PLUS balance is positive. Repayment plans, the portfolio summary and the loan-type recipient counts stay diagnostic only, each with its reason in the receipt.

Every family binds at one reference date, the latest quarter every bound table publishes (FY2026 Q2, 31 March 2026), as a stock unaged at the 2024 build period: target aging gains a declared `stock_at_source_reference_date` alignment, which only these targets carry. FSA's "Not Reported" row is allocated to every reported location in proportion to its reported value; Puerto Rico and "Other" are outside the host universe and leave with their share. The 51 allocated state levels make one in-scope control, and the age, debt-size and loan-type rows bind as their table's shares of it, so every family sums to the same total.

`compile_us_fiscal_target_registry` binds the facts only with `student_loan_targets`, which the fiscal-refresh release tool sets with `--scf-education-debt`; the release materializes the rows from the stage's stored person columns, accepts their age and balance bands in its restated-filter guard only on that materializer, records every FSA fact's disposition in `us_source_coverage.json`, and keeps the opt-in family off the target-parity contract. Default compiles and releases are unchanged, and the pinned US feed carries no FSA facts until it moves to a Chronicle commit with the packages. The `ED · Federal Student Aid` provider and its four categories join the calibration label registries, so the family shows on the calibration dashboard once a release carries it.

Fit receipts are in `experiments/1074-fsa-calibration-receipts.md`, generated by `experiments/fit_1074_fsa_calibration.py` on Modal through `experiments/modal_1074.py` against a candidate feed (the pinned feed plus chronicle#347's FSA rows, built at its merge commit on Chronicle main).
