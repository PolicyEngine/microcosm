Add a register of the ACS local lane's reviewed default fills and their means-tested consumers, and gate the release on it (microcosm#1022). Being in the staging `reviewed_engine_input_nulls` register only meant a fill was allowed. The new packaged register `microcosm/build/us_runtime/acs_local_reviewed_fill_consumers.yaml` has one entry for each of the 59 columns the lane still fills with a policyengine-us default (release 767312d60 filled 79; the stack now fills 20 of them natively, and the ACS vehicle count is a reviewed fill again since the microcosm#1064 review, because ACS `VEH` counts vehicles available, not owned). Each entry lists every means-tested program the column reaches in the pinned engine's static dependency graph and, for each program, a note: `harmless` with the reason, or `known_bias` with a direction (`overstates`, `understates` or `mixed`). The program roots are the engine's `gov.household.cbo_means_tested_transfers` list, plus the ACA PTC, WIC, Lifeline, Head Start, EITC and CTC, the state aggregate `household_state_benefits`, and the county general assistance and relief programs policyengine-us models. The `acs_local_reviewed_fill_consumers` gate in `microcosm.build.us_runtime.acs_local_reviewed_fill_consumers` reads both engine passes' fill manifests. At finalize (a hard failure bound to the evaluated bytes) and again at package, it fails on a fill without an entry, on an entry whose fill value or spines differ from the manifest, on a declared consumer without a note, and on an entry for a column in `NEVER_DEFAULT_FILLED`. An entry the release did not use is reported, not failed. Packaging ships the register as `reviewed_fill_consumer_register.json` and records its digest and known biases in the build manifest. A new reviewed limitation, `acs_reviewed_default_fill_consumers`, lists the known biases, and the input-coverage note points to the register. A new engine-tier test recomputes each entry's consumers with `PolicyEngineUSVariableMetadataIndex` and fails when one is missing or stale, when a CBO means-tested program has no root, or when the installed engine is not the one the register was reviewed against. Adding an entry for a new reviewed fill is a data-only change to the YAML. No rebuild is needed; finalize and package must be re-run.
