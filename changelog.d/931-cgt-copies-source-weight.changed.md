The CGT support split (`cgt_support_split`) counts each selected household's copies on its weight scaled to its source, `ceil(K × w / 60)`, where `K` is the frame's geographic clone count (#931). A geographic clone carries 1/K of its source household's weight, so a selected clone whose weight is an exact 1/K replica of its source's (which the per-clone strata of the later #931 commits give) gets the count its source gets on a one-clone spine, and each copy weighs at most 60 / K. The stage reads `K` from the frame: one more than the largest `household_clone_index`, or 1 where the column is absent, as on every national spine today. It refuses, by count, indices that are not exactly 0 to K − 1. The selection walk still accumulates each row's own weight, so selection nests only approximately: the household at which a band's walk reaches its support mass, and households tied on wealth, can be selected in some clones and not others, lowest household id first. For K > 1 the quotient is snapped to the nearest integer when it is within K ulps of it, so a weight that is an exact multiple of 60 keeps its count at any K; at K = 7 the rounding of the clone weight would otherwise add a copy for some exact multiples. The snap gives up the converse case: a quotient within K ulps above an integer counts one copy fewer than at K = 1. At K = 1 nothing is snapped, and the count is `ceil(w / 60)` bit for bit, as before.

- **Receipt and gate.** The stage receipt's parameters gain `clone_count`, and nothing else is new. The stage-health gate compares K times the heaviest copy, and K times the heaviest selected household, with the maximum copy weight, so a receipt at K > 1 no longer fails falsely. The identity tool's recompute (`tools/verify_uk_identity_stability.py`) reads K from the pre-split table, as the stage reads it from its input, and its receipt keys are unchanged. Folding a family's copies back re-sums them, which can land ulps from the family's weight: for a weight a few ulps above a multiple of 60 (at K > 1, by more than the snap absorbs), or where an exact-total correction or the #970 anchor's and the residential arms' roundings moved a member. So neither the count check nor the family test recounts the fold. At any K, a family of more than three members is held exactly where at least four of its members show their two clone halves at equal weights and every member that does shares one pair sum c, whatever the others carry: its stored count n must be the count of a weight w with fl(w / n) = c. A member that took the split's own fallback correction keeps equal halves at another weight, since the incidence clone halves it exactly, so its family keeps the bracket. The split's own exact-total correction, the incidence clone's and the residential split's each move one row, so one of those four or more is uncorrected, and equal halves mean the anchor and the arms left its pair sum at exactly w / n, so c is exactly w / n. The stage counts n on each clone's own weight and gives every member fl(w / n), and the snapped count still rises with w, so this holds at K > 1 as at K = 1. Where every member shares c but fewer than four show equal halves, the hold reaches one float either side of c, the most the anchor's and the arms' roundings move an uncorrected member where the floats either side of c are as wide, and two floats where c sits within two floats of a power of two, below which they are half as wide; c lies in (45 / K, 60 / K] to within the snap, which can leave it a few floats above 60 / K (at most 16 for K up to 15, by design arithmetic over weights at and up to 40 floats above each multiple m × 60 / K, m from 4 to 3,999), and that range holds 16 at K = 3, 8 at K = 6 and 7, and 4 at K = 12 to 15. Every other family keeps the bracket: the stored count must lie between the counts at the folded weight times 1 − 1e-9 and 1 + 1e-9. The family test, which decides whether a household is a family at all, is held the same way at the K-aware count: a household the rule selects is possibly a family where its count at the folded weight times 1 + 1e-9 exceeds one and possibly whole where its count at the folded weight times 1 − 1e-9 is one, its stored state accepted in between, and the hold's counts decide where it applies. That bracket opens for a household whose source-scaled weight K × w lies within a relative 1e-9 of a multiple of 60 (at K = 1, its own weight), where it accepts n or n + 1 copies, a whole household or a family of two among them. A stored count moved there on its own is still reported, by the flag and family-weight checks, but a household the stage split consistently into one copy more there, every member at w / (n + 1), is not. Where the hold applies, that blind spot narrows from 4.5 to 9 million floats of w on each side of a multiple to a few floats next to the count's step, for some counts n. At K = 1 the step sits a float above the multiple, which leaves at most the multiple itself where the family is held exactly, or the multiple and the float either side of it where the hold reaches one float. At K from 2 to 15 the snap puts the step 3 to 17 floats above the multiple of 60 / K, which leaves at most the float on either side of the step where the family is held exactly, or up to three floats from it in the one-float tier, so a family on the multiple itself split into one copy more is reported. The receipt's `copies_recomputed` stays the exact recount from the re-summed fold, and its tolerance policy says all this.
- **Declarations.** The `copy_rule` text in the code dictionary, `sources.yaml` and `source_stages.json` now reads "copies = ceil(geographic clone count x household weight / maximum_copy_weight), the quotient snapped to the nearest integer within clone-count ulps when the clone count exceeds one; every copy carries weight / copies, except where none of the 64 heaviest households left whole can carry the exact-total correction, when the shared fallback can put it on a root or copy, off weight / copies by about one ulp of the total weight; of those 64, a selected household left at one copy can carry the correction only where its corrected weight keeps its one copy and the walk's selection, a check the fallback does not make". - **Exact total.** The exact-total correction lands on a household the split left whole: a positive-weight household it did not divide, unselected or selected at one copy, whose weight the split left as it was. The stage tries the 64 heaviest of them (`CGT_SUPPORT_CORRECTION_CANDIDATES`), heaviest first with tied weights in row order, and the first that can carry the correction takes it, so every root and copy weighs bitwise w / n. A selected household left at one copy can carry it only where its corrected weight keeps its one copy and the walk's selection, the one-copy check `copy_rule` names, kept as a reading of the whole-households-first rule, so wherever one of the 64 carries it, the rule rerun on the stage's output, as the identity tool reruns it, divides the same households; on the synthetic probe frames, 11 corrections would otherwise have lifted a one-copy household to a weight that counts two copies, and the rule rerun on the stage's output would have counted a family the stage never made. Before, the stage tried only the heaviest unselected household.
