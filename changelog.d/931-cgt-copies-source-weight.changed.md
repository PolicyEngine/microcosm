The CGT support split (`cgt_support_split`) counts each selected household's copies on its weight scaled to its source, `ceil(K × w / 60)`, where `K` is the frame's geographic clone count (#931). A geographic clone carries 1/K of its source household's weight, so a selected clone whose weight is an exact 1/K replica of its source's (which the per-clone strata of the later #931 commits give) gets the count its source gets on a one-clone spine, and each copy weighs at most 60 / K. The stage reads `K` from the frame: one more than the largest `household_clone_index`, or 1 where the column is absent, as on every national spine today. It refuses, by count, indices that are not exactly 0 to K − 1. The selection walk still accumulates each row's own weight, so selection nests only approximately: the household at which a band's walk reaches its support mass, and households tied on wealth, can be selected in some clones and not others, lowest household id first. For K > 1 the quotient is snapped to the nearest integer when it is within K ulps of it, so a weight that is an exact multiple of 60 keeps its count at any K; at K = 7 the rounding of the clone weight would otherwise add a copy for some exact multiples. The snap gives up the converse case: a quotient within K ulps above an integer counts one copy fewer than at K = 1. At K = 1 nothing is snapped, and the count is `ceil(w / 60)` bit for bit, as before.

- **Receipt and gate.** The stage receipt's parameters gain `clone_count`, and nothing else is new. The stage-health gate compares K times the heaviest copy, and K times the heaviest selected household, with the maximum copy weight, so a receipt at K > 1 no longer fails falsely. At K = 1 both checks and their messages are unchanged. A receipt without a positive integer `clone_count` is refused. The identity tool's recompute (`tools/verify_uk_identity_stability.py`) reads K from the pre-split table, as the stage reads it from its input, and its receipt keys are unchanged. Folding a family's copies back re-sums them, which can land ulps from the family's weight: at K = 1 for a weight a few ulps above a multiple of 60, or where an exact-total correction fell on a member, and at K > 1 by more than the snap absorbs. So the count check does not recount the fold. At K = 1 a family of more than three members whose clone pair sums are all one weight c is held exactly: its stored count n must be the count of a weight w with fl(w / n) = c. The split's own exact-total correction, the incidence clone's and the residential split's each move one row, so such a family has a member no correction moved, whose pair sum only the later stages' roundings can have moved off w / n. Where at least four members show their two clone halves at equal weights, one of those is uncorrected, and equal halves mean the #970 anchor and the residential arms left its pair sum at exactly w / n, so c is exactly w / n. Elsewhere the hold reaches one float either side of c, the most the anchor's and the arms' roundings move an uncorrected member. Every other family, and every family at K > 1, keeps the bracket: the stored count must lie between the counts at the folded weight times 1 − 1e-9 and 1 + 1e-9. That bracket opens for a family whose source-scaled weight K × w lies within a relative 1e-9 of a multiple of 60 (at K = 1, its own weight), where it accepts n or n + 1 copies. A stored count moved there on its own is still reported, by the flag and family-weight checks, but a family the stage split consistently into one copy more there, every member at w / (n + 1), is not. Where the hold applies, that blind spot narrows from 4.5 to 9 million floats of w on each side of a multiple to at most the multiple itself, or the multiple and the float either side of it where the hold reaches one float. - **Declarations.** The `copy_rule` text in the code dictionary, `sources.yaml` and `source_stages.json` now reads "copies = ceil(geographic clone count x household weight / maximum_copy_weight), the quotient snapped to the nearest integer within clone-count ulps when the clone count exceeds one; every copy carries weight / copies, except where none of the 64 heaviest households left whole can carry the exact-total correction, when the shared fallback can put it on a root or copy, off weight / copies by about one ulp of the total weight; of those 64, a selected household left at one copy can carry the correction only where its corrected weight keeps its one copy and the walk's selection, a check the fallback does not make". - **Exact total.** The exact-total correction lands on a household the split left whole: a positive-weight household it did not divide, unselected or selected at one copy, whose weight the split left as it was. The stage tries the 64 heaviest of them (`CGT_SUPPORT_CORRECTION_CANDIDATES`), heaviest first with tied weights in row order, and the first that can carry the correction takes it, so every root and copy weighs bitwise w / n. A selected household left at one copy can carry it only where its corrected weight keeps its one copy and the walk's selection, the one-copy check `copy_rule` names, kept as a reading of the whole-households-first rule, so wherever one of the 64 carries it, the rule rerun on the stage's output, as the identity tool reruns it, divides the same households; on the synthetic probe frames, 11 corrections would otherwise have lifted a one-copy household to a weight that counts two copies, and the identity tool's recompute would have found a family the stage never made. Only where none of them can does spi_support's shared fallback place it, unchanged, and that row can be a root or a copy, then off w / n by about one ulp of the frame's total weight: thousands of ulps of its own weight, a relative error below 1e-12 on the synthetic frames. The fallback does not consult that check, so its row can also be a whole household, among them a one-copy household the check passed over; the rerun can then divide a different set. The cap bounds the cost where no whole household can carry the correction: on a synthetic vector of 15 × 78,188 rows, the size of a K = 15 spine, 64 such households and the fallback take about 0.4 s, against 0.02 s for the one candidate before.
