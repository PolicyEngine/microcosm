The UK SPI Table 3.6 employment, self-employment and private-pension amounts by total-income band are projected from 2023-24 to the calibration year by HMRC's own band-specific growth (Income Tax Liabilities Table 2.5 total income by band, the calendar-year window the count, total-income and tax rows already use) instead of a flat national index (microcosm#1063). An amount held in a fixed nominal band moves with the band's membership as earners cross its edges; the flat average-earnings index raised the GBP 15,000-20,000 band's employment amount 10.7% while HMRC projects the band's total income down 2.3%, which left the frame 20-31% short of the three lowest employment bands at design weights and pulled the calibration into over-weighting low-paid employees. Under the band growth the three lowest employment targets move from GBP 18.7bn, 53.8bn and 190.4bn to 17.7bn, 47.5bn and 172.5bn. Dividend and savings-interest amounts keep their own indices, since those amounts move with rates rather than band membership.
