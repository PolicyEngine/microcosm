Bind SOI's income tax after credits rows (A06500 amounts and N06500 returns, national and by state) to Form 1040 line 22, read as `income_tax_before_credits` less `income_tax_capped_non_refundable_credits`, instead of `income_tax`. `income_tax` nets every refundable credit and adds the net investment income tax and three other Schedule 2 Part II taxes, so the rows compared a different quantity with SOI's. A new `SOI_DIFFERENCE_CONCEPTS` register names the two engine variables; the fiscal-refresh builder materializes the positive part of their difference per tax unit, and the compatibility recertification scorer applies the same difference through a `difference` combine mode, which refuses an unknown mode or the wrong number of inputs. No SOI row binds `income_tax` any more. On the published Microcosm US release at its final weights, the national amount row moves from +0.33% to -1.90% of SOI's $2,358.7B and the national returns row from -0.26% to +2.17% of 113.6M; `experiments/1539-soi-income-tax-after-credits-receipts.md` has every state.
