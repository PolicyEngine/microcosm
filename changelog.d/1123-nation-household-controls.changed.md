Each nation's official household estimate for 2025 controls its census household cells (LFS for England's regions, NRS for Scotland, Welsh Government rolled forward for Wales, Census 2021 rolled by LPS dwelling stock for Northern Ireland); the UK household composition takes the nations' sum, A15 uprates census cells to it with the LFS UK total kept as a diagnostic, and census tenure cells partition their authority's households (microcosm#1123).
