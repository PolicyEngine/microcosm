Two national rows from HMRC's Property Rental Income Statistics 2026 (tax year 2024-25) join the UK target contract as measured diagnostics for the Scottish Budget 2027-28 property rate costing: Scottish landlords' rental receipts before expenses (Table 9, £2.82bn) and their allowable expenses (Table 11, £1.29bn), each carried to 2025 by policyengine-uk's per-capita GDP index. The receipts are measured on `property_rental_income`. PRIS counts residential finance costs among the expenses (Table 8: £12.82bn of the UK's £34.75bn), while `property_income` is the profit before them, so the expenses row measures receipts less profit plus `property_finance_costs`. Both regional tables count partnerships as well as individuals, and the spine carries individuals. Two signed exclusions hold the rows out of the solve, since PRIS stays a diagnostic (María's ruling of 8 October 2026), and the evaluation measures them on every candidate. The landlord counts stay out: they conflict with the SPI's.
