`frs_take_up`'s Child Benefit, Pension Credit and Universal Credit draws and `frs_person_draws`' apprentice walk now count within each geographic clone, so clone 0 of a clone-first spine takes a one-clone spine's flags (#931; María's rulings of 6 and 10 Nov). Before, the anchored residual took `int(rate × n)` once over every clone's benefit units, and the walk interleaved every clone's employees and stopped once, so clone 0's flags were not one clone's. The stages' other draws were already keyed on each row's own id.

- **Shared `strata`** (for Max's review). `stochastic_assignment.assign_binary_with_anchored_residual` takes a keyword-only `strata`, one integer per row. Each stratum's rows take the flags they take alone: their own `int(rate × n)` target, the need their own anchors leave and the threshold their own non-anchored rows fill. Without `strata` the function runs as before; with every row in one stratum it gives the same flags bit for bit, and without an anchor strata change nothing. Strata that do not align with the draws, and strata without an integer dtype, every column with a missing label among them, are refused in fixed sentences that name no count, label or row. The module names no country, and only UK modules and UK tools import it.
- **frs_take_up.** `derive_frs_take_up` takes `clone_index` and passes it as `strata` to the three anchored draws. The stage reads each benefit unit's clone from `household_clone_index` through the person memberships with the new `geographic_clone_indices`, which shares its mapping with `geographic_clone_zero_rows`, so its consumes do not change. A frame without the column passes None.
- **frs_person_draws.** `derive_is_apprentice` takes `person_clone_index`, as do `derive_frs_person_draws` and `derive_frs_person_draws_with_receipt`, and each clone walks its own employees to the rate times its own weight aged 21 to 24, with the unchanged nearest-target walk. The stage reads the index through `geographic_clone_indices`. The receipt keeps its keys and their formulas: the masses and the realized weight are sums over every clone, `target` is the rate times the weight aged 21 to 24 over every clone, and `cap_bound` compares the eligible weight over every clone with that target, as the `apprentice_draw` stage-health check recomputes them. The supports are still counted among clone 0's people in true source households, and withheld below the floor of three as before. A misaligned or non-integer index, or a `person_clone_zero` that disagrees with it, is refused in a fixed sentence that names no count or row.
- **E4** (`tools/verify_uk_identity_stability.py`) gives both recomputes each row's clone, read once from the scoped frame and carried by id onto the permuted tables, and holds stored `is_apprentice` flags to the walk within each clone. Its `tolerance_policy` text says so.
- **What moves.** At one clone, no value: both stages' outputs and receipts are as before, bit for bit, with or without a one-clone index, and E4's receipt differs only in its `tolerance_policy` text. On the synthetic H2 fixture the legacy oracle's final frame, its stage records and every stage's evidence digest are identical before and after, and the fixture regenerates byte-identically. Above one clone, which no build has yet run on this code, the three anchored flags and `is_apprentice` move, each clone meeting its own target. Clone 0's anchored flags are one clone's exactly. Its `is_apprentice` flags are one clone's at any power-of-two clone count, and elsewhere except at a near-tie of the walk. Whatever reads these flags follows: `frs_brma` reads `would_claim_uc` through `LHA_category`. (Later in #931 `area_brma`, in its place, does.) `spi_benefit_coherence`'s Universal Credit redraw and `uc_take_up` still count over every clone.
- **Keys.** The frs_spine root and 48 of the 55 stages after it hash `stochastic_assignment`, so every spine node re-keys, and both spine gate nodes with them. Of the gate phases' own codes, the transferred spine phase's hashes `stochastic_assignment` and moves; the assembled phase's does not. No node hashes the identity tool. The 45 kernels the transport registry registers, from which NZ's graph is composed, keep their hashes, and no NZ, BE, AM or US module imports an edited file. No declaration, generated file, `gates.json` or schema changes: `frs_take_up`'s notes, `frs_person_draws`' `rate_derivation` and the take-up contract's policy line follow with the geography declarations.
