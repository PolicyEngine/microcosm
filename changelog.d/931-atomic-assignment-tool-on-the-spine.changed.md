`tools/measure_uk_atomic_assignment.py` measures the clone-first spine's own area draw (#931): G2, the draw of the spine's `atomic_area_assign` stage (`spine_geography`), on a bound spine checkpoint, in process, with no engine and no graph. Since the dense line stopped drawing areas earlier in #931, the tool refused; it now measures in process, and its graph route and the readers of the retired dense nodes' manifests go. It records the assignment-only receipt the arms read and the Northern Ireland district check of decision 9. No build runs it, and its outputs stay with the run.

- **What it reads.** `--input-h5` is a spine checkpoint, hashed (and held to `--input-sha256` where one is given), loaded and authenticated by `calibration_run.load_bound_spine_checkpoint` against its build sidecar and spine gate report (`--input-sidecar` and `--input-spine-gates`, the H5's `.build.json` and `.spine_gates.json` by default), as the dense and national lines bind a spine. The tool keeps its base rows: the households on no copy branch (geographic clone 0), read through the shared copy-path reader on the UK copy lineage, with each one's true source, region, IMD decile, council-tax band and band flag. A household's weight is its mass in its areas: the spine's household weights as the checkpoint holds them (importance weights, once the SPI support channel has moved part of each stratum's mass onto its copies), summed over every row that keeps its areas, its geographic clones and the copies later stages make of them; SPI-channel rows (`atomic_area_conditioning.uk_support_channel_copies`), which draw areas of their own, are left out. It refuses, naming columns only, a table without those inputs, the source columns or the clone index, a source with two base rows, geographic clones that disagree on an input and more than one source year. `--supports-dir` holds the three conditioned supports, `<source>.npz` each as `tools/build_uk_conditioned_area_supports.py` writes them; each must be the conditioned register's bytes and the support it extends the register's (`country_adapter.uk_atomic_support_register`), unless `--no-pin-supports` (synthetic supports only). Supports that place a constituency or local authority in two regions are refused.
- **How it draws.** For each clone count in `--n-clones` (default `1,15`), the base rows go through the spine's geographic expansion (`expand_uk_geographic_clones`, G1) and geography identity (`key_uk_households_by_clone_path`, G1b) on a frame of the base households alone, so every clone draws on the key the spine gives it; each clone carries 1/K of its household's weight. Two laws (`--laws`, both by default) replace `legacy` and `keyed`:
  - `region_only`, today's v1 definition (`atomic_area_support.uk_atomic_assignment_definition`, by census households within the FRS region) at `--region-only-seed` (the dense role's seed, 42, by default), over the registered supports the conditioned ones extend, drawn, derived and validated by the shared operator on the key and the region, as the dense line drew it;
  - `conditioned`, G2's own draw (`spine_geography.draw_uk_spine_areas` with `uk_conditioned_assignment_definition` at the spine's seed, 0), its fallback levels included.
  Each resolves the engine's local authority as the spine's local-authority stage does (`uk_local_authorities`).
- **What it records**, per cell {law} x {K}, at the spine's weights with no solve:
  - expected rows per constituency and local authority, and their variance, read off the definition the law's draw runs (`expected_area_support`): each row draws an area among its support's rows that match every constraint input it carries, its region and, where its level keeps one, its decile, in proportion to the weight column its stage gives it, its band's where its level keeps the band and the declared default otherwise, each on its own key. Under the region-only law this is the old single-stage expectation, now on the supports' census households: the region's rows times the area's census household share within it;
  - realized rows, Kish ESS and distinct source households on the supports' roster of areas, the 50-floor breach tables, z-scores against each law's own spread (the sum of p(1 - p)), the ladder gate's verdict and London's weighted household share;
  - Northern Ireland's local government districts: each one's census household share beside its expected rows, rows, sources, z-score and weighted share, with the largest published |z| and share gap;
  - identity stability: the same draw on reversed rows and on the clone-0 subset, unique keys and, where the checkpoint carries G2's columns, whether the spine drew the same areas; whether every clone's area at each lower K stays at the highest; and, for the conditioned law, the source households at each fallback level, rounded;
  - the spine's file, digest, K, rounded source households and sampling rung (fraction, seed and rung token, never its household counts), the supports' and definitions' digests and the definitions' streams.
- **Disclosure.** The evidence and the receipt carry no unit record, say `"publishable": false`, and the tool refuses an output path inside a checkout (`receipt_counts.outside_work_trees`) before it reads any input. Neither is published or quoted: an area's rows, and the region-only law's expected rows, sum to each region's exact base households times K, so the rounded source counts protect only the receipt and the terminal. The evidence declares `disclosure_rule: 2`:
  - an area's rows, ESS and sources read "<n", and its z-score is blanked, where its rows, or the source households of positive weight they rest on, are fewer than `--minimum-count` (now at least 3, CD171's floor); when a cell is written, a region resting on fewer source households than the minimum has every figure of its areas withheld, its expected rows included;
  - one more area of its level and region is withheld beside them, or every area there when none has more rows than the minimum, or where the region's total (its rows, which the cell records) would still leave an unpublished count, or the suppressed counts' sum, one value; the same audit then reads the counts the law publishes for the same areas at its other clone counts as bounds, since a clone keeps its area as K grows, and where a whole group is still pinned it is withheld in the law's other cells too;
  - in a region where a constituency-by-local-authority piece rests on fewer rows or source households than the minimum, every constituency count is withheld, so the two levels cannot be differenced;
  - the conditioned law's per-area and per-district expected rows and z-scores, which are linear in the base households of its draw cells, feed its summaries only;
  - the level summaries, the breach tables (`size_evaluation.area_support_tables` over the published areas) and Northern Ireland's districts cover published areas only, each district reading its local authority's figures as withheld there.
  `resuppress_evidence` re-applies the rule to a written file, and refuses rule-2 evidence below the floor. It does not treat the difference between an area's counts at two clone counts, the clones between them that land there, as a cell of its own. Evidence without the key, written before this change, keeps the rule it was written under, so it stays a fixed point. The receipt repeats the re-suppressed evidence's summaries, with its level minima from 3 to 9 banded; where London's share is withheld, the ladder gate's verdict leaves out its London check. The source-household counts of the spine, the cells, the fallback levels and Northern Ireland read "<n" below the minimum and are rounded through `receipt_counts` from it up, and the refusals of the spine's loaders, expansion and identity, which can count rows, are reduced to the check that refused.
- **Command line.** `--ladder`, `--ladder-sha256`, `--provenance-json`, `--ledger-facts`, `--sample-fraction`, `--sample-seed`, `--seed`, `--expected-constituency-vintage`, `--graph-store`, `--out` and `--calibration-year` go; `--region-only-seed` is new, and every option has a help text.
- **What moves.** The tool only. No kernel or gate phase code hashes a file under `tools/` (an engine-free probe over the 84 kernels of the UK registries), and no node key moves on the declared spine, the dense graph on it and on a bound-spine stand-in, or the national graph; NZ transport's 31 kernels keep their hashes. No declaration, generated file, `gates.json` or schema changes.
