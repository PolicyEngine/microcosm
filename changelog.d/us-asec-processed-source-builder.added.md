Add Microcosm's own builder for the processed CPS ASEC inputs, so a new ASEC year no longer depends on the archived US data package. `us_runtime.asec_processed_source` and `tools/build_us_asec_processed_source.py` read a Census public-use archive verified against its pin and write `census_cps_YYYY.h5` with the archived loader's five tables. Rebuilds of income years 2024 and 2025 equal the pinned files table for table, and tax units match the archived construction on 20,000 synthetic households. The builder refuses the three record structures (a person in two married pairs, a self parent pointer, a repeated line number) that make the shared construction drop a person or crash.
