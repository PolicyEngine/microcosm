The UK lock moves from policyengine-uk 2.162.14 to 2.164.2 (microcosm#1415). 2.164.0 (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2712) adds the ISA annual subscription limit, a cash ISA limit that current law leaves unset, per-person cash and stocks-and-shares ISA subscription and balance inputs, and the 20% Lifetime ISA withdrawal charge on a first home above the £450,000 cap; its household `cash_isa` and `stocks_and_shares_isa` sum the person balances. The release therefore stops storing those household totals, and the engine reads the `isa_subscriptions` stage's person columns. 2.164.1 moves the economic assumptions to the OBR's October 2026 Economic and fiscal outlook, and the vendored engine pins move with it: average earnings at 2025 fall 1.1% and household interest income 6.1%, while GDP per head grows by the same 8.8% from 2023 to 2025. On the national surface the eight SPI Table 3.10 self-employment bands, which mixed income per head takes to 2025, compile 0.55% lower, and the dividend bands move by under 0.001% (the compile-parity receipt is re-signed); the savings interest bands grow by the OBR's October savings income growth since microcosm#1420, so the engine pins no longer move them. The annual-income earnings index, the weight-year population path and the bonus-sacrifice anchor's earnings and population growth (factor 1.1149 to 1.1123) move too, and the CGT projection gate's pinned growth path is re-pinned to the October path (its horizon stays 2030, though the path now runs to 2031). 2.162.15 to 2.164.2 also change Housing Benefit, Income Support and Pension Credit rules, project the Scottish Carer Supplement and the Carer's Allowance earnings limit, and run the property allowance election on only the households with a choice. Concept coverage drops the two household ISA totals as inputs and gains the four person ISA columns and Housing Benefit's pre-assessed permitted-work disregard, which the build leaves at its default.
