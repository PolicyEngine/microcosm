`orr.rail_government_support` is held out of the national objective as a measured diagnostic, from 2026-10-20 to 2026-12-11 (María's ruling of 2026-10-20, microcosm#1278). The row binds ORR table 7270's broad total of government support to the railway. The frame's household rail subsidy sits far below that total at design weights, so a bound row closes the gap by moving weight onto rail-using households, and with it untargeted columns that correlate with rail use. The ruling holds the row out only if a national arm with it held out, run beside the calibration-period fix's arm, passes the gates and keeps the share of rows within 10 percent. Whether the household subsidy is measured against table 7270 or table 7271's narrower by-source series is still an open ruling (microcosm#890 G). The register has 58 measure exclusions (57).
