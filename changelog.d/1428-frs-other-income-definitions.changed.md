FRS rows now book NS&I income on SPI's definitions (#1428). NS&I Direct Saver and the other NS&I and bank products go to `savings_interest_income`, because SPI has counted all interest paid gross or net as bank interest since 2016-17 (Table 3.7 note 4); gilts stay in `other_investment_income`. Income from an annuity not bought with pension funds or from a trust or covenant (FRS PENTYPE 4 and 5) stays in `private_pension_income`, where policyengine-uk's means tests (Pension Credit, Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction, Income Support), household income and tax read it. Its part is stored in a new calibration-only descriptor, `annuity_and_trust_income`, and SPI Table 3.7's other-income rows read `other_investment_income_spi_concept`: the engine's other income plus that share of the engine's private pension, as the table counts both payments as other income (note 6). A row the SPI stage draws holds the tape's pension, which excludes them, so its descriptor is zero. The FRS spine now refuses a pension tab without PENTYPE.
