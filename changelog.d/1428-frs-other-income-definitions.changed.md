FRS rows now book savings income on SPI's definitions (#1428). NS&I Direct Saver and the other NS&I and bank products go to `savings_interest_income`, because SPI has counted all interest paid gross or net as bank interest since 2016-17 (Table 3.7 note 4); gilts stay in `other_investment_income`. Income from an annuity not bought with pension funds or from a trust or covenant (FRS PENTYPE 4 and 5) moves from `private_pension_income` to `other_investment_income`, the "other income" Table 3.7 counts (note 6). The FRS records the whole annuity payment, of which only the income element is taxable.
