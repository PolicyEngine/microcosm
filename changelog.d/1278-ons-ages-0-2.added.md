The national UK contract binds ONS single years of age 0, 1 and 2 for all persons (microcosm#1278, #1095), on the 15-19 split's precedent. The 0-4 bands by sex bind, but the single years inside them were free to move. Each row reads the ONS mid-year estimate for the UK at one single year of age, the series the 0-4 bands sum, and counts people with `age >= n` and `age < n + 1`. Unlike the 15-19 split, the 0-4 sex bands keep their incumbent mapping and stay bound: the new rows cover both sexes and stop at age 2, so neither band is their sum. The contract has 421 targets (418 before). The registry scope has 362 (359): three more contract-only declarations (176) beside the same 186 mapped targets. The national references and membership give 1,322 active references (1,319), and the 2025 compile-parity receipt signs the three rows as `ledger_only`, with their rationale.
