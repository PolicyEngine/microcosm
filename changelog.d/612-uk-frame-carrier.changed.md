UK national build stages now run `Frame -> Frame` on the populace kernel carrier: `UKNationalDataset`, `load_uk_national_dataset`, `write_uk_national_dataset`, and `validate_uk_national_dataset` are retired in favour of `uk_national_frame`, `load_uk_national_frame` (returning the frame plus a `UKStagingProvenance` record carried beside it), `write_uk_national_frame`, and `validate_uk_national_frame`; the SPI stage's certified-candidate fence binds the load provenance through `bind_staging_provenance`; the staging H5 payload (keys, column order, dtypes, values, root attrs) is unchanged, and the rowwise clone keeps its own carrier — its artifact is not a national frame (`clone_index` on every entity table violates Frame's column-uniqueness rule).
