The UK `was_wealth` stage holds its wealth columns coherent with tenure and with each other (microcosm#1063). Tenure enters every chain segment as one four-way category built the same way on both sides: the donor's from `Ten1R8` and `DVPriRntR8`, the recipient's from the frame's `tenure_type`, with council and housing-association renters together as social renters. The engine's `is_renting`, which omits `RENT_FROM_HA`, is no longer a predictor, so housing-association renters stop matching owner donors. `main_residence_value` is fitted and drawn on the owner tenures only and `mortgage_debt` on the mortgaged tenure only, each zero by rule elsewhere; the rule leaves out the mortgages on other property that donors off a mortgaged tenure hold (about a tenth of the donor's mortgage mass), and the receipt records that mass. Totals are derived, not drawn: `property_wealth` is the sum of land, the main residence, other houses, buildings and a drawn remainder; `gross_financial_wealth` is savings, the cash ISA, the share-like holdings and a drawn remainder; `net_financial_wealth` is gross less consumer debt and the student loan balance. The pinned round-8 tab satisfies each identity on every row, and the cleaning step refuses a donor that does not. The chain runs seven segments with their own child seeds (the first four roots are unchanged). A new release-blocking stage-health gate, `uk_stage_was_wealth_coherence`, requires zero rows with mortgage debt off a mortgaged tenure, zero with a main-residence value off an owner tenure, and zero identity violations, and bounds the share of owners without a main-residence value by the donor's own; `uk_stage_was_wealth_support` now also gates `private_pension_wealth`. The four-way tenure grouping lives in `uk_runtime.tenure_constants`, which the local targets also read.
