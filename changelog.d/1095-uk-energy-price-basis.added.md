Every UK H5 that stores domestic energy spending now declares its price period in the root `energy_price_basis` attribute, `{"period_type": "calendar_year", "period_value": 2024}`: the calendar-2024 prices `lcfs_consumption` sets every household spend column at, which the stage-time gate `uk_stage_lcfs_consumption_basis` holds (microcosm#1113). From policyengine-uk 2.148.1 (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2571) the engine projects declared electricity, gas and domestic energy spending by its consumer price index and keeps undeclared spending at its stored prices in every later year, so a later year costed on a Microcosm release no longer prices energy at 2024 prices (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2526, its remaining item 2; microcosm#1095). On a file that declares it, each household's electricity, gas and domestic energy spending moves to a later year by the engine's energy price index from 1 January 2024, every household alike, where an undeclared file keeps its stored spending; releases built before this change stay undeclared. An engine-tier test loads a written file through `Microsimulation` and checks 2025 and 2026 against the index, and a copy without the declaration against the stored spending (Vahid's review of microcosm#1383, item 3). The calibration's 2025 measurement of the energy rows moves with it.
