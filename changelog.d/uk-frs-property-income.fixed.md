The UK FRS spine's `property_income` no longer counts CVPAY, the rent a boarder or lodger pays the householder, as the payer's own income. It counts a loss on other property (ROYYR1 with RENTPROF 2, which the FRS stores as a positive amount) as zero rather than income, counts sub-letting rent (SUBRENT, asked of every household) for every tenure rather than only owner-occupiers, and floors SUBRENT and ROYYR1 at zero separately, so an FRS missing-value code in one cannot cancel the other. The rules match policyengine-uk-data#503 and #509, and the two give the same property income on every FRS 2024-25 person record.
