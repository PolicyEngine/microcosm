The UK FRS spine's `property_income` no longer counts CVPAY, the rent a boarder or lodger pays the householder, as the payer's own income. It counts a loss on other property (ROYYR1 with RENTPROF 2, which the FRS stores as a positive amount) as zero rather than income, counts sub-letting rent (SUBRENT, asked of every household) for every tenure rather than only owner-occupiers, and floors SUBRENT and ROYYR1 at zero separately, so an FRS missing-value code in one cannot cancel the other. The rules match uk-data#503 and uk-data#509, and the two give the same property income on every FRS 2024-25 person record. The household `subrent` column is floored at zero too, so missing-value codes no longer reach later stages as negative amounts. Known gaps, documented in `frs_property_income`: the householder's lodger receipts are not credited, sub-letting and lodger income is taxed without Rent a Room relief, and ROYYR1 nets mortgage capital and interest, so it sits below the SPI's net income from property for mortgaged landlords (microcosm#1106).
