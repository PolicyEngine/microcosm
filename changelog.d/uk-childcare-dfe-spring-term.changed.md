The DfE funded-childcare rows are measured at the spring term, as DfE's January census counts them: each row counts the children funded in the term that starts on 1 January of the model year's April-to-March span, by their age on 31 December, through four person measures (`uk_runtime/dfe_spring_term.py`) built on policyengine-uk 2.147.2's date-of-birth school start and working-parent term start. No row reads `current_education`. The universal and early-learning measures count every child of the DfE age, as the term after the birthday does, where the engine still gates them on the age on 6 October; DfE's rule of one entitlement per child holds at age 2. The working parent registrations at 9 to 11 months (January 2025) are bound with their own approved uprating hold, beside ages 1, 2 and 3, universal nursery age 3, and the working parent 2 to 4, early learning and universal-only rows, which also move to the spring term; the age-4 cells stay signed out, as the bound rows fix them. The ONS single-year note names the mid-2025 estimates' release date (1 October 2026).
