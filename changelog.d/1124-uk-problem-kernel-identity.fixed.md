The UK local solve binds the row-metadata target-weight rules end to end (microcosm#1124): `prepare_uk_full_solve` computes a `grain_family_equal` or `nation_grain_family_equal` (or `*_sqrt_count`) vector from the row carrier of its own targets and keeps the carrier on the prepared solve, the rotated holdout weights its held rows through the same carrier, and the graph problem binds a `target_loss_weight_receipt` (shares by grain, family and nation, and the vector's digest) that the build manifest copies into `solve`. The dense posture, the CLI choices, the holdout diagnostics schema and the candidate scorer admit the new names; the national role and release candidates still refuse them. The `uk.full.problem` kernel's implementation hash now also covers `local_rowwise`, `national_doctrine` and `target_weights`, where its loss weights are built, so an edit to those modules re-keys the problem instead of replaying a stale vector; the rotated-holdout kernel's hash covers the doctrine and rule modules for the same reason.
