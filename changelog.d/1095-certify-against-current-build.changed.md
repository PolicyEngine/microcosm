UK cuts check and certify their candidate against the current build of their own line, as the Hub's `releases.json` names it, instead of the enhanced FRS (microcosm#1095, now that the migration in microcosm#665 is complete): national against national, dense against dense. A cut resolves its reference once, refusing a build that is withdrawn or not certified, and keeps its record, the dataset and the evidence the gates read from it with the run (`tools/run_uk_cut.py`'s new `reference` step, or `tools/resolve_uk_reference.py` outside it). The comparison of the candidate's target fit with the reference's is a report with no verdict (`score_vs_reference.json`), built by one country-neutral head-to-head module that the US comparison tool now shares. The input-mass gate compares each column's total with the reference's at the reference's own solved weights, within the largest relative drift measured between the last two national builds, and no column may lose more than 90% of the reference's total (the largest drop between those builds, rounded up for headroom), since a symmetric tolerance above one fails no drop short of zero; it withholds the figures of the columns fewer than three source households carry. The export-surface gate compares with the reference's exported columns, so the declared extras shrink from 250 to the 32 the current national build does not export (the dense line's geography and clone columns, and six that microcosm#1550 adds), and an entry the reference now carries is reported as redundant rather than failed. The input-coverage gate requires every loader input the reference carries, measured by the gate's own rule, less the coverage manifest's reviewed exclusions. The gross receipts that microcosm#1550 stops writing, which the current builds still carry, are excluded against those builds in all three gates until a promotion moves the reference past them. A cut can pin a listed build as its reference (`--reference-build`) for a line with no current build, recorded as pinned, and each certification records the change since a fixed baseline, chained from its reference's record without loading the baseline. The certification and the release contract move to schema 2 with a `reference` block, the dense build takes `--input-mass-reference` and `--reference-surface` from its own line's current build, and the frozen enhanced-FRS facts stay only as a historical record.
