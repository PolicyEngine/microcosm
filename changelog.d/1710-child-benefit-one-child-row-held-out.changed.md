Hold HMRC's one-child Child Benefit families in payment (`hmrc.child_benefit.families_in_payment.children_1`) out of the UK solve (#1710). HMRC counts claimant families by the children in payment, and its one-child count is above the FRS's benefit units with one dependent child of any kind, while the other family sizes agree within a few percent; the claim draw only chooses which eligible units are paid, so the row could be met only by inflating one-child families. The row stays measured on every evaluation, and the other family sizes stay bound.
