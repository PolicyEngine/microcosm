The US self-employed health premium attribution (the `other_health_insurance_premiums` stage) now covers people whose only profitable business is a Schedule F farm (`farm_operations_income`) or a partnership's Schedule K-1 box 14 code A earnings (`partnership_self_employment_net_earnings`), not only Schedule C. Each business qualifies on its own, so a loss in one never cancels another's profit; the Medicare-proxy and measured-ESI exclusions are unchanged. This matches the per-business Form 7206 limit in PolicyEngine/policyengine-us#10120.
