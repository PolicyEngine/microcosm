Bonus-sacrificed pension contributions are now imputed (microcosm#1340; María's ruling of 2026-10-22, as a stage anchor). policyengine-uk 2.149.0 adds `pension_contributions_via_bonus_sacrifice`, which the £2,000 NICs exemption cap from April 2029 and the annual allowance tests read summed with salary sacrifice (pe-uk#2572 section 1). The FRS records no bonus sacrifice, so the `salary_sacrifice` stage converts FRS bonuses out of pay into bonus sacrifice: a new carrier, `frs_bonus_amount`, holds each adult's gross occasional bonuses over the last 12 months from the JOB tab, and the candidates are adults whose pay without the bonus reaches the upper earnings limit, the 2% NICs band the OBR costs bonus sacrifice at. Candidates are taken in the order of identity-keyed draws until the engine's projection of the converted bonuses to 2027-28 is nearest the OBR's bonus-sacrifice base above the £2,000 threshold (£13.0bn; OBR supplementary forecast information, 5 February 2026, Table 1.1, vendored from the pinned feed as `obr_salary_sacrifice_costing_facts.json`). The base starts after the calibration year, so it anchors the stage instead of binding the calibration. `uk/bonus_sacrifice_anchor.json` carries the OBR row, the locked engine's earnings and population growth to 2027-28 and the upper earnings limit; `tools/pin_uk_bonus_sacrifice_anchor.py` writes it, and an engine-tier test holds it to the installed engine. The HMRC salary-sacrifice rows, which ASHE builds, keep binding the salary route alone. The new column joins the export allow-list, and the carrier leaves at the release boundary.
