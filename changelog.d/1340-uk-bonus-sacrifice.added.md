Bonus-sacrificed pension contributions are now imputed (microcosm#1340; María's ruling of 2026-10-22, as a stage anchor). policyengine-uk 2.149.0 adds `pension_contributions_via_bonus_sacrifice`, which the £2,000 NICs exemption cap from April 2029 and the annual allowance tests read summed with salary sacrifice (pe-uk#2572 section 1). The FRS records no bonus sacrifice, so the `salary_sacrifice` stage imputes it: a new carrier, `frs_bonus_amount`, holds each adult's gross occasional bonuses over the last 12 months from the JOB tab, and the candidates are adults with a bonus whose pay reaches the upper earnings limit, the 2% NICs band the OBR costs bonus sacrifice at. A sacrificed bonus is never received, so the FRS records it in neither pay nor its bonus amounts: a candidate's received bonus marks a bonus-paying job and its size, and the sacrifice, the bonus up to half the pay, is added beside pay, which stays as it is (María's ruling of 2026-10-24 on Vahid's review of microcosm#1383). An SPI-channel adult's FRS bonus is its twin's, beside the tape's pay, so it draws a bonus share of pay from a base-FRS adult in its pay cell instead. Candidates are taken in the order of identity-keyed draws until the engine's projection of the sacrificed bonuses to 2027-28 is nearest the OBR's bonus-sacrifice base above the £2,000 threshold (£13.0bn; OBR supplementary forecast information, 5 February 2026, Table 1.1, vendored from the pinned feed as `obr_salary_sacrifice_costing_facts.json`). The base starts after the calibration year, so it anchors the stage instead of binding the calibration. `uk/bonus_sacrifice_anchor.json` carries the OBR row, the locked engine's earnings and population growth to 2027-28 and the upper earnings limit; `tools/pin_uk_bonus_sacrifice_anchor.py` writes it, and an engine-tier test holds it to the installed engine. The HMRC salary-sacrifice rows, which ASHE builds, keep binding the salary route alone. The new column joins the export allow-list, and the carrier leaves at the release boundary.
