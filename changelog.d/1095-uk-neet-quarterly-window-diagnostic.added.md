ONS's NEET estimates (Young people not in education, employment or training, UK: August 2026, NEET Table 1, PolicyEngine/chronicle#420 item 1) join the contract as a measured diagnostic for the young people work review (Y3 of the November event-data plan, microcosm#1095):

- `ons.neet.people_aged_16_to_24`: the United Kingdom's young people aged 16 to 24 who were NEET, as the mean of the four Labour Force Survey quarters inside calendar 2025 (January to March up to October to December), not seasonally adjusted, since the four quarters' mean carries the whole year's season.

The model counts people aged 16 to 24 who are neither employed nor self-employed (`employment_status`), nor in full-time education (`is_in_full_time_education`, the FRS answer since Y2), nor in approved training. The FRS records no part-time study or other training, which ONS counts as education, so the proxy and ONS's count measure different things. A signed exclusion holds the row out of the solve; its `approved_on` is drafted for María's signature.

The ledger compiler gains `quarterly_window_average` (María's call of 8 November 2026): the mean of one series over an explicit, ordered list of `YYYY-Qn` quarters, one fact per quarter, under the `source_window` policy. Each quarter of an LFS table is its own record set, so the window's series check reads record-set ids and layout values with their quarter dropped. That reading is confined to the window, so no other reference's series identity moves. Like the monthly windows, it needs no uprating hold. The contract's allowed operations, the reference authoring list and the UK hold rules carry it.
