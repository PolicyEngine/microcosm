SPI-channel rows now take their FRS twin's contributory JSA and ESA where the row's own work stays within each benefit's limits, and none outside them (microcosm#1095 B10; María's ruling of 29 October 2026). Before, `spi_benefit_coherence` zeroed contributory JSA on every SPI row and left contributory ESA with the stage-2 forest's draw, which ignores the benefit unit's claim history. A contributory award follows the person's National Insurance record and incapacity, which the copy keeps; what the copy changes is its work. ESA's limits are permitted work: under 16 hours a week, and earnings up to policyengine-uk's `esa_exempt_work_higher_earnings_limit` at the frame's year (£183.50 a week in 2024-25). JSA's are its own (Vahid's review of microcosm#1426): under 16 hours a week, earnings no more than the prescribed amount (the age-related amount plus the £5 disregard, less 1p: £95.49 a week from 25 in 2024-25), and an award cut by the earnings above the disregard. Earnings are the row's employment income plus any self-employment profit over 52 weeks, and hours its twin's usual hours. The stage-health gate checks the rule on every SPI row, the receipt counts support in source households under the floor of three, contributory ESA leaves the QRF tail-concentration surface (no forest draw survives in it), and the stage spec, the coverage manifest and the H2 fixture follow.
