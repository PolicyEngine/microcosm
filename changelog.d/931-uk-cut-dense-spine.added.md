`tools/run_uk_cut.py dense-spine` builds only the dense line's spine, so a machine larger than the dense calibration's can build it (#931). It fetches every input but the Chronicle artifact. Its preflight checks the checkout, the environment, a signing key registered to sign (a release-candidate spine is shippable only once signed), the registers' expiries and the host, with a 400 GB free-disk floor by default; it reads no reference and starts no emitter. It builds the spine as a release candidate at the local doctrine's 15 geographic clones, with `--stage-profile-dir`, into `<cut>/dense-spine/`, apart from the national cut's spine, and keeps the per-node memory profile in `<cut>/dense-spine-profile/`. Like every step it is resumable, and `--retry-spine` moves an unfinished attempt and its profile aside. Once the spine is done it hard-links the H5, its `.sha256`, `.build.json`, `.spine_gates.json` and `.hmrc_replay.json` and the profile into `<cut>/dense-spine-handoff/`, and prints their upload to `policyengine/microcosm-uk-build-inputs` under `spines/<label>-<commit>/`, a separate, deliberate call.

- **Inputs.** The three conditioned atomic-area supports now have a hosting location: `policyengine/microcosm-uk-build-inputs`, under `atomic-area-supports/<the sha256 of their sources.yaml pins as canonical JSON>/` (`path_in_repo_pin` `conditioned_atomic_supports`), so a re-pin of the register moves the path and an older checkout still finds its files. `run_uk_cut.py inputs` prints each source's `path_in_repo`.
- **The national cut** is unchanged: `spine_argv` takes the dense spine's H5, clone count and profile folder as options whose defaults give the national argv as before, and `spine_done` takes another spine's H5.
- **What moves.** No kernel hashes the runner or its inputs manifest, so no key, dataset or receipt moves.
- **Tests** (invented data): the supports' path follows the register; the dense argv parses under the spine's own parser as a release candidate at 15 clones with its profile folder; the dense spine fetches exactly the spine's inputs; a dense spine is built once, gets the key, and is handed over by hard links with its upload printed; an unfinished one is kept and moved aside only on retry; its preflight runs no reference, Hub write, contract or surface check, and a failed one builds nothing.
