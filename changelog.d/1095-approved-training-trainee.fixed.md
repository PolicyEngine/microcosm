The UK `frs_education` stage sets `is_in_approved_training` from the FRS TRAINEE flag on child-table records, porting uk-data#561 (microcosm#1095). The FRS counts a 16- to 19-year-old as a dependent child only in full-time non-advanced education or unwaged government training, so a child-table record with TRAINEE = 1 is a dependant in approved training. The stage read the adult table's TRAIN2 scheme codes instead (microcosm#1063), which flagged adults on DWP employment schemes and no dependant, so those dependants failed the qualifying-young-person education-or-training condition for Universal Credit, Pension Credit, Child Benefit and child tax credit. No adult-table record is flagged now, a tab without TRAINEE refuses the build, and the entry age and the age-19 terminal-date flag follow the new flag. No engine change is needed. With the flag, fewer than 10 FRS source households separate DWP's count of Universal Credit households with five or more children from its two-child-limit counts; before it, the frame told them apart only through the dependants the missing flag miscounted. So `dwp.uc.households_children_5_or_more` is held out of the calibration until 26 November 2026 (`calibration_measure_exclusions.json`, María's ruling on microcosm#1271), and the two-child-limit rows stay bound.
