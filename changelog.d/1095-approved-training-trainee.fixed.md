The UK `frs_education` stage sets `is_in_approved_training` from the FRS TRAINEE flag on child-table records, porting uk-data#561 (microcosm#1095). The FRS counts a 16- to 19-year-old as a dependent child only in full-time non-advanced education or unwaged government training, so a child-table record with TRAINEE = 1 is a dependant in approved training. The stage read the adult table's TRAIN2 scheme codes instead (microcosm#1063), which flagged adults on DWP employment schemes and no dependant, so those dependants failed the qualifying-young-person education-or-training condition for Universal Credit, Pension Credit, Child Benefit and child tax credit. No adult-table record is flagged now, a tab without TRAINEE refuses the build, and the entry age and the age-19 terminal-date flag follow the new flag. No engine change is needed.
