The UK national calibration weights its targets by loss family (`loss_family_equal`, María's ruling of 25 November 2026, microcosm#1710). A row's loss family is its register family, except that DWP's Universal Credit family splits into three by contract target (`UK_NATIONAL_LOSS_FAMILIES`): the award amount bands by family type; UC households in total, by family type and by number of children, with people aged 16 to 21 and 22 to 24; and the housing element by tenure with the six single-row tables. Each of the three takes one family's share of the objective, where under `family_equal` each composition row weighed under 1/4,000 of it. A bound row of the split family whose contract target is not listed is refused. Only the national loss weights change: the register family stays every row's family for source months, holds, exclusions, the support floor, gates, diagnostics and the mass reason, and the local doctrine's rules read it as before. `family_equal` stays a receipted per-run override.
