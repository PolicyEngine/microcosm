The UK SPI housing shell now keeps every council-tax band inside the bands its nation uses (microcosm#1278). Its classifier conditions on region but had no nation mask, so SPI-channel households in England and Scotland could draw band I, and Northern Ireland households a band other than the FRS placeholder D. No target counts those rows, and a computed council tax would charge a band missing from a nation's ratio table at the Band D ratio. A drawn band outside the nation's bands is now drawn again from the classifier's probabilities over those bands, with its own identity-keyed uniform: every valid draw is unchanged, the redrawn rows follow the classifier conditioned on the nation, and the stage receipt counts the redraws by nation. The bands live in the new `uk_runtime.council_tax_constants`, which `frs_spine` and `local_targets` now import, and a new release-blocking spine gate, `uk_council_tax_band_domain`, checks every household at the transferred boundary. For it, the shared `enum_domain_gate` takes optional per-partition domains, and a declared domain must equal the shared constant.
