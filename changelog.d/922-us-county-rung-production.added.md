Make the ACS-local county rung (`--geographies state,cd,county --soi-mode state_county`) a full production calibration. It trains new ACS county household targets under new ACS state household targets, and scores four held-out ACS county families (earnings, households with earnings, approximate household income and its brackets) against a pro-rata baseline without changing the loss or the weights. It gives every supported county an A–D reliability grade in `county_reliability.json` and a packaged `county_reliability.csv`. `--county-min-support` (default 3) fills empty counties with same-PUMA households and records each move in `county_support.json`. Kalawao shares a declared SOI cell with Maui.
