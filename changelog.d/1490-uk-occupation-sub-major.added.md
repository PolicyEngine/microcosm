A new UK spine stage, `aps_occupation`, draws each person's SOC 2020 sub-major group, policyengine-uk's `soc2020_sub_major_group`, within the major group the earlier stages settle (microcosm#1490). The FRS codes the major group only; the donors are the Annual Population Survey's adults with a minor group, April 2024 to March 2025 (UK Data Service SN 9408, a caller-supplied licensed tab pinned by sha256 and passed with `--aps-tab`). Each major group gets its own `CategoricalClassifier` blend over its sub-major groups, on age, sex, region, employment-status group, annual pay and SIC 2007 section, the predictors both surveys record alike. A person with no major group keeps NONE, and the signed NONE default for the column is retired.

Draws are keyed on `person_source_id`, so an SPI copy draws its FRS twin's group, except for the SPI income recipients, which key on their own `person_id`. The receipt records held-out evidence on five person folds per major group (the APS file has no household identifier) and the FRS rows' expected shares against the donors'. A stage-health gate, `uk_stage_aps_occupation_fit`, holds them to declared bounds, and a terminal gate, `uk_soc2020_sub_major_group_enum_domain`, keeps the column inside the engine's enum. microcosm-fit's classifier gains `unseen_categories="ignore"`, which gives a category the fit never saw no indicator, and counts the rows that held one.
