`microcosm.build.uk_runtime.receipt_counts` now records the study conditions behind the UK disclosure floor (microcosm#1278; the open question on microcosm#796). No study the build reads sets a minimum cell above CD171's working floor of ten: the FRS, LCFS, ETB, WAS and NTS add nothing to the End User Licence. The SPI 2022-23 Public Use Tape (SN 9422) adds two conditions: no attempt to attribute a record, and analyses of the tape go to HMRC at least ten working days before distribution or publication. The weighted-integrity baselines tool no longer asks whether a study requires a threshold of 30.
