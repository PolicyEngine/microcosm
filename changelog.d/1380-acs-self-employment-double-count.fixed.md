The ACS input transfer no longer stacks donor self-employment components on top of the measured ACS total (microcosm#1380). ACS SEMP already covers farm income and income from one's own business, professional enterprise or partnership, and policyengine-us 2.2.1's taxable self-employment income adds SSTB self-employment, farm operations income and partnership self-employment net earnings to ordinary self-employment income. So in a recipient that carries `self_employment_income_before_lsr`, a component the QRF drew onto a row whose total it did not also draw is now kept at zero, and the transfer's provenance records the cells kept at zero and the nonzero draws discarded. The components stay QRF targets, so every other chained draw is unchanged. A new `acs_self_employment_identity` gate requires the self-employment leaves of every ACS person who reports SEMP to be finite and sum to SEMP × ADJINC / 1e6 within $1, and to be zero where SEMP is blank. ACS staging and both ACS local-area release stages that load staging refuse a frame that fails it. The package stage grades the calibrated H5 it ships and binds the verdict to that file's hash, so a checkpoint calibrated from pre-fix staging cannot be packaged; staging built before this fix must be rebuilt.
