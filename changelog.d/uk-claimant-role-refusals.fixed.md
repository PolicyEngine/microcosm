The FRS spine now refuses a benefit unit without one or two adult-table records directly, rather than relying on the DEPCHLDB-based UC claimant rule, which let such a unit through wherever DEPCHLDB is 0 beside child records. It also refuses a person whose supplied `is_uc_claimant` differs from `is_claimant_or_partner`, the role policyengine-uk derives it from. The policyengine-uk adapter now checks that a supplied `is_claimant_or_partner` and `is_hbai_dependent_child` reach the model as unchanged inputs, as it already did for `is_uc_claimant`. Neither refusal fires on the pinned FRS 2024-25, so no built column changes.
