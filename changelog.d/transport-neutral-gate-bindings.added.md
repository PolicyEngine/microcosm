Added `TRANSPORT_GATE_REGISTRY` (`microcosm.build.transport.gate_bindings`), the country-neutral gate bindings a transport country passes to `gates.battery@1`: `per_family_fit`, `aggregate_admin`, `calibration_reference_coverage`, `target_profile_coverage`, `nonnegative_columns`, `exported_nonzero`, `formula_owned_export`, `weight_ess` and `weight_ratio`, each reading evidence the transport graph produces and taking thresholds only from `gates.json`, plus `validate_required_gate_parameters`, which refuses a manifest that omits a required threshold. New Zealand's `gates.json` now declares the non-negative and formula-owned columns those gates check, and marks the support and release-input-coverage gates `not_applicable` until the transport code that produces their evidence exists.
