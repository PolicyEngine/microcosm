Added `TRANSPORT_GATE_REGISTRY` (`microcosm.build.transport.gate_bindings`), the country-neutral gate bindings a transport country passes to `gates.battery@1`: nine implemented comparisons (`per_family_fit`, `aggregate_admin`, `calibration_reference_coverage`, `target_profile_coverage`, `nonnegative_columns`, `exported_nonzero`, `formula_owned_export`, `weight_ess` and `weight_ratio`) and two frozen pending bindings (`support` and `release_input_coverage`). Thresholds come only from `gates.json`; surface tolerances cannot override aggregate thresholds, and `validate_required_gate_parameters` refuses a manifest that omits a required threshold. Weight-concentration comparisons normalize by the maximum weight to avoid overflow and underflow without dropping positive support, the reported median positive weight is the exact median of the original positive weights, rounded once, and reviewed exclusions require non-empty string reasons. New Zealand's manifest declares the non-negative and formula-owned columns those gates check and keeps support and release-input coverage applicable and blocking: missing named producer artifacts yield `evidence_absent`, and supplied artifacts still fail until their evidence checks are implemented.
