`microcosm.build.firms`: country-neutral mechanics for synthetic firm populations, taken out of the UK firm port so another country can reuse them (#1705). `draw_in_band` maps caller-drawn uniforms into a band (log-uniform for an open band, keeping the input's type and dtype); `stratified_thin` thins a candidate draw by stratum with exact base weights; and a `RegistrationSpec` declares a register's bands as excluded, registrable or selected, which `registration_propensity` and `assign_scope_and_registration` use to reconcile a business register with a tax-registration count. The functions reproduce the UK port's outputs exactly under a UK-shaped spec.
