`tools/uk_target_surface_receipt.py --communal` sizes the age-universe change planned on microcosm#1278. Beside the 18-24 student rule it now gives, for every local ten-year age band and 80 and over, each area's census communal-establishment share and how many areas reach 20% and 25%: the amount a private-household target would sit below the total-resident row the incumbent evaluation gates at 25%. It also composes the region-tier shares from local authorities and reads NISRA CT0105 for Northern Ireland's districts, marked approximate (CT0105 counts communal residents only, so its bands are spread evenly over single years against NISRA's mid-2025 district population). `--communal-only` writes that receipt without compiling the target surface. The tool's first tests use synthetic facts. An age label such as "Aged 2 years and under" now reads as 0-2.
