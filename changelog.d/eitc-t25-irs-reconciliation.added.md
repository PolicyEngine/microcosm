A fixture holds the 274 pinned-feed rows for TY2023 Publication 1304 Table 2.5 and the TY2024 filing-season EITC by AGI, verbatim. Two Hypothesis properties on the real layout check that the compiled AGI bins and total rows both add to any filing-season EITC by AGI, and that each split's bins add to its total row under uniform growth. Before #1227 the compile halved the total rows (11.92 million returns and $34.52 billion), and these tests fail on that code.
