New Zealand's geography assignment now gives each of its four layers (`ta_code`, `region_code`, `as_area`, `as_area_alt`) the source, vintage and relation of the transport support column it reads, so `nz.geo.assign` no longer refuses with "layer source, relation or vintage differs". The assignment had declared one source, vintage and relation for all four layers, and those labels differed from the support columns. `tools/build_nz_transport_graph.py` now copies the labels from the support definition, and NZ's golden spec summary moves with the regenerated `geography_assignment.json`. A new test runs the committed assignment on the committed transport support through assignment, derivation and the gate, and checks that changing any label refuses. Because the draws key on the assignment's hash, households' sampled areas change.
