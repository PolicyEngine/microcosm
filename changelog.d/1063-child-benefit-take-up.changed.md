Redraw UK Child Benefit claims and opt-outs after the SPI income chain against HMRC's August 2025 statistics (#1063 fold-in). `frs_take_up` drew `would_claim_child_benefit` at a flat 89% (HMRC, 2022) over every benefit unit with the FRS reporters added on top, and `child_benefit_opts_out` at a flat 23% over every benefit unit whatever its income; the engine pays on the first and never reads the second, so 96% of eligible children were claimed for and paid, and `obr.child_benefit` sat about 20% over.

A new spine stage, `child_benefit_take_up`, runs after `pension_credit_take_up`. One temporary engine materialization gives each person's eligibility (child or qualifying young person) and adjusted net income. A family claims for all its children or none, so the draw is by family, keyed on the age of its eldest eligible child: a reporter claims, and the other families claim at rates solved from the eldest age down so that the claimed share of the eligible children of each single year of age is HMRC's published claim rate (Table 15, May 2025: 68.8% at age 0 rising to 96.6% at 15, 86.6% overall, opted-out families included). The published opted-out share of registered families (684,635 of 7,552,330 at August 2025) of the claiming families then opts out, drawn among claiming families that do not report receipt and whose highest adjusted net income is at or above the income at which the High Income Child Benefit Charge takes the whole benefit, then inside the taper for any remainder. The stage stores claims-and-not-opted-out in `would_claim_child_benefit`, the flag the engine pays on, and the opt-out in `child_benefit_opts_out`.

On the 1 October spine at design weights the redraw claims for 86.7% of eligible children, opts out 9.0% of claiming families (all from families charged the whole benefit) and leaves 11.1m children in payment, down from 13.5m. The published rows are vendored from the pinned Chronicle feed as `hmrc_child_benefit_statistics.json`. A stage gate, `uk_stage_child_benefit_take_up`, holds the claimed share overall and by age and the opted-out share; the terminal take-up signal gate no longer measures the two flags. The roster moves from 36 to 37 stages.
