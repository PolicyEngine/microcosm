US post-export scoring removes the variable modules that each reform's tax-benefit system build adds to `sys.modules`, which policyengine-core never removes, once the reform's consumer opens its next reform or finishes, or the scorer closes. On the eight-household guard fixture the modules held about 0.04 GiB per reform pass, about 4 GiB over a full build's 98 reform passes (estimate). `EngineVariableModuleScope` in `microcosm.build.us_runtime.engine_lifecycle` is the explicit open/close form of `temporary_engine_variable_modules` that this uses; a scope left unclosed does nothing when garbage-collected. Scored values are unchanged.
