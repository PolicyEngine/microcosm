The UK and US hold-outs share their last two steps (microcosm#1759). `microcosm.build.holdout` gains `HoldoutSplit` (a split's training rows and held rows), `fold_splits` (the UK's rotated folds as splits) and `held_estimates` (the held rows' estimates from the target matrix and the solved weights, in float64). The UK rowwise hold-out (`uk_runtime.local_rowwise.rotated_uk_local_holdout`) and the US local hold-out (`microcosm.build.target_holdouts`, through the new `role_split`) both use them; the two draws stay apart on purpose (rotated folds over UK local target rows, hashed blocks over US units). No hold-out result changes: `test_holdout.py` holds the new functions to the replaced UK and US expressions byte for byte, and the US hold-out tests compare with verbatim copies of the old tool code. The UK hold-out node now also hashes `microcosm.build.holdout`, which it ran without hashing. The UK hold-out keeps its constants (5 folds, seed 20260529, in `uk_runtime/local_rowwise.py`); a stricter UK hold-out would start there.
