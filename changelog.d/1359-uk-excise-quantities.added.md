The UK LCFS stage now writes policyengine-uk's excise inputs, so alcohol, tobacco and gambling duty no longer score zero on Microcosm data (#1359). The chain draws five shares on the donor, appended after its twenty targets so every existing draw is unchanged: tobacco within P602's off-trade lines, on-trade alcohol within P611, gambling stakes within P609, and within the stakes the National Lottery's share and the betting and gaming share of the rest. The new `level_excise_quantities` step then shares FY2024-25 national figures out to households in proportion to that spending, at prior weights:
- HMRC's alcohol clearances by product over the engine's strengths, with beer and cider draught shares from the draught-relief clearances;
- HMRC's tobacco clearances;
- each gambling duty's dutiable base: HMRC's receipts, or Machine Games Duty's liabilities, over the engine's rate. Lottery Duty's base follows the National Lottery stakes and every other base the betting and gaming stakes, so the society and Irish lotteries carry none. General Betting Duty's base is split by the Gambling Commission's gross gambling yield.

No spending column moves, the VAT base included. `household_tax` rises by the three duties, about £24.8bn at FY2024-25 (£13.06bn alcohol, £8.17bn tobacco and £3.62bn gambling), so `household_net_income` falls by the same, and with it every net-income, decile and inequality output on the data; HBAI incomes do not move. The levels are national while spending and incidence stay at diary level, so a household's alcohol or tobacco duty is one multiple of its own spending: about 2.2 times its tobacco spending, against 0.41 times its alcohol spending.

The gambling inputs follow keys that the new `top_up_gambling_participation` step brings to the Gambling Commission's Gambling Survey for Great Britain 2025, since the diary records a stake for about a fifth of the households that play. 62.7% of households now carry Lottery Duty and 29.8% the other gambling duties, against 10.7% and 2.0% on the diary's draws:
- each adult's chance of playing in four weeks and in twelve months is GSGB's rate for its sex and age band, and a household's combines its adults at a theta fitted on the donor (0.9 for the National Lottery, 0.08 for betting and gaming);
- within nine household strata, households without a stake are added in an order a donor propensity sets, until each stratum's four-week and then twelve-month share is met;
- an added household's stake is drawn from the donor's positive stakes, and an occasional player's is a tenth of that. Households the diary saw keep their stakes, and no spending column moves.

The stage notes give these and the other known biases: fewer households carry tobacco duty than smoke, other tobacco includes e-cigarette refills, the draught shares are national, alcohol volume follows spending, which leans it towards pub drinkers, and the top-up assumes the diary misses gamblers evenly and takes GSGB's participation as the level, though the Commission found that its estimates move with how the survey invitation is worded. The gambling inputs need policyengine-uk 2.151.0 or later. The new stage-health gates `uk_stage_lcfs_consumption_excise_quantities` and `uk_stage_lcfs_consumption_gambling_participation` recompute every level and rate from the vendored `excise_facts.json` and `gambling_participation_facts.json`.
