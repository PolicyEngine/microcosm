The UK LCFS stage now writes policyengine-uk's excise inputs, so alcohol, tobacco and gambling duty no longer score zero on Microcosm data (#1359). The chain draws five shares on the donor, appended after its twenty targets so every existing draw is unchanged: tobacco within P602's off-trade lines, on-trade alcohol within P611, gambling stakes within P609, and within the stakes the National Lottery's share and the betting and gaming share of the rest. The new `level_excise_quantities` step then shares FY2024-25 national figures out to households in proportion to that spending, at prior weights:
- HMRC's alcohol clearances by product over the engine's strengths, with beer and cider draught shares from the draught-relief clearances;
- HMRC's tobacco clearances;
- each gambling duty's dutiable base: HMRC's receipts, or Machine Games Duty's liabilities, over the engine's rate. Lottery Duty's base follows the National Lottery stakes and every other base the betting and gaming stakes, so the society and Irish lotteries carry none. General Betting Duty's base is split by the Gambling Commission's gross gambling yield.

No spending column moves, the VAT base included. `household_tax` rises by the three duties, about £24.8bn at FY2024-25 (£13.06bn alcohol, £8.17bn tobacco and £3.62bn gambling), so `household_net_income` falls by the same, and with it every net-income, decile and inequality output on the data; HBAI incomes do not move. The levels are national while spending and incidence stay at diary level, so a household's duty is one multiple of its own spending: about 2.2 times its tobacco spending, 0.55 times its National Lottery stakes and 6.3 times its betting and gaming stakes, against 0.41 times its alcohol spending. The stage notes give these and the other known biases: fewer households carry each duty than smoke or gamble, other tobacco includes e-cigarette refills, the draught shares are national, and alcohol volume follows spending, which leans it towards pub drinkers. The gambling inputs need policyengine-uk 2.151.0 or later. The new stage-health gate `uk_stage_lcfs_consumption_excise_quantities` recomputes every level from the vendored `excise_facts.json`.
