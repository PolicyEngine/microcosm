The US child-support signal gate's floor for each support channel falls from 0.002 to 0.001, in the gate and in both `gates.json` entries (`us_base_child_support`, `us_national_child_support`). The PUF tax-detail channel's share is a noisy draw: its QRF trains on at most 5,000 ASEC people, and the default in-sample sign gate draws a rare class below its rate. The first base on the income-year 2023-2025 ASEC pool drew 0.18% for `child_support_expense` there and stopped. The floor still refuses a dead channel; the pooled bands are unchanged. `tools/diagnose_us_child_support_gate.py` reruns the stage from a base's checkpoint, for several seeds and with the cross-fitted sign gate, and reports the shares without writing to the checkpoints.
