The NTS bus stage reads the single-ticket share from the years under England's £2 fare cap (2023 and 2024), as the FRS 2024-25 base year is, and its ticket receipt adds fare-mix lines for England outside London: the share of point-of-use boardings above £2 before the cap and their mean fare, and the share paid at £2 under it, each withheld below 10 boardings or 10 households. The receipt now weights boardings by the full trip weight, as the stage's share does (microcosm#1331).
