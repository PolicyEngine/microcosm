The UK `lcfs_consumption` stage draws `uses_energy_prepayment_meter`, the household input policyengine-uk reads from 2.123.3 for the Energy Price Guarantee prepayment discount, July 2023 to March 2024 (microcosm#1110; PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2098; uk-data#539 tracks the same input). An LCFS donor prepays when it last put money on an electricity, gas or combined key or meter (b1751, b1701, b233, which the LCF asks only of prepayment-meter households) or its diary records a slot-meter payment (c45114, c45214): electricity-only prepayment counts, and a household with no meter payment, including one that did not answer, does not. The declared `impute_prepayment_meter` step fits that flag with a weighted regime-gated QRF on the chain's predictors and draws it at uniforms keyed on the household id, so no other draw moves; its receipt records the donor's and the frame's weighted shares and the Great Britain households flagged. The locked policyengine-uk does not read the variable yet, so the release export drops the column, and an engine-lane test fails once the lock reaches a release that does.
