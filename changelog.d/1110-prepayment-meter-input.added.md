The UK `lcfs_consumption` stage draws `uses_energy_prepayment_meter`, the household input policyengine-uk reads from 2.123.3 for the Energy Price Guarantee prepayment discount, July 2023 to March 2024 (microcosm#1110; PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2098; uk-data#539 tracks the same input). An LCFS donor prepays when its prepayment-meter energy payment (b489) or that payment's gas part (b490) is nonzero, the fields the stage already reads to split electricity and gas: electricity-only prepayment counts, and a household with no prepayment amount, including one that did not answer, does not. The declared `impute_prepayment_meter` step fits that flag with a weighted regime-gated QRF on the chain's predictors and draws it at uniforms keyed on the household id, so no other draw moves; its receipt records the donor's and the frame's weighted shares and the Great Britain households flagged. The locked policyengine-uk does not read the variable yet, so the release export drops the column, and an engine-lane test fails once the lock reaches a release that does.
