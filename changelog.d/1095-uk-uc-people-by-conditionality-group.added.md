Four measured diagnostics split DWP's people on Universal Credit aged 16 to 21 and 22 to 24 by conditionality regime (B5 of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095; María's signature of 12 November 2026), from Stat-Xplore's People on Universal Credit by age and conditionality regime (PolicyEngine/chronicle#420 item 3) at the four quarterly points of 2025. policyengine-uk's `uc_work_related_group_apart_from_earnings` leaves out the earnings rules that move earners into DWP's "Working – no requirements" regime, so the split has two groups: "No work requirements" against the model's NO_REQUIREMENTS, and every other regime against every other value. Each pair adds to its bound age row. The rows are held out of the solve: the model's NO_REQUIREMENTS group runs low, since the build writes none of the pregnancy, adoption, student and foster inputs and policyengine-uk models no students under regulation 14. No 16 to 17 row and no 18 to 21 or 18 to 24 regime row exists, so the 16 to 17 count is never shown alone or by difference.
