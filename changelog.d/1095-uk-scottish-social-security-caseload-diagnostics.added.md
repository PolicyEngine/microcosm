Two national rows from Social Security Scotland's caseload statistics (PolicyEngine/chronicle#420 item 7) join the contract as measured diagnostics (B10 of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095; María's signature of 12 November 2026): `scotgov.adp.caseload_scotland`, against people in Scottish households with `pip` above zero (the spine reads Adult Disability Payment as PIP), and `scotgov.carer_support_payment.in_payment_scotland`, against carers with `carer_support_payment` above zero. Each takes the mean of the month-end caseloads from July to December 2025, after the case transfers from DWP finished. Two signed exclusions hold them out of the solve. Adult Disability Payment and Carer Support Payment are not calibrated to Social Security Scotland: the data hold about a third fewer Scottish recipients than its caseloads, and binding the spending would stretch those households' weights beyond the build's limit (P4 of the plan).
