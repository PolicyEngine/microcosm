The UK LCFS stage no longer leaves a household's electricity or gas above the domestic meter definitions behind the DESNZ total it levels to (microcosm#1164; the extreme energy values item of #1207; María's ruling of 2026-10-12). NEED's quality-assurance annex counts a gas meter as domestic under 73,200 kWh a year and an electricity meter under 50,000 kWh on profile classes 1 and 2. The energy columns are exempt from the donor support clip because the NEED rake and the DESNZ level set them, so a few drawn households sat far above any domestic use, and since #1167 the housing total contained them. The `price_domestic_energy` step declares `household_kwh_ceiling`; after the level, `cap_household_kwh` sets a household above it to the ceiling and hands the kWh it loses to the fuel's other households in proportion to their use, so the DESNZ total holds. The `energy_rake` receipt counts the households capped and the share of the total they gave up, the NEED fit is read after the cap, and the `energy_rake` gate checks the declared ceilings, that no household is left above them and that the total held.
