The UK spine's Universal Credit take-up redraw (`uc_take_up`, microcosm#1334) now fits the households paid the limited capability for work and work-related activity (LCWRA) element, jointly with the age fit (B3 of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095; María's choices of 11 November 2026).

At the spine's design weights the households paid the LCWRA element started about a quarter below DWP's count, and Y1 showed the gap is take-up, not the limited capability flags. The stage's one engine materialization now also returns `uc_LCWRA_element` on the temporary copy where every unit claims, so a unit has the element where its award would include it.

Within each pool, a drawn unit's claim odds are the pool's base odds times each ratio that applies: the age fit's for a unit with a claimant or partner aged 16 to 24, and a second ratio for a unit with the element. A young unit with the element takes both, and the four groups' rates together still realize the pool's count, each in the order of the uniform. The two ratios are solved in turn over Great Britain, at the stage's household weights, each with the other held, until neither ratio's log moves by more than 0.0001 (at most eight rounds). They aim at DWP's young people count and at DWP Stat-Xplore's Households on Universal Credit with the LCWRA element, paid (Payment Indicator Yes), January to December 2025 and averaged: 2,272,425, the value the bound `dwp.uc.households_lcwra_element` row compiles. Where no ratio reaches its count, it holds at the nearer end of its range and the receipt says so.

A shortfall that a pool passes to its neighbours now fills only drawn units with neither a young claimant or partner nor the element, so a passed shortfall moves neither count.

- The facts are vendored from the pinned feed into `dwp_uc_lcwra_households_facts.json`: Great Britain's LCWRA rows, paid and nil awards, of the twelve monthly record sets, which the stage verifies against the pin.
- The stage receipt's `lcwra_fit` block records the rule, DWP's count, the LCWRA households before and after the fit, the support bands and the ratio, withheld where fewer than three source households carry the drawn units with the element.
- The manifest declares the new artifact, its verify operation, `uc_LCWRA_element` among the materialized predictors, and three new keys on `assign_award_band_take_up_residual` (`lcwra_units`, `lcwra_targets`, `lcwra_fit`), which the sources schema now carries.
- Each pool's own count is unchanged. Fixed claims (units that report Universal Credit, and units moved off or kept on their legacy benefits) keep their claims whatever the ratios, so the LCWRA count can stay off DWP's at the spine's design weights. The calibration still binds `dwp.uc.households_lcwra_element`.
- The carer element has no ratio for now. B3's carer part takes carer status from DWP's record of the element on linked claims, and whether the carer element also needs a ratio is read on the combined arm.
