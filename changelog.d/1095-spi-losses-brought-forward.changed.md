The SPI channel moves the tape's losses brought forward (LOSSBF) out of `self_employment_income` into `loss_relief` and `ni_class_4_losses_brought_forward`, the inputs policyengine-uk relieves them against: income tax under ITA 2007 s.83 and Class 4 under SSCBA 1992 Sch 2 para 3 (uk-data#528, microcosm#1095). The stage-1 chain still draws the profit net of those losses, as the tape holds it. Last of all, so that no earlier draw moves, it draws the year's profit before the losses and the losses themselves. After the FRS-only fill and the HMRC aggregates, a drawn row's profit becomes the year's profit and both inputs take its losses. Where the drawn net profit is positive, the losses are its gap to the year's profit. Where it is zero, they are at least that profit. So on every row the profit after the relief is the drawn net profit. Nothing is counted twice: the losses move, they are not copied, and each programme reads one version. Income tax and Class 4 give the same results as before. Universal Credit, HBAI and market income now read the year's profit, as the law intends. The HMRC aggregates and the four HMRC self-employment rows read the profit after the relief. The rows bind `self_employment_income_after_loss_relief` and band on `total_income_after_loss_relief`, two measures that the UK measure resolver computes from the engine, so at fixed weights the rows keep their values and their band membership. FRS rows carry no losses brought forward. The pinned incumbent does not carry the two inputs, so they join the reviewed extra columns of the export surface. The engine carries both losses into each projected year at their nominal value, while the profit grows with its index.
