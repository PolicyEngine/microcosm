The UK dataset-size search and refit each take their own L2 penalty (microcosm#1124): `select_uk_dataset_size` gains `selection_l2_lambda/anchor/basis` and `refit_uk_dataset_size` also `refit_l2_lambda/anchor/basis`, validated by `uk_size_l2` (chi-square basis only, since the record basis only shrinks mass under free mass; anchors `initial` and `uniform`, where the refit's `initial` is the normalized, optionally floored Horvitz–Thompson baseline). An off penalty passes no L2 keyword to the solver, so default runs, their solver options and their size receipts are unchanged; an on penalty is receipted (`selection_l2`, `refit_l2` with the chi-square distance to its anchor, `refit_iterate_selection`). A reused selection must have been searched under the requested L2 and loss weights; a full-pool size refuses size-stage settings. The size checkpoint binds `selection_l2` in its identity only when it is on, so existing checkpoints keep loading, and its loader and writer check it against the search's own options. A typed `UKStageTargetWeighting` seam runs either stage under a named rule other than the dense solve's (the size-experiment harness; graph builds never pass it), and `UKSizeDraw.from_payload` parses a stored draw for the refit node and the harness alike.
