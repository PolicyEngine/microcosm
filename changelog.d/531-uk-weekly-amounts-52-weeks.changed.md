Every weekly FRS amount now annualises at 52 weeks a year (María's ruling of 2026-10-23, uk-data#531, microcosm#1095): benefits, maintenance, earnings, hours, council tax and rents, in the spine (`frs_spine.WEEKS_IN_YEAR`), the HMRC replay leaves and the private-rent localisation. policyengine-uk reads an annual amount back to a weekly or monthly one at policyengine-core's 52 weeks before a weekly or monthly rule tests it, so at 365.25 / 7 every such amount read 0.34% high at Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction, Pension Credit, the non-dependant deductions and the hours tests. The State Pension, at 52 since María's ruling of 2026-10-20, is no longer an exception: the spec's `annualize_periodic_amounts` operation declares 52 for every amount, and `STATE_PENSION_WEEKS_IN_YEAR` leaves the spine. LCFS spending keeps 365.25 / 7 and NTS trips 52.14, since no weekly rule reads them back. A test holds every reader to the one constant.
