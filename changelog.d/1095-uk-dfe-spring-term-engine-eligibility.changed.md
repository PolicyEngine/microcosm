The DfE spring-term universal measure reads policyengine-uk's own eligibility, `universal_childcare_entitlement_eligible`, in place of its own DfE-age gate (microcosm#1095). Since policyengine-uk 2.156.1 (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2588) the engine starts the universal and 2-year-old hours in the term after the birthday on a dataset that supplies none of its legacy childcare inputs, as the release does, so a child still before school in the spring term is eligible exactly when DfE counts them. The 2-year-old measure keeps its DfE-age check, since a child who turns three by 31 December takes the universal hours in the spring term.
