The UC capital stage follows policyengine-uk 2.136.1, which values property apart and shares it across a household's claimants and partners: its texts and engine tests say so, and its receipt counts any benefit unit left on the unavailable sentinel beside a unit that records the household's property, the one case the engine counts twice. The stage's texts also note that both recorded figures project to later years by GDP per head, while from 2.139.1 the engine projects other residential property by house prices.
