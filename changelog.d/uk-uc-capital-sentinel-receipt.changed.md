The UC capital stage follows policyengine-uk 2.136.1, which values property apart and shares it across a household's claimants and partners: its texts and engine tests say so. A benefit unit left on the unavailable sentinel beside a unit that records the household's property, for UC or Pension Credit, is the one case the engine counts twice: the stage's receipt counts such units, and the UC capital gate now refuses any. The stage's texts also note that both recorded figures project to later years by GDP per head, while from 2.139.1 the engine projects other residential property by house prices.
