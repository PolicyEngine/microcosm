Allocate the UK SPI support channel's prior mass by region × State Pension age status (#1069). The channel's 10,000 households are drawn unweighted and every adult in them carries a taxpayer's SPI income, and a region-only allocation gave each of them the region's average weight, so the channel held more pension-age mass than the FRS design gives pension-age households (5.67m against 4.44m pension-age households in the FRS channel, 13.9m people aged 66 and over against about 12.1m at design weights). Within each region the allocation now splits households with a member at or over the engine's State Pension age from the rest; the channel takes 50% of the stratum mass of households without one and the declared pension-age share of those with one. The receipt records both shares and the pension-age masses, and the stage gate checks both.
