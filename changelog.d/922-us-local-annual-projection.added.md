The US annual static-aging builder projects a build of the local-area release, `microcosm-us-2024-local`, for the County Files. It reads the base from its build folder (`--base-build-dir`, plus `--base-artifact-root` when the H5 sits beside `releases/`) and refuses an H5 whose SHA-256 differs from the build's manifest or ledger. `--years` writes exactly the requested years. Each projected year is `microcosm_us_<year>_acs_local.h5`, in the base's fixed nullable layout with its metadata row and root attributes. Every unfactored column, including all geography and person attributes, is checked equal to the base, and each year's receipt records how state and county weighted shares drift. See `docs/us-local-annual-projection.md`.
