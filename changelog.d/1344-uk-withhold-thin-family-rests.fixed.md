A published UK total less some of its published parts can no longer give back a cell that fewer than ten source households carry (Vahid's second review of microcosm#1344, item 17, and four adversarial reviews of the fix). `uk_withheld_row_sets` only asked whether published rows give back a combination of withheld rows, so the Universal Credit family total less its bound payment bands gave back the unbound band above £2,500 a month, which no row carries. A residual check now runs before the span check and on each of its passes. A total's parts are the published rows inside it, and households whose parts hold the same shares form an atom, which every combination of the total and its parts treats alike. A test comes first: when every atom's share column lies within a tenth of the rounding share of the span of the atoms that rest on ten or more source households (as a unit column, with each row scaled, and summed over its stored entries), no combination of the total and its parts with weights between minus one and one can be thin, and the total is clear (a family total over bands whose every cell rests on ten households or more). Otherwise the check takes the total less any set of members whose shares stay within one (an exact branch-and-bound that tries every set, with a part of constant share also counted at share one; past a work cap, or for a total too large to search, greedy groups instead, and a warning names it), less any one part, and less any two parts, which gives back two overlapping parts' overlap. While one of these rests on one to nine source households, the part with the fewest carriers among those it subtracts is withheld. The note on a row withheld this way says it would give back "a row or a cell". Not checked: a total that is no row (a sum of published regions), a part that reaches outside its total, and, on a total the first test does not clear, combinations at other weights than these.
