A published UK total less its published members can no longer give back a cell that fewer than ten source households carry when that cell is no row (Vahid's second review of microcosm#1344, item 17). `uk_withheld_row_sets` only asked whether published rows give back a combination of withheld rows, so the Universal Credit family total less its bound payment bands gave back the unbound band above £2,500 a month, which no row carries, and the same can hold for any total whose bands stop short of its top. Before the span check, and again after each row it withholds, every published total is compared with the groups of its published members that partition part of it (a member restricts the total to some of its units; members whose shares summed pass the total's on a household stand in different groups, so bands and regions of one total are checked apart): while the total less a group rests on one to nine source households, the group's member with the fewest carriers is withheld too.
