Route A's driver (`tools/route_a/route_a.sh`) and its Modal base template (`docs/us-modal-stage-route-a-rerun-base-plan.json`) now pool main's default ASEC years, income years 2023-2025 (ASEC 2024-2026), instead of 2022-2024, from the first certified build after November 2026 (Max's d2603). The template names a new registration, `us-puf-support-base-income-2023-2025`, which runs the rerun registration's command on the default pool and stages the 2023 ASEC archive as its own `asec_weeks_unemployed_zip` input to seed the LKWEEKS cache. `test_route_a_asec_pool.py` fails when either file's years leave `ASEC_DEFAULT_POOL_INCOME_YEARS` or a pin leaves its registry, and `check_commit` refuses a build commit whose registries lack a pooled pin. The November 2026 certified build keeps 2022-2024 and the driver of its own commit, and main keeps the `us-puf-support-base` and `us-puf-support-base-rerun` registrations as they were.
