The ACS local-area release's calibrate stage weights each training target's loss with the national release's formula instead of equally. Both releases now call one implementation, `microcosm.build.us_runtime.target_loss_weights`; the national weights are unchanged bit for bit (Route A's 5,694 recorded weights are a golden test). An explicit ACS local row mapping files ladder population rows as counts and gives each state's `pop_cd_*` rows, and each `state_cd` reconciliation block, one concept budget. On the 09-23 release surface SOI's share of the loss moves from 85.6% to 90.2%, Census population from 10.9% to 6.1%, Medicaid from 1.1% to 2.0% and SNAP from 2.3% to 1.7%. `--target-family-loss-multiplier FAMILY=MULTIPLIER` is new. The weights' digest joins the solver settings, so `--resume` refuses weights calibrated before this change. Future builds only; no release is rebuilt.
