The ACS local-area release's calibrate stage weights each training target's loss with the national release's formula instead of equally. Both releases now call one implementation, `microcosm.build.us_runtime.target_loss_weights`; the national weights are unchanged bit for bit (Route A's 5,694 recorded weights are a golden test). An explicit ACS local row mapping files ladder population rows as counts, gives each state's `pop_cd_*` rows and each district SOI concept one concept budget, and refuses rows it cannot classify. On the 09-23 release surface SOI's share of the loss moves from 85.6% to 90.2%, Census population from 10.9% to 6.1%, Medicaid from 1.1% to 2.0% and SNAP from 2.3% to 1.7%. Without a multiplier this costs district population fit (microcosm#1105: 11 of 436 districts beyond 10% at the release settings); `--target-family-loss-multiplier FAMILY=MULTIPLIER` is new, and the recipe and manifests record it. The weights' digest joins the solver settings, so `--resume` refuses weights calibrated before this change, and finalize no longer marks a weighted solve's concentration as the reviewed one. Future builds only; no release is rebuilt.
