The graph kernels that compile targets now include `microcosm.calibrate.geography_constants` in their implementation hashes, so an edit to a geography label recompiles the targets instead of reusing a stored surface that carries the old label (microcosm#1492). `ledger_targets` labels a target's geography from `UK_GEOGRAPHY_ID_TO_LABEL` when the Chronicle facts give no name, and `uk_runtime/target_weights` reads `UK_WIDE_GEOGRAPHY_IDS` for the UK loss weights, both through `from … import`, which `source_hash` does not follow. The module joins the transport compile modules (hashed by `targets.compile@1`, `takeup.compare@1`, `takeup.gap@1`, `transport.create@1`/`@2` and `transport.quantile_map@1`/`@2`), the UK target compile modules (hashed by the full graph's target, selection, measures and problem kernels and the national graph's target and problem kernels) and the UK problem kernel's loss-weight hash. The UK list also gains `microcosm.calibrate.registry`, `target` and `hierarchy`, which the transport list already hashed. The UK target and problem kernels also now hash code they ran unhashed: `calibrate.target_selection` (full graph), `calibrate.matrix` and `calibrate.artifacts`, `calibrate.solve` (the full problem's loss scales and the national problem's effective sample size; `calibrate.adam@1`, whose hash the UK calibration kernels embed, already hashed it), and `uk_runtime.weighted_integrity` and `uk_runtime.calibration_run`, whose exclusion clock, surface provenance and band-edge check `full_targets` runs. The transport compile list and its 18 exclusions move from `transport/target_kernels.py`, which 13 kernels hash, to `transport/compile_modules.py`, which none does, as the UK list already sat apart. Two checks now pin the hashes: a shared list's imports must be listed or excluded, reading every `microcosm.*` import rather than only `microcosm.build` ones, and the walk from every module a compile kernel hashes, its own module included, must meet only hashed modules, the list's exclusions or the imports no review has classified yet, recorded in the tests and tracked by microcosm#1497 (67 modules for the UK, 35 for transport), a record that can only shrink. Node keys move once: the target compilation of every transport country and everything after it; the UK target, selection, measures, problem and national target and problem nodes and everything after them, including the UK solve; and, because `transport/target_kernels.py` itself changes, `calibration.prepare@1` nodes, `gates.battery@1` nodes at a phase before compilation, and `takeup.assign@1` nodes given no compiled surface.
