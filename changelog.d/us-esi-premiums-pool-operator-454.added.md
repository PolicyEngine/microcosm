The US stacked pool produces `employer_sponsored_insurance_premiums` (microcosm #454). `with_us_esi_premium_inputs` is now a pre-clone pool operator: it runs on the CPS-source rows with its factor set against the anchor times those rows' share of the pool's household mass, so each ASEC person carries the premium a single-source build assigns. The early gap-fill transfers the column to ACS rows as family `source_operator_esi_premiums`, and a new derive-stage operator, `with_us_esi_premium_pool_anchor`, clears a transferred premium where the person's source record has no wages and scales the transferred rows to the same weighted total per unit of household mass. The pool-wide column then equals the single-source column. Both ESI gates grade a pooled frame: the MEPS-IC cell proofs cover the rows that carry the raw coverage columns, and the anchor-universe total adds the transferred column at those rows' employed share. A pool needs an ASEC raw-stage artifact written after the coverage columns were restored. The ACS local release lane is not covered.
