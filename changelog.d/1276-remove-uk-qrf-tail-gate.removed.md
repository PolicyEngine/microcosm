The UK national release-cut battery no longer runs the QRF tail-concentration gate (`uk_qrf_tail_concentration`), and its exclusion register (`qrf_tail_reviewed_exclusions.json`) is gone. On the UK surface the gate's top-100 share could only bite on a column with fewer than 100 independent draws whose CGT-clone copies took it past the 104-row floor, so its verdict measured how little mass those copies carry rather than whether an imputation broadcast a donor tail; and at the armed 0.99947 threshold the #462 incident it was built for (89% of a column's mass in 100 records) would have passed. The data contract drops the gate's pins, detail schema and validation, and re-pins the release-cut part and full-battery digests. `uk_qrf_tail_concentration_columns` and the weighted-integrity baselines tool stay as the measurement surface for the replacement microcosm#1276 asks for: a source-fidelity check of each imputed column against its donor.
