The ACS local lane's staging writes ACS rows' employer coverage and direct-purchase coverage from the ACS items instead of the shared transfer's draws (microcosm#1720, A6; `acs_local_coverage_anchors`, `build_optional_acs_multispine(coverage_anchors=True)`).

- The loader reads four more optional person items: HINS1 (insurance through a current or former employer or union), HINS2 (insurance purchased directly from an insurance company), HIMRKS (Subsidized Marketplace Coverage: 0 categorically ineligible for a subsidy, 1 direct purchase with a premium subsidy, 2 direct purchase without one) and its allocation flag FHIMRKSP. HIMRKS and FHIMRKSP stay on the staged ACS person rows as raw source items, under the names `ACS_HIMRKS` and `ACS_FHIMRKSP` (as `ACS_MIL`).
- `has_esi` is HINS1 == 1, the counterpart of the donor's ASEC `NOW_GRP`. `has_esi` bars the premium tax credit, CHIP and the self-employed health insurance deduction, so those pools are now measured on ACS rows.
- The two direct-purchase flags follow HINS2. Where it is 2, both are False, whatever HIMRKS says (such conflicts are counted). Where it is 1, HIMRKS code 1 sets the Marketplace flag True and keeps the drawn non-Marketplace flag (ruled 25 Nov 2026); under codes 0 and 2 and a blank, the transfer's draws stand unless both are False, and then one draw, keyed on SERIALNO and SPORDER, picks the type at the donor ASEC role's shares among direct purchasers by age band (under 65, 65 and over). Code 2 cannot tell Marketplace coverage at full price from other direct purchase, and code 0's universe is not documented, so no rule reads either. The choice among direct purchasers is one function, `choose_acs_direct_purchase_types`.
- The step runs on the ACS-only frame after every transfer, so the shared plan, its chain and every other draw are unchanged (a test proves it), and before the self-employed premium attribution, which now reads the native `has_esi`. Its receipt (`acs_local_coverage_anchors` in the staging summary) records the agreement of each native value with the draw it replaces, the donor's type shares, the HIMRKS codes among HINS2 = 1 rows (row counts and weighted shares), the allocated count, the agreement of code 1 with the drawn Marketplace flag, and the HINS2 = 2 conflicts; it grades nothing. No register entry changes: the three columns had none.
- This change writes no ACA take-up anchor from HIMRKS.
