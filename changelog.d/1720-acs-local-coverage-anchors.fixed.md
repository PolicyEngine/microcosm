The ACS local lane's staging writes ACS rows' employer coverage and direct-purchase coverage from the ACS items instead of the shared transfer's draws (microcosm#1720, A6; `acs_local_coverage_anchors`, `build_optional_acs_multispine(coverage_anchors=True)`).

- The loader reads two more optional person items, HINS1 (insurance through a current or former employer or union) and HINS2 (insurance purchased directly from an insurance company).
- `has_esi` is HINS1 == 1, the counterpart of the donor's ASEC `NOW_GRP`. `has_esi` bars the premium tax credit, CHIP and the self-employed health insurance deduction, so those pools are now measured on ACS rows.
- The two direct-purchase flags follow HINS2: both False where it is 2; where it is 1 the transfer's draws stand unless both are False, and then one draw, keyed on SERIALNO and SPORDER, picks the type at the donor ASEC role's shares among direct purchasers by age band (under 65, 65 and over). `HIMRKS` (Subsidized Marketplace Coverage), which could split the types, is not loaded pending a ruling.
- The step runs on the ACS-only frame after every transfer, so the shared plan, its chain and every other draw are unchanged (a test proves it), and before the self-employed premium attribution, which now reads the native `has_esi`. Its receipt (`acs_local_coverage_anchors` in the staging summary) records the agreement of each native value with the draw it replaces and the donor's type shares; it grades nothing. No register entry changes: the three columns had none.
