The CPS-ORG wage imputation (`impute_us_org_wages`) and the ACS input transfer (`transfer_acs_inputs`) fit and draw one QRF target at a time, through `RegimeGatedQRF.start_chain` and `fit_draw_next`, and free each target's forests and sign classifier before the next target is fitted. Before, earlier targets' forests were still referenced while later targets were fitted, so several targets' forests were in memory at once: up to 16 in the ACS transfer without a target bank (two availability patterns of up to 8 targets), 2 with a target bank, and 2 in CPS-ORG. Imputed values are byte-identical. In `tools/_legacy/build_us_acs_multispine_base.py`, `--max-targets-per-fit` now sets how many targets are chained in one sequence; it no longer bounds how many targets' forests are in memory. `acs_transfer.py`'s new bytes move the US seed-map and seed-protocol digests, which are repinned.
