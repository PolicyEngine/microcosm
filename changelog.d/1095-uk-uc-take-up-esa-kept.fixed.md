The UC take-up redraw keeps a reported income-related ESA award off a Universal Credit claim. From policyengine-uk 2.149.0 a claim for Universal Credit ends a claimant's or partner's income-related ESA (pe-uk#2563), and the earlier single-rate draw sets many units that report the award, and no UC, to claim it, so the engine would end their ESA at the calibration year. A unit whose claimant or partner reports income-related ESA, and that neither reports Universal Credit nor is moved off its legacy benefits, now claims none, as a reported receipt is a fact; the closure draw reads the same flag, and the stage receipt bands the units it keeps off Universal Credit (microcosm#1095). Housing Benefit, which the engine also ends on a claim, keeps the stored flag pending the Housing Benefit and JSA PR (microcosm#867, microcosm#869).
