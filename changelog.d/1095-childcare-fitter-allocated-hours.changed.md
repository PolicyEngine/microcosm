`tools/fit_uk_childcare_takeup.py` (tool version 4) follows policyengine-uk's childcare rules since pe-uk#2177 (microcosm#1095). Universal and targeted hours now stay beside the working-parent hours. A family that does not claim them on their own still receives them, allocated first from a participating child's working-parent hours (DfE guidance A1.11). So the expectation basis gains a third engine run, with the extended flag on and the targeted and universal flags off, and a targeted row's expected count adds that allocation at the extended rate times one minus its own rate. The universal row counts universal-only children, so every run leaves out a child who receives extended hours. The fit runs on a spine built before the extended-hours anchor moved (#1126), so it sets the hours the take-up stage draws under the current contract in place of the stored ones.
