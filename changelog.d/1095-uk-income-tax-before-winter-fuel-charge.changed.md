The OBR income tax row, HMRC's income tax by total income band, the ten regional HMRC income tax rows and the 32 HMRC taxpayer filters read income tax before the Winter Fuel Payment charge (`income_tax_before_winter_fuel_payment_charge_annual`), which policyengine-uk adds to `income_tax` from 2.157.0 (María's ruling of 2026-10-25, microcosm#1095). HMRC's statistics predate the charge, and HMRC recovers 2025's payments through 2026-27 tax codes and self assessment, so the FY2025-26 receipts in the OBR row's window hold almost none of the recovery. The filters select the same people either way, since anyone the charge reaches pays income tax before it. `obr.winter_fuel_allowance` needs no change: from 2.157.0 its column is the gross payment, like DWP's spending.
