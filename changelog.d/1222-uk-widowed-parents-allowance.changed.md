FRS benefit code 6, Bereavement Support Payment and its predecessors, now splits by rate (microcosm#1222, María's ruling of 2026-10-22). A weekly amount within 1% of a Bereavement Support Payment rate's weekly equivalent (GBP 350 or GBP 100 a month, frozen since April 2017, at 12/52 or at four weeks) stays `bsp_reported`; an amount at Widowed Parent's Allowance's 2024-25 rate of GBP 148.40 a week (within 1%), or below it as a reduced award, goes to `widowed_parents_allowance_reported`, the input policyengine-uk 2.149.0 adds (pe-uk#2572); any other amount, above the allowance's rate and at no rate (a lump sum, a part month or a misreport), stays `bsp_reported` (Vahid's review of microcosm#1383, item 12). Unlike Bereavement Support Payment, the allowance is taxable, Universal Credit unearned income, a welfare benefit for the benefit cap and an overlapping benefit for Carer's Allowance and Carer Support Payment, and Housing Benefit counts it less £15 a week. The column goes wherever War Widow's Pension goes: the SPI channel restores it from the FRS twin, and it joins the export allow-list, since the enhanced FRS keeps the whole code in `bsp_reported`.
