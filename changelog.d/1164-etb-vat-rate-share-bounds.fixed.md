The UK `etb_vat` stage keeps `full_rate_vat_expenditure_rate` a share of expenditure after VAT, in [0, 1] (microcosm#1164; the ETB half of uk-data#572). The rate is `totvat × (1 − reduced-rate share) / standard rate` over `expdis − totvat`, and the stage refused only a zero denominator. So the ETB 2023 donor kept the households whose VAT exceeds their disposable expenditure, with negative rates, and the few whose little expenditure after VAT gives a rate above one, and the QRF drew both onto recipients. The derive step now declares `rate_bounds: [0, 1]`; it drops donors with no expenditure after VAT, caps the rest at one, and fails on a negative `totvat`. A `donor_rate` receipt counts the rows dropped and capped. The output is declared non-negative, and the committed support bound is the declared range rather than a rounded donor extreme.
