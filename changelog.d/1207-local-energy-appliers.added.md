Two UK uprating appliers for microcosm#1207's local-authority energy rows, in `uk_runtime/domestic_energy_targets.py`. `desnz.energy_trends.gb_domestic_level` multiplies each authority's DESNZ sub-national consumption by one factor per fuel, so that Great Britain's authorities sum to the `lcfs_consumption` stage's Energy Trends level (the rows keep only DESNZ's split between areas). Northern Ireland's part of that UK level is taken out at Great Britain's share of UK households (ONS LFS, the level's year) until Chronicle carries Northern Ireland's consumption (PolicyEngine/chronicle#356). `desnz.subnational.gas_meter_share` multiplies an authority's census households by its gas meters over its electricity meters. A missing meter row, or a share outside (0, 1], is refused. Both stamp `uprating_hold`, so the holds register declares the rows. `household_energy_kwh` reads each household's kWh back from its stored spend at the stage's declared prices. With it, the calibration diagnostics' `consumption_drift` block gains electricity and gas kWh at design and final weights wherever the frame carries the household's region.
