Every HMRC row on total income reads it after losses brought forward, as HMRC counts it (microcosm#1095). The SPI and Income Tax Liabilities rows by total-income band now band on `total_income_after_loss_relief`, and the regional rows read and filter on it. The engine's `total_income` adds the year's profit. Since the SPI channel moved the tape's losses brought forward into `loss_relief`, that profit is the profit before the relief, so without this change a row with such losses would move up a band.
