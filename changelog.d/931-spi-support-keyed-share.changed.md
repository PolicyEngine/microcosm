The SPI support channel (`spi_support_channel`) copies its zero-weight SPI households as a keyed Bernoulli share instead of a fixed count drawn by row position (#931). A household is copied when its uniform, keyed on its own `household_id` under the stage's seed (42) and the new salt `spi_support_channel:inclusion`, falls below π = min(1, 10,000 × the sample fraction / the distinct `source_household_id` values entering the stage); at π = 1 every household is copied without reading a uniform. The manifest's 10,000 is now the share's numerator, so a one-clone spine's copy count is binomial, with mean min(10,000 × the sample fraction, the source households), instead of exact: about 1,000 at f010, by design arithmetic (10,000 × 0.1). Each household's draw reads only its own id, the seed, the salt and π, so row order does not matter. π counts true sources, so it is the same at every geographic clone count, and clone 0 of a cloned spine, whose ids are the one-clone ids, draws exactly the one-clone copies, while the other clones draw on their own ids (María's rulings of 7 Nov).

- **Gate.** `e7_spi_synthetic_preclone` is renamed `e7_spi_synthetic_share`. Its declaration carries `maximum_zero_weight_rows_rule: {rule: binomial_share_band, sigma: 4}` instead of the integer 10,000. After the draw the stage resolves it to min(n, ceil(n × (π + band(π, n)))) over the n household rows entering the stage, and runs the unchanged zero-weight gate. The band is the repo's binomial band, moved unchanged from `stage_health.latent_attribute_tolerance` into `stochastic_assignment.binomial_share_tolerance(*, target, rows, sigma)`; `latent_attribute_tolerance` stays, as the same band at four sigma. In `sources.schema.json`, `stack_zero_weight_donors` requires `salt`, a zero-weight declaration takes either the integer maximum or the rule, and `allocate_zero_weight_prior_mass` accepts `geographic_clone_stratum`. The stage's source sentence, draw text and notes describe the share.
- **Per-clone strata.** Where the household table carries `household_clone_index`, the prior allocation's strata also split by geographic clone, so each clone keeps its own mass and its SPI weights depend on its own households and copies only. The manifest declares this as `geographic_clone_stratum: household_clone_index`. A positive-mass stratum without an SPI copy is refused with a count of strata, not their names. Without the column the strata are as before: a frame without it, or with a one-clone index, allocates bit for bit as the region and pension-age strata did, given the same copies. The small-clone-stratum fallback comes later in #931.
- **Receipt.** The stage evidence gains `expected_spi_households` (10,000 × the sample fraction), `inclusion_probability` floored to two decimals, `source_households` rounded by the receipts' rule, and the gate's name, rule and sigma, not the maximum it resolved to. `spi_households` stays exact, because the stage gate reads it.
- **Bindings.** spi_spine, the module of stages 14, 16 and 18, rounds the receipt, so `frs_hmrc_spine_leaves` joins the counting stages. spi_support now draws through the keyed-draw helpers, so `high_value_home_support` and `cgt_residential_split`, which bind it, bind them too. The spine gate kernel's hash names `stochastic_assignment` beside `stage_health`, since the latent-attribute checks' band now lives there.
- **What moves.** At K = 1 the SPI copy set is redrawn, with a binomial count around 10,000 × the sample fraction where that is below the source households. Per-stratum SPI weights move with it, and so does every SPI-channel value from `hmrc_spi_income_spine` on, the high-value and CGT selections that rank SPI rows, and calibration. Base FRS rows keep their stage-16 weights, which are their incoming weights times stratum shares that do not depend on the draw, except the row the exact-total correction lands on. On synthetic frames, `spi_income_band_donors` seats the same households, carriers and bands under the old and new draws, at the same seating scale; its funding factors and mass scale, and with them its donors' and base rows' weights, move only by rounding, since its stratum masses sum the redrawn copies' weights. The empty-stratum refusal reads the new draw, so a small frame that built before can refuse, and one that refused can build. The H2 fixture's 135 households at a sample fraction of 135 / 10,000 give π = 1 (10,000 × 0.0135 is 135.0 in float), so its copies are unchanged; its declarations and the stage's contract hash are regenerated, with the coverage manifest's source digests and `source_stages.json`. Every node re-keys.
