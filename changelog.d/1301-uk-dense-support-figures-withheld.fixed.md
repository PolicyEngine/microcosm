The files a dense UK release ships no longer carry per-area support figures, an area's rows, effective sample size or distinct source households (microcosm#1301). The `uk_local_area_support` gate's failures and reviewed exclusions name the floors an area misses (`rows < 50`) instead of the measured values, each reviewed exclusion records only those shortfalls and its signed record, and each geography level reports the areas checked and below the floor in place of its minima. The weakest-area rows of the diagnostics and the candidate manifest's support-limited misses carry `support_below_floor` in place of the figures; `UKAreaFit`'s figure fields become optional, so earlier diagnostics still parse. The candidate manifest's `support` block counts the areas checked and below the floor, overall and by level. The figures stay in the build's `.area_support.csv`, which no release ships. The dense release contract walks every shipped JSON document, the signed gate report and the release manifest's strings included. It refuses a support figure, as a number or as numeric text, in an area's record (one with an `area_code`, a `geography_id` or an area `code`, or anything beneath an area key), in a code-keyed map under the figure's name, or in a table with an area column (parallel lists or pandas `{row: value}` columns); a minimum, maximum or median of one outside the declared floors; and a shortfall worded with the measured value. A shipped `*.json` the walk cannot parse fails it. The refusal names where the figure is, never its value. Weights and per-cell estimates are unchanged. The full gate node's code identity now also covers `gates` and `terminal_gates`, so an edit to a gate evaluator or a register loader re-keys the gate nodes.
