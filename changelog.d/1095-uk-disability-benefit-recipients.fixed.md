The UK spine now puts each disability benefit on the household member it is received for (A3a of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095). The FRS records Scotland's Child Disability Payment on a parent's BENEFITS row, with WHOREC1 to WHOREC5 naming the child, and the spine summed each row by its own person, so a Scottish child's payment counted as a parent's: the parent took the DLA categories and disability flags, and the child lost the disabled child elements. Attendance Allowance, DLA and Child Disability Payment, PIP and Adult Disability Payment rows now move to the household members WHOREC names when it names anyone other than the row's own person, split evenly between two or more. Rows naming nobody, only their own person or someone outside the household stay where they are. DLA for children in England and Wales already sits on the child's own row. The stage's checkpoint evidence counts the moved rows in rounded counts and support bands.
