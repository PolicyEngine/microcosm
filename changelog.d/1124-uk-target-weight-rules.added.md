Four named UK local target-weight rules that read row metadata beyond the grain (microcosm#1124): `grain_family_equal` (equal grain shares, then equal family shares inside each grain), `nation_grain_family_equal` (a fourth `nation_region` grain for every national row below the UK-wide aggregates, splitting on the new shared `UK_WIDE_GEOGRAPHY_IDS`), and a `*_sqrt_count` variant of each that shares a cell's count budget by the square root of the target value while keeping every grain, family and cell budget. They are computed in `uk_runtime.target_weights` from one row-aligned carrier (`UKTargetRows`) built identically from declared targets and from a stored ordered problem, with a weights digest and a share receipt by grain, family and nation, and a typed stage seam (`UKStageTargetWeighting`) for the size experiments. `uniform` and `grain_equal` are unchanged bit for bit; the doctrine default stays `grain_equal` and release candidates still refuse every other rule.
