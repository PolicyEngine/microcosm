The UK firm generator (`uk_runtime/firm_generation.py`) now follows `PolicyEngine/firm-microsim-paper` (microcosm#1705). Employment is drawn per sector from the ONS sector-by-employment rows. Weights are calibrated on two declared universes: the ONS VAT/PAYE frame (a new population row and the employment rows count frame rows only), and the HMRC VAT-registered subset, whose trader-count and net-VAT rows weight each frame firm by its band's registration propensity (HMRC count over frame mass, paper issue #37). Zero-turnover HMRC traders join the solve as an out-of-frame stratum, with a new Negative_or_Zero trader-count row. HMRC sector counts are rescaled to the band total, as the paper does, because they include traders of unknown band. Turnover is drawn uniformly inside each ONS band (log-uniform in the open band), and the £1-to-threshold net-VAT band is no longer calibrated. Optional stratified thinning (`sample_window_fraction`, `sample_tail_fraction`, `UKFirmGenerationConfig.fast()`) keeps every SIC × band × window stratum's base-weighted total and passes base weights to the solver as initial weights. After calibration, `vat_scope` and `vat_registered` are assigned by seeded weighted selection per band. Rows gain `base_weight`, `in_frame` and `vat_scope`.

Two new modules sit beside it. `uk_runtime/firm_rules.py` is the rules seam: a `UKFirmRules` protocol whose outputs are keyed by `firm_id`, so a policyengine-uk firm-rules adapter can replace the Axiom VAT evaluator, which is now wrapped by `LegacyVATEvaluatorRules` and kept as an optional cross-check. `uk_runtime/firm_universe.py` holds thinning, propensity and scope/registration selection. `uk_runtime/firm_table.py` exports a firm table with policyengine-uk's firm column names in pounds (`to_policyengine_uk_firm_table`), with the input-VAT imputation named as a data assumption. `docs/firm-support-plan.md` now names policyengine-uk as the firm rules home.
