The ACS local lane no longer counts a respondent's partnership income twice (microcosm#1387). ACS SEMP already includes income from one's own partnership, and the 2024 ACS form tells respondents to report jointly received income wholly under one person, yet the transfer drew the PUF donor's nonpassive `partnership_income` onto those rows. A new keyword-only `semp_covers_partnership_income` on `transfer_acs_inputs`, `acs_transfer_execution_contract_identity` and `build_optional_acs_multispine` (off by default; only the legacy ACS base builder turns it on) keeps a newly drawn `partnership_income` at zero on rows with a finite nonzero SEMP, on tax-unit heads and spouses whose head or spouse reports one, and on rows with blank SEMP, before the passive partnership/S-corp subset is reconciled to line 32. `s_corp_income` is kept: ACS counts incorporated owners as wage workers. The rule refuses, before any fit, a plan that transfers `partnership_income` without `s_corp_income`, a recipient without `SEMP`, `person_tax_unit_id` or `tax_unit_role_input`, a `SEMP` value that is not a number, a recipient whose persons aged 15 or over all have blank `SEMP` (an extract that lost it), and a tax-unit role outside HEAD/SPOUSE/DEPENDENT, including non-ASCII lookalikes and undecodable bytes. An imputation mask that is not a boolean array on the person rows is refused. The partnership provenance record carries a receipt: per row set, the cells kept at zero, the nonzero draws discarded, and the discarded draws in whole dollars, unweighted and weighted by ACS household design weights; the count of persons aged 15 or over with blank `SEMP` and under 15 with a finite one; and the passive draws left on discarded rows whose S-corp income is nonzero, which the gate cannot see. Off the flag every contract payload and draw is unchanged; the US spec-engine attestation pins move because `acs_transfer.py` is an attested kernel module. The `acs_self_employment_identity` gate now also requires `partnership_income` to be zero on those three row sets, the passive subset to be zero wherever the person's or tax unit's line 32 is zero, finite line-32 values, numeric `SEMP` and well-formed tax-unit roles, and it needs the line-32, tax-unit and role columns. A blank `partnership_income` on those rows fails as an unmodeled cell, apart from the double-count clauses, and the gate records `SEMP` against the income universe's age. The release tool names a rebuild only when a failure is a double count. The gate is not behind the flag, so ACS local staging and calibrated checkpoints built before this fix fail materialize, consumer export and package and must be rebuilt.
