The ACS local lane no longer counts a respondent's partnership income twice (microcosm#1387). ACS SEMP already includes income from one's own partnership, and the 2024 ACS form tells respondents to report jointly received income wholly under one person, yet the transfer drew the PUF donor's nonpassive `partnership_income` onto those rows. A new keyword-only `semp_covers_partnership_income` on `transfer_acs_inputs`, `acs_transfer_execution_contract_identity` and `build_optional_acs_multispine` (off by default; only the legacy ACS base builder turns it on) keeps a newly drawn `partnership_income` at zero on rows with a finite nonzero SEMP, on tax-unit heads and spouses whose head or spouse reports one, and on rows with blank SEMP, before the passive partnership/S-corp subset is reconciled to line 32. `s_corp_income` is kept: ACS counts incorporated owners as wage workers. The rule refuses a plan that transfers `partnership_income` without `s_corp_income`, a recipient without `SEMP`, `person_tax_unit_id` or `tax_unit_role_input` (before any fit), and a tax-unit role outside HEAD/SPOUSE/DEPENDENT. The partnership provenance record carries a receipt: per row set, the cells kept at zero, the nonzero draws discarded, and the discarded draws in whole dollars, unweighted and household-weighted. Off the flag every contract payload and draw is unchanged; the US spec-engine attestation pins move because `acs_transfer.py` is an attested kernel module. The `acs_self_employment_identity` gate now also requires `partnership_income` to be zero on those three row sets, the passive subset to be zero wherever the person's or tax unit's line 32 is zero, finite line-32 values and well-formed tax-unit roles, and it needs the line-32, tax-unit and role columns. The gate is not behind the flag, so ACS local staging and calibrated checkpoints built before this fix fail materialize, consumer export and package and must be rebuilt.
