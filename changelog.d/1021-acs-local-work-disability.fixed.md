Read disability and weeks worked from the ACS itself on the ACS rows of the ACS local-area release (microcosm#1021). ACS rows got `is_disabled` and `is_blind` from ASEC donors through the transfer, even though the ACS asks the same six disability-difficulty items, and the ACS loader never kept weeks worked. The loader now keeps `WKWN` and `DEAR`/`DEYE`/`DOUT`/`DPHY`/`DREM`/`DDRS`. A new local-lane stage, `microcosm.build.us_runtime.acs_local_work_disability`, runs on the ACS-only frame before the ASEC transfer, so the transfer (which fills only missing cells) skips these columns. It writes `is_disabled` (any difficulty item is 1, or `SSIP` > 0 under age 65, matching the ASEC eligibility-inputs definition) and `is_blind` (`DEYE` == 1) on every ACS row, reading each item from its minimum question age. It also carries `weeks_worked` from `WKWN` under the `WKHP` universe rules: blank together with `WKHP`, 0 only where `WKL` confirms no work, and missing under 16. The ASEC rows and the shared pool mapping are unchanged. `weeks_worked` is staged but not exported, because the pinned policyengine-us gives it a `formula_2025`; it ships once policyengine-us#9660 removes that formula. `is_veteran` is documented but not exported, and `is_incapable_of_self_care` stays on the transfer. Staging gates the stage with a per-spine `acs_local_work_disability_signal` gate and records a receipt. The release tool refuses a staging summary without that receipt, and the reviewed-null fill refuses to default-fill either flag. Finalize hard-fails on the gate (missing, constant or non-boolean flags, ACS flags that differ from their native items, or a receipt showing transfer-imputed cells), and packaging requires the gate to pass and be bound to the packaged H5. Staging must be rebuilt.
