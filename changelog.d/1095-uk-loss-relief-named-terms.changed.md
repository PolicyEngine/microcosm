The HMRC loss-relief measures become named terms of the binding interpreter's expression grammar, declared once in `UK_LOSS_RELIEF_TERMS` (microcosm#1095): `self_employment_income_after_loss_relief` is `max(self_employment_income - loss_relief, 0)` and `total_income_after_loss_relief` is `total_income - self_employment_income + self_employment_income_after_loss_relief`. The grammar gains one function, `max(<sum>, 0)`. The measure resolver evaluates a named term from its leaves, all from one source (the frame's columns at the frame's own period when the frame stores every leaf, the engine otherwise), and each `_annual` form on the annual income simulation, so the bindings keep their names and their values. The measurement-period receipt reads a value expression with the interpreter's parser, so a subtraction reads as two names.
