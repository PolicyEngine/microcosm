The SIPP/SCF liquid-asset stage carries each household's draw on the declared `is_household_head` (the CPS reference person, `P_SEQ == 1`), as `us/source_stages.json` declares and the housing stages use, instead of the lowest `A_LINENO` (part of #1215). A household whose flag does not mark exactly one person, or a frame without the flag, keeps the lowest-line rule. The two rules disagree in 243 of the certified frame's 57,240 households; on the 2024 income year, where `A_EXPRRP` is observed, the flag is the reference person in every household and the line rule misses 73.
