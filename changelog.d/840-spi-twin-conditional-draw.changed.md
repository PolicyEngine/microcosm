The UK SPI income stage (`hmrc_spi_income_spine`) now conditions its tape forest on four bridge facts (main source, pay, trade pages, State Pension age) and draws each SPI-channel recipient's income leaves at the position its FRS twin holds among FRS people like it, located on cross-fitted FRS reference forests (`FittedRegimeGatedQRF.conditional_uniforms`, the inverse of `predict_from_uniforms`), with identity-keyed uniforms where the twin has no analogue; a twin with no taxable income keeps its values, and the stage flags the rows it drew for the landlord stage (microcosm#840, uk-data#529). The method, the receipts and the no-record rule are described in `docs/uk-income-anchors-280.md` under "The SPI draw follows the twin".
