A new CI job, `engine-uk-after-merge`, runs on pushes to main only, after merge (#931). It runs the new `engine_after_merge/uk/` test category, UK engine tests too slow for every pull request. Its first module is the clone-first H2 test, which runs the whole UK spine at one and at four geographic clones through the engine, about 27 minutes per leg. `tools/run_engine_uk_tests.sh` takes the job whose files it runs, `engine-uk` by default.
