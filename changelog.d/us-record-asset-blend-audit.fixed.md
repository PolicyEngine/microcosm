US fiscal-refresh releases record the SCF-wealth gate's verdict and summary, including the SIPP/SCF financial-asset blend audit (seed, period, per-source household counts, bank-asset medians and low-tail shares), in both `build_manifest.json` (`gates.scf_wealth_signal`) and `calibration_diagnostics.json` (`build.scf_wealth_signal`) (part of #1216). The stage keeps the audit in `DataFrame.attrs`, which the H5 writer drops, so no earlier release records whether the SIPP half ran. The record is scoped to the blend-stage frame and pins its household id set (count and SHA-256); when a published H5's household ids hash to that value, the seed and period reproduce each household's source draw.
