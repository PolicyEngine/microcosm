The UK calibration holds two of the three ONS national balance sheet land rows out of the solve as measured diagnostics (#931; María's ruling of 6 November 2026, workstream 2 of microcosm#1521). `ons.land.corporate_land_value` (E43B, non-financial corporations' land) reads policyengine-uk's `corporate_land_value`, which hands out the engine's own aggregate by each household's share of the weighted corporate-sector wealth: a released file meets that aggregate at any weights, and the solve, fitting the column measured at the starting weights, pinned only the weighted mass of the WAS corporate and private pension wealth, not land. `ons.land.land_value` (E44R, the total economy) also counts the land of financial corporations, government and NPISH, which neither the household row (E44N) nor the corporate row covers, while the engine's `land_value` is the sum of the two columns those rows read, so beside them it asked for £323,750m that no weights could supply. Both are measured on every evaluation, under reviewed exclusions to 2027-02-06. `ons.land.household_land_value` stays bound, and its binding note now names the households sector, the one E44N covers, not households and NPISH; the two held rows' binding notes record the hold.

- **What moves in the solve.** Two rows leave it on both lines. Both lines give each target family an equal share of the objective and split it evenly over the family's bound rows (on the dense line, within each grain, where the land rows are amounts), so `ons.land.household_land_value`, now the `ons_land` family's only bound row, carries the family's whole share, three times its loss weight before. No other row's weight changes, since the family keeps a row. The calibrated weights move slightly through both effects, which a calibrate-only rerun on the move's spine is to measure together. The released corporate land value still equals the engine's aggregate, and the released land value moves only through household land.
- **What else moves.** The national frozen scoring register (`national_target_registry.json`, the approved register) loses the two rows, so a national run given a frozen register derived before this change refuses, as with `obr.ni_employer`; the dense line checks its frozen register against the reconciled register, which keeps them. The incumbent and release-cut scores, `uk_target_fit` and `calibration_diagnostics.json` read only the rows the solve binds, so the two rows leave them. Neither line resolves `land_value` or `corporate_land_value` any more: the dense measure receipt's `national_inputs` falls by two, and a per-block dense run's `block_sensitivity.present_in_this_run` is empty. The dense release lists the two exclusions among its limitations (`measure_exclusion:<name>`, with the first 300 characters of each reason). The edited files are UK country-spec resources, so the national and dense target, measure and solve nodes re-key; spine nodes, `source_stages.json` and the H2 spine fixture do not move.
