The UK spine's SPI support channel now shares each region and pension-age stratum's channel mass among its copies by their source households' raw FRS design weights (GROSS4, the incoming spine weight), damped to the square root, instead of giving every copy the same weight. The channel's 10,000 households are drawn uniformly, so equal copy weights reproduced the FRS respondent mix rather than the design's population: young families, single adults and one-earner couples were over-represented and households of three or more adults under-represented. Copy `j` of stratum `s` takes `M_s * share_s * f_j / sum_k f_k`, with `f` the copy's design weight over the stratum copies' mean, so each stratum's channel mass stays exactly its declared share, the pension-age stratum keeps its 0.2 share, and the hosts and their incomes do not move. The shape is declared on `allocate_zero_weight_prior_mass` as `prior_shape: design_damped` with `prior_shape_exponent: 0.5` (`design_bounded` with `prior_shape_bounds` is also understood), and the stage parser refuses any other declaration. A design shape needs design input weights, so shaping copies by calibrated or later stages' weights, the defect of microcosm#630, is refused. The guard of #420, that every copy's source household has a positive design weight, is restored. The square root was chosen over the full design shape and the shape clipped to [0.5, 2] or [0.67, 1.5] by re-solving the release calibration on the same hosts: it kept fewer rows at the weight-ratio cap than equal weights and the highest Kish effective sample size, with loss within 2.2 percent of equal weights, while the [0.5, 2] clip put more rows at the cap and widened the £125,000 to £150,000 band's standard error. The stage receipt records the shape and its adjustment's 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles with banded counts, never a minimum or a maximum, and the allocation's mass-change record names the shape.
