Concept mappings now refuse a `scale`, `sum`, `share` or `fraction` binding that computes from a concept whose temporal basis is not `annual_flow`. The rule checks the concepts a binding computes from, not the household reference person some bindings read to place a value. Today that refuses any of the four on the stock `liquid_financial_assets`, and a scale of `usual_weekly_hours` or `take_up_seed`. Before, a weekly scale of the stock, or a sum of it with a year's income, validated. Every committed mapping already meets the rule, so no binding, coverage report, golden or pinned hash changes.
