The UK LCFS and ETB VAT stages band the donor counts their receipts record at source, since a stage receipt is build output that can leave the machine (microcosm#1164, from the review of microcosm#1260). The new `uk_runtime.receipt_counts` rounds donor sizes and training rows, writing one to nine as "<10" and rounding to the nearest ten below a thousand and the nearest hundred above: the road-fuel and gas-payer training rows and the fuel-flag donor households. Counts of rows picked out by an extreme value are banded as "<10", "10-49", "50-99", then to the nearest hundred: the ETB VAT rows dropped and capped, and the LCFS donor floor's rows raised, per column and in total. The ETB VAT receipt records no donor size, which beside the donor year's sample size would give the dropped count back. An unweighted share of donors is floored to two decimals, since an exact share gives back its count, and a weighted share carried by fewer than ten donors is written "<10". A count a gate must read as an exact zero stays exact.
