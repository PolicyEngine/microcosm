2024-25 Band D council tax for every Great Britain local authority, and the statutory band formula, in `microcosm.build.uk_runtime.council_tax_band_d` (#931). No stage reads them yet; council-tax localisation will.

- **England and Wales.** `uk/council_tax_band_d_england_wales_2024_25.json`, built by the new `tools/build_uk_council_tax_band_d.py` from two raw-only Chronicle registrations (chronicle#413). Each file is held to the sha256, size and manifest its registration records at the Chronicle commit the UK feed pins.
  - England: MHCLG's 2024-25 Table 8 (area council tax for a Band D dwelling occupied by two adults), read with the standard-library ODS reader `ods_tables.read_ods_tables`. Each amount must lie within 1e-9 of a whole number of pence and is kept as that pence.
  - Wales: StatsWales' composition of average band D council tax (the 2024-25 total for each billing authority area), read with `csv` and kept as published. Each authority is joined to its April 2023 code by its exact name on `local_authority_names.json`, and the all-Wales row is a declared exclusion.
  - The codes read must be exactly the roster's English and Welsh codes. `--built-on` is explicit, and `--check` rebuilds the resource and compares it with the committed one, `built_on` aside.
- **Scotland.** `uk/scotgov_band_d_council_tax_2024_25.json`, vendored by `tools/vendor_uk_ledger_facts.py` from the pinned feed: CTAS 2026's 2024-25 average Band D for the 32 council areas (`scotgov.ctas2026.band_d.fy2024`; the `local_authority` level leaves out the Scotland row). Its register entry in `ledger_fact_vendor_selections.json` names `uk_runtime.council_tax_band_d` as its consumer and carries the OGL v3.0 attribution.
- **The loader.** `load_uk_council_tax_band_d()` merges the two over the roster's Great Britain codes as exact rationals, each read as a `Fraction` through `Decimal`. Scottish rows are read through `vendored_rows`, so a copy taken from another feed is refused before any row is read, and each Scottish amount is kept as the pence it lies within 1e-9 of.
  - It refuses a Northern Ireland code, a code off the roster or of a nation the resource does not cover, a missing or repeated authority, and a value that is not a positive finite decimal.
  - It also refuses a wrong schema version, kind, resource, fiscal year or roster vintage, and a Scottish row of another record set, concept, unit, period, level or vintage.
  - Refusals name codes, fields and record ids, never an amount. Test payloads replace the packaged resources as in `load_hmrc_isa_statistics`.
  - `check_scotland_band_d_declaration` is the runtime arm of the `verify_vendored_fact_resource` operation a stage will declare for the Scottish resource.
- **The statute.** `UK_COUNCIL_TAX_BAND_RATIOS` gives each nation's band proportions to Band D as exact fractions: England and Wales 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 13, 15 and 18 ninths for A to H, and Wales 21 ninths for I; Scotland 240, 280, 320, 360, 473, 585, 705 and 882 over 360 for A to H, in force from 2017-18. `UK_COUNCIL_TAX_SINGLE_ADULT_DISCOUNT` is a quarter, and `uk_statutory_council_tax(band_d, nation, band, *, single_adult)` returns the exact bill and refuses Northern Ireland, a band the nation does not use and a float Band D. Nations and band sets are read from `council_tax_constants`, which is unchanged.
- **Shared with the tool.** The tool takes the England-and-Wales resource's name, kind, schema version, method, fiscal year, pence tolerance and its amount checks (`band_d_pence`, `band_d_decimal`) from the runtime module, so the two cannot drift.
- **Keys.** The two packaged resources, the register entry and `country_package.json` move the UK country fingerprint and the solve fingerprint. The kernels that hash them (the spine gates, and the full line's target compilation and selection, measures, problem, preflight, gates, holdout and national nodes) move, and the nodes after them re-key. No stage declares the new resources, so no stage contract moves; no H2 file or value moves, and no other country's key moves.
- **Tests.**
  - `tests/engine_free/uk/test_uk_council_tax_band_d.py`: the committed resources load over exactly the roster's Great Britain codes; England and Scotland are at pence and Wales has at most six decimals and is not rounded to pence; the England-and-Wales `sources` equal the tool's pins; the Scottish resource carries the feed pin. It also covers the loader's refusals on invented amounts, the runtime arm, and every band of every nation under the statute, which restates the proportions so a typo or Scotland's pre-2017 proportions fail.
  - `tests/engine_free/uk/test_uk_council_tax_band_d_tool.py`: the tool on synthetic inputs, and its `--check` on the committed resource when the registrations are mounted.
  - `tests/engine/uk/test_uk_council_tax_band_d_engine.py`: the proportions equal policyengine-uk's at 2024 within 1e-9 and the single-adult discount exactly, and every Great Britain code's engine key, and no Northern Ireland one, is a key of the engine's Band D amounts.
