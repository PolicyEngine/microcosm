The UK firm export maps ONS legal status 4 (public corporation or nationalised body) to `COMPANY` and marks it `firm_is_public_body`, so policyengine-uk charges it corporation tax and excludes it from the Employment Allowance; it previously mapped to `OTHER` (microcosm#1705). Every status 7 body (non-profit body or mutual association) is now exported with `firm_is_charity`, an assumption since ONS does not split charities out, which gives it policyengine-uk's charitable rate relief. (Its Employment Allowance charity exception matters only for public bodies, and no status 7 body is one, so the allowance does not move.) Limited liability partnerships are not drawn apart: ONS does not name them and they most likely sit with companies (status 1), a caveat only. The build receipt's `data_years` now also records the year each input group's source describes (`ons_attributes` for the ONS UK Business 2025 tables), apart from `rule_period`.
