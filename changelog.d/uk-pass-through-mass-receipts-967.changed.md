Every UK column-writing source stage (`was_wealth`, `lcfs_consumption`, `etb_vat`, `etb_services`, `regional_property_uprating`) now records a household-mass conservation receipt under its own module constant, declared by a `record_mass_conservation_receipt` operation in the sources spec; the coverage manifest requires exactly that record, so the terminal family gate asserts positively that each stage left household mass unchanged (the E8 pattern on every stage). The generator refuses a stage that declares no receipt instead of inventing one, and the loader again requires a reason on every family. Spines built before this change carry none of the five receipts, so their families fail the release-cut coverage gate until rebuilt.
