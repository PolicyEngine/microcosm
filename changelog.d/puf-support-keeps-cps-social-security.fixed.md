The PUF-support channel no longer erases CPS-reported Social Security: for `puf_tax_detail` people whose CPS record reports Social Security (`SS_VAL > 0`), the four `social_security_*` components keep their CPS values after PUF finalization instead of being replaced by the PUF prediction (#1178). Before this, 11.58M weighted people in the default `populace-us-2024-spm-20260915` and 10.06M in the tag-only Route A release carried $0. Nonreporters keep PUF imputation. US fiscal-refresh releases also write a held-out `us_ssi_social_security_holdout.json` comparing the share of SSI recipients with Social Security, ages 18–64 and 65+, with SSA's SSI Annual Statistical Report 2024, Table 9 (27.35% and 57.13%); it reports and does not gate. Future builds only; published releases are unchanged.
