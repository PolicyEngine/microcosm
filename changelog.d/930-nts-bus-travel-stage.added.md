A person-grain `nts_bus_travel` stage joins the UK spine between `was_wealth` and `lcfs_consumption` (microcosm#930, the structural fix María ruled on 2026-09-15 for microcosm#890's rake-then-bind bus fares). It reads the National Travel Survey microdata (UKDS SN 5340, End User Licence; Household, Individual and Trip tables pinned like the other licensed donors; England residents only since 2013) through a declared codebook (`clean_nts_travel_tables`: column names, region, income-band, sex, main-mode and frequency codes, survey years, banded ages), fits one regime-gated QRF on the interview frequency-of-use band (the variable behind NTS0313; 0 is the non-user band) conditioned on age band, sex, cars, household size, the three-band gross household income and region (`impute_bus_use_band`, identity-keyed uniforms, rounded to the nearest band, devolved recipients on a declared proxy region), assigns each person the diary's weighted mean annual local-bus trips for their band and residence group split into the bus-in-London and other-local-bus series (`assign_trips_from_band_means`, W5 x JJXSC over W2 persons annualised by 52.14 weeks; a household sum `household_local_bus_trips`), and emits the statutory concessionary eligibility of the person's area (`assign_bus_pass_eligibility`: England outside London at 66, London under 18 or 60+, Scotland under 22 or 60+, Wales 60+, Northern Ireland 65+). If the extract carries no frequency column the band is drawn from the vendored NTS0313/NTS0621 shares and declared band midpoints are scaled to the vendored NTS0303 rate, receipted as such. Stage-time gates: a support clip on the band and trips, and `bus_travel_facts`, a population fact check that recomputes the NTS0313 user share and the NTS0303 trips per person from the vendored rows; the frame's car availability against NTS0205 is a receipt line. The artifact pins are placeholders refused at runtime until the licensed tabs land; the H2 fixture drives the stage with synthetic tabs (30 stages), and the graph, kernel registry, spine tool (`--nts-household-tab`, `--nts-individual-tab`, `--nts-trip-tab`), coverage manifest, export surface and gate digests carry the stage.
