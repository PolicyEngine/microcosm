`PolicyEngineUSVariableMetadataIndex(follow_helpers=True)` reads through the engine's module-level helper functions in `variables/` (microcosm#1675). The static consumer index interprets such a helper once per distinct call context and records its reads under the helper's own name, which `variable_dependency_closure` does not follow, so a variable's graph stopped where its formula called one. On policyengine-us 2.145.3 that hid, among others, free and reduced-price school meals' reads through `school_meal_daily_rate` and the self-employed health insurance deduction's Form 7206 inputs. The index now also records each helper read under every variable whose formula reaches that call context, directly or through other helpers (`helper_consumers`), and the `follow_helpers` view walks them. The default view is unchanged, receipt for receipt, so the pool's pinned SSI dependency contract and the US engine ABI lock stay as they are. The reviewed-fill register's engine-tier walk uses the new view.
