Key the UK Child Benefit claim draw on each family's eldest eligible child under 16, and redraw the SPI copies' Child Benefit reports before the draw reads them (#1508).

The draw claims for all of a family's children or none, and keyed each family on its eldest eligible child. A family whose 16-to-19-year-old's claim had lapsed lost its younger children's claims with it, and the rates at younger ages could not make up for them. Families are now keyed on their eldest eligible child under 16, or on their eldest when none is under 16, and the rates are solved for ages 15 down to 0, then 19 down to 16. A young person who lives with younger siblings is then claimed for at the younger child's rate, so ages 17 to 19 can sit above HMRC's claimed shares; the stage gate reports such clipped ages without holding them.

The SPI income chain gave each SPI copy its benefit reports from its age, gender, region and incomes, without its children. A copy with no eligible child could report Child Benefit, and copy families reported it too rarely below the charge start and too often where the charge takes the whole benefit. A reporter always claims and is never opted out, so the stage now redraws the copies' reports first: a copy family reports at the weighted share of FRS families that report in its cell (eligible children 1, 2 or 3 or more, by the charge band of its highest adjusted net income), coupled to its FRS twin so that a copy in its twin's cell keeps the twin's report, and a copy with no eligible child takes its twin's reports. A new report carries the engine's `child_benefit_entitlement` on the benefit unit head. FRS rows are unchanged. `child_benefit_reported` joins the stage's rewrites and `child_benefit_entitlement` its engine predictors; the receipt gains `copy_reports`, and the stage gate also requires FRS rows unchanged, every cell on at least 30 FRS source households and the copies' reporting share within 0.05 of its cells' rates.

On the b2179c spine at the stage's weights (survey year 2024), copy families report at 69% after the redraw against 46% before and 68% expected from their cells; 87.1% of eligible children are claimed for against 87.0% at HMRC's claimed shares by age; ages 0, 1 and 17 to 19 clip; 7.9% of claiming families opt out against 9.1%, with the fully charged pool exhausted; and families paid with an income above the charge start fall from about 0.89m under the old rules to 0.75m, 0.58m of them reporters.
