The `was_lisa` stage anchors Lifetime ISA holders to HMRC's 2024-25 payers (microcosm#1415). The WAS round-8 model implied about 660,000 holders on the spine against HMRC's 1.14 million Lifetime ISA accounts subscribed to in 2024-25; every payer holds an account and pays into one a year, so the ownership logit now shifts up on ages 18 to 47, the ages that can hold and pay into one in 2024-25, until the holders at those ages who survive the financial-wealth cap equal that count. The same keyed uniforms decide ownership, so the anchor only adds holders. A new gate, `uk_stage_was_lisa_anchor`, checks the count.
