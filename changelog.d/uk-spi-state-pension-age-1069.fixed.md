Make the UK SPI income stage respect State Pension age (#1069). SPI donor ages are now drawn within each AGERANGE band by the ONS mid-2023 single-year populations of the donor's sex, vendored from the pinned Chronicle feed as `uk/ons_single_year_age_populations.json` and declared as a stage artifact. Donors receiving State Pension (`SRP > 0`) are drawn at or above the engine's State Pension age, and a band's below-SPA population share is drawn from its non-recipients, with any shortfall receipted. The 65–74 band edge is corrected so that 74-year-olds fall in band 6. Band carriers draw from their own SPI age band when that pool holds 20 records (composites never age-match), then narrow by region as before. A deterministic guard zeroes State Pension, Pension Credit and Winter Fuel reports below State Pension age: the SPI State Pension leaf before the stage-2 receipt bridge, the base channel's State Pension reports before stage 2 trains on them, and the SPI channel's three reports after stage 2. A receipt records the rows, weighted people and pounds removed at each step.
