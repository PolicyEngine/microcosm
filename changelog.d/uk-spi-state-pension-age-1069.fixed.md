Make the UK SPI income stage respect State Pension age (#1069). SPI donor ages are now drawn within each AGERANGE band by the ONS mid-2023 single-year populations of the donor's sex, vendored from the pinned Chronicle feed as `uk/ons_single_year_age_populations.json` and declared as a stage artifact. Donors receiving State Pension (`SRP > 0`) are drawn at or above the engine's State Pension age, and a band's below-SPA population share is drawn from its non-recipients, with any shortfall receipted. The 65–74 band edge is corrected so that 74-year-olds fall in band 6. The stage-1 forest is queried at the middle of each person's year of age (whole-year age plus half a year), because donors carry a fractional age: at the whole year a 66-year-old sat on the forest's split at State Pension age and drew a 65-year-old donor's zero State Pension leaf about half the time. Band carriers draw only donors on their own side of State Pension age, then from their own SPI age band when that pool holds 20 records (composites never age-match), then narrow by region as before. A deterministic guard zeroes State Pension, Pension Credit and Winter Fuel reports below State Pension age: the SPI State Pension leaf before the stage-2 receipt bridge, the base channel's State Pension reports before stage 2 trains on them, and the SPI channel's three reports after stage 2. A receipt records the rows, weighted people and pounds removed at each step.
