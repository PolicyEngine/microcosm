The UK release and the calibration engine no longer receive the Wealth and Assets Survey `property_wealth` total (microcosm#1106, the uk-data#543 defect). policyengine-uk derives `property_wealth` from `main_residence_value`, `other_residential_property_value` and `non_residential_property_value`; a persisted copy overrode that sum, has no uprating index (the published aa31bdf6 year files hold it flat from 2026 to 2030 while the sum grows 13%), and also counted owned land and the overseas and other-property remainder. The release export and the measure resolver's scratch H5 now drop it, and the resolver's receipt names what it left out; the spine keeps it for the WAS stage's own checks. It is a reviewed export exclusion, a declared engine-derived exclusion in the coverage ledger (144 required inputs and one reviewed exclusion), and the coverage gate now reads the frame the release writes.
