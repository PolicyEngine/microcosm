The UK spine's Universal Credit take-up redraw (`uc_take_up`, microcosm#1334) now fits young claimants by age (Y4 of the November event-data plan, microcosm#1095; María's call of 9 November 2026).

Y2's full-time education flag takes full-time students off Universal Credit, but the redraw refits each family type's award-band pools to DWP's caseloads without reading age. So it refilled the single caseload with other entitled singles. Claimants and partners aged 16 to 24 in Great Britain stayed well above DWP's count of people on Universal Credit at those ages, already at the spine's design weights.

Within each pool, the drawn units with a claimant or partner aged 16 to 24 (`is_claimant_or_partner`, at the stored age that policyengine-uk carries to the entitlement year) claim at one odds ratio against the pool's other drawn units, and the two rates realize the pool's count together. The ratio is solved over Great Britain, at the stage's household weights, so that those young claimants and partners in entitled units that claim come as near DWP's count as the redraw allows. That count is DWP Stat-Xplore's People on Universal Credit aged 16 to 24, every conditionality regime, with the single years summed in February, May, August and November 2025 and averaged: 762,585, the value the `dwp.uc.people_aged_16_to_24` diagnostic compiles. Where no ratio reaches DWP's count, the ratio holds at the nearer end of its range and the receipt says so.

A shortfall that a pool passes to its neighbours, where DWP's count exceeds every unit it entitles, now fills only the receiving pools' drawn units with no claimant or partner aged 16 to 24 (María's second call of 9 November 2026). On the November arm's spine the passed shortfalls had filled the single pools around the under-25 standard allowance to every drawn unit, so the young count could not fall to DWP's whatever the ratio. What no pool can take with older units stays unplaced, so the family type's caseload falls short of DWP's count by it. A pool's own count still draws on all its drawn units.

- The fit can't take young claimants below what fixed claims and pools' own counts leave: units that report Universal Credit, units moved off or kept on their legacy benefits, and pools whose own count needs more claims than their other drawn units can give keep young units claiming whatever the ratio. So the count can still stay above DWP's at the spine's design weights. The solve binds the 16 to 21 and 22 to 24 rows (María's call of 10 November 2026), and the 16 to 24 diagnostic stays a held-out check on their sum.
