The UK spine's Universal Credit take-up redraw (`uc_take_up`, microcosm#1334) now fits young claimants by age (Y4 of the November event-data plan, microcosm#1095; María's call of 9 November 2026).

Y2's full-time education flag takes full-time students off Universal Credit, but the redraw refits each family type's award-band pools to DWP's caseloads without reading age. So it refilled the single caseload with other entitled singles. Claimants and partners aged 16 to 24 in Great Britain stayed well above DWP's count of people on Universal Credit at those ages, already at the spine's design weights.

The redraw keeps every pool's count. Within each pool, the drawn units with a claimant or partner aged 16 to 24 (`is_claimant_or_partner`, at the stored age that policyengine-uk carries to the entitlement year) claim at one odds ratio against the pool's other drawn units, and the two rates realize the pool's count together. The ratio is solved over Great Britain, at the stage's household weights, so that those young claimants and partners in entitled units that claim come as near DWP's count as the redraw allows. That count is DWP Stat-Xplore's People on Universal Credit aged 16 to 24, every conditionality regime, with the single years summed in February, May, August and November 2025 and averaged: 762,585, the value the `dwp.uc.people_aged_16_to_24` diagnostic compiles. Where no ratio reaches DWP's count, the ratio holds at the nearer end of its range and the receipt says so.

- The facts are vendored from the pinned feed into `dwp_uc_people_by_age_facts.json`: Great Britain's rows of the four months' every-regime record sets, which the stage verifies against the pin.
- The stage receipt's `age_fit` block records the rule, DWP's count, the young claimants and partners before and after the fit, and the ratio, withheld where fewer than three source households carry the drawn young units.
- The manifest declares the new artifact, its verify operation and three new keys on `assign_award_band_take_up_residual` (`young_people`, `age_targets`, `age_fit`), which the sources schema now carries.
- No family type or award band count moves.
- The fit can't take young claimants below what fixed claims and full pools leave: units that report Universal Credit, units moved off or kept on their legacy benefits, and pools whose count needs more claims than their other drawn units can give keep young units claiming whatever the ratio. So the count can stay above DWP's, and the 16 to 24 diagnostic and its 16 to 21 and 22 to 24 parts stay held-out checks.
