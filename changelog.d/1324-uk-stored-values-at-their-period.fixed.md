UK calibration now measures a plain-column target's value at the binding's period (microcosm#1324). A value variable the frame stores holds the frame's own year (2024), so at the 2025 calibration period the measure resolver computes it through the engine, as it already did for every engine-computed variable; before, the stored column was read as it is and 76 £ rows compared 2024 values with 2025 targets. The engine values for stored names are injected under a reserved `engine_period__` name, so the stored columns that filters, conditions and bands read are never overwritten; those keep their stored reads, and a test lists the variables they read for review. A binding may declare the frame's period to read the stored column on purpose (ONS road fuel at calendar 2024 and the seven bus and rail outturn rows held without an index), and any other declared period refuses. The self-employment profit after loss relief reads the stored columns only at the frame's period.
