The national calibration binds HMRC's 2024-25 company cars (Benefits in kind statistics, July 2026), five rows in a new family, `hmrc_benefits_in_kind` (María's ruling of 3 November 2026): Table 1's car recipients (920,000) and car taxable value (£3,070m), and Table 2C's recipients by fuel, fully electric (465,000), petrol including hybrids (390,000, the engine's `PETROL` and `ALTERNATIVE_FUEL`) and diesel including hybrids (60,000). The counts count people with a company car list price, held at 2024-25 since no index projects them. The taxable value is the engine's company car benefit in 2024-25, through a new dated measure, `company_car_benefit_2024` (`measurement_period` 2024), since the calibration year's appropriate percentages are a point higher; the measure resolver refuses a binding that measures a dated car benefit at any other period, as it does a dated CGT measure. Two signed holds cover the rows in `uprating_holds.json`. The company car fact fence keeps the vendored facts out of the solve, as imputation seeds, and names the bound rows in a new `calibrated_facts` field.
