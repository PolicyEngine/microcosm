Stop the US PUF tax-detail imputation from predicting each income item from the recipient's own survey value of that item (#982). The PUF QRF now conditions on filing status, unit size, head age, spouse age, head sex, dependent count, the unit's weighted mid-rank income share within its own population, and whether the unit has earnings, so top-ranked survey households draw top-ranked PUF income vectors instead of copies of their topcoded survey income, and survey units without earnings rarely draw PUF earnings.
