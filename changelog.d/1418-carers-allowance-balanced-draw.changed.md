The Carer's Allowance take-up stage now balances its draws by weight within strata (#1418), as policyengine-uk's `stratified_uniform` does. The newly entitled carers' identity-keyed uniforms are placed within strata of route (in-household or not), the benefit unit's survey-year means-tested benefit receipt, and the survey-year weekly earnings band (one weekly rate wide above the limit, open above three). Each stratum then claims the take-up rate of its eligible weight to within one record, and the outside pool's draw is balanced the same way at its measured share. Independent draws reach the rate only in expectation: on the few source households over the limit, one draw had put the 2031 taper's cost well below its expectation. The rate, the pool and the caseload are unchanged. The receipt reports each stratum's realised share against its target, support-banded. Each stratum's placement is rotated by a start drawn per stratum (systematic sampling), keyed on its code and its lowest-draw member's identity, so a carer in a stratum of one or two records claims with probability equal to the rate rather than always or never, in any build, and a change of membership re-draws a stratum only when that member changes. The stage refuses a take-up or pool stratum whose drawn weight misses its share by more than its heaviest record, the most the rotated placement allows, and the receipt records the check (`strata_within_one_record`). The stage keys its cache on the shared balanced-draw helper too, so a change to the placement re-runs it on a resumed graph store.
