The Carer's Allowance take-up stage now balances its draws by weight within strata (#1418), as policyengine-uk's `stratified_uniform` does. The newly entitled carers' identity-keyed uniforms are placed within strata of route (in-household or not), the benefit unit's survey-year means-tested benefit receipt, and the survey-year weekly earnings band (one weekly rate wide above the limit, open above three). Each stratum then claims the take-up rate of its eligible weight to within one record, and the outside pool's draw is balanced the same way at its measured share. Independent draws reach the rate only in expectation: on the few source households over the limit, one draw had put the 2031 taper's cost well below its expectation. The rate, the pool and the caseload are unchanged. The receipt reports each stratum's realised share against its target, support-banded. The stage refuses a take-up or pool stratum whose drawn weight misses its share by more than one record, and the receipt records the check (`strata_within_one_record`).
