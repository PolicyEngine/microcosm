Add a spine and target diagnostic for any pooled build (`microcosm.calibrate.spine_target_shares`, `tools/spine_target_shares.py`): for each target, the share of its weighted total that each set of rows (spine) carries, against that spine's share of the weight among the records the target is about. It is report-only, reads a saved constraint matrix, weights and spine labels (or an ACS local checkpoint directory), runs no engine and no solve, and can withhold targets with few distinct carriers (microcosm#1729).
