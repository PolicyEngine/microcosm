UK firms gain region and legal status, and the policyengine-uk firm table gains the four inputs that depend on them (microcosm#1705). `uk_runtime/firm_attributes.py` draws `ons_region_code` given SIC division × turnover band (ONS UK Business 2025, Table 8) and `ons_legal_status` given industry group × turnover band (Table 13), with seeded categorical draws per cell after calibration, falling back from the cell to the row total and then to the UK total. The two draws are independent given their cells. Table 15 (region × legal status) is held out: on a thinned 2024-25 build, the weighted joint distribution is within 0.033 total variation of it. The tables are committed under `tests/fixtures/uk/firm_attributes_2025/` with workbook provenance, and `tools/extract_uk_firm_ons_attribute_tables.py --check` re-extracts them from the paper repository's workbook.

`to_policyengine_uk_firm_table` emits `firm_legal_form`, `firm_region`, `firm_in_england` and `firm_is_public_body` when both ONS columns are present, and the unchanged base table otherwise. It never falls back to policyengine-uk's defaults (`LONDON`, `COMPANY`). Two mappings are assumptions: ONS legal statuses 4–7 map to `OTHER`, which takes those bodies out of corporation tax in policyengine-uk, and statuses 5 and 6 (central government, local authority) set `firm_is_public_body`. `UK_FIRM_POLICYENGINE_UK_INPUT_STATUS` records every one of policyengine-uk's 52 formula-less firm inputs as set, derived, assumed or not set; paybill, profit, capital expenditure, R&D, rateable value and the remaining flags stay unset, with no placeholder values, until sources arrive. The validator now refuses missing values in every column.

A committed snapshot of policyengine-uk's firm variables (`tests/fixtures/uk/policyengine_uk_firm_inputs.json`, from PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2940, refreshed with `tools/snapshot_policyengine_uk_firm_inputs.py`, which reads the source with `ast`) lets an engine-free test check that every exported column is a policyengine-uk firm input and that every enum value is a member.

`uk_runtime/firm_target_candidates.py` declares value-free M2 target candidates: four ONS UK Business tables available as fixtures, and DBT business population estimates, ONS ABS, HMRC corporation tax, capital allowances, R&D and PAYE RTI statistics, and MHCLG/VOA business rates pending Ledger sources. Each candidate names the policyengine-uk inputs it would inform. None is wired into the solver, and `uk/uk_firms_targets.json` is unchanged.
