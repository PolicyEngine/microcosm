UK firms gain region and legal status, and the policyengine-uk firm table gains the five inputs that depend on them (microcosm#1705). `uk_runtime/firm_attributes.py` draws `ons_region_code` given SIC division × turnover band (ONS UK Business 2025, Table 8) and `ons_legal_status` given industry group × turnover band (Table 13), with seeded categorical draws per cell after calibration, falling back from the cell to the row total and then to the UK total. Legal status is also weighted by the employment band through Table 12 (see `1705-uk-firm-legal-status-employment.changed.md`). The two draws are independent given their cells. Table 15 (region × legal status) is held out: on the 20-iteration thinned 2024-25 test build (seed 42), the weighted in-frame joint distribution is 0.033 in total variation from it (0.035 once legal status is coupled to the employment band through Table 12). The tables are committed under `tests/fixtures/uk/firm_attributes_2025/` with workbook provenance, and `tools/extract_uk_firm_ons_attribute_tables.py --check` re-extracts them from the paper repository's workbook.

`to_policyengine_uk_firm_table` emits `firm_legal_form`, `firm_region`, `firm_in_england`, `firm_is_public_body` and `firm_is_charity` when both ONS columns are present, and the unchanged base table otherwise. It never falls back to policyengine-uk's defaults (`LONDON`, `COMPANY`). The legal-status mapping is in `1705-uk-firm-legal-status-mapping.changed.md`: statuses 5–7 map to `OTHER` (outside corporation tax), status 4 to `COMPANY`, statuses 4–6 set `firm_is_public_body` and status 7 sets `firm_is_charity`, all assumptions. `firm_in_england`, which policyengine-uk defines by the firm's property, comes from the enterprise's region. `UK_FIRM_POLICYENGINE_UK_INPUT_STATUS` records every one of policyengine-uk's formula-less firm inputs (71 at `0d1c25010`) as set, derived, assumed or not set; paybill, profit, capital expenditure, R&D, rateable value and the remaining flags stay unset, with no placeholder values, until sources arrive. The validator now refuses missing values in every column.

A committed snapshot of policyengine-uk's firm variables (`tests/fixtures/uk/policyengine_uk_firm_inputs.json`, from PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2940, refreshed with `tools/snapshot_policyengine_uk_firm_inputs.py`, which reads the source with `ast` and refuses a dirty checkout unless `--allow-dirty` is passed) lets an engine-free test check that every exported column is a policyengine-uk firm input and that every enum value is a member.

`uk_runtime/firm_target_candidates.py` declares value-free M2 target candidates: three ONS UK Business tables available as fixtures, and ONS UK Business Table 3, DBT business population estimates, ONS ABS, HMRC corporation tax (capital allowances included), R&D and PAYE RTI statistics, and MHCLG/VOA business rates pending Ledger sources. Each candidate names the policyengine-uk inputs it would inform. None is wired into the solver, and `uk/uk_firms_targets.json` is unchanged.
