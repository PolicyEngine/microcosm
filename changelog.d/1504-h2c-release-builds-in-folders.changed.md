Builds of the UK national, dense and local-area releases publish into their own folders through the emitter. These releases (`microcosm.data.contract.publishes_to_build_folder`) hand each build to the separately installed `microcosm-emitter` with the interface's `publish_release` call, and its release writer puts the build in `releases/<release>/<build>/` and records it in the repository's `releases.json` in one commit; Microcosm no longer commits, tags or writes `latest*.json` pointer files for them. The new `microcosm-publish-release-build` command (`microcosm.build.release_publication`, in `microcosm-build`) checks the build with the release contract, hands every file over with its role and SHA-256, refuses before it starts an emitter when `HF_HUB_OFFLINE` or `TRANSFORMERS_OFFLINE` is set, hands nothing over unless the emitter's write-access check passes, publishes with or without `--make-current`, makes a published build current with `--promote`, and succeeds only when the emitter's receipt is `published` and, when current was asked for, names the build as `current`. The build is the calibration attempt ID: both manifests name `release` and `build` as strings, the release manifest records `built_at`, `built_with_model_package` and `built_with_core_package` at its top level and a `role` on each artifact (`dataset`, `calibration_weights`, `calibration_diagnostics`, otherwise the artifact's key), and no artifact pins a `revision`. The fields `build_id`, `attempt_id` and `cut_tag` are gone from their manifests, and the assemblers' `--cut-tag` option with them. Each release's role in `releases.json` is `national` or `local_area`, from its `dataset_role`. Both UK assemblers print a publication command and a separate promotion command: while the repository still has its `latest*.json` pointer files the emitter refuses every change to a current build, so each new dense build is promoted once those files are deleted, and can be published with `--make-current` after that. The release contract checks this layout for those releases and refuses the tagged one. `microcosm.data.load` reads the current build of the UK 2025 national and dense releases from `releases.json` (or the listed build `build=` names, a withdrawn one with a warning), then its manifest and dataset from the build's folder; `unverified_root=True` is refused for such a release, and the loader no longer follows `latest-<line>.json` pointers. `microcosm-publish-release` and `tools/publish_release.sh` keep publishing every US release, the June 2023 UK release and `populace-uk-<year>-<tier>-k<N>` releases with tags, `latest.json` and their Slack alerts, refuse the build-folder releases, and lose `--promote-line`. Publications into build folders send no Slack alert until the dashboard posts release alerts from `releases.json`. The stable releases proposed for US builds, `populace-us-2024-national` (`national`, the default) and `populace-us-2024-local` (`local_area`), are recorded in `microcosm.data.contract.PROPOSED_US_RELEASES` but not used until they are agreed with the US data builder.
