The US share mechanisms of the ACS local release run on the shared cross-grain operator that the UK uses (`microcosm.build.cross_grain`), with no target change (microcosm#1760, Stage A). `CrossGrainRule` takes an optional `allocator`: with none, the operator runs its standing rule with no float operation added or removed, so the UK outputs are the same; the UK graph hashes the file, so UK target nodes and the nodes after them compute again on the next UK run. The new shared `microcosm.build.cross_grain_controls` holds the allocators, derived controls, factor band, completeness and self-consistency helpers. `us/cross_grain_declarations.json` declares the US levels, the four mechanisms (district SOI shares, county population, county SOI shares, county households) with their registers, and an inventory of each family that two levels measure; `us_runtime.target_nesting` is the adapter. `state_cd_soi_surface`, `with_county_populations`, `state_county_soi_surface` and `acs_household_surface` keep their names and signatures. Every surface on the pinned feed and ladder has the bytes it had. The materialize stage also writes the operator's own receipts to `target_nesting.json`. See `docs/us-target-nesting.md`.
