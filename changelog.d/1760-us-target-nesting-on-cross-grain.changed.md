The US nests its local target rows on the shared cross-grain operator that the UK uses (`microcosm.build.cross_grain`), with no target change (microcosm#1760). The operator gains general options that the UK does not declare, so the UK outputs are the same; the UK graph hashes the file, so UK target nodes and the nodes after them compute again on the next UK run. The options: a bound of a bridge measured from the median factor, one-sided, that can unbind a leg; the declared members of a leg; bridge selectors; an optional `allocator`; and `cross_grain_metadata`, the keys of a reconciled row. The shared `microcosm.build.cross_grain_controls` holds the row-level pass (`reconcile_rows`) and the allocators that keep the US values bit for bit until the feed re-pin. `us/cross_grain_declarations.json` declares the US choices under the UK's key names (bridge selectors, a derived control, relations, local routes, members). `state_cd_soi_surface`, `with_county_populations`, `state_county_soi_surface` and `acs_household_surface` keep their names and signatures, and each target value on the pinned feed and ladder is the value it was. **The metadata keys change**: district and county rows carry `cross_grain_parent`, `cross_grain_value_before`, `cross_grain_factor`, `cross_grain_control` and `cross_grain_members` in place of the `state_cd_*` and `state_county_*` keys, and the district receipt takes the fields `bridge`, `level_bridge_out_of_band` and `factor_by_measure`. So the bytes of the `state_cd` registry, roles and receipts move. A refusal that the operator makes has the operator's text; the check that the district file agrees with itself is gone (the compile holds that sum); a concept with two parent sources is refused. The county SOI bridge declares the bound of the district bridge (1.25 around the median factor across states, unbind): a state outside it loses its county rows of that measure and the receipt names it; no state is outside it on the pinned county facts, so no county value changes. The materialize stage also writes the operator's receipts to `target_nesting.json`. See `docs/us-target-nesting.md`.
