The ACS local release takes `--county-target-loss-cap-scope`. `run` (default) applies the county profile's loss cap to every row of a county build, as before. `county_rows` applies it to the county rows only, so the state and district rows keep `--target-loss-cap` when county rows join the solve. The caps are declared by level through the shared `target_loss_caps`, and the solver gets the equivalent one-cap loss, so its cap stays one number (microcosm#1729).
