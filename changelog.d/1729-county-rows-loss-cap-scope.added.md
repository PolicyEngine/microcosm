The ACS local release takes `--county-target-loss-cap-scope`. `run` (default) applies the county profile's loss cap to every row of a county build, as before. `county_rows` applies it to the county rows only, so the state and district rows keep `--target-loss-cap` when county rows join the solve. The caps are declared by level through the shared `target_loss_caps`, and the solver gets the equivalent one-cap loss, so its cap stays one number. Under `county_rows` the summary and `calibration_diagnostics.json` carry a `target_loss_caps` block: the declared caps and the past-cap census against each row's own cap (`row_cap_census`), because the solver's own census reads every row against the equivalent cap (microcosm#1729).
