A Ledger target reference can declare an `observation_period`, the period its facts are selected at when that differs from the target period the model is measured at. Selection, the latest-period choice and the resolved-fact guard read it, the target keeps its own period, and the compiled metadata records it. Operations that compose periods of their own refuse it (microcosm#1095 D5). A restamp to another target period moves a declared observation period by the same number of years (`restamp_reference_period`), reading its spelling as the reference's match policy does: under the selector's `period_type` for an `exact` match, and untyped for the default `latest_not_after` cutoff. An exact period the selector's type cannot read is refused when the reference is built. A spelling read two ways has no one spelling a year later, so it refuses to move: an untyped exact period that reads as both a month and a fiscal year ("2011-12"), and a cutoff that the selector's type reads otherwise. A row whose observation period cannot move stops the compile, naming the row. The authoring tools move a contract's observation period from the period it is written for (`observation_period_base`; for the UK, `UK_OBSERVATION_PERIOD_BASE`, 2025, kept apart from the generators' default period), so authoring the DfE rows at another period reads the January census after that model year.
