A Ledger target reference can declare an `observation_period`, the period its facts are selected at when that differs from the target period the model is measured at. Selection, the latest-period choice and the resolved-fact guard read it, the target keeps its own period, and the compiled metadata records it. Operations that compose periods of their own refuse it (microcosm#1095 D5). A restamp to another target period moves a declared observation period by the same number of years (`restamp_reference_period`), classified as the ledger's own period parser reads its spelling, and a row whose observation period cannot move stops the compile, naming the row. The authoring tools move a contract's observation period from the period it is written for (`observation_period_base`; `UK_TARGET_REFERENCE_PERIOD`, 2025, for the UK), so authoring the DfE rows at another period reads the January census after that model year.
