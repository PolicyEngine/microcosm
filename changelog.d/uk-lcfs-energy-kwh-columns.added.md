The `lcfs_consumption` stage stores each household's annual electricity and gas kWh for calendar 2024 (`electricity_kwh`, `gas_kwh`) beside the spend the energy rake prices them to, as candidate-only export columns for the energy dashboards. The local DESNZ targets' conversion of the stored spend gives back the same kWh for every household. Electricity sits at the level ONS household spending buys, and gas at DESNZ Energy Trends' domestic volume. The columns are not policyengine-uk's monthly `electricity_consumption_kwh` and `gas_consumption_kwh` inputs, and the engine reads neither. No draw or target changes.
