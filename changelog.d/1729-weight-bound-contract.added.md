State one contract for the solver's weight bound (`microcosm.calibrate.weight_bound`, docs/calibration-local-area-machinery.md): `max_weight_ratio` bounds each record against its weight on the frame the solve was given, never against a warm start, and a new frame is a new reference. Tests hold the solver to it. `weight_bound_reference` declares another reference (the mean weight, or the mean weight of the record's area) and `weight_bound_report` measures any weight vector against it, report-only; the solver still enforces the bound against the frame's weights only (microcosm#1729, microcosm#493).
