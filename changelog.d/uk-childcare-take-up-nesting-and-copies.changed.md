The UK childcare take-up draws now nest the universal 15 hours under the working-parent entitlement, re-draw the copies' childcare take-up, and fit and calibrate the DfE childcare rows without children in reception. `frs_take_up` keeps the extended draw unchanged and draws `would_claim_universal_childcare` as `would_claim_extended_childcare` OR u < the contract's `universal_childcare` rate, which is now the rate among benefit units not claiming the extended hours (the operation declares `nested_under`, and the stage refuses a manifest that drops it). Independent draws let a family over the £100k limit hold extended-yes and universal-no, and policyengine-uk then allocated its child's first 15 hours to the universal entitlement only once the limit was removed. The take-up signal gate targets the nested marginal, r_e + (1 - r_e) * r_u, and fails on any benefit unit claiming the extended hours without the universal ones. A new spine stage, `childcare_copy_take_up`, which runs after every copy stage and before `private_school_attendance` (whose net-income ranking counts the childcare entitlements), re-draws `would_claim_tfc`, the extended, universal (nested) and targeted flags and `maximum_extended_childcare_hours_usage` for the SPI support, high-value home support and CGT copies on their own `benunit_id`, through `frs_take_up`'s helper at the same rates, seed and salts, so a copy no longer inherits its host's draws; native units keep theirs, and the anchored flags and `would_claim_uc_childcare` are untouched. The roster moves from 46 to 47 stages. `tools/fit_uk_childcare_takeup.py` (tool version 5) fits the universal rate on the conditional basis, E = r_e * sum(with extended) + (1 - r_e) * r_u * sum(without), with no allocated term, and leaves children in primary education out of the working-parent and universal-only rows, as DfE's January registrations leave out reception; the two calibration bindings carry the same `current_education != PRIMARY` filter. The contract's numeric rates are unchanged until the fitter refits them.
