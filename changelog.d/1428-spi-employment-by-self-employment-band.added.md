The national calibration binds SPI 2023-24 Table 3.10's individuals with employment income by self-employment band, `hmrc.spi.employment_income.count_by_self_employment_income_band`, over the eight bands from £1 to £10,000 that the self-employment counts and amounts already bind (#1418; María's choice of 2026-10-30). With the self-employment counts alone, the solve could meet each band with traders who have no pay, while in Table 3.10 more than half of the traders below £3,000 also have employment income; an employee's side profit is taxed at their marginal rate, so the mix of small traders decides what the Budget's trading allowance measures cost. Each person with pay over the year (`employment_income_annual`, the variable Table 3.6's employment rows read) counts in their own self-employment band, taxpayer or not. Table 3.10's column is wider, income assessed under ITEPA 2003 plus other miscellaneous income, so like Table 3.6's employment rows the binding leaves out miscellaneous income and the contributory benefits; benefits in kind count on SPI rows, whose pay is the tape's PAY + EPB + TAXTERM, and not on FRS rows. Counts grow to 2025 with HMRC's projected growth of all taxpayers (Income Tax liabilities statistics Table 2.5), as the self-employment counts do, and the zero band and the bands from £10,000 are signed out row by row.
