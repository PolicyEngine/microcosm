On a county SOI surface, minimum county support in the ACS local release also gives every county's SOI rows support (microcosm#1661). Materialize computes each household's AGI as the state SOI AGI row stores it, one 100,000-household chunk at a time, before the support step. The step then moves `--county-min-support` same-PUMA households with a positive AGI into any county whose households all store an AGI of 0, whose county AGI row would otherwise be empty and refused after the engine pass. The counties it fills first get households with a positive AGI first, and no donor county is left without a household with a nonzero AGI. A county that still has none is refused before the engine pass. The moves are recorded in `county_support.json` (`minimum_support.soi_support`, moves with `"reason": "soi_support"`) and replay as before. `tools/check_us_acs_county_agi_support.py` applies the same moves.
