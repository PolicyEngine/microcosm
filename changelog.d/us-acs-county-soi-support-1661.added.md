On a county SOI surface, minimum county support in the ACS local release also gives every county's SOI rows support (microcosm#1661). Materialize computes each household's AGI as the state SOI AGI row stores it, one 100,000-household chunk at a time, before the support step. The step then moves `--county-min-support` same-PUMA households with a positive AGI into any county whose households all store an AGI of 0, whose county AGI row would otherwise be empty and refused after the engine pass. A county it fills because it has no household gets one with a positive AGI: when none of its K picks in rank order has one, the K-th gives way to the next household in rank order that has one (`"reason": "positive_agi"`), so the other picks keep their PUMA's AGI mix, and `minimum_support.soi_support.first_pass` reports the movers' share with a positive AGI beside their PUMAs' share. No donor county is left without a household with a nonzero AGI. A county that still has none is refused before the engine pass. The moves are recorded in `county_support.json` (`minimum_support.soi_support`, moves with `"reason": "soi_support"`) and replay as before. `tools/check_us_acs_county_agi_support.py` applies the same moves.
