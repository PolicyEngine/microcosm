The UK LCFS stage splits the diary's fuel, light and power (p537) into electricity and gas by its labelled parts, from the LCF User Guide's derived-variable labels and P537 definition (microcosm#1164). The derivation it replaces, ported from policyengine-uk-data, read b226 as electricity paid by direct debit and b489 and b490 as prepayment-meter payments. The labels make b226 the gas component of a combined utilities bill (b227 is the electricity one) and b489 and b490 the electricity and gas account payments less rebates (b175 less b178, b170 less b173), so it gave combined-bill households, most of the donor, their gas as electricity; took gas away from electricity for households with two accounts; split an electricity account between the fuels; and folded oil and solid fuels into the drawn energy. Each fuel is now its account payment less its rebate, its prepayment key or meter payment, its part of a combined bill or meter payment, and the diary's account and slot-meter lines; a combined payment left unsplit goes by the household's own electricity share. Oil, bottled gas, solid fuels and heat stay in the housing total. A `donor_energy_split` receipt records the donor's electricity, gas and other fuels against p537. Negative amounts (refunds) are read as zero. The NEED rake and the DESNZ level still set each fuel's level, but which households carry electricity and which gas now follows what they reported.
