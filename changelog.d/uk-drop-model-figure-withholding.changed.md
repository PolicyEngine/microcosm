A target row resting on fewer than ten source households, and a row that would give one back by difference, keep their estimates and errors in the calibration diagnostics, `targets.csv`, the gate reports and the run's evidence summaries; the fit gates judge every row; totals, losses and the size receipt are the solve's own; staged epochs carry their loss; and BRMA rows no longer carry a carrier verdict. This removes the differencing check, which ran for hours on the dense surface, and `--withhold-thin-rows-only`. Release certification refuses a run built while rows were withheld, and the release contract still checks the withheld rows of earlier releases.
