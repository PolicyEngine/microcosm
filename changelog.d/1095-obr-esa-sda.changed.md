`obr.esa` binds every benefit on OBR's "Incapacity benefits" line that policyengine-uk carries: `esa_income + esa_contrib + incapacity_benefit + sda` (uk-data#582, microcosm#1095). OBR table 4.9 footnote 2 says the line includes Incapacity Benefit, ESA, Severe Disablement Allowance and the incapacity part of Income Support, and excludes the health element of Universal Credit. The engine pays Income Support as one benefit, so its incapacity part stays a residual of the row with no declared difference (María's ruling of 2026-10-16). Severe Disablement Allowance is a small part of the line: DWP reports GBP 53.8m for England and Wales in 2024-25, against OBR's GBP 12.41bn. The Scottish Government pays the Scottish part, GBP 5.0m in 2024-25, outside DWP's line; at well under 0.1 percent of the row it stays inside the row's Great Britain scope, with no per-term mask.
