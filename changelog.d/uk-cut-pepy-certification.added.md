`tools/certify_uk_cut_in_policyengine_py.py` certifies a published UK cut into policyengine.py on a fresh branch: it refuses when another pull request is already certifying the cut, checks the regional route before writing anything, runs certify-data, the lock, TRO, constant and snapshot regeneration and the strict bundle check, measures the UC floor and runs the tests, then drafts the pull request with every judgment call left as a checklist.
