The UK build now writes `cares_for_severely_disabled_person`, the input policyengine-uk reads from 2.177.3 wherever a carer's hours reach Carer's Allowance's 35-hour line, from the FRS care tab (A3 of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095; María's choices of 2026-11-10). `frs_spine` reads the care tab into each carer's main care recipients, the people given their largest weekly band, as a stage-internal carrier the release drops. `frs_disability` sets the link true where those recipients include a household member with Attendance Allowance, DLA or Child Disability Payment care at the middle or highest rate, or PIP or Adult Disability Payment daily living, and false where they include only members without one. It draws a carer of someone outside the household, with no qualifying member, at the share of in-household carers whose recipient qualifies (María's decision (a) of 2026-10-28). A carer with care hours and no link in the care tab keeps the engine's default, true. The SPI channel takes the link again on its households from the benefits it re-imputes, and `carers_allowance_take_up` reads it for its pool in place of its own draw, so the claims the stage opens and the Carer's Allowance and Carer Support Payment the engine pays follow one rule. The engine default the build signed for the input is retired, and the release exports the column.
