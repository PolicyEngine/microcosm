The UK build now writes `cares_for_severely_disabled_person`, the input policyengine-uk reads from 2.177.3 wherever a carer's hours reach Carer's Allowance's 35-hour line, from the FRS care tab (A3 of the November event-data plan's PR3, microcosm#1095; María's choices of 2026-11-10). `frs_spine` reads the care tab into the people each carer gives at least 35 hours a week, by the band the tab gives each person cared for, as a stage-internal carrier the release drops. The line is per person: the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976 reg. 4(1) need 35 hours a week caring for the severely disabled person, and reg. 4(1A) counts care for two or more people only where one of them gets 35 hours on their own; UC Regs 2013 reg. 30(1)(a) and SSI 2023/302 reg. 5(2) read the same conditions into the carer element and Carer Support Payment (Vahid's review of microcosm#1583, Should 1; María's decision of 2026-11-12). `frs_disability` sets the link true where those people include a household member with Attendance Allowance, DLA or Child Disability Payment care at the middle or highest rate, or PIP or Adult Disability Payment daily living, and false where they include only members without one, or nobody, as for care spread over several people. It draws a carer who gives someone outside the household the line, with no qualifying member, at the share of carers giving only household members the line whose recipient qualifies (María's decision (a) of 2026-10-28). It refuses an engine minimum other than 35 hours, which the care tab's bands mark exactly. A carer with care hours and no link in the care tab keeps the engine's default, true. The SPI channel takes the link again on its households from the benefits it re-imputes, and `carers_allowance_take_up` reads it for its pool in place of its own draw (its restated notes signed by María on 2026-11-11), so the claims the stage opens and the Carer's Allowance and Carer Support Payment the engine pays follow one rule. The engine default the build signed for the input is retired, and the release exports the column.

Who each carer cares for stays inside the build: the release drops `frs_care_recipients`. So policyengine-uk's `is_cared_for_by_carer_benefit_recipient` still guesses the legacy severe disability premium's carer condition (IS Regs 1987 Sch 2 para 13(2)(a)(iii), HB Regs 2006 Sch 3 para 14(2)(a)(iii)), giving a carer to a claimant or partner in their own benefit unit first. With the carers of someone outside the household that A3 draws, that guess can take the premium off a member whom the care tab says nobody cares for.
