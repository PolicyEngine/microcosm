The ACS local release takes `--weight-bound-reference`. `own` (default) bounds each household at `--max-weight-ratio` times its own starting weight, as before. `mean`, `state_mean` and `district_mean` bound it at `--max-weight-ratio` times one shared weight (the mean starting weight overall, or of its state or district), through the solver's `max_weight_reference`, so a donor row gets no larger a bound than the ACS rows beside it. The default adds no keyword to the solve and no key to the solver stamp (microcosm#1729).
