The UK `nts_bus_travel` stage bands recipient households on gross cash income, the concept of the NTS household income band, rather than the engine's `household_gross_income` (microcosm#1164). The engine's figure adds capital gains and the imputed value of NHS, education and transport spending, which put about a fifth of households (pensioners, families with school-age children and capital-gains households above all) a band higher than their cash income, and the NTS shows bus use falling with income. The recipient's band income is now its HBAI net income with policyengine-uk's HBAI deductions added back, the same `uk_runtime.household_income` helper the LCFS NEED band uses, and the stage declares those engine variables. Bus-use bands, trips and the bus fares and bus support the LCFS and ETB services stages price from them move.
