The LCFS stage now draws household spending on liquid fuels (central-heating oil, paraffin and bottled gas, COICOP 04.5.3) and solid fuels (coal, coke, wood and peat, 04.5.4) into `liquid_fuel_consumption` and `solid_fuel_consumption`, the inputs policyengine-uk 2.149.0 reads (pe-uk#2572; microcosm#1278; María's ruling of 2026-10-22: LPG with liquid, heat in the remainder). They are the last chain targets, so every earlier draw is unchanged; they are uprated to calendar-2024 prices as every diary spend column is; and the housing total nets their draws and adds them back, so it always holds them, while hot water, steam and ice stay in its remainder. The engine counts them inside housing, so `consumption` does not move. The recomposed-totals receipt's `uncarried` block becomes `published_fuels`: ONS 04.5.3 and 04.5.4 beside each fuel's donor total at diary prices and the frame's draw, which the stage-time gate recomputes from the vendored rows. COICOP puts liquefied hydrocarbons under 04.5.2 (gas), so ONS's 04.5.3 leaves out the bottled gas the liquid column holds, and the receipt gives the donor's liquid fuels without it too (Vahid's review of microcosm#1383, item 8). Both columns stay at the diary's level, about twice ONS's, unlevelled where electricity and gas are levelled: no engine formula reads them yet, but a costing on them would come out near twice ONS's spend. `ons.household_energy_expenditure`, which the contract keeps for registry parity and the reference surface signs out as superseded by its fuel rows, is measured on electricity, gas, liquid and solid fuel spend, and its signed rationale no longer says the frame lacks the last two. The consumption drift block reports the two fuels where the frame carries them, and `uk_consumption_drift_flags` gives warn-only flags at #1278's R1 thresholds: a total moving more than 2 percent, or a share more than half a point, between the design and the final weights.
