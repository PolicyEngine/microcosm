The Route A driver's block ladder pins are now settings, as #1650 made the feed's: `LADDER_SHA` and `LADDER_BYTES` default to the published run's d713 ladder, so a build with another ladder sets `LADDER`, `LADDER_SHA` and `LADDER_BYTES` together. Under `LOCATION_RULE=block_v1`, `verify_inputs` also lists the ladder's arrays with `PYTHON` and records an input problem when the ladder lacks the per-block `puma` array that `load_us_location_ladder` requires, or cannot be read. `tools/build_us_block_ladder_artifact.py` on main writes that array.
