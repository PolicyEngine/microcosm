The clone-first UK spine's area, local-authority, BRMA, fuel-relief, SPI-copy area and private-rent transforms (#931), unwired: no stage declares them and no node binds the three new modules, so no output moves. Each transform checks the stage it runs as against the reviewed declaration its module states (name, household grain, outputs and rewrites, the reviewed operations verbatim and its artifacts, each with `runtime_sha256_required`), which the geography declarations will carry.

- **The conditioned area draw** (`microcosm.build.uk_runtime.atomic_area_conditioning`).
  - The definition over the three conditioned supports, held to canonical bytes, keeps the FRS region as a required constraint and the IMD decile as an optional one. England and Wales and Scotland sample each household by the area's households in its observed council-tax band, or by all the area's households where it has none; Northern Ireland, which has domestic rates, samples by households. The layers are the ten ladder columns, and the stream is `uk-spine-atomic-area-v1` with seed 0.
  - The fallback ladder runs from L0 (decile and band) to L3 (region and households); Northern Ireland takes L1 and L3.
  - The draw reads the decile as "1" to "10" (missing for 0) and the band only where `council_tax_band_observed` is true. A row the SPI support channel copied reads neither, so it draws at L3.
  - The shared chooser picks each household's level with the definition's own rule and default.
- **The area stages** (`microcosm.build.uk_runtime.spine_geography`).
  - G2, `UKAtomicAreaAssignStageTransform` (`atomic_area_assign`), draws, derives and validates every household's area on one narrow frame and writes the three assignment and ten ladder columns as `StringDtype("python")`. A validation that fails refuses the stage.
  - G3, `UKLocalAuthorityStageTransform` (`local_authority`), resolves `local_authority` from `local_authority_code` through the committed names roster, never defaulted, as `StringDtype("python")`.
  - G4, `UKAreaBRMAStageTransform` (`area_brma`, to take `frs_brma`'s slot), makes one engine call over the whole frame for `LHA_category`. Great Britain reads `brma` off the drawn LSOA (England and Wales) or output area (Scotland) through the committed area-to-BRMA layer. Northern Ireland draws from the census counts with `frs_brma`'s keys, seed and salts, so its values are `frs_brma`'s. `brma` stays an object column, and a frame G4 cannot place is refused before the engine call.
  - G5, `UKRFDRStageTransform` (`rfdr`), sets `in_rural_fuel_duty_relief_area` from the drawn output area through the committed RFDR layer. It refuses a layer that flags an output area its system's support does not hold, and reports a system with no flagged household instead of refusing.
  - `UKSPICopyAreasStageTransform` (`spi_copy_areas`, to run after `spi_income_band_donors`) gives every SPI copy and income band donor a geography key over its own copy path, an area drawn at L3 with G2's definition, and then its own local authority, BRMA and flag. As María ruled on 11 Nov (C-9), Northern Ireland's copies draw their BRMA from the census counts on their own benefit units, with G4's seed and salts, after one engine call over their households alone. That call carries no clone index, so it is one simulation over every clone's copies, as at one clone: the UK adapter refuses a call over part of its pool, which a clone without a Northern Ireland copy would otherwise make it. Great Britain copies' areas are checked against the layer before the call. Each rewrite keeps its column's dtype, and every other row is left as it was.
- **Private rents** (`microcosm.build.uk_runtime.spine_private_rent`). G7, `UKPrivateRentStageTransform` (`private_rent`, to run after `spi_housing_shell`), re-draws each Great Britain private renter's rent with `private_rent_localisation.localise_rents`, every row its own spine household keyed on its `geography_household_key`. Tie-breaks are keyed on `(key, "rent_rank")` on the stream `("sha256-u53-v1", "uk-spine-private-rent-v1", 0, 0)`, so tied renters and the clones of one renter take ranks of their own and clone 0's tie-breaks are the same at every K. The sampled rung's thin-region rule counts true source households, and the stage is strict unless the spine is sampled. Only `rent` is rewritten: reported Housing Benefit, Universal Credit and mortgages, the weights and every other column are unchanged, and the stage records its household mass receipt.
- **Evidence.** Every count is distinct true source households through `rounded_count`, never per area or per cell, and no key or code of a household is printed. G2 (by system, level and fallback reason), G3 and G4 (by grain, and Northern Ireland's sources drawn) count over every clone. G5's flagged sources, the copy stage's copies, donors and flagged copies, and G7's re-drawn sources count among clone-0 rows; G7 also gives the cells ranked against their region or against Great Britain. The copy stage's evidence says its validation and roster check did not run on a frame without a copy.
- **Changed modules.**
  - `frs_brma.draw_household_brmas` draws the BRMAs of any subset of households with today's keys; `add_frs_brma` calls it and its values are unchanged. Its refusals no longer print household or benefit-unit ids.
  - `rfdr_area_layer.area_flags` gains `roster`. Its malformed-code refusal no longer prints a count or a code, and its unknown-system refusal names the accepted systems instead of the frame's values.
  - The BRMA and RFDR layer docstrings describe the spine's stages.
- **Keys.** The stage kernels that bind `frs_brma` (`frs_brma`, `was_wealth`, `was_lisa`, `uc_take_up`, `uc_deduction_attributes` and `childcare_copy_take_up`) re-key with it, and so do the dense line's geography BRMA and RFDR nodes (`uk.full.geography.brma@1` and `uk.full.geography.rfdr@1`, after the layer modules' edits), with every node downstream of them. No transport key (NZ, BE, AM), US key or battery phase moves, and no node hashes the three new modules. No declaration, generated file or `gates.json` changes.
- **Values.** None moves. On the synthetic H2 fixture, the legacy oracle's final frame (every table, column and dtype, the weights, the strata and the mass log), its stage records and every stage's evidence digest are identical before and after this change, and the fixture regenerates byte-identically.
- **Tests**, on toy conditioned supports whose public area codes resolve against the committed resources (`test_support/microcosm_build/uk_conditioned_support_fixtures.py`), invented households, a recording stand-in for the rules engine and a stand-in for the support channel's two stages:
  - each household's level and region, the decile read as "1" to "10", and the chooser called with the definition's own rule, default and ladder;
  - clone 0's areas at K = 1, 2 and 3, and its copy keys, areas and BRMAs at K = 2 and 3, equal to K = 1's, and row-order independence;
  - G2's and the copy stage's validation refusing a ladder the mapping does not give, and the written dtypes;
  - Great Britain's BRMAs against the layer, Northern Ireland's against `frs_brma`, and `frs_brma`'s household pick written out;
  - the copy stage's own keys and Northern Ireland draws, and its engine call as one simulation under the UK adapter's clone rule at K = 2 and 3 with Northern Ireland copies in clone 0 alone;
  - G7 against `localise_rents` on a copy-free frame;
  - count-free refusals, the frame's before any engine call;
  - every evidence count byte-equal at K = 1 and K = 3.
