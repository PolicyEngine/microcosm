The gambling participation gate (`uk_stage_lcfs_consumption_gambling_participation`) lets the stake scale fall to 0.3 instead of 0.5 (María's ruling of 2026-11-03, microcosm#1359). The scale is what the stage multiplies the added regular players' drawn stakes by to bring their mean to the seen players'. With the LCFS chain's sign gates cross-fitted, the chain draws betting and gaming for more of the diary's households, at about the donors' own stake per player. So the seen players' mean falls, and with it the scale, from 0.63 to about 0.41, while the stake draws themselves do not move. Incidence and the duty bases are unchanged: every key still reaches its GSGB participation, and every base its published level. A draw that puts the added players' mean more than about 3.3 times the seen players' still fails, as one under 0.4 times does.
