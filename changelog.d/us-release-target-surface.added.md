`tools/build_us_fiscal_refresh_release.py --target-surface national_state` calibrates the national release to national and state targets only, dropping every congressional-district-classified target (CD geography rows and rows sourced from the SOI congressional-district file) after the target-parity and profile-coverage gates have run on the full compiled surface. On the pinned feed it keeps 5,719 of 32,867 compiled targets (5,268 state, 451 national). Both manifests record the selection; the default (`full`) is unchanged and its manifests carry no new key.
