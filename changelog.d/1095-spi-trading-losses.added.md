The SPI income stage draws `trading_loss` for the rows it redraws, as its FRS-only stage 2 draws the other inputs it reads no tape value for: the loss comes from the FRS rows, conditioned on the drawn incomes (uk-data#528, microcosm#1095). A redrawn row whose drawn `self_employment_income` is positive keeps no loss, so profit and loss never both hold an amount, as on the FRS rows.
