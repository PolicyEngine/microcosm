Every UK H5 the producer writes, and every dataset the build hands policyengine-uk in process, declares the savings income projection rule `obr_savings_income_2022_2025` in the root attribute `policyengine_uk.savings_income_projection` (policyengine-uk 2.165.2; microcosm#1095, microcosm#1418). policyengine-uk then grows savings interest, other investment income and ISA interest by the OBR's savings income per person (October 2026 EFO Table 4.5) in the fiscal years 2022-23 to 2025-26, and by GDP per head in other years. That is the path on which the SPI income stage carries the donors' interest to the frame's year and the SPI Table 3.7 interest targets reach the calibration year (María's ruling of 2026-10-29). Before, the engine grew the frame's 2024 interest to 2025 by GDP per head, 4.6 percent, against 0.3 percent per person on the OBR series. A file that declares no rule still loads as GDP per head and keeps that reading when written again, so a reference file never gains the producer's rule, and the export check refuses a release that does not declare the producer's rule.
