The UK stages that band or match households on HBAI income read the engine's figure before negative incomes are reset to zero (`hbai_household_net_income_before_reset_to_zero`; policyengine-uk 2.132.4, pe-uk#2402), under the predictor name they had: the LCFS consumption predictors and NEED energy band, the NTS income band, the ETB VAT rate's disposable income and the ETB services predictors (microcosm#1095; Vahid's review of microcosm#1271). The surveys these stages match to do not reset their incomes, and the gross income the stages build, HBAI net income plus the HBAI deductions, holds only before the reset: after it, a household with negative income would be given its deductions as gross income. On the build's data the figure equals the HBAI net income policyengine-uk 2.130.1 returned. An engine test pins the reset and the gross income of a household with negative income.
