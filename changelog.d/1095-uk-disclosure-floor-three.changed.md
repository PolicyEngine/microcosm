UK stage receipts, stage-health gates and report tools withhold a figure only when it rests on one or two distinct source households (CD171's absolute floor), not on fewer than ten (María's ruling of 2026-10-29, microcosm#1095). One constant, `receipt_counts.UK_DISCLOSURE_FLOOR`, sets the floor. A withheld figure reads `"<3"`. Support and extreme counts gain a `"3-9"` band, and rounded record counts print three to nine exactly. The statistical floors stay: the zero-support floor, donor-cell minimums, the private-rent tool's ten-row group rule, and the SPI coherence tool's cell floor for correlations, medians and quantiles, since a median of a few records is one record's own value.
