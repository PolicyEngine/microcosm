UK stage receipts, stage-health gates and report tools withhold a figure only when it rests on one or two distinct source households (CD171's absolute floor), not on fewer than ten (María's ruling of 2026-10-29, microcosm#1095). One constant, `receipt_counts.UK_DISCLOSURE_FLOOR`, sets the floor. A withheld figure reads `"<3"`. Support, extreme and rounded record counts read `"3-9"` from three to nine, so a count just above the floor never narrows a small group by difference. The statistical floors stay: the zero-support floor, donor-cell minimums, the private-rent tool's ten-row group rule, and the SPI coherence tool's cell floor for correlations, medians and quantiles, since a median of a few records is one record's own value. The SPI Public Use Tape's own two conditions (SN 9422) are unchanged: no attempt may be made to attribute a record to a named individual, and material that includes analyses of the tape goes to HMRC at least ten working days before it is distributed or published.
