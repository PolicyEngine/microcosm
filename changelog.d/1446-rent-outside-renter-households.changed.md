The US housing input gate now fails when a person outside a renter household carries positive `pre_subsidy_rent` (microcosm#1446). The support base built at c0513dbae, before the rent alignment fix in microcosm#1444, passed the gate with 66,623 such people; the base rebuilt at 9434c504b has none. `with_us_housing_inputs` returned a frame unchanged whenever the gate passed, so a base built before the fix kept its misplaced rent; it now draws that rent again, and fiscal refresh stops at the housing gate instead of releasing it. The ACS rent draw now gives heads of owner and no-cash-rent households zero rent. The shared QRF's zero-or-positive classifier gives each of them a small chance of positive rent: before this change, seeds 1 to 7 on the Route A pre-clone frame each put rent on one to four of them, and seed 0 on none.
