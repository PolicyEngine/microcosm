The CGT income proxy and the area self-employment rows read the profit after losses brought forward (microcosm#1095). The CGT stages band people and draw their gains on the sum of the stored income columns, which stands in for HMRC's total income. The area target matrix reads the engine's `self_employment_income` directly. Since the SPI channel moved the tape's losses brought forward into `loss_relief`, both read the year's profit before that relief, which put some SPI rows in a higher income band and changed where the CGT stages placed gains. Both now read the profit after the relief, so their income bands are as before.
