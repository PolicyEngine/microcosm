The housing shell's council tax on the rows the SPI support channel copied, at each copy's reference Band D, in `microcosm.build.uk_runtime.spi_housing_council_tax` (#931), unwired: no stage uses it yet and nothing imports the module, so no key or value moves. Later in #931 group (e), the housing shell (`spi_housing_shell`) prices its bills with it after its chained draws and declares what the module states.

- **The reference.** A copy's draw inputs are missing by rule, so the SPI-copy area stage (`spi_copy_areas`) draws its area at L3, its region by households. `uk_spi_housing_council_tax_inputs` takes each Great Britain copy's reference Band D as the exact expected 2024-25 Band D of that cell under the draw's own weights, from the shared `draw_cell_expectations` with the arguments the council-tax localisation (`council_tax_localise`) passes for the same rows.
  - It reads each row's region, support channel and copy lineage, on a narrow frame of the region alone whose two draw inputs are present and missing (`uk_spi_copy_draw_frame`). It reads no area column, geography key, IMD decile or band flag.
  - Its rows are those whose `household_support_channel` is `spi`, the housing shell's rule, and they must be exactly the rows the support channel's lineage marks as its copies, SPI copies and income band donors. The supports' digests are keyed by the supports given.
- **The bill.** `uk_spi_statutory_council_tax` prices a Great Britain row by the 2024-25 statute (`council_tax_band_d.uk_statutory_council_tax`) at its reference, for its nation and drawn band, less the single-adult discount where `council_tax_single_adult_raw` is 1: one correctly rounded conversion of the exact bill. `uk_spi_council_tax_bills` returns that on Great Britain rows and the chained draw, bit for bit, on Northern Ireland rows, by María's ruling of 14 November. The branch is the nation of the row's region, never a value of the draw.
  - The bill reads neither the drawn area nor its local authority. The localisation multiplies a copy's bill by its drawn authority's Band D over the same reference, so it stays the one step that localises the bill, and the paid bill it keeps for the WAS chain carries no drawn area.
- **Checks and evidence.** `uk_spi_housing_council_tax_checks` returns booleans: every Great Britain bill at the statute of its reference, every Northern Ireland bill as chained, every reduction within its bill, and every rebate equal to its reduction. `uk_spi_housing_council_tax_evidence` returns the block the housing shell's receipt is to carry under `council_tax`: the method, the reference, the copy level, the England-and-Wales and Scottish Band D resources with their digests and the Scottish feed's source commit, the supports' digests and the checks. It has no number leaf, never copies the feed, and its keys are the same whatever systems, nations or clones a frame holds.
- **The declaration pieces** the housing shell's stage is to declare: the localisation's Band D artifacts and its Scottish `verify_vendored_fact_resource` parameters, and a `derive` of `council_tax` whose formula states the bill.
- **Refusals** name columns, dtypes and declared constants only, never a household's id, area code, authority code, band, value or count. A Northern Ireland row sent to the statute, and a band its nation does not price, are refused by the module's own messages before the statute is called.
