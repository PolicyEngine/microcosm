`docs/release-contract.md` puts the release contract on paper (ARCH-1, cos d1160): the end state, the releases in service on 2026-10-15, and contracts C1 to C5 (release manifest and certification, loader compatibility, staging and run outcome, the Logbook, publish and registry), each as the code enforces it today and as the plan's version 1 would, with how far each milestone had got on 2026-11-03. It says how C1 version 1 keys by declared country instead of release-id strings, records where the recertification lane and C2 version 1 disagree, carries microcosm#378's parity rule, and replaces the branch-only `us-uk-release-path.md`. It changes nothing that publishes.

`tools/generate_release_contract_fixtures.py` writes version 1 of the contract's golden fixtures under `packages/microcosm-data/tests/fixtures/release_contract/v1`, following the staging-fixture pattern: the UK national line's manifests and an inventory of what the contract requires of that line (C1), the `latest.json` and `latest-national.json` payloads and a registry snapshot (C5), with `SHA256SUMS` and `--check`. The UK national assembler's manifest construction moves into `uk_national_release_manifests`, a pure function the generator also calls; its output is unchanged.
