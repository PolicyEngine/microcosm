US certification closes the post-export scorer when any error stops it during scoring, before the error is raised: when the reform-coverage smoke fails at the certified tier (or with an evidence-tier failure no owner matches), when a stage raises, or on a SIGTERM. A pooled scorer's workers then stop at once, those still running a unit (reform validation's first units, which start while the smoke's last are running) are killed instead of running until the process exits, and the batch files are removed. An error from closing is printed, and the first error is the one raised. A failed smoke that certification goes on past (the allow option, or an evidence failure with an owner) cancels nothing.
