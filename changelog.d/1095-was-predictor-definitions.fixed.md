The UK WAS wealth stage's predictors measure the same things on the donor and the recipient (microcosm#1095, uk-data#486/#495). The donor's council tax was `CTAmtR8`, a single instalment (weighted mean £479 against £1,395 for the annual amount paid, `DVCTaxAmtAnnualR8`). Its children were `NumCh18R8`, which also counts the 16- and 17-year-olds that `NumAdultR8` counts, so the two overrun the household size on 5.6% of donor rows. The recipient's adults and children were the engine's age-18 counts. The donor now reads `DVCTaxAmtAnnualR8` and `NumChildR8` (`NumChildR8` + `NumAdultR8` is the household size on 99.99% of rows). The recipient's adults and children are its FRS claimants and partners and its other members (`is_uc_claimant`), which the Lifetime ISA stage reads through the same surface. A new `rental_income` predictor pairs the donor's net rent `DVNetRentAmtAnnualR8_aggr` with the household's property income: WAS investment income excludes rent, so the property draws had no income of their own, and on the 30 September national build 91% of households with rental income held no other residential or non-residential property. The stage refuses a manifest whose predictor lists drift from the code's.
