HMRC's Child Benefit families in payment by number of children bind as five national rows (microcosm#1095): one, two, three, four, and five or more children (HMRC's five-to-nine and ten-or-more columns together), from the Child Benefit Statistics of August 2025. Each counts the benefit units whose `child_benefit` is above zero by their children on policyengine-uk's own definition, a child or qualifying young person who is not the claimant or partner (the new measure `child_benefit_child_count`). The rows carry an in-year snapshot hold, signed by María on 2026-10-25. Only Child Benefit spending bound before, so nothing fixed the size of the claiming families.
