Three UK national calibration rows, `hmrc.itl.savings_tax_at_{basic,higher,additional}_rate`, bind the Income Tax on savings at each rate that Table 2.6 of HMRC's Income Tax liabilities statistics (July 2026) projects for all payers, at the calendar-2025 window of the 2024-25 and 2025-26 projections (microcosm#1418). They are the base the Budget's savings income rates are costed on (Personal Tax Model on SPI 2023-24: taxable savings income above the Personal Allowance, the Personal Savings Allowance and the starting rate, ISAs excluded). Each row measures the rate times the engine's savings income at that rate on each person's year, through three new annual measures (`basic_rate_savings_income_annual`, `higher_rate_savings_income_annual`, `add_rate_savings_income_annual`); a value expression may now scale a name by a positive constant for that. SPI Table 3.7's bank and building society interest rows stay.
