Measure how Scotland's 18 Broad Rental Market Areas overlap its 32 council areas, and settle whether ONS's BRMA-grain private rents can become council targets (#1090, folding in #1092). `uk/scotland_brma_la_overlap.json` holds Census 2022 private-rented and all households for the 63 BRMA × council intersections, summed exactly from UV404 by output area in the pinned feed, with each output area's BRMA from the area-to-BRMA layer and its council from the Scotland atomic support; `tools/build_uk_scotland_brma_la_overlap.py` rebuilds it and `uk_runtime/scotland_brma_la_overlap.py` reads it. The table shows they cannot: 18 councils span more than one BRMA, 29 councils and 17 BRMAs form one connected block, and a household-weighted mean of BRMA rents would give every council inside a BRMA that BRMA's average (up to 30.9% from the council's own mean in Rent Service Scotland's 2020-21 market evidence, `experiments/1090-scotland-brma-la-rent-dispersion.json`). The decision is recorded in `docs/uk-scotland-private-rent-brma-1090.md`.
