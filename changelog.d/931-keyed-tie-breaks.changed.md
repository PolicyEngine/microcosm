Six walks on the UK spine that ordered exactly tied rows by id now order them by an identity-keyed uniform, then by id (#931; accepted as designed on 7 Nov). Ties broken by id alone put the lowest id first every time, and the copies and geographic clones of one household differ only in their copy ordinal, so one copy ordinal won every tie: the first copy or clone at stages 28, 42 and 44, and the last at stage 26, which takes the top of an ascending order. On synthetic clone pairs the lower clone index ranked first in every tied pair, and in about half of them once ties are keyed. Values move only where an exact tie touches a cut or a position schedule.

- **Shared helper.** `microcosm.build.stochastic_assignment.keyed_tie_order(primary, ids, *, seed, salt)` returns `np.lexsort((ids, u, primary))`, with `u` the records' `keyed_tie_uniforms(ids, *, seed, salt)`: `stable_identity_uniforms` of `integer_record_keys(ids, label=salt)`. It is a secondary sort key, the form stage 27 already uses, so on distinct values the order is the id order's bit for bit; it draws the uniforms of tied records only. Each record is keyed on its own id, never on `spine_household_keys`, which strips the geographic branch and would tie the clones again, and the rank-inside-a-tie form is not used, since it moves records that have no tie off their cut.
- **Stage 42** (`cgt_support_split`): households tied on investable wealth, seed 0, salt `cgt_support_split:wealth_tie`. High-value families tie exactly on the four wealth columns, since no stage from 26 to 42 writes them.
- **Stage 26** (`high_value_home_support`): owners tied on home value before the weighted mid-rank, which is unchanged; seed 0, salt `high_value_home_support:value_tie`.
- **Stage 28** (`property_components`): landlords tied on profit in the receipts walk, under the walk seed and a new `tie_salt`, `allocate_property_rental_income:tie`. The stage refuses an empty tie salt and one equal to a salt its finance-cost draws key the same person ids on. The walk's one offset stays as it is (ruling 6).
- **Stage 44** (`hmrc_cgt_gains_spine`): one uniform per person under the stage's seed (552), salted `hmrc_cgt_gains_spine:rank_tie:<period>`, drawn once per stage call and read by every cell walk, pooled walk, plan and the sub-AEA remainder, which take it as `_ranked`'s keyword-only `tie` (without it, person_id alone breaks ties, as before). The plans and the remainder walk the rank ascending, so a tie's member with the higher uniform takes the lower stratum.
- **Stage 47** (`hmrc_cgt_asset_type_spine`): the BADR walk and the main asset type walk, under the BADR seed (555), salted `assign_badr_qualifying_gains:tie:<period>` and `assign_main_asset_type:tie:<period>`.
- **Declarations.** The two support splits declare their tie-break as new `seed` and `salt` keys and the receipts walk its `tie_salt`, in the code dictionaries, the strict schema (three operations), `sources.yaml` and `source_stages.json`; the selection, stratum rule, order, ordering, mapping, realization and values texts, the stage notes and the receipts walk's and asset types' receipt order texts say the above, and "no draw, no seed, no salt" and "deterministic, no seed" are gone. The build sidecar's declared seeds register the splits under their salts and the receipts walk's seed under its tie salt.
- **Stage 42's rule B.** The split's walk, `keeps_the_split`'s rerun of it and the identity tool's recompute all pass the declared pair (`cgt_support_tie_break()`); the selection takes no default, so neither the rerun nor the tool can walk ties in another order. The exact-total correction's candidate order (the heaviest households left whole first, tied weights in row order) is unchanged: it picks the row of a rounding-sized correction rather than drawing anything. `copy_rule` names the cap of 64 candidates but not their order; tied weights take row order, so of a whole household's geographic clones, which weigh the same, the one in the earliest row is tried first.
- **No nesting promised.** All six walks are pooled. The uniform is a function of each row's own id, so clone 0's equals the `K = 1` row's and every clone draws its own.
- **What moves.** Nothing where no exact tie touches a cut or a position schedule. Elsewhere, counted on synthetic frames:
  - At stage 42, a band's crossing household when it sits in a tie group: with three clones per household, 2 to 8 of about 850 households per frame changed copy count, and clone 0, which carried the most families in all six frames, carries the most in one. At one clone none moved on those frames, whose synthetic tied families are light, most members at or under 60, the weight above which a selected household takes a second copy. Where a band's crossing falls in a tie group whose members weigh more, as a high-value family's can, the keyed order changes which members are copied at one clone too.
  - At stage 26, the tie group across a region's cut: 0 to 5 of 3,000 owners per frame.
  - At stage 28, no untied landlord changes band, since the same landlords sit above it in either order. Where a tie group shares one weight, as the copies of one household do, only its own landlords move: on three frames of six-row families, about two thirds of the tied landlords' receipts moved, none outside the tie groups, and the band counts held. Where a tie group of unequal weights straddles a band boundary, as different households reporting one profit do, the keyed order puts another tied landlord at the boundary. Tied landlords then cross it, the bands' record counts and masses change, and the receipts of the other landlords in the bands on either side move with them; inside a band whose members hold, a reordered tie group can still move later landlords' receipts by rounding. On three frames of 2,400 landlords, half of them on 60 round profits at weights that differ, 24 to 49 tied landlords changed band and the record counts of 4 to 7 of the 11 bands changed; no untied landlord changed band, and the receipts of 398 to 941 of the about 1,200 untied landlords moved, by at most about 0.3 per cent.
  - At stage 44, often, since the blended rank key ties gainers even on distinct inputs: 43 to 99 of about 1,800 gainers moved amount where wealth varies, and none where it does not.
  - At stage 47, nothing, since plan amounts do not tie (three frames bit for bit).
  - Node keys: every spine node re-keys, since `stochastic_assignment` changed and the spine's root binds it; values move from stage 26 on. On a bound spine the national line re-keys 5 of its 6 nodes, all but the bound checkpoint, since the national target, problem and gate kernels hash the country spec, which this change edits.
