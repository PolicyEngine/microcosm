The national HMRC income rows now measure each person's income over the tax year, as HMRC's statistics count it. The 49 declarations of `hmrc_spi`, `hmrc_spi_region`, `hmrc_itl` and `hmrc_itl_marginal_rate` read the annual measures for their values, their total-income bands and filters, their taxpayer test and their rate band (`employment_income_annual`, `self_employment_income_after_loss_relief_annual`, `private_pension_income_annual`, `state_pension_annual`, `total_income_after_loss_relief_annual`, `income_tax_annual`, `tax_band_annual`), and `obr.income_tax` reads `income_tax_annual`. Dividends, property and savings income have no spells and stay as they are. Target ids, facts, values and metric names do not change: only the person-side measure each row adds up moves, so a recent leaver who paid tax in the year counts as a taxpayer, and a part-year earner sits in the band of what they received. Poverty, benefits and the engine's published tax keep reading current income. The five local `hmrc` rows, the CGT taxable-income bands and the salary-sacrifice reliefs stay on current income for now; the local rows follow once the local line computes its metrics from their declarations.
