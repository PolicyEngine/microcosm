Pension Credit reads the benefit unit's recorded capital (`pension_credit_reported_capital`, pe-uk#2018 and #2070, uk-data#513, microcosm#1095). It takes the same value as Universal Credit's `uc_reported_capital`: FRS `TOTCAPB4`, with SPI-channel units taking their capital redraw. Zero or more replaces every capital source in Pension Credit's assessable capital. The unavailable sentinel −1 is the engine's own default, so the household proxy applies there.
