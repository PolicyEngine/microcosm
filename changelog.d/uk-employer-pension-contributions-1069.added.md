Draw UK employer pension contributions from ASHE rate bands and bind workplace pension contributions (#1069 c9; PolicyEngine/chronicle#302 via #305). The FRS HMRC spine leaves no longer set employer contributions to three times the employee contribution. Each member of an employer scheme (FRS `PENPROV.STEMPPEN` 1 to 4; occupational first when a person reports several) draws a contribution rate within an ASHE 2024 Table P10 rate band for their scheme type and job sector (`JOB.JOBSECT`), and the rate applies to their pay. The bands are vendored as `ashe_employer_pension_contribution_rates.json` (81 rows); all-sector rows are the public and private bands weighted by Table P2 jobs. Converted salary-sacrifice records now also lose the sacrificed amount from `employment_income`.

Three targets: DWP's employer contributions into workplace pensions in calendar 2025 (£104.2bn, Great Britain, all savers, 2025 earnings terms) against the engine's employer contributions plus salary sacrifice, which ASHE and DWP count as an employer contribution; DWP's gross employee contributions (£66.2bn: the employee part net of tax, £45.5bn, plus its tax relief, £20.7bn) against the engine's employee contributions; and the 2024-25 salary-sacrifice amount, HMRC Table 6.2's employer National Insurance relief divided by the 13.8% employer rate (about £24.6bn, a declared conversion until the relief rows bind directly). The salary-sacrifice users total (7.7m) leaves the fit; the two headcounts either side of £2,000 stay bound.
