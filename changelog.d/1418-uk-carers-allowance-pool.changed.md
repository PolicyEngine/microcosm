The Carer's Allowance draw opens the claim flag only to carers of a severely disabled person (microcosm#1418): the allowance is paid only for caring for someone policyengine-uk holds `is_severely_disabled_for_carers_allowance` (SSCBA 1992 s. 70(2)), which the FRS care hours do not record. A carer whose care inside the household alone reaches 35 hours (the new stage-internal `frs_care_hours_in_household`, the largest of the FRS HOURAB, HOURAH, HOURCB and HOURCH bands, dropped at release) joins the pool when another household member is severely disabled at the survey year; a carer whose hours are not all given inside the household joins on a second identity-keyed uniform below the share of in-household carers reporting no allowance who have such a member. `care_hours` and the Universal Credit carer element are unchanged. The receipt's `realized_share` is now `drawn_share_of_over_limit_pool`, beside banded pool counts before and after the restriction, inside and outside the household.
