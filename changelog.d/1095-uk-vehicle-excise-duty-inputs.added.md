The LCFS consumption stage now writes policyengine-uk's vehicle excise duty inputs for the cars households keep (microcosm#1095 B3, VED). No stage wrote them, so every household's duty was zero. The new `assign_car_attributes_from_nts` step reads the same NTS kept cars as J2 and K:

- **Which cars.** A household's vehicles less its members' company cars (an employer pays their duty) go one to each other adult, oldest first. policyengine-uk holds one car a person, so a car beyond the adults is left out and the receipt counts it.
- **Which fuel.** A household the fuel flag leaves without road fuel keeps zero-emission cars only. One that buys fuel holds a petrol, diesel or other car first, and each further car is zero-emission at the household's own per-car share (the fuel flag's).
- **Which car.** Each car is a kept NTS car of its class in the household's income fifth, drawn by a keyed uniform at its interview weight. The weight is scaled so each fifth and age band keeps its share of the kept cars, since fewer of the newest and oldest cars carry the registration record the CO2 bands come from.
- **The inputs.** `car_fuel_type` (a diesel registered from 2021 meets RDE2), `car_co2_emissions` (the band's midpoint on the scale the car's duty regime reads), `car_engine_size` (none for a zero-emission car), `car_first_registration_date` (1 October 2025 less the car's age, drawn within its NTS band, as an ISO date) and `car_is_ved_exempt` (DLA's higher-rate or PIP's enhanced mobility component exempts the member's own car, or the oldest car-holder's in their benefit unit). `car_list_price` keeps the engine's default, so no car pays the expensive-car supplement.

The five columns join the release export's allow-list, since the enhanced FRS never carried them, and the gate battery's digests in the microcosm-data contract move with it. The H2 fixture's synthetic vehicle table gains the three NTS columns the step reads. Vehicle excise duty is measured, never bound: no target carries it.
