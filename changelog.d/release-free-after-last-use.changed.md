The US fiscal refresh release (`tools/build_us_fiscal_refresh_release.py`) frees large objects once nothing reads them, instead of keeping them until `_main` returns. It deletes the ACS predictor-join result once its receipt is taken, passes each of the nine donor-table loader calls straight into its imputation stage, deletes `base_frame` after the export input-mass check, loads the export reference frame inside that check (`_export_input_mass_gate_with_reference_h5`), and deletes `release_engine` after the H5 write. After the solve it replaces each constraint matrix, the refit's and, under L0 selection, the selection's over the full frame, with its shape and nonzero count through the new `microcosm.calibrate.without_constraint_matrix`; the calibration diagnostics' target surface reads only those two numbers, and any later use that needs the entries raises `ConstraintMatrixDroppedError` (the placeholder is `DroppedConstraintMatrix`). After the start-up check of the release input-coverage manifest it clears the module's cached PolicyEngine-US parameter system (`clear_installed_list_parameter_baseline_cache`), so the system is not kept through the source stages and the calibration; the reform-coverage smoke builds it once more. Written outputs are unchanged.
