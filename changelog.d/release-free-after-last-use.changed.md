The US national build (`tools/build_us_fiscal_refresh_release.py`) frees large objects once nothing reads them, instead of keeping them until `_main` returns. It deletes the ACS predictor-join result once its receipt is taken, passes each of the nine donor-table loader calls straight into its imputation stage, releases `base_frame` after the export input-mass check, loads the export reference frame inside that check (`_record_export_input_mass_gate`, which also holds the check's shadow evaluation and its error handling), and releases `release_engine` once the written H5's stored-input check has run. After the solve it replaces each constraint matrix, the refit's and, under L0 selection, the selection's over the full frame, with its shape and nonzero count through the new `microcosm.calibrate.without_constraint_matrix`; the calibration diagnostics' target surface reads only those two numbers, and any later use that needs the entries raises `ConstraintMatrixDroppedError` (the placeholder is `DroppedConstraintMatrix`). After the start-up check of the release input-coverage manifest it clears the module's cached PolicyEngine-US parameter system (`clear_installed_list_parameter_baseline_cache`), so the build no longer keeps that system through the source stages and the calibration. Written outputs are unchanged.
