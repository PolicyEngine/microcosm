Scottish `water_and_sewerage_charges` on the UK spine is the charge the household pays (microcosm#1095, uk-data#499). The spine read each household's discount factor as `CWATAMTD`/`CWATAMT1`, but `CWATAMTD` sits below the gross water charge even where no discount applies (a median 0.83 for undiscounted households on the 2024-25 tab, about £1 a week below in bands B and D to G), which put Scottish charges about 13% to 16% below the gross charges less the status discount, and council tax reduction recipients got no water charges reduction. The charge is now the gross `CWATAMT1` + `CSEWAMT1` less the household's status discount (25% or 50%, from `CTDISC` and `CT25D50D`), at 65% for council tax reduction recipients, the Water Charges Reduction Scheme's maximum 35% reduction; a household with no gross water cell pays its recorded `CWATAMTD`, and the helper refuses a raw tab without the discount and reduction cells.
