The UK LCFS stage receipt reports how much of ONS household spending each COICOP division captures (microcosm#1113): each of the twelve division totals at prior weights against ONS Consumer Trends for calendar 2024, less the classes a household diary does not record (narcotics, owner-occupiers' imputed rent and FISIM), on ONS's domestic concept, with the twelve together also against the national concept. The block is report-only, and the Consumer Trends resource vendors the division and total rows it reads.
