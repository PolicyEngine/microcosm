The `frs_person_draws` stage no longer draws `is_apprentice` for full-time students (Vahid's review of microcosm#1535, nit 3). The FRS's full-time education question never codes an apprentice as in full-time education, so an employee aged 21 to 24 with `is_in_full_time_education` (from `frs_education`) is not one. The draw's target is unchanged, r times everyone aged 21 to 24 at the frame's weights; only the pool it flags from narrows, and the receipt names the excluded flag. The stage refuses a frame without the flag, and the graph node declares it.

In policyengine-uk 2.174.1 the flag on these ages changes only the s.9B employer NICs relief: the apprentice minimum wage applies under 19 or in a first year the build never sets, Care to Learn ends at 20, the build stores `is_in_non_advanced_education` itself, and the 16 to 19 Bursary Fund's qualifying-education test reads a `current_education` the build never writes. The stage's signed eligibility text is restated; María signed it on 9 November 2026.
