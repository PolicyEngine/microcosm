The UK Child Benefit take-up stage gate (`uk_stage_child_benefit_take_up`) now holds each measured age within the larger of 0.05 and three standard errors of a share drawn at its published rate over its child rows (#931; María's ruling of 10 Nov). A measured age, one with at least `minimum_age_child_rows` child rows whose solved rate was not clipped, was held within a fixed `maximum_age_claim_rate_deviation`, 0.05, of HMRC's published rate for it. The band is `maximum_age_claim_rate_standard_errors` × sqrt(p (1 − p) / n), with the new parameter declared as 3, and p and n the receipt's `published_rate` and `eligible_child_rows` for the age. It treats the age's child rows as independent draws at p, an approximation as ruled: in the stage, siblings share their family's draw, reporters always claim and the realised rate is weighted.

- **Why.** #931's keyed redraws move each age's realised claim rate by sampling noise. At an age with few child rows that noise can exceed a fixed 0.05, and because the draws are keyed, such a failure would repeat on every rebuild, including the national build.
- **Floor.** Three standard errors exceed 0.05 only below 3,600 p (1 − p) child rows, so an age with more rows keeps 0.05 and only thin ages loosen. At a published rate of 0 or 1 the term is 0, so the floor holds. The overall claim-rate check and the opt-out check are unchanged.
- **Report.** A failing age's line names the tolerance used at that age, "(tolerance t: the larger of 0.05 and 3 standard errors of a share at the published rate over that age's child rows)", and the gate's details count the measured ages held to more than the floor (`ages_tolerance_above_floor`). The gate refuses a missing, non-finite or non-positive `maximum_age_claim_rate_standard_errors`. The stage-health binding now routes the parameter, since the battery refuses a declared parameter its binding does not route.
- **What moves.** The gate's verdicts at thin ages: an age whose realised rate is more than 0.05 from its published rate but within its three standard errors now passes. No stage output or generated file moves: the gate reads the receipt and draws nothing. Of the 99 UK kernel hashes 14 move, and no phase code: the spine gates' (`stage_health`, `battery_bindings` and the solve fingerprint, which `gates.json` is part of); `uc_deduction_attributes`' and `uc_take_up`'s (`stage_health`); the full build's target compilation, selection, measures, problem and rotated holdout (the solve fingerprint), its preflight and calibrated gates (`battery_bindings` and the solve or country fingerprint) and its certification readiness (`microcosm.data.contract`, which the re-pin edits); and the national targets, problem and gates (the country fingerprint; the gates also hash the gate registry's modules). Node keys move from the assembled spine gate on: 64 of the gated spine's 81 nodes. On a bound spine the full build re-keys from its target compilation on, so it re-solves, and the national line re-keys all but the bound checkpoint. The contract re-pins the gate battery's policy, gates-manifest and spec-fingerprint digests and the release certification's spine part; the calibration seam, release cut and dense line digests do not move.
