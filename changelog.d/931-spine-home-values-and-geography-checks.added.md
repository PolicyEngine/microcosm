- **The area home values** (`microcosm.build.uk_runtime.area_home_values`, G8, form F). Called with the WAS chain's segment-2 draw of `main_residence_value`, each owner household with a positive draw takes its weighted mid-rank among the owner households of its FRS region with a positive draw, both support channels, at the stage's household weights, ties broken on a draw keyed on the household id (`property_localisation.owner_uniforms`). Its stream is fixed, `("sha256-u53-v1", "uk-spine-area-home-values-v1", 0, 0)`, with seed source NONE, as ruled for `regional_home_values` on 9 Nov (C-43). An England and Wales owner's value is its assigned constituency's knots at that rank (`uk/high_value_home_value_knots.json`), divided by its FRS region's regional uprating factor on the draw, so the uprating, dividing by the kept draw, returns it to the knot value to one rounding. Scotland, Northern Ireland, renters and owners without a positive draw keep the draw bit for bit, and the draw is kept on every row as `main_residence_value_rank_basis`. It refuses, by texts that name columns and declared constants only, an England and Wales owner whose `constituency_code` is outside England and Wales or another owner's inside it, a constituency without knots and a localised value that is not positive. `area_home_values_declared` holds a WAS stage's declaration of `localise_main_residence_value` to the reviewed operation the module states, its two artifacts (each with `runtime_sha256_required`), the `main_residence_value_rank_basis` output and a support clip exempting exactly `main_residence_value` and `property_wealth`, and refuses a half declaration. The knots' region map `FRS_REGION_TO_KNOTS_REGION`, now read-only, and the knots' file name `KNOTS_RESOURCE` move here from `regional_home_values`, which imports them. `clone_zero_rows` says which household rows are geographic clone 0's; the WAS stage's cap counts by it too.

No value, sum or factor.
