Build the dense line on the bound spine's clones, areas and sampling (#931). The dense line no longer samples, expands, keys or places anything of its own: its targets, measures and problem run on the spine's version as it stands, its pool is that version whole, and its clone count K, its sampling rung and its geography binding are the spine's. It still refuses every spine whose roster is not geography-first (`spine_posture`), so no dense build runs until the spine declares its area stages. (Later in #931 it declares them, and the dense line waits on the WAS chain's home-value localiser alone: `931-spine-assigns-areas`.)

- **Graph.** `uk.full.sample`, `uk.full.normalize`, `uk.full.expand` (with `uk.full.expand.owned`), `uk.full.identity`, `uk.full.locations`, `uk.full.geography_mapping`, the shared support imports, assignment, derivation and gate (`uk.full.geography.*`), `uk.full.geography.local_authority` and the UK distribution gate `uk.full.geography_gate` leave the dense graph, with their eight kernels and the population receipt, location draw and geography gate artifact types. `microcosm.build.uk_runtime.graph_population` keeps `context_frame`, `population_columns`, `population_slices` and the pool checkpoint, `uk.full.pool@1`, now a keep-all FILTER on `uk.full.spine_checkpoint` (`--input-h5`) or the spine's endpoint (`--spine-request`) with no typed edge, declared bitwise and hashing its own module and `national_frame`. `append_uk_population_nodes`, `uk_pool_validation_inputs`, `GEOGRAPHY_ASSIGNMENTS` and `UK_GEOGRAPHY_IDENTITY_INPUTS` go. The UK registry no longer registers the shared atomic-geography kernels; NZ's transport registers its own. The dense geography gate's ladder and installed-engine local-authority checks (#953) and the pool's refusal of a failed shared geography validation leave with their nodes; the spine's own geography gates take them over when they are registered later in #931, and until then the roster refusal holds every dense build.
- **Targets.** `append_uk_target_nodes` takes `population` with no default, and the full build passes the spine's version. The measures lose their edge to the dense geography gate and their check of it, and take the spine's transferred-gate admission the sample node held, where the graph declares the spine's gates: they refuse to read the version unless that stored report admits it, before they read anything else.
- **Configuration.** `UKFullBuildConfig` loses `sample_fraction`, `sample_seed`, `geography_assignment`, `source_vintage`, `constituency_vintage` and `source_lineage_modulus`. `n_clones` (default 1, the declared spine's) is the bound spine's K and `effective_sample_fraction` is `source_sample_fraction`, the spine's rung. `uk_full_graph` no longer takes `spine_weight_kind` or `atomic_geography_definition`, and refuses a composed spine whose `geographic_expand` node declares another K than the configuration's, naming both. `build_uk_country_graph` no longer takes `atomic_geography_definition`.
- **K, sampling and geography from the spine.** `prepare_full_build` reads them from the authenticated sidecar (`geographic_clone_count`, one where a sidecar predates the record; `sampling`; `release_posture`; the `geography` block) or from the spine request's `--n-clones`, `--sample-fraction` and `--sample-seed`, and sets the run's `n_clones`, `sample_fraction` and, where the spine was sampled, `sample_seed` to them before the run's parameters, its candidate identity digest, its size-checkpoint identity, its staging rung and both Logbook rungs record them. A spine whose sampling fraction is not a scale-ladder rung is refused there, naming the rungs. The attempt's build id, minted before preparation reads the spine, takes the spine's rung read ahead off the bound spine's sidecar or the spine request's `--sample-fraction` (f100 where that read fails, and preparation then reports the real error); preparation refuses an attempt whose build id carries another rung than the spine it binds, naming both rung tokens, and a refusal before the spine is bound records the build id's rung on its Logbook row. The request bindings gain a `spine` block (clone count, sampling and release posture), and their `geography` block is the spine's binding with its clone count beside it. The rowwise manifest reads its `sampling`, `identity.sampling`, rung and release verdict's full-rung leg from that spine block and its `geography.ladder_gate` from the export descriptor's geography check on the calibrated frame; its weight-kind chain names `geographic_expand` for the clones.
- **Bound-spine checks.** `_assert_spine_sidecar_binds_frame` also holds the sidecar's `geographic_clone_count` to the frame's clone count, naming both, and the bound spine's checkpoint kernel hashes `geographic_clones` for it. `spine_provenance_from_sidecar` adds the spine's clone count, `geography`, `sampling` and `release_posture`. The national build record, the national calibration diagnostics' build block and the dense manifest's `identity.spine.spine_provenance` carry that provenance, so they gain the four fields; a national build on a spine without clone indices records K = 1 and computes the same dataset.
- **Flags.** `microcosm-build-uk` no longer takes `--geography-assignment`, `--atomic-support-ew`, `--atomic-support-scotland`, `--atomic-support-ni`, `--atomic-support-sha256-ew`, `--atomic-support-sha256-scotland`, `--atomic-support-sha256-ni` or `--source-lineage-modulus`; a script that still passes one is refused as an unrecognized argument, on either role. The dense role refuses a `--sample-fraction` other than 1.0, any `--sample-seed`, `--candidate-clone-counts` with `--input-h5`, and `--n-clones` with `--candidate-clone-counts`, by name, before any input is read. `--n-clones` is an assertion of the spine's K: the dense role no longer fills it from the doctrine, and preparation refuses one that differs from the spine's, naming both, and on a spine request pointing at the request's own `--n-clones`. `--candidate-clone-counts` plans the spine request once per count, each as the request's own `--n-clones`. The engine blocks are held to one or the spine's K by the configuration; on validation only an asserted `--n-clones` is compared. `--expected-constituency-vintage` is still taken and recorded in the run's parameters, but its one reader, the legacy location draw, is gone, so no dense node checks the ladder against it; its help says so.
- **Release-candidate refusals.** A sampled spine is refused as a release candidate at preparation, where its rung is read, on either route. The release-candidate refusal of `--n-clones` other than the doctrine's leaves validation, as do the support pins' requirement and the legacy assignment's refusal; `--sample-fraction` other than 1.0 is refused on every dense build (above). The spine's clone count is held to the doctrine's, and its geography binding to the conditioned register, by later commits of #931. The ladder, Ledger and input pins, the target rule, the epochs, the measure exclusions, the local target levels and the signing key are refused as before.
- **The dense sampler.** `national_sampling` keeps the rung tokens, the default seed and the spine root's sampler; `sample_uk_spine_frame` and `uk_spine_source_family_units`, which drew whole source families for the dense line, go, with their re-exports.
- **Texts.** The row-wise lane's clone refusals (`rowwise_dataset`) point at the dense graph on a geography-first spine and promise no later group; `tools/build_uk_rowwise_dataset.py` says it serves spines without clone indices. `geographic_identity`, `household_lineage`, `geographic_clones` and `diagnostics` no longer describe the dense identity node as a key's writer or a column's reader: `household_lineage`'s three lineage refusals no longer open "Geography identity", its `UK_OPTIONAL_LINEAGE_COLUMNS`, which nothing reads now, goes, and `geographic_clones`' docstring names the national gates among the kernels that hash it. `tools/measure_uk_atomic_assignment.py`'s graph route, which ran the dense line's geography nodes, refuses with a pointer to the spine's own draw, its command before it reads any input, and the helpers that composed its requests go; later in #931 the tool measures the spine's own draw in process. The Orrery roster loses its geography group and the retired population nodes.
- **What moves.** Keys and records only; no dataset value. No spine stage's code changes: `household_lineage`, `geographic_clones` and `geographic_identity` change in docstrings, comments and refusal texts (and `household_lineage` loses a constant nothing reads), and `national_sampling` loses only the dense sampler, which no spine stage calls; the dense line still builds on no spine. On the synthetic H2 fixture the legacy oracle's final frame, stage records and stage evidence are identical before and after.
  - Of the kernels the UK registries register, 84 remain (96 before: the eight dense population kernels and the four shared atomic-geography ones go), and 64 change hash: 39 of the spine registry's 58 (the root, `uk.create@1`; the 35 stages that hash `household_lineage`, `geographic_identity` among them; and `cgt_support_split`, `was_wealth` and `was_lisa`, which hash `geographic_clones` alone), and the 25 `uk.full` kernels that hash an edited module. `uk.spine-gates@1`, `uk.claim@1`, `uk.identity@1` and 17 stages keep theirs. A spine build's root also hashes `national_sampling` through its sampler. So every spine node re-keys, and every dense and national node after it.
  - The pool was the last platform-bitwise UK kernel, so no dense node key carries the platform now (on the declared spine 16 of the dense graph's 23 nodes did); a shared graph store can serve them across platforms on their own bitwise declarations.
  - Of the gate phase codes only the full battery's terminal code moves.
  - A dense run's input pins no longer carry the three atomic-area supports, its recorded parameters drop `source_lineage_modulus`, and its size-checkpoint identity drops `source_lineage_modulus` and the `geography` block, so its input-pins digest, candidate identity digest and size-checkpoint identity change, and a size checkpoint cut before this change does not resume. The bound spine's provenance gains four fields (above).
  - The coverage manifest's `--check` passes; no declaration, generated file, `gates.json` or schema changes.
- **Tests.** `test_uk_full_population_graph.py` and `test_uk_atomic_identity_graph.py` are removed with the nodes they ran, and `test_uk_national_sampling.py` now tests the root's sampler. Their tests of live code move: the lineage reader's branch paths, residential arms and refusals to the new `test_uk_household_lineage.py`, which runs `uk_household_clone_paths` itself, and the row-wise lane's clone and source-lineage refusals to `test_uk_rowwise_dataset.py`. The full-graph stand-in (`test_support/microcosm_build/uk_full_population_graph.py`) is a clone-first, geography-first bound spine: the toy households at K clones in the copy-id table's geographic field, keyed as `geographic_identity` keys them and placed on the toy supports with the shared operators, with a BRMA and a fuel-relief flag; a bound checkpoint of it takes a stand-in sidecar whose geography block is under the leaf's keys. New tests cover each retired flag, the dense role's spine-bound refusals on both routes, K, sampling and seed taken from the spine on both routes (a seven-clone spine without `--n-clones` records seven, in the parameters, the identity digest and a written manifest), a disagreeing `--n-clones`, a sidecar whose K is not its frame's, the engine blocks held to the spine's K, `--candidate-clone-counts` planning a spine request once per count, a release candidate on a sampled spine on both routes, a spine off the scale ladder, the build id's rung read ahead and held to the spine's, the measures' admission, the pool on the spine's version and its keep-all mask, the manifest's spine-bound sampling and ladder gate, the dense allow-list pin (every column a geography-first spine adds to the export surface is in `UK_ALLOWED_EXTRA_EXPORT_COLUMNS`), the local area support gate's row, effective-sample-size and source floors in the K-sensitive constants table, and the measurement tool's refusal. The `geographic_identity` stage's keys are held to `household_draw_key` itself.
