England outside London's bus support takes off BUS01c's older and disabled concessionary journeys, as the fare pricing already does, so the concessionary travel reimbursement is spread over the travel it pays for (microcosm#1385). The eligibility basis now states the residuals: youth-scheme journeys stay priced, disabled-pass holders are not yet flagged, and under-5s ride free though BUS01c counts no concessionary journey for them.
