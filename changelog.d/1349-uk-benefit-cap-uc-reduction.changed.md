The fifteen DWP benefit-cap rows (`dwp.benefit_cap.capped_households` and its fourteen amount bands, Benefit cap tables to November 2025, Table 4) now bind `uc_benefit_cap_reduction_after_floor`, the reduction the Universal Credit award actually loses, at benefit-unit grain (microcosm#1349). They bound `benefit_cap_reduction`, which adds the Housing Benefit cap and keeps the gross regulation 81 amount, while DWP counts Universal Credit households by the amount the award loses. The total was a 0/1 household indicator, the incumbent's measure; it now counts the same benefit units as the bands, which partition it, and the build's complementary withholding (`uk_withheld_row_sets`) covers the closer difference. The measurement block names the Universal Credit reduction (`uk.benefits.universal_credit.benefit_cap_reduction`), and `tools/diagnose_uk_benefit_cap.py` reads the bound variable. The OBR Universal Credit rows split by cap status keep `benefit_cap_reduction`. In the target references only the total's entity moves, from household to benefit unit; no published value moves.
