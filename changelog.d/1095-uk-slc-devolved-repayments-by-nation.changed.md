SLC's devolved student loan repayments are one row per nation, `slc.repayments.wales_total`, `slc.repayments.scotland_total` and `slc.repayments.northern_ireland_total`, each declaring its nation's geography in its selector, in place of one devolved row that compiled no fact at the Great Britain pin (microcosm#1095 R4). Each carries SLC's 2024-25 outturn to 2025 by policyengine-uk's OBR average earnings index, since repayments are a share of earnings above the plan thresholds (María's ruling of 2026-10-30). Wales binds; Scotland and Northern Ireland are held as diagnostics until their borrowers' own plan thresholds are modelled (María's ruling of 2026-10-29).
