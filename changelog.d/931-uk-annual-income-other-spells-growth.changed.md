The annual-income simulation carries each input's other spells by policyengine-uk's own growth parameters (#931). The factor is the product, over each year after the dataset's up to the measurement year, of one plus the engine's rate in the series its dataset projection uses for the input: `gov.economic_assumptions.yoy_growth.obr.average_earnings` for pay and `gov.economic_assumptions.yoy_growth.obr.per_capita.mixed_income` for profit. It replaces the median of the engine's year-on-year ratios over the rows a simulation held, a statistic of whichever engine block ran, so every per-clone block and one engine over the pool now use the same factor. The engine's rows must still agree with it within 1e-5, or the resolver refuses. This moves data on the national and dense lines: an annual input with other spells can move by one float32 step, and with it the `*_annual` matrix entries, calibrated weights and H5 bytes. `other_spells_engine_factors` changes in every resolver receipt that carries it.
