The UK LCFS stage's report-only division capture now records where an over-draw sits (microcosm#1278). For each COICOP division it gives the share of spend held by the top 1% and the top 10% of households, on the frame at prior weights and on the donor at its design weights, and the frame's share over the donor's. A share resting on fewer than ten distinct source households is reported as "<10", as every receipt count is; the frame counts source households, not the copies it stacks of one. So is a top-10% share whose households beyond a shipped top 1% rest on one to nine sources, since the two shares' difference would give back their spend. Furnishings and health on the 2023-24 diary, and recreation and education on the 2024-25 one, drew above the donor; the next build's receipt says whether the excess sits in the tail or the body.
