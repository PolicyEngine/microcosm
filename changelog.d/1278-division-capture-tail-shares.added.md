The UK LCFS stage's report-only division capture now records where an over-draw sits (microcosm#1278). For each COICOP division it gives the share of spend held by the top 1% and the top 10% of households, on the frame at prior weights and on the donor at its design weights, and the frame's share over the donor's. The floors count the distinct source households that spend anything: the frame counts source households, not the copies it stacks of one, and refuses a frame that marks copies without naming their sources. A share is "<10", as every receipt count is, when one to nine of them sit in its top group, outside it (the division's total less the top group's spend would give back theirs), or between it and a shipped smaller fraction's group (the two shares' difference would). Furnishings and health on the 2023-24 diary, and recreation and education on the 2024-25 one, drew above the donor; the next build's receipt says whether the excess sits in the tail or the body.
