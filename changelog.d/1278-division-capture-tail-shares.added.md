The UK LCFS stage's report-only division capture now records where an over-draw sits (microcosm#1278). For each COICOP division it gives the share of spend held by the top 1% and the top 10% of households, on the frame at prior weights and on the donor at its design weights, and the frame's share over the donor's. A share carried by fewer than ten households is reported as "<10", as every receipt count is. Furnishings and health on the 2023-24 diary, and recreation and education on the 2024-25 one, drew above the donor; the next build's receipt says whether the excess sits in the tail or the body.
