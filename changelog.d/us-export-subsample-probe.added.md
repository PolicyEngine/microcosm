`tools/sample_us_export_households.py` writes a seeded, stratified, whole-household subsample of a US release export H5 with a verified receipt. Strata are support channel by source year; certainty households are kept at their source weight (every carrier of a probe expecting fewer than 30 sampled carriers, and every household whose weighted input mass in a probe is at least twice what one sampled carrier represents); drawn households carry ratio-estimator weights that conserve each stratum's weight total. `tools/probe_us_post_export.py` runs the release's post-export stages on the subsample through the release tool's own functions (stored-input gate, QRF tail gate, take-up participation and the stale count-calibrated check, reform-coverage smoke, reform validation, demographics, source coverage) and labels every verdict authoritative or informational: on a subsample, a smoke verdict is authoritative when its effect is at least 4 design-based standard errors from its floor with a variance estimate resting on at least 30 effective households, or when it is a take-all probe no other household's effect reaches; failures of the probe itself are reported apart from the release's. The defaults are calibrated on the published Route A export (`docs/evidence/us-export-subsample-design`). `--reference-release-dir` compares against a full-size build and checks that the probe scored the build's exact baseline plans.
