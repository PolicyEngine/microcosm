`tools/sample_us_export_households.py` writes a seeded, stratified, whole-household subsample of a US release export H5 (strata: support channel by source year; a take-all stratum keeps every carrier of a reform-coverage probe whose expected sampled carriers fall below 5; ratio-form Horvitz-Thompson weights that conserve every stratum's weight total) with a verified receipt, and `tools/probe_us_post_export.py` runs the release's post-export stages on it through the release tool's own functions (stored-input gate, QRF tail gate, take-up participation and the stale count-calibrated check, reform-coverage smoke, reform validation, demographics, source coverage). Every verdict is labeled authoritative or informational from design-based standard errors; `--reference-release-dir` compares against a full-size build and checks that the probe scored the build's exact baseline plans.
