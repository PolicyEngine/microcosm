Seed SNAP and TANF take-up on the ACS rows of the ACS local-area release (microcosm#1019). Every ACS SPM unit previously reached both engine passes with `takes_up_snap_if_eligible` and `takes_up_tanf_if_eligible` missing, and the reviewed-null fill gave them the engine default, so every eligible ACS unit took both programs up. `microcosm.build.us_runtime.acs_local_take_up` now fills only missing ACS-row cells before either fill: `receives_snap` reporters take SNAP up and non-reporters draw to the `snap_take_up` stage's national FNS rate, and TANF uses the take-up contract's seeded rate. Materialize records the seed and an assignment digest, and the consumer export must reproduce it. The reviewed-null fill refuses to default-fill either column, finalize hard-fails on a new per-spine `acs_local_take_up_signal` gate (missing, constant, unanchored or out-of-band take-up), and packaging requires that gate passing and bound to the packaged H5. The other ACS take-up flags stay a reviewed limitation (microcosm#1022).
