The SPI income stage anchors the drawn copies' Gift Aid and charitable investment gifts to the SPI tape's own weighted totals (#1428; María's ruling of 2026-10-31). Both are sparse leaves that the stage-1 chain draws after, and conditioned on, the income leaves, so a change to the savings draws reshuffles which few donors' gifts the copies draw. Each column's total over the drawn copies, at the stage's household weights, is scaled to the tape's FACT-weighted total. A column that the tape or the copies carry none of is left as drawn. The scaling fixes the totals, not how concentrated each column is. The stage evidence records each column's factor, never a total or a count.
