Graph evidence now reads a file's bounded bytes, without parsing it as evidence JSON, wherever it only hashes or copies them: checkpoint provenance references, the upstream copies `save_graph_schema` makes, and the re-check and flattening in `publish_run_evidence`. A full-data UK spine manifest is within the 32 MiB byte bound but past the metadata bound that parsing applies (1.4 million items), so every UK build from a full-data spine failed while saving its graph schema (`--input-h5`) or publishing its spine checkpoint evidence (`--spine-request`).
