`ALLPAY2`, the FRS 2024-25 "Amount of allowance: organisation", now counts once on the UK spine, in `miscellaneous_income` (microcosm#1320, uk-data#627; María's ruling of 2026-10-20). It was summed into both `miscellaneous_income` and `private_transfer_income`, so every total that adds the two counted an organisation's allowance twice; `private_transfer_income` now sums `APAMT`, `APDAMT`, `PAREAMT`, `ALLPAY3` and `ALLPAY4`. A new test raises each field of an FRS household's adult, child, household, odd-job and pension rows in turn and refuses any field that moves more than one person income column. The spousal-maintenance split #1320 also raises waits on uk-data#627. The H2 spine fixture is regenerated.
