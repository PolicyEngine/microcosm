A second UK local release build can reuse the first build's solve after a gate-only sign-off (microcosm#1301). The nodes the dense solve depends on now key on a solve fingerprint, `ResolvedCountrySpec.fingerprint_excluding`, over every packaged UK resource except `uk_runtime.solve_scope.UK_GATE_ONLY_RESOURCES`: `target_fit_reviewed_exclusions.json`, `local_target_fit_reviewed_exclusions.json`, `input_mass_reviewed_exclusions.json` and `local_area_support_exclusions.json`. These are the target compilation, selection, measure and problem kernels and the compilation node's `country_contract_sha256`, the rotated holdout, the spine gates, and the source preflight, which now runs under its own kernel, `uk.full-gates.preflight@1`. The calibrated gates keep the full fingerprint, so a sign-off re-keys them and what follows them, never the solve; each gate kernel refuses the other's phase. The degenerate register stays in the solve fingerprint, since the release contract pins its digest, and the national graph keeps the full one. `--gate-review-date` sets the calibrated gates' own clock, by default `--review-date`; it may not precede the solve clock or, when given, be later than today, and the export bindings record both clocks as `review_date` and `gate_review_date`. `--require-solve-reuse` requires the spine, preflight and every other strict ancestor of `uk.full.gates.calibrated` from the graph store before calibration, and fails with `SolveReuseError` naming the missing nodes rather than re-solving; it is refused with `--resume forbid`. Before anything runs, it is also refused without `--graph-store` naming build 1's store, since the default lies under `--out`; with a gate clock outside the window of any entry the build depends on: before the approval or after the expiry of a gate-only or degenerate-register entry the calibrated gates hold on the local line, or after the expiry of a measure exclusion the solve applied. The refusal names those entries and the usable range, which ends at the drafter's assembly deadline (the two share `rowwise_cli.earliest_expiry`, and a lockstep test keeps them aligned). And, as on the national line, it is refused into an `--out` that already holds an attempt (`build.json`, `failure.json` or a Logbook spool row). The national role refuses both flags. A new test scans the build source and tools so that no target-compile, solve, spine or generator module names a gate-only register, checks that every gate reading one is a terminal gate, and perturbs each one against every solve kernel's hash. Node keys from target compilation down, and an in-graph spine's gates and the stages after them, move once, so no store from before this change is reused.
