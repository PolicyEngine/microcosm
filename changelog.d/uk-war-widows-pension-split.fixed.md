The FRS spine writes War Widow's or Widower's Pension (FRS benefit code 9) to policyengine-uk's own `war_widows_pension_reported` input, added in 2.135.7 (pe-uk#2382), instead of adding it to `bsp_reported` (#1222): the pension rises with CPI, while Bereavement Support Payment's rates are frozen. An SPI-channel row keeps its FRS twin's value of the new column, as it does for `bsp_reported`, and the column follows `bsp_reported` through the graph, the SPI stages, the benefit coherence gate and the H2 fixture.
