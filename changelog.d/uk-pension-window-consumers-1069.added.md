Add the consumer operations the UK pension targets need (microcosm#1069 c2). The new `monthly_window_count_x_mean` value operation averages a published count times its published mean over declared months. Two operands name the count and mean concepts, since DWP names its measures `recipients` and `mean_weekly_amount`, not `*_count` and `*_mean`, and the mean operand's `period_factor` annualises a weekly mean (52). Each month's count and mean must come from the same published cell and publication, even though Stat-Xplore files them in sibling record sets. The engine-index uprating applier restates each window month's mean at the rate the engine pays in the calibration year: April onward at that year's rate, January to March at the previous year's. Only a February point moves, per ruling R1. The UK State Pension level compiles from the Stat-Xplore cube to £138.6bn for Great Britain and £3.7bn for Northern Ireland in calendar 2025 at 2025 rates. The amount-band parser reads DWP's half-open labels ("£20.00 to under £40.00", "Under £20.00") with their own published upper edges. `dwp_state_pension` and `dwp_pension_credit` join the source-month families. The generator pins `dfc_ni.*` targets to Northern Ireland and `dwp.winter_fuel_payment.*` to England and Wales (`K04000001`, now a labelled geography).
