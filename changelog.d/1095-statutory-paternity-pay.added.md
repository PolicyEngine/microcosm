The UK root stage sets `statutory_paternity_pay` from the FRS adult table's SPPADJ ("SPP amount", a weekly amount for the adult's main job), annualised like `statutory_sick_pay` and `statutory_maternity_pay` beside it (uk-data#575, microcosm#1095). Nothing set it before, so every adult had none. policyengine-uk reads it in Class 1 National Insurance earnings and in the Universal Credit and Tax-Free Childcare paid-work tests. A tab without SPPADJ refuses the build. The column is new on the export, so it joins the reviewed extra columns of the export-surface gate; few adults report it, so its signed entry gives survey support as a band only.
