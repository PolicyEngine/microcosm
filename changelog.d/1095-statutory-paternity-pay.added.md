The UK root stage sets `statutory_paternity_pay` from the FRS adult table's SPPADJ ("SPP amount", a weekly amount for the adult's main job), annualised like `statutory_sick_pay` and `statutory_maternity_pay` beside it (uk-data#575, microcosm#1095). Nothing set it before, so every adult had none. policyengine-uk reads it in Class 1 National Insurance earnings and in the Universal Credit and Tax-Free Childcare paid-work tests, and from 2.128.0 also as Universal Credit earned income and in household benefits and HBAI income (pe-uk#2365). On an engine before 2.128.0 the column was only a National Insurance base, so it lowered the net income of the households that report it; this PR locks 2.130.1. Whether SPPADJ times the weeks in a year (365.25/7) is the right annual amount for a payment that lasts at most two weeks is still open (uk-data#575): it matches sick and maternity pay, and a grossed weekly amount from a reference week is the FRS's usual annual estimate in aggregate, but not per person. A tab without SPPADJ refuses the build. The column is new on the export, so it joins the reviewed extra columns of the export-surface gate; few adults report it, so its signed entry gives survey support as a band only.
