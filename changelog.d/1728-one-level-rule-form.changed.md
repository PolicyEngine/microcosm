Each country now declares how to read a target's geography level in one form, a `level_rule` (microcosm#1728, from the review of microcosm#1755). `us/spec/target_sets.yaml` gives the US rule in full: the first of `ledger_geography_level` and `geography_level` that has a value, any level accepted. The short `level_keys` form is removed from `microcosm.calibrate.target_sets`, with `target_level`; `target_set_census` takes the rule. `us_runtime.target_loss_weights.target_geography_level` reads the declared rule in place of its own copy of that reading, so a US target has one level in the selection, in the loss weights and in the summaries. No level changes: `test_us_target_sets.py` holds the reader to a verbatim copy of the replaced code, and on the pinned feed every compiled target sets `ledger_geography_level` only.
