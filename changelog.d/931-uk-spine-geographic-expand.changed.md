The UK spine gets its geographic clones after its source stages (#931). `frs_hmrc_spine_leaves` and `frs_annual_income_spells`, which join the raw FRS tabs on raw person ids, move ahead of `frs_take_up`, and two new stages follow them: `geographic_expand` (G1) gives every FRS household K geographic clones, and `geographic_identity` (G1b) keys each clone. `frs_take_up`, `frs_person_draws`, `frs_household_draws` and `frs_brma` then run on every clone (María's ruling of 10 Nov, decision 3). The spine build runs one clone by default, so no value moves. Positional stage numbers in the #931 fragments, comments and tests still follow the order before this change: 14 is `frs_hmrc_spine_leaves`, 15 `frs_annual_income_spells`, 10 to 13 the draws and `frs_brma`, and 16 onward as before.

- **Order.** The two moved stages read only employment and self-employment income, age and gender. The draws read none of their outputs and write none of their inputs, and none of these stages sets weights. `frs_brma` stays the assembled checkpoint's last stage, so the assembled phase now covers the moved stages, G1, G1b and the draws, and the assembled gate admits `spi_support_channel`. (Later in #931 `area_brma` takes its place and the boundary with it: `931-spine-assigns-areas`.)
- **Declarations.** The sources schema and `ALLOWED_SOURCE_OPERATION_KINDS` gain two strict operation kinds.
  - `expand_geographic_clones` declares the entity, the clone-index columns, the ordinal bound of 100, the copy-id table's `geographic_support` field at radix 10^9, clone-major order, an equal split of design weights, the household source columns, conservation to the split's rounding, a declared factor of 1.0 and a mass reason that names no clone count.
  - `key_households_by_clone_path` declares the source `frs` and `frs_release.json`, which G1b also declares as its one artifact with `runtime_sha256_required`, so the release's bytes enter its stage contract.

  Each transform refuses any other declaration of its operation. G1 refuses any artifact, and G1b any but that one.
- **Clone count.** No declaration names K. `uk_spine_graph(geographic_clones=K)` puts it on G1's node, a whole number from 1 to 100, and refuses K above 1 for a manifest without G1. So K enters that node's key and every later one, never a stage contract.
  - G1 is an EXPAND that conserves mass at design weights. Its kernel refuses a transform whose clone count differs from its node's, printing only the two counts.
  - The spine build runs G1 at the clone count `--n-clones` asks for, one by default, and builds its engine with that count. `_rules_engine`'s `clones` defaults to None, so the full build's engine installs are unchanged.
  - The H2 registry builds G1 and its engine from the fixture's `config.geographic_clones`.
  - The dense line's `uk.full.identity` declares the geography key as a rewrite where the spine carries it, so the dense graph still compiles.
- **Source columns.** `source_household_id`, `source_year` and `source_household_key` are G1's outputs. `spi_support_channel` no longer declares or writes them, and its copies inherit them. It and the dense expansion default them from a household's own ids only on a table without `household_clone_index`, and refuse, naming the columns, a clone-indexed table that lacks any of them. `source_person_id` and `source_benunit_id` are not minted (decision 5). Their names live in the `geographic_clones` leaf beside the clone-index names (`UK_GEOGRAPHIC_SOURCE_COLUMNS`, `SOURCE_YEAR_COLUMN`, `SOURCE_HOUSEHOLD_KEY_COLUMN`), and so does the geography key's (`GEOGRAPHY_HOUSEHOLD_KEY_COLUMN`, which `atomic_area_support.IDENTITY_COLUMN` re-exports). So G1, G1b, the SPI support channel and the dense line read one definition, which their keys hash.
- **Counting and folding.** `frs_take_up`, `frs_person_draws` and `frs_household_draws` are given `source_household_id` and `household_clone_index`, so their receipts count true source households on every clone. `spi_employment_shell`, `spi_housing_shell`, `uc_capital_coherence` and `property_components` are given `household_clone_index`.
- **Root carriers.** `frs_spine` writes two typed household carriers, and `council_tax_band` itself is unchanged:
  - `imd_decile` (int64): the household's decile on its own nation's index of multiple deprivation (IMDE, IMDW, IMDS or IMDN), 1 to 10, else 0;
  - `council_tax_band_observed` (bool): False in Northern Ireland, for a code outside 1 to 9, for a band the household's region does not use and for an unknown region.

  The root refuses a household tab without one of the four IMD variables, naming it. Both carriers leave at the release boundary and stay out of every receipt and of the spine sidecar's nonzero shares. The root binds `calibrate.geography_constants`, from which both read the household's nation. The area assignment of #931 group (c) is their first reader.
- **microdf.** microdf-python's version joins every spine node's numerical dependencies (`not-installed` when absent), and the sidecar records it as `microdf_python` beside `engine_batches` when a call batches (decision 2). `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` are unchanged; the next lock bump declares it.
- **Texts.**
  - The `copy_rule` of both support splits, `high_value_home_support` (stage 26) and `cgt_support_split` (stage 42), names the order the exact-total candidates are tried in.
  - The notes of `isa_subscriptions` (stage 53) say tied balances share one weighted mid-rank and the Lifetime ISA reuses `balance_rank_weight` (decision 1), and `docs/uk-isa-subscriptions-1415.md` steps 3, 5 and 6 follow the code.
  - The weight rule of `spi_income_band_donors` (stage 17) names one clone's donor count, and its draw a spine without the clone index.
  - The bonus draw of `salary_sacrifice` (stage 48) draws within the adult's own clone from cells pooled over every clone, and says the lowest cell joins the one above.
  - The source and notes of `spi_support_channel` (stage 16) and `private_school_attendance` (stage 52), the notes of `spi_income_band_donors`, `cgt_support_split`, `salary_sacrifice` and `uc_capital_coherence`, and `frs_spine`'s notes (the two carriers) say what each does on a clone-first spine.
  - `childcare_copy_take_up`'s notes say `frs_take_up` draws each geographic clone on its own ids before any other copy exists, and that geographic clones keep those draws.
  - Earlier #931 fragments that said G1 was unwired, or that no spine carries a clone index, now say what this change does.
- **Bindings.** The national gates hash `geographic_clones`, since their support-family fold reads the clone index on the national frame of a clone-first spine. The copy-id readers gain G1. The stage table keeps only the source column's name, with its reason rewritten, and the kernel table's clone-index reason no longer covers the national gates.
- **Dense and national.** The dense expansion refuses a spine carrying G1's clone index at any `n_clones`, naming #931 group (c), which builds the dense line on one. No national or dense build runs on such a spine before (c) (decision 4). The coverage manifest's new `geographic_expand` family requires G1's mass record under its declared reason, so a release from a spine built before this change fails the coverage gate too.
- **What moves.** No value at one clone. On the synthetic H2 fixture, the legacy oracle's final frame keeps the parent's rows, existing columns, weights, strata and mass records, in a new column order. It gains the three clone indices at 0, `geography_household_key` (which copies made after G1b repeat until (c)), the two root carriers and G1's mass record, and the funding strata of `spi_income_band_donors` read `REGION|0`.
  - Every UK spine node re-keys. Seven stage contracts move with their declarations (`frs_spine`, `spi_support_channel`, `spi_income_band_donors`, `high_value_home_support`, `cgt_support_split`, `salary_sacrifice`, `private_school_attendance`), and G1's and G1b's are new.
  - Outside the UK, adding the two kinds to `source_manifest` re-keys the shared transport gate battery, so NZ's terminal gate and its export and package nodes re-key. The US, NZ, BE and AM specs keep their fingerprints.
  - `source_stages.json`, the H2 fixture and the coverage manifest are regenerated.
