`tools/compare_uk_firm_taxes_to_receipts.py` compares weighted policyengine-uk firm tax totals for 2024-25 with official receipts (HMRC cash receipts for VAT, employer NICs, Apprenticeship Levy, onshore corporation tax and Bank Surcharge; MHCLG NNDR3 England business rates), committed with URL, edition and access date in `tests/fixtures/uk/firm_tax_receipts_2024_25.csv` (microcosm#1705). It refuses a tax total without an explicit `inputs_populated` flag, prints "input not populated" rather than a gap where a tax's inputs are unset, flags the VAT row as an in-sample check because the generator is calibrated to HMRC VAT liability, and flags the employer NICs and Apprenticeship Levy rows as resting on an assumed paybill (generator headcount times ONS ASHE section mean pay), so they are not a validation.
