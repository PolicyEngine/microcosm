A single PUF-channel QRF draw can no longer carry more mass than the population slice its recipient stands for. The #982 income-rank predictor sends the top-ranked survey tax units, each standing for several hundred units, onto the PUF's top-ranked returns, which stand for one return or less: the synthetic Forbes-tail records weigh about 0.13 each. One such draw put $141B of line-32 mass on a single weight-644 unit in the #1251 rebuild. QRF finalization now caps each raw amount draw at the positive mass, per unit of slice weight, of the donor's top slice of the recipient's own weight share, and floors losses from the bottom slice, on every amount output (booleans and origination years excepted). The rule is bound into the primary PUF execution config, which moves to schema 6; every bounded output writes a tail-bound diagnostics record.
