The UK national and full-build solves apply a signed sparse-band support floor to the UC payment-distribution bands (microcosm#1095). `uk/calibration_measure_exclusions.json` moves to schema 3 and declares the floor once, in a `support_floor` block that María signed on 2026-10-16 (expires 2027-01-08): a band row of the four `dwp.uc.payment_distribution_*` targets whose compiled matrix row reaches fewer than 10 distinct source households (`household_source_id`) with a positive prior weight is dropped from the solve, not pooled, because the solve could fit it only by stretching those few weights and a fit that rests on fewer than 10 source households is a cell the release must not report. The floor runs on the compiled problem, before the loss weights, families and target metadata are drawn, in the national problem node and in the full build's measures node. Its receipt reports each family's dropped published total, and the run refuses outside the approval window, when the drops pass the block's ceilings on rows or on published share, and when a per-row exclusion names a band the floor would drop (the national node compiles those rows only to measure them). The drops join the problem's measure exclusions as `kind: support_floor`; the national gates, the written diagnostics, the full-build target-surface and reference-coverage gates and the release certifier read them back and check them against the declared floor, since none of them can recompute support. The register written as the scoring surface stays the approved one. On the head spine the floor drops 19 band rows, 3.1% of the bands' published total, against ceilings of 24 rows and 5%, which leave a margin of five rows and about two points: ten bands the solve bound before and the nine that per-row exclusions held out, whose entries retire. The other twelve per-row band entries are well supported and stay, each on its own renewal.
