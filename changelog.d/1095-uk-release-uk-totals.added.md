The UK release now carries the UK totals of the components of policyengine-uk's two share keys. These are `corporate_wealth`, `private_pension_wealth` and the twelve spending categories (policyengine-uk 2.178.0, PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2901), as fourteen `uk_total_*` household columns, the same on every row. The release boundary writes them as each component's sum at the file's household weights, summing them again after the weights move to the dataset year, as `policyengine_uk.utils.uk_totals.uk_totals` sums them (`national_frame.uk_household_totals`). policyengine-uk's `shareholding` and `consumption_shareholding` then divide by the UK's totals. So a simulation of part of the file, such as a region, an extract or a household subset, gives each household its share of the national totals: its business rates, corporate Stamp Duty Land Tax and land, consumer-incident tax changes and the carbon tax's corporate part. Before this, each part shared the national totals out among its own households alone. A simulation of the whole file reads the same shares as before. The fourteen inputs leave `UK_ENGINE_DEFAULT_INPUTS`, and the export surface allows them as new since the reference build (María's call of 19 November 2026; her ruling D-e on PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2896).
