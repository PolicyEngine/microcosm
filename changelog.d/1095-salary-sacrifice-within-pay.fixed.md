The UK `salary_sacrifice` stage limits every salary-sacrifice amount to the pay it comes out of, porting the final rule of uk-data#518 (microcosm#1095). A sacrifice is pay given up for a pension contribution, so no amount, reported or predicted, now exceeds the pay the person still receives, and a person without pay has none; microcosm#1089 had done this only for people not asked the question. The limit bites above all on SPI-channel rows, which keep their FRS twin's answer beside pay redrawn from the SPI. The forest trains only on respondents with pay. The headcount conversion moves a contribution into salary sacrifice up to half the pay and leaves the rest as an employee contribution; it moved the whole contribution before and floored pay at zero, which could leave a converted record with a sacrifice and no pay. The stage refuses if any sacrifice ends above the pay that remains. policyengine-uk 2.126.2 ignores a sacrifice with no pay behind it (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2063), so the stored column now agrees with what the engine relieves. The stage's manifest gains `clamp_maximum` and `move_pay_share_cap`, the evidence records how many amounts the limit and the half-pay cap touched, and the identity tool restores a converted record's contribution as what it kept plus what moved.
