The ACS local release takes `--l2-anchor` and `--l2-anchor-weights`, which it passes to the shared solver's `l2_anchor` and `l2_anchor_weights`. `initial` (default) is each record's own starting weight, as every solve has used; `uniform` is the mean starting weight; `--l2-anchor-weights FILE` makes the `--l2-lambda` penalty pull toward a declared design without a change to the starting weights. The default adds no keyword to the solve and no key to the solver stamp (microcosm#1729).
