`TargetRegistry` checks that one geography id has exactly one label across a registry (microcosm#1335). A US registry refuses a second label with `GeographyLabelConflictError`, naming the id, both labels and the first target carrying each; every other country's registry reports it as a `GeographyLabelConflictWarning`, because UK publishers name some regions, nations and local authorities differently. Builds assemble their registry before calibration, and the US ACS local materialize stage assembles its admin and ladder population targets into one registry before the engine pass, so a conflict stops a build at its start. A lean checkpoint written before this change with two labels for one US geography is refused when the calibrate stage reloads it, with a message to re-run `--stage materialize`.
