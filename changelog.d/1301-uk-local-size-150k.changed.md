A sized UK local release now ships 150,000 households instead of 100,000 (microcosm#1124 as amended for microcosm#1301): at K=15 the size search could not reach 100,000 above the solver's L0 floor of 1e-7. The K=15 pool, the exact-count draw at π_hi 0.5, the refit baseline floor of 0.5 and the chi-square refit L2 penalty are unchanged, and the ruling text no longer names the rotated holdout or the incumbent-surface comparison as publication requirements. The size search runs in its own L0 bracket, from the new `UK_LOCAL_L0_SEARCH_FLOOR` (1e-10) up to 10; the bracket is a parameter of the search, draw, refit and holdout nodes, and the size receipt records it as `selection_search_bracket`. `--selection-initial-lambda` outside that bracket, or without `--dataset-households`, is refused when the command is parsed, and the national role refuses the flag. The pre-flight checks the 150,000-household doctrine, the search bracket of a sized run and the new doctrine constants.
