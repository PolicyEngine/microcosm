Nine national target rows bind DfT BUS01a local bus passenger journeys for FY2024-25 in London and the eight other English regions (family `dft_bus_journeys`, microcosm#1207). The frame carries NTS trips, not boardings, so the resolver's new household measures `bus_in_london_boardings` and `other_local_bus_boardings` price each household's trips at the boardings per trip that the `lcfs_consumption` stage priced each series at. A persisted column of either name is refused. London's row counts every resident's bus-in-London trips. Each other region counts its residents' other local bus trips. The rows constrain the frame's trip level through London and England outside London, and add the split between the eight regions.
