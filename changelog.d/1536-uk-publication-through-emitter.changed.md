After the UK repository's switch to `releases.json`, the UK national, dense and local-area releases publish and change their current build only through the emitter (microcosm#1536). The UK national and dense assemblers write build-folder manifests (the build is the calibration attempt ID; `build_id`, `attempt_id` and `cut_tag` are gone, and the national assembler's `--cut-tag` option with them) and print the `microcosm-publish-release-build` publication and promotion commands. The release contract checks those releases in the build-folder layout only and refuses the tagged one. `microcosm-publish-release` and `tools/publish_release.sh` refuse them and lose `--promote-line` and line promotion, so they publish only releases that keep tags and `latest.json`, which are every US release until its stable releases are agreed. When the emitter refuses a change with `POINTER_FILES_PRESENT`, or the command's own listing finds a `latest*.json` file, the advice is to remove the file that appeared and run the command again, not to publish through `tools/publish_release.sh`. The June 2023 UK release and `populace-uk-<year>-<tier>-k<N>` releases have no publication path in the UK repository after its switch.
