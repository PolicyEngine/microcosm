The Carer's Allowance take-up draw (`carers_allowance_take_up`) now draws Scotland too (María's ruling of 2026-10-23; microcosm#1319). In Scotland the claim flag is Carer Support Payment's, and since policyengine-uk 2.146.1 (PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#2473) the engine pays it only under its own earnings limit. So a Scottish carer whom the 2024-25 limit kept from claiming is drawn at the newly entitled rate as elsewhere, and is paid only where Carer Support Payment's rules allow. The receipt reports Scotland apart (`scotland_eligible_support`, `scotland_eligible_weighted_persons`, `scotland_drawn_support`) in place of the closed-region check.
