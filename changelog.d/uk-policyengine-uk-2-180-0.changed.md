The UK lock moves from policyengine-uk 2.179.2 to 2.180.0 (microcosm#1710), the newest release; the UK side's policyengine-core stays at 3.34.10 and the US side at 3.32.5. 2.180.0's stored-draw check still names only policyengine-core 3.34.9 as qualified (`QUALIFIED_CORE_VERSIONS`), as 2.179.2's did. 2.180.0 adds one engine input, `would_claim_uc_housing_costs_element` (policyengine-uk#2951), whose false leaves the Universal Credit housing costs element out of an award; the new `uc_housing_costs_claim` stage stores it (B1 of microcosm#1710). The other rule changes since 2.179.2: Housing Benefit takes the rent, less the meals deduction, as the eligible rent of a private renter in a houseboat, caravan or mobile home instead of capping it at the Local Housing Allowance (2.180.0; the spine records `accommodation_type`), and the Universal Credit education protection for a claimant moved off legacy benefits now needs a full-time course that reg 12(2) covers (2.179.4). The texts that describe the locked engine were checked against the diff from 2.179.2 to 2.180.0 and now cite 2.180.0; the concept coverage (404 inputs), the uprating pins and bonus anchor (stamps only), the core pin test, `APPROVED_UV_LOCK_SHA256`, the release input coverage manifest and the H2 spine fixture follow.
