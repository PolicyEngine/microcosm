`parameter_gated_threshold` bindings now honour their `filters`, `household_conditions` and a declared `groupby_variable` band after the gate, and accept a geography fan-out predicate, so a gated measure (the AEA-gated CGT taxpayer proxy) can be sliced into published age, region and size-of-gain rows instead of publishing the national population on every row. Numeric universe constraints on a Chronicle fact (`cgt_gain >= 3000`, `age >= 16`) are stamped as `ledger_filter_<variable>_lower_bound` / `_upper_bound` metadata so banded measures can read edges that publishers state as constraints rather than dimensions.
