The UK SPI income stage's stage-1 tape and reference forests now read the SPI age band, not an age (uk-data#628). The 2022-23 SPI tape gives age only as a band (AGERANGE), so the age the stage draws for each donor inside its band carried no income pattern: the forest learned none, and FRS twins in one band were drawn from conditionals that differed only by that noise. The predictor `spi_age_band` is the tape's own AGERANGE code, with composite records (-1) as a band of their own, and for a frame person the band of their whole-year age; `at_or_over_spa` still carries the State Pension split inside the 65-74 band. The cross-fit fold cells key on the band too, since they key on the predictor values. The drawn donor ages are kept for the band pools' State Pension side. The stage receipt's `stage1_age_predictor` names the rule.
