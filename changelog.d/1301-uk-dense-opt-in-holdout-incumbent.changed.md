The dense UK line's rotated holdout and incumbent-surface comparison are opt-in diagnostics rather than publication requirements (microcosm#1301). `--rotated-holdout` replaces `--skip-holdout`, which is removed with no alias: a dense build skips the holdout unless asked and records the skip, and the national role refuses the new flag. The pre-flight accepts a holdout that was not requested and still requires five folds and a finite mean when it was, in agreement with the request. The scorer reads the active local reference count from the membership file and scores a candidate with no holdout as `holdout_basis: none_requested`. `tools/evaluate_uk_dataset_size.py` runs steps 30 and 40 only with `--incumbent-diagnostic` or an explicit `--steps`. Certification readiness is non-gating: an absent scorecard is `not_requested` and one that misses only its measured limits is `diagnostic_failed`. The release contract always joins the candidate's own evidence to the released bytes; the score, the surface evaluation and the incumbent extraction manifest ship all together or not at all, their identities refuse, and their measured limits are recorded in the manifest rather than enforced. The source coverage carries a `holdout.claim`, an `incumbent.compared` and a `size` discriminator. The assembler takes `--incumbent-manifest` as optional, requires the run's full gate report, prints a `--tag-only` publish command, and writes the limitations the contract checks against the coverage: `no_out_of_sample_claim` or `rotated_holdout` (`claim: report_only`), `no_incumbent_comparison` or `incumbent_surface_diagnostic`, `engine_measurement`, `size_doctrine` for a sized release, and `reviewed_exclusion:<gate>:<name>` for the reviewed exclusions `uk_target_fit`, `uk_local_target_fit` and `uk_input_mass_parity` stood on, with names and reasons only. The coverage records those names per gate under `signed_deferrals.reviewed_exclusions`, and the contract requires exactly those limitations. The full gate report does not ship, so the build records the names each of those gates stood on in the candidate manifest's `reviewed_exclusions_stood_on`, and the contract holds the coverage's names to that record; it also checks the `uk_local_target_fit` names against the signed local gate report. A dense manifest must carry its `area_support` block, whose `below_floor` codes are exactly the areas the signed report's area-support exclusions stood on. The coverage's holdout and size records and the `engine_measurement` and `size_doctrine` limitations must restate the shipped candidate manifest, so an edited coverage cannot switch the holdout claim or the size.
