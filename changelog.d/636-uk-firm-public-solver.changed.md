The UK firm generator solves its weights with the public `microcosm.calibrate.solve.solve_constraint_system` on a float32 sparse matrix, instead of the solver's private `_optimize` (microcosm#636). A local check on the small test fixture, across three configurations, gave weights bit-identical to the old path; CI does not pin this, and a test now refuses any private import from `microcosm.calibrate`.
