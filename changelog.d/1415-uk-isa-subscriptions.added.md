The UK spine imputes ISA subscriptions per adult (microcosm#1415). A new last stage, `isa_subscriptions`, splits each household's cash and stocks-and-shares ISA balances across its adults (`cash_isa_balance`, `stocks_and_shares_isa_balance`) and draws who paid into which type in 2024-25 and how much (`cash_isa_subscriptions`, `stocks_and_shares_isa_subscriptions`): subscribers and their amounts follow HMRC's 2023-24 Table 9.7 by income, size of subscription and type paid into, raked to Tables 9.8 and 9.9, and grow to 2024-25 by Table 9.4's account and amount growth within the £20,000 overall limit. Every Lifetime ISA holder aged 18 to 49 pays in (`lifetime_isa_payments`), drawn to Table 9.4's 2024-25 Lifetime ISA amount, so policyengine-uk's Lifetime ISA bonus is no longer zero. No ISA fact is a calibration target; the national build reports them as checks. See `docs/uk-isa-subscriptions-1415.md`.
