A failed best-effort upload after a passed staging read-back no longer fails the build (microcosm#1301). Once read-back has given its verdict, the verdict owns the delivery's `last_error_code`: the refresh that follows a passed read-back cannot set `UPLOAD_FAILED`, which the delivery contract refused as a contradiction and which escaped the dense driver before it recorded the staging delivery and the Logbook row. A failed refresh stays visible as an upload attempt without a success, and the remote copy may still show the delivery as it was before read-back.
