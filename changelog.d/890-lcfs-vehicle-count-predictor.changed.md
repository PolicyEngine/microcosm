Retire the UK LCFS stage's WAS-to-LCFS has-fuel bridge (microcosm#890 A and E2). The consumption QRF now conditions on the vehicle count on both sides: LCFS `a124` on the donor and the `was_wealth` draw of WAS `vcarnr8` on the recipient (the FRS carries no vehicle variable), both rounded and clipped to the declared 0-5, replacing the bridged 0/1 `has_fuel_consumption` predictor whose donor side was an uninformative QRF prediction of a random flag. The recipient fuel flag stays `num_vehicles > 0` times an identity-keyed draw, but its rate is now one minus the zero-emission share of the vendored DfT VEH1103 licensed-car stock (2024, Great Britain, battery-electric and fuel-cell cars; plug-in hybrids count as fuel buyers), declared on the stage's `assign_binary_from_rate` operation in place of the hand-copied `nts_ice_share` anchor. The stage records a `has_fuel_consumption` evidence block (the rate receipt with source record ids, design-weighted vehicle and positive-fuel shares on the donor, vehicle and flagged shares on the recipient). `lcfs_consumption_anchors.json` and the `was_bridge_donor` artifact are removed, `bridge_donor_column_via_qrf` leaves the manifest vocabulary, `--was-tab` is no longer required to build the LCFS stage, and the graph's `lcfs_consumption` node reads only the two LCFS sources. The LCFS and ETB stages now declare the vendored resources their operations read as `public_aggregate_reference` artifacts, and the vendor register lists both stage modules as present consumers (each module refuses a declaration naming a resource it does not consume).
