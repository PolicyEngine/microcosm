The UK local surface binds pension income by area (#1418): the new `hmrc.pension_income.amount` rows give each constituency's and local authority's taxpayers' private and State Pension income together, from HMRC SPI 2023-24 Tables 3.15 and 3.14 (count times mean). They are grown to calendar 2025 by HMRC's all-ranges total income growth in ITL Table 2.5, the growth the national State Pension and private pension bands show together to within 0.1%. A fan-out sum bridge then reconciles them per grain to those bands summed, the first bridge to sum two national fan-outs. At source the bands sit 3.0% above the constituency cells and 3.2% above the local-authority cells: the area tables leave out the taxpayers HMRC places in no area, who are more often pensioners. The bridge refuses a source gap past 4% or an index gap past 0.6%. The frame reads each taxpayer's private and State Pension over the year, `total_pension_income_annual`. Two authorities with no SPI measures, as for employment, are signed deferrals, and the metric is appended last at both grains so no earlier metric renumbers. The `hmrc.pension_income.count` rows bind the taxpayers with either pension at HMRC's own area level, grown by ITL Table 2.5's all-ranges taxpayer growth. No national row counts that union: the national bands count State Pension and private pension recipients apart, and most taxpayers of State Pension age are both. The rows are declared a subset of ITL's taxpayers and a local family with no higher control.
