The UK local surface binds pension income by area (#1418): the new `hmrc.pension_income.amount` rows give each constituency's and local authority's taxpayers' private and State Pension income together, from HMRC SPI 2023-24 Tables 3.15 and 3.14 (count times mean). They are grown to calendar 2025 by HMRC's all-ranges total income growth in ITL Table 2.5, 1.5% below the growth the national State Pension and private pension bands show together (×1.134 against ×1.151). A fan-out sum bridge then reconciles them per grain to those bands summed, the first bridge to sum two national fan-outs. At source the bands sit 3.0% above the constituency cells and 3.2% above the local-authority cells: the area tables leave out the taxpayers HMRC places in no area, who are more often pensioners. So the reconciled cells take ×1.045 at both grains on the compiled surface, where the two authorities without SPI measures are implied from the constituencies. The bridge refuses a source factor outside 2.5% to 3.7% above one, since the gap runs one way, or an index gap past 2%: each bound is the measured gap plus or minus half a point. HMRC rounds the area counts to the thousand, so the rounded-thousand floor (`uk/local_small_cell_rule.json`) defers the 20 count cells it publishes at 5,000 or fewer (17 constituencies, 3 local authorities), where the rounding can reach 10%, reading each at its published value, before the index, and their amount cells with them, since each amount is the count times its mean (María's ruling of 2026-10-29). The deferred cells still count in their leg's control, grown by their bound neighbours' index, and in the bridge's totals at source. The frame reads each taxpayer's private and State Pension over the year, `total_pension_income_annual`. Two authorities with no SPI measures, as for employment, are signed deferrals, and the metric is appended last at both grains so no earlier metric renumbers. The `hmrc.pension_income.count` rows bind the taxpayers with either pension at HMRC's own area level, grown by ITL Table 2.5's all-ranges taxpayer growth. No national row counts that union: the national bands count State Pension and private pension recipients apart, and most taxpayers of State Pension age are both. The rows are declared a subset of ITL's taxpayers and a local family with no higher control.
