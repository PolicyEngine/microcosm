`tools/build_uk_e6_support_bounds.py` refuses to move a committed rounded support bound while the file's donor tab digests are unchanged (microcosm#1164). Each bound is a donor extreme rounded outward; re-derived from the same tabs after a change in the declared uprating, the cleaning or the floor, a bound that rounds to a different value narrows the extreme between the two thresholds. A lower bound may still move to zero, the floor, which reads no donor value.
