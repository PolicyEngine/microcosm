The UK LCFS stage holds each recomposed total, housing and transport, to the donor's realised maximum of that total, the support every drawn column is clipped to (microcosm#1164). The recomposition writes a total as its draw less the drawn parts plus the levelled parts, so a remainder near the top of the diary's range plus a levelled energy bill larger than the drawn one could leave the committed support bound the terminal `uk_support` gate holds the emitted totals to; on the 2024-25 diary, whose housing bound is far tighter than 2023-24's, some households did. The `recompose_from_remainder` step declares `maximum_total: donor_support`; a held total never falls below its parts, the excess comes out of its remainder, and the receipt counts the rows held and the weighted mass taken, which the `recomposed_totals` gate's identities include.
