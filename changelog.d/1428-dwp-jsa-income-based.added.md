The national calibration binds DWP's income-based Jobseeker's Allowance spending, `dwp.jsa.amount_income_based` (benefit expenditure and caseload tables, Spring Forecast 2026), at the calendar-2025 window of the fiscal years, as the pension-age Housing Benefit row is (#1428; María's ruling of 2026-10-31). Income-based JSA closes with the move to Universal Credit: £91.0m in FY2024-25 and under £1m from FY2025-26. Without the split, the calibration meets OBR's total JSA by scaling income-based reporters together with contributory claimants, far past DWP's income-based spending.
