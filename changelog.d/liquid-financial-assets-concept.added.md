Add `fact:person.liquid_financial_assets` to the concept schema (version 2): a person's deposits, shares and investment-fund units, and bonds at the reference date, held as a quantile-mapped stock. The Axiom New Zealand mapping binds it to the Accommodation Supplement's `accommodation_supplement_cash_assets` as a deferred `Family` sum, which no code executes until benefit units are built. The policyengine-us, policyengine-uk and Axiom Belgium mappings list it as unmapped with a reason. The US reason names the donor columns `bank_account_assets`, `stock_assets` and `bond_assets`. The coverage reports and the schema digest are regenerated. The adapter modules themselves are unchanged, so no adapter source hash moves.
