A US national build no longer has a tier: the release tool takes `--evidence-release` only to preview a dry run, records every release-gate failure, writes its own files to `<out>/build/` and exits 3 when its release gates failed. `tools/certify_us_national_build.py` chooses the tier and places the build in that tier's release directory: certified by default, into `microcosm-us-2024-national`, or `--evidence-release` with `--evidence-failure-owners`, into `microcosm-us-2024-evidence` under the build ID with an `-evidence-` segment, once every failure has an owner. The standing owners moved from the tool to the packaged `us/evidence_failure_owners.json`, and a build may be certified at a later commit that changes only tests, docs, changelog fragments and that register; both manifests record the certifying commit in a new `certification` block. The build record is schema 3. What the written H5 fails in its own checks (the stored-input premise, the export audit) is recorded on a green build too, and no tier certifies it. A failed build's files are no `--incumbent-diagnostics`, and the release-gate preflight refuses them as its export input-mass reference. Route A's `ROUTE_A_CERTIFY_TIER` and `ROUTE_A_EVIDENCE_FAILURE_OWNERS` choose the tier, and its release stage keeps a build that failed its gates (microcosm#1585, item 11).
