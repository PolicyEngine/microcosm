A new spine stage, `property_purchases`, imputes each household's annual purchases of additional dwellings and of non-residential property (`additional_residential_property_purchased`, `non_residential_property_purchased`), porting uk-data#573 (a draft, at `07f6b460f`; uk-data#556; microcosm#1095). From policyengine-uk 2.134.1 (pe-uk#2241) both are inputs that default to nil, so without them a purchasing household pays SDLT on its main home only. The stage runs at the spine's design weights after the regional uprating and the high-value support copies:
- a household whose stock of a kind is at least £40,000, the chargeable consideration HMRC's Stamp Tax tables cover, can buy once a year, at a hazard proportional to its stock;
- the threshold on each household's exponential event score is set so that the weighted count of purchasers in England and Northern Ireland, where SDLT applies, comes closest to HMRC's transactions, and the same threshold applies in Scotland and Wales;
- each price is a band mean drawn at HMRC's band shares, times one scale fitted so the weighted England-and-Northern-Ireland value equals HMRC's, never below £40,000 and never above the household's stock.

The targets come from HMRC's Annual Stamp Tax Statistics 2024 to 2025 (corrected), vendored from the pinned Chronicle feed as `hmrc_stamp_tax_facts.json`: Table 6b's 2024-25 higher-rates transactions and value, which cover every buyer, allocated to households by individuals' shares of Table 8a's residential transactions and value, with Table 6b's bands and the five above £2m combined; and Table 8b's 2024-25 individuals for non-residential purchases. Receipts are checked, not fitted. The draws read identity-keyed uniforms, so they differ from uk-data's positional streams while following the same method.
