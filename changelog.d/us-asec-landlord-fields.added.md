US builds pooled from CPS ASEC now carry two raw Census household fields on the household table, beside `H_TENURE`: `HPUBLIC` (the household lives in a public housing project: 0 not in universe, 1 yes, 2 no; asked of every household not owner-occupied) and `HLORENT` (the rent is lower because a government pays part of it: 0, 1, 2; asked only where `HPUBLIC` is 2). Together they separate public-landlord tenants from subsidised private ones, which NZ's Accommodation Supplement needs and `receives_housing_assistance` cannot. Both are already in every processed `census_cps_YYYY.h5` household table (income years 2022-2025); they are mapped by `H_SEQ` exactly as `H_TENURE` is, a missing value or a code outside 0-2 is refused, and a pool that has them in some years but not others is refused. No stage reads them. PUF clone households should keep them as they keep `H_TENURE` (inferred, not run here); ACS rows in ACS-pooled builds have no such field and are left missing, as `H_TENURE` is.
