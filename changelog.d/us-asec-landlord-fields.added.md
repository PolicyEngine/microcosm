US builds pooled from CPS ASEC now carry two raw Census household fields on the household table, beside `H_TENURE`: `HPUBLIC` (the household lives in a public housing project: 0 not in universe, 1 yes, 2 no; asked of every household not owner-occupied) and `HLORENT` (the rent is lower because a government pays part of it: 0, 1, 2; asked only where `HPUBLIC` is 2). Together they separate public-landlord tenants from subsidised private ones, which NZ's Accommodation Supplement needs and `receives_housing_assistance` cannot. Both are already in every processed `census_cps_YYYY.h5` household table (income years 2022-2025); they are mapped by `H_SEQ` exactly as `H_TENURE` is, a missing value or a code outside 0-2 is refused, a pool that has them in some years but not others is refused, and duplicate household rows that disagree on any carried field (`H_TENURE` included) are refused instead of keeping the first row's codes. No stage reads them. Both PUF clone halves keep their CPS household's codes, and an ACS household in an ACS-pooled build, which has no such field, is left missing, as for `H_TENURE` (tested through the clone and the ACS spine, and through spine assembly, the clone and the nullable H5 export as the stacked build orders them; later stages and the release gates were not run with the new fields).
