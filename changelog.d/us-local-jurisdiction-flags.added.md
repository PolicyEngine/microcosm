Derive the policyengine-us input-only local flags `in_denver`, `in_yonkers`, `in_wilmington` and `pa_philadelphia_wage_tax_resident` from household geography through a SHA-pinned registry checked against the 2020 Gazetteer, P.L. 94-171 counts and the block ladder. The block-ladder line writes all four; the PUMA and pooled ACS lines write the county-rule flags and record the place-rule flags (Yonkers, Wilmington) as unavailable instead of writing `False`. The L0/refit export and the fiscal refresh release builder re-derive the flags from an existing base's own geography, and the builder derives them again after frozen-support selection and on the export support, so a base built before this change, or a selection that keeps only block-anchored rows, no longer exports without them. Both ladder gates now fail a table that lacks a flag its geography decides. The annual static-aging lane, which keeps its parent's columns and base-year bytes, now refuses a parent that lacks or contradicts a flag its geography decides, before any model work, and records the parent's flag receipt in the candidate manifest.
