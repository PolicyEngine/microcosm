UK builds no longer upload their graph to the runs application. Their staged dataset bundle, and a failed build's graph evidence bundle, now hand the graph schema (`graph.schema.json`), the execution overlay (`graph.execution.json`) and each linked `upstream-<sha256>.json` file to the emitter under the interface's Orrery input roles (`orrery_schema`, `execution_overlay`, `orrery_upstream`), and the failure bundle now names its build for the collector too. The emitter submits them to the collector as one `orrery-inputs` submission once the bundle's commit lands; every emitter a build starts has its collector submissions on. The collector builds the Orrery graph files and keeps a licensed build's private. Since a UK bundle with recorded execution now makes two collector submissions, the build warns about each one the collector or the emitter turns down, naming its kind, even while the other is still being sent or awaits its final status. `graph.orrery.json` is still written, for viewing offline. `--publish-orrery` now only prints a warning, and the build's Orrery receipt records `skipped` (`local_only`) whatever the flag says.
