The UK WAS wealth and Lifetime ISA stages (`was_wealth`, `was_lisa`) read household net income on HBAI's concept on both sides (#931), as the LCFS, ETB services and NTS stages do (microcosm#1164). The donor's income is WAS `DVTotinc_bhcR8`, net regular household income before housing costs less council tax. The recipient's is policyengine-uk's `hbai_household_net_income_before_reset_to_zero`, in place of `household_net_income`, which also takes off stamp duty, the TV licence and the business rates and corporate stamp duty that `shareholding` spreads over the households the engine simulates, and adds benefits in kind. Both sides call the predictor `hbai_household_net_income`.

- **What moves.** The WAS chain keeps its segments, order and seeds, but every WAS draw can move. So do the Lifetime ISA draws and the later stages that read wealth: means-tested capital, CGT selection, property components and high-value support. A sampled f010 arm measures the change.
- **Declarations.** `was_wealth` declares the donor column in its `direct_maps`, and the stage refuses a declared direct map its donor cleaning does not read. `was_lisa`'s declared engine reads now list what it materializes, without `num_adults` and `num_children`, which come from the FRS family roles. Its realised profile keys its income tertiles `by_hbai_household_net_income_tertile`, and the ownership coefficients `log1p_hbai_household_net_income`.
