The UK WAS wealth and Lifetime ISA stages (`was_wealth`, `was_lisa`) match the WAS donor's household net income to the engine's closest concept, its HBAI net income (#931), which the LCFS and ETB services stages also read as a predictor (microcosm#1164). The donor's income is WAS `DVTotinc_bhcR8`, net regular household income before housing costs less council tax. The recipient's is policyengine-uk's `hbai_household_net_income_before_reset_to_zero`, HBAI income before housing costs and before negative incomes are reset to zero, in place of `household_net_income`, which also takes off stamp duty, the TV licence and the business rates and corporate stamp duty that `shareholding` spreads over the households the engine simulates, and adds the imputed value of NHS, education and transport spending. Both sides call the predictor `hbai_household_net_income`. The two incomes are close but not the same: the recipient's also counts HBAI's in-kind items (free school meals, school milk and fruit, the free TV licence's value, Healthy Start vouchers and the Tax-Free Childcare top-up) and the Warm Home Discount, which WAS's regular income is not documented to include, and how WAS treats pension contributions, maintenance paid, student loan repayments and negative incomes is not verified.

- **What moves.** The WAS chain keeps its segments, order and seeds, but every WAS draw can move. So do the Lifetime ISA draws, the later stages that read wealth (means-tested capital, CGT selection, property components and high-value support), and so the calibrated weights on both lines. A sampled f010 arm measures the change.
- **Declarations.** `was_wealth` declares the donor column in its `direct_maps`, and the stage refuses a declared direct map its donor cleaning does not read. `was_lisa`'s declared engine reads now list what it materializes, without `num_adults` and `num_children`, which come from the FRS family roles. Its realised profile keys its income tertiles `by_hbai_household_net_income_tertile`, and the ownership coefficients `log1p_hbai_household_net_income`.
