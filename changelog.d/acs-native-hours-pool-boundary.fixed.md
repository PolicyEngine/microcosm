The US multispine pool builder accepts the native ACS usual-hours mapping. Since `map_acs_native_inputs` began mapping `WKHP`, both pool entries refused the real ACS 2024 archive before assembly because the operator boundary had no contract for `weekly_hours_worked_before_lsr`. The boundary now declares it, recomputes it from the raw ACS columns, and requires the column and receipt to match. ACS rows keep their measured hours; the ASEC-to-ACS gap fill covers only the cells the source leaves blank (everyone under 16). Stage checkpoints written from an ACS arm without raw `WKHP` are rebuilt.
