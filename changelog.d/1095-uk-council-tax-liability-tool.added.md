`tools/compare_uk_council_tax_liability.py` compares the engine's council tax (`simulated_council_tax` and `simulated_council_tax_less_discounts`, PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk#1855 and #1864) with the stored FRS bill, `council_tax`, on a dense release (CT-C, microcosm#1095, #953, #736). It writes weighted totals and ratios for Great Britain, each nation and each region, and weighted means and ratios for each local authority and band that at least ten distinct source households carry. A cell never gives a total or a weighted count, so a published total less the published cells cannot give a withheld cell back. It is a private diagnostic and never recalibrates.
