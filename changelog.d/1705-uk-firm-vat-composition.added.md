Add an opt-in, columns-only UK firm VAT composition (`firm_vat_composition.py`, `--vat-composition-dir`, needs `--consumption-dir`). It attaches Microcosm-only `firm_sut_*` columns by SIC section: the standard-, reduced- and zero-rated and exempt shares of turnover, the export share, the input VAT rate and the input ratio. These come from the ONS Supply and Use Tables 2024 (Blue Book 2026), read through the VAT Act 1994 Schedules 7A, 8 and 9, with ABS 2024 turnover as the gross-sales base for trade. No policyengine-uk input changes, so VAT is still scored on all-standard-rated supplies. The receipt reports the section shares and checks eight relief bases against HMRC tax relief statistics (January 2026); these are never targets. The calibration seam (`UKFirmVATCompositionRules`, `rescale_uk_firm_input_costs`) is kept as experimental code. It failed review: the VAT liability band accuracy was 0.998973 against a 0.999 bar, and the held-out error of VAT by section doubled.
