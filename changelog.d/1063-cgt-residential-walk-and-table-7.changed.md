The UK `hmrc_cgt_asset_type_spine` stage walks the residential-property flag within each HMRC gain band and holds the Table 7 composition in the type assignment (microcosm#1063). One weighted systematic walk over every liable gainer let the balance a heavy row left behind be paid by the next row whatever its weight, so a light row at the top of the gains order was flagged far more often than its own probability, and one such row could move the realised residential gains by most of the Table 8 total. Each gain band now has its own walk, offset and balance. The receipt reports per band the pool, the expected and realised count and gains, and the walk's own bounds: the count within `max(offset, 1 - offset)` of the band's largest weight, the gains within that weight times (largest gain - `min(offset, 1 - offset)` x smallest gain). It also reports the share of the realised residential gains on the ten largest flagged stakes and the largest single stake in the pool against the target. `uk_stage_hmrc_cgt_asset_type_spine_summary` holds every band to those bounds and the totals to the wider of the 5 percent band and the summed band bounds; the Bernoulli sigma term and its `maximum_gains_sigma` parameter are retired, because the sigma assumed independent draws and shrank when rows were split. A zero-weight row can no longer be flagged. The main asset type is fitted once per claimant status: Business Asset Disposal Relief and Investors' Relief claimants to Table 7's shares among the three types the reliefs apply to, non-claimants to the shares that restore the published composition overall. It is realised by an error-diffusion walk in descending gain order instead of an independent draw per person, so the realised gains by type track the fit rather than following a handful of heavy rows. That assignment is deterministic, and its seed (554) is retired. The receipt reports the composition by claimant status against Table 7; the fact fence and the diagnostic-only status of the composition are unchanged.
