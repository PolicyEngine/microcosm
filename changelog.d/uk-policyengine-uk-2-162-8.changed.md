The UK lock moves from policyengine-uk 2.162.3 to 2.162.8 for the apprentice minimum wage's split from `is_apprentice` (policyengine-uk#2683, released in 2.162.8 because 2.162.7 never reached PyPI): the apprentice rate now applies only under 19 or with `is_in_first_year_of_apprenticeship`, which the build leaves at its default (False, signed), so the employees `frs_person_draws` flags keep their age-related minimum wage (#1373). The move also uprates reported council tax reduction with each household's council tax in projected years (policyengine-uk#2680), which changes values read at 2025 from the 2024 frame wherever the engine uses reported council tax reduction; screens income-related ESA and income-based JSA reports separately for people outside the claimant couple (policyengine-uk#2679); holds Universal Credit advance repayments at their cash amount under allowance reforms (policyengine-uk#2574; no baseline change); and annualises fiscal parameters past 2040 (2.162.4).
