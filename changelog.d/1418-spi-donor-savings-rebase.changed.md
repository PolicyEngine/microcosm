The SPI income stage now rebases the 2022-23 tape's interest and other income to HMRC's published 2023-24 Table 3.7 amounts band by band (the tape banded by its own total income; the published rows are vendored as `uk/hmrc_spi_table_3_7_savings_rows.json`), then carries both to the frame's year on the OBR's savings income growth (October 2026 EFO, Table 4.5), the path the Table 3.7 band targets take. Before, each column moved by its engine index from 2022 to 2024: other income by GDP per head (x1.09) where SPI's own other income grew x1.65 in the single year to 2023-24, and interest by ONS household interest (x2.27) where SPI's grew x2.69 in that year. The frame then carried about 70 percent of the Table 3.7 savings rows at design weights, and the solve met them by stretching the weights of the few households that carry them. On the tape, the £1m-and-over band's other income outweighs its interest, as in the published table, so the rebase also undoes the split the two indices had flipped (#1418 savings follow-up).
