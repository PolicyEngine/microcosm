`microcosm.build.uk_runtime.hbai_validation` sets four statistics of a UK dataset beside DWP's Households Below Average Income estimates for FYE 2025 and their 95% confidence intervals, read from the pinned Chronicle feed with the release they came from: the median equivalised household income and the share of individuals in relative low income, each before and after housing costs (microcosm#731, uk-data#487). The dataset is measured at engine year 2024, which is FRS FY2024/25 and so HBAI FYE 2025; the scorecard (`docs/evidence/uk-poverty-968/scripts/scorecard_731.py`) compared that year with FYE 2024 before. Each statistic is reported inside or outside its interval; the validation never binds a row and never fails a build (María's rulings of 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-20). The scorecard's other HBAI-shaped rows stay as diagnostics without a reference.
