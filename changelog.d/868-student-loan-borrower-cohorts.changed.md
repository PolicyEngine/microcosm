The UK `student_loans` stage reads the FRS borrowers below the repayment threshold and dates each borrower's course from the age they left full-time education (María's ruling of 2026-10-16, microcosm#868). The `frs_education` stage stores two adult-table answers for it: `reports_outstanding_student_loan` is SLOS ("Do you have an outstanding student loan?"), which adults who make no repayment answer, and `age_left_full_time_education` is EDUCLEFT, with 0 for no answer. A reported borrower now repays, as before, or, in England or Wales, reports an outstanding loan and is not in full-time education. A course starts three years before its borrower left full-time education, or at 18 without an answer, and its start year sets the plan. Before 2026 a repayer never takes Plan 5, and a repayer whose dates point to a course from 2023/24 takes Plan 2: nobody repays a Plan 5 loan before April 2026, so SLC counts no Plan 5 borrower above the threshold in academic year 2024/25. The Plan 2 top-up walk takes nobody in full-time education, since a student is not yet liable to repay; the Plan 5 walk keeps the age rule. The four SLC borrower rows bind the stock the stage tops up to, SLC academic year 2024/25, whose reference date falls inside calendar 2025: Chronicle stores it under the opening year 2024, so each row pins that period and a signed in-year-snapshot hold declares it. Before, the rows bound academic year 2025/26 while the stage topped up to 2024/25. Both new columns are new to the export surface and join its reviewed extra columns. A tab without SLOS or EDUCLEFT refuses the build.
