A placed private renter's rent now comes from its assigned area's list of rents (microcosm#1262, B2; María's rulings of 2026-10-15/16). `tools/build_uk_brma_rent_quantiles.py` writes `uk/brma_rent_quantiles.json`, 25 quantile knots of the weekly rent for every Great Britain BRMA and LHA category B to E: England from VOA's April 2026 shadow list (lets from October 2024 to September 2025), Wales from Rent Officers Wales' list for October 2023 to September 2024 (Table 2 of FOI ATISN 25142), Scotland from the Scottish Government's 2025 quartiles and median by BRMA and bedrooms with the shape of Rent Service Scotland's 2016 to 2021 lettings outside them (implied means within 7 per cent of the published ones). A cell with 20 to 49 lets takes its own median and its nation's pooled shape. The post-geography node `uk.full.private_rent` (`--private-rent-localisation brma_list`, the default with area BRMAs) keeps each Great Britain private renter's rank among its FRS region and bedroom category's FRS-channel renters, in both channels, and reads that quantile off its assigned BRMA's list; one scale per region holds the region's FRS mean rent on the census private-renter mix, so the lists set the pattern within a region and the spread within a BRMA but not the level, and the national line, which keeps FRS rents, agrees on regional levels. Category A is never used; owners, social renters, zero rents and Northern Ireland are untouched; the boarder, lodger, subletting, service-charge and reported Housing Benefit columns stay as reported. `ons.rent.private_rent` stays bound.
