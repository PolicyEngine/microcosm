The `nts_bus_travel` stage reads the National Travel Survey stage and ticket tables (two more pinned SN 5340 artifacts) and gives every person `local_bus_single_fare_share`, the share of their fare-paying local-bus boardings paid at the point of use rather than covered by a season ticket or non-concessionary pass: the boardings a per-journey fare cap binds on (microcosm#930, María's ask of 2026-09-23; the parameter side is policyengine-uk#1871). A diary bus stage is a boarding (`NumBoardings` counts the vehicles) classed by how it was paid for: no ticket record and a positive boarding cost is a fare paid at the point of use, a ticket record with a concessionary code (`SpecialTicket_B01ID` 7 to 11) a concessionary pass, any other ticket record a season ticket or pass, no record and zero cost free travel. Per band and residence group the share is the boarding-weighted ratio of point-of-use to all fare-paying boardings, assigned per person as the trips-weighted combination of the two series' cell shares; the fare paid per boarding by residence group and survey year, the boarding-class shares and the NTS boardings per bus trip are receipted beside it, nothing derived from the licensed rows committed. The column joins the export surface and the stage's E6 support bounds (0 to 1); the spine tool takes `--nts-stage-tab` and `--nts-ticket-tab`, and the H2 fixture's synthetic tabs gain stages and tickets.
