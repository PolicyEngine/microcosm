`tools/run_uk_cut.py` publishes a UK national build through the build-folder publisher (`microcosm-publish-release-build`, which hands it to the emitter) without making it current, and prints a `--promote` command. It reads the build the assembler prints instead of a tag the assembler has not printed since the UK switch, publishes nothing once `releases.json` lists the build, and no longer checks for a `release-staging` branch.
