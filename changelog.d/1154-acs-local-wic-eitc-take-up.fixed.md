The ACS local lane draws WIC and EITC take-up for its ACS rows instead of leaving the engine default, `True`, under which every eligible ACS person took WIC up and every eligible ACS tax unit claimed the EITC (microcosm#1154, microcosm#1675).

- WIC: staging runs the national `wic_claim_input` generator, unchanged, on the ACS-only frame after the shared transfer has completed `is_pregnant` and `own_children_in_household`, and before pooling (`acs_local_wic_take_up`, `build_optional_acs_multispine(wic_take_up=True)`, the design of microcosm#1156). The donor's decisions are kept, and must be complete before the ACS spine is loaded. The release tool refuses a null `takes_up_wic_if_eligible` before any reviewed fill (`NEVER_DEFAULT_FILLED`), the take-up stage refuses an incomplete column, and the take-up gate grades it on both spines.
- EITC: the release tool's take-up stage fills the ACS tax units' `takes_up_eitc` with the take-up contract's seeded draw at the National Taxpayer Advocate's rates by number of qualifying children, as it already draws TANF. At the default, the state SOI EITC targets shrank the weights of eligible ACS units instead of choosing claimants among them. The receipt records the ACS share by number of children, the assignment digest covers the cells, and the take-up gate grades the flag on both spines and the ACS share against the contract's band.
