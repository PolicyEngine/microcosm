The UK root stage sets six of policyengine-uk's eleven owned-company inputs from the FRS job tab, the inputs through which Universal Credit treats a company's owner as self-employed (UC Regs 2013 reg 77, pe-uk#1965; microcosm#1095). A director (DIRECTOR 1, or NATURE 1 "Director") who owns at least half of the company (DIRPERC 100%, more than 50% or 50%; María's ruling of 2026-10-16) stands as its sole owner or partner and works in it (`stands_as_sole_owner_or_partner_of_company`, `is_engaged_in_owned_company_trade`). The company is their main employment when the job is their first (`owned_company_is_main_employment`). It carries on a property business when the main job's SIC division is 68, real estate, and a trade otherwise (`owned_company_carries_on_property_business`, `owned_company_carries_on_trade`). A company held in another job carries on neither, since the FRS codes the industry of the main job only, so the engine's company-owner rules stay off for it. The income share (`owned_company_income_share`) is the dividends the job records over 12 months, after tax: the engine reads the company's profit, which the survey does not ask, and the dividends paid out of it are the part it sees. The stocks and the intermediaries inputs keep the engine's defaults. On SPI-redrawn rows the stage-2 FRS fill draws the six inputs last, the flags as 0 or 1, so they follow the drawn incomes, and the root stage's rules then hold on those rows too. The six columns are new to the export surface and join its reviewed extra columns; a job tab without the director answers refuses.
