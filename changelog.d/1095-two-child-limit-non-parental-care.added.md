The UK spine now sets policyengine-uk's `child_limit_non_parental_caring_exception`, the two-child limit's exception for non-parental caring arrangements (UC Regs 2013 Sch 12 para 4; CTC Regs 2002 reg 12), for children in kinship or friends care (microcosm#1095). Nothing set it before, so the engine paid no child element for such a third or later child born from 6 April 2017.

The `frs_relationships` stage marks a child-table member whom no adult of their own benefit unit claims as a natural, adopted or step child, read from either side of the household grid (codes 3 and 4 from the child, 7 and 8 from the adult), and whom no local authority placed there. That covers a child living with grandparents, siblings, other relatives or friends, and a baby whose parent is a child of the claimant's unit. The multiple births, adoption and non-consensual conception exceptions stay at the engine's default: the FRS has no twin field and its birth dates are blank.
