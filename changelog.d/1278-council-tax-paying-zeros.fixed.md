A UK household where someone pays council tax but whose derived bill is zero now takes its cell's mean bill (microcosm#1278). From April 2024 the band and some other council-tax questions left the FRS field interview, and DWP imputes those answers during its validation (FRS 2024-25 background information and methodology, section 1.3), yet some households that answer yes to CTPaid ("Does anyone living here pay Council Tax?") and report no council tax reduction carry a zero `CTANNUAL`, which `frs_council_tax` kept, leaving England's share of zero bills well above Wales's and Scotland's. A household with `CTPAID` 1, `CTREB` not 1 and a zero `CTANNUAL` is now imputed as a missing bill is, from the mean of the positive non-recipient bills in its region, band and single-adult cell. A non-recipient's zero bill otherwise stays zero: the household does not have to pay (`CTPAID` 3), or says the council pays (`CTPAID` 2) without reporting a reduction. Reduction recipients keep their rules, and the bill level, low against published Band D averages, waits until after the Budget. The stage's `impute_cell_means` declaration names the new missing rule and the stage refuses a raw tab without `CTPAID`. The release input coverage manifest and the H2 spine fixture are regenerated.
