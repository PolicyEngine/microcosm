The UK national scorer (`tools/score_uk_national_candidate.py`, core now in `microcosm.build.uk_runtime.candidate_score`) scores the candidate and the incumbent on the surface both can materialize: a target whose measure the incumbent lacks is pruned from both arms and reported (stderr warning naming every absent measure; `incumbent_unresolvable_pruned` in the receipt) instead of refusing the score, band edges come from the full register, and the receipt's `evaluation` block decides rule 1 (#578) with a `verdict` the release-cut certifier requires to be `passed`. `--no-prune-incumbent-unresolvable` restores the refusal.\n
