The Move to UC claim draw (`uc_legacy_closure_take_up`) now reads each benefit unit's legacy and Universal Credit reports from its claimant and partner only (`is_claimant_or_partner`; microcosm#1095). Since policyengine-uk 2.146.0 (pe-uk#2549) the engine closes a legacy award through its owners, so an award another member claims in their own right closes on its own. The draw counted every member's reports, so such an award could put the family in the draw, or keep it out as a Universal Credit unit.
