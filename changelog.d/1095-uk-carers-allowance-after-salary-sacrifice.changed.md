The UK Carer's Allowance take-up stage now runs after `salary_sacrifice`, so its earnings test reads the pay that salary sacrifice converts into pension contributions (microcosm#1095 B5). That also puts it after the CGT stages' copies, so its draws are keyed on lineage: a member of a CGT support copy, incidence clone or residential arm draws as the member at the same rank of the household it copies (`lineage_roots`), and the balanced placement takes a source and its copies in one stratum as one record of their joint weight (`lineage_balanced_positions`), so the two claim together as before. Original rows keep their draws. The stage spec, the coverage manifest and the H2 fixture follow.
