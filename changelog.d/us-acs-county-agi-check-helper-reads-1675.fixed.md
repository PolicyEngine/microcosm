`tools/check_us_acs_county_agi_support.py` reads AGI's dependency closure through the engine's helper functions (`follow_helpers`), so it now sees the AGI inputs read only inside a helper, such as the two Form 7206 inputs of the self-employed health insurance deduction (microcosm#1675). Through the `get_behavioral_response_measurements` helper, the behavioral responses read `household_net_income`, and through it every benefit and the take-up flags R2's stages fill. An engine pass without a reform, as R2's and the check's are, never reads through them: `labor_supply_behavioral_response` and `capital_gains_behavioral_response` return 0 while the simulation has no baseline, and the labor supply elasticity effects are computed only where the labor supply response is nonzero. So the walk stops at those four variables too (`NO_REFORM_UNREAD`), and the report lists them in `agi_closure_stops_at`.
