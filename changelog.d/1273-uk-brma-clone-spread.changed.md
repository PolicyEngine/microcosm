The UK local pool spreads each spine household's BRMA across its K clones: `uk.full.brma_table` computes each household's exact single-draw BRMA distribution, an LHA-rate order over its region's BRMAs and a lineage-keyed uniform before expansion, and `uk.full.brma` gives clone c the BRMA at quantile `(u + c/K) mod 1`, so each clone keeps the single-draw distribution while the clones average its noise. A terminal `uk_local_brma_enum_domain` gate rechecks the rewritten values; the spine's `frs_brma` stage and its pins are unchanged.
