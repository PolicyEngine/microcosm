The US base build produces `employer_sponsored_insurance_premiums` and `pre_tax_health_insurance_premiums` (microcosm #454). The `meps_esi_premiums` stage, declared but unable to run because the pinned ASEC inputs lack the Census policyholder fields, now reads `NOW_OWNGRP`, `NOW_HIPAID`, `NOW_GRPFTYP2`, `PEMLR` and `NOEMP` restored from the pinned Census person files. Each employed current ESI policyholder takes the MEPS-IC 2025 employer share for their coverage tier, firm size and State (census division for government employers), scaled so the weighted total equals BEA NIPA Table 7.8 line 17 ($977.0B for 2024); dependents, retirees and the unincorporated self-employed carry zero. An eligible worker's reported premium is routed through payroll at the MEPS-IC firm-size pretax-offer rate. A release must now carry both inputs with signal and hold the employer total within 5% of the BEA anchor and at or below NHE Table 24, on the dense and the sparse export; the verdict ships as the `esi_premiums` gate evidence. The multispine pool does not run the stage yet.
